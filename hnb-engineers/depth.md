# HNB Engineers - story angle

Respect first: a Pune firm since 1973, four decades in sewage and water treatment, 1,500 MLD commissioned, O&M on about 1,000 MLD of plants, and one of the first Namami Gange HAM projects finished at Haridwar (CRISIL, 1 Oct 2025). Operating income Rs 221.53 Cr in FY25, networth Rs 256.1 Cr, adjusted debt/networth 0.26x, order book about Rs 1,279 Cr on 1 Jun 2025.

The question: where do bills wait? CRISIL's one named weakness is working capital. Gross current assets were 345 days at March 2024 and 412 days at March 2025, a 67-day rise while operating income moved from Rs 206.45 Cr to Rs 221.53 Cr. CRISIL ties it to heavy fourth-quarter billing and 90-120 days of credit to government departments and urban local bodies, with payments that "can sometimes get stretched". A rating note cannot say which of those days sit in measurement, certification, the client's desk or a late release. That is the gap: which site, which bill, which desk, since when.

Do not claim: ERP, interest rate, debtor days, any saving. Use GCA days only, same CRISIL basis both years.
