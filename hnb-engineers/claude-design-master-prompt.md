Paste everything below this line into Claude Design. Upload the Crestline logo file before you send it. No company logo is supplied.

## 1. What you are building

A 16-page A4 portrait booklet titled "Working-capital review", printed and posted to the Board of Directors of HNB Engineers Private Limited. You build ONE design and produce one edition, for the single reader in the reader table (see "Reader editions" below).
It answers one question for the reader: what if Crestline were already running at HNB Engineers, how much money and how much time would it save?
Every word and every figure is supplied in the Content section at the end of this prompt.

## 2. Reader editions

Build ONE 16-page design. Then produce one edition of it for the reader in the reader table in the Content section.
- The edition carries three reader-specific things: the cover's "PREPARED EXCLUSIVELY FOR" lines (that reader's name and title), the page 2 letter (that reader's letter text and address block), and the page 2 salutation.
- Every other page is body copy exactly as given. Do not tailor any body page to a reader.
- Name the edition after its reader as given in the table. That label is for the file only and is never printed.
- Body copy is written in a neutral voice. Quotes are credited by name and title, as the Content gives them; never rewrite a credit into "you".

## 3. Rules you must follow

1. Use ONLY the text and figures in the Content section. Do not write new copy, captions, taglines, teasers, pull quotes or headings. Where the Content gives no text for a place on a page, leave it empty.
2. Copy every figure exactly as written: same digits, same decimals, same currency sign, same unit. Do not round, re-total, convert or recompute anything.
3. Headlines exactly as given in the Content, split into two lines as shown. Never reword them.
4. Footnotes exactly as given, on the page they belong to. Where a footnote carries a tag in brackets, print the caveat line that tag stands for (listed under "Keys" in the Content) in its place, in full.
5. Text inside quotation marks in the Content is printed copy. Words outside quotation marks (for example "Data:", "Printed copy:", "Footnote:", "Text only", and notes about decisions and checks) are instructions to you and are never printed.
6. No references to how this was made in printed text. Reference codes, file names, tags in brackets, notes to the team and status words used as markers are never printed. A source is printed only in the words the Content gives for it.
7. No printed negatives about the booklet itself: never print what is missing, unknown, unchecked or left out ("not available", "we could not find", "TBD", "placeholder"). Where nothing is given, leave the space empty. Print a "not public" line only where the Content gives it as quoted copy.
8. No em dashes and no en dashes anywhere. Use a comma, a full stop, "to" or a middle dot (·) as the Content does.
9. Ownership words stay as written: the client certifies and pays; HNB Engineers measures, prepares, submits and escalates. Do not swap them.
10. No HNB Engineers logo is supplied. The company is named in text only. The cover shows the reader's name only and no company mark. Never draw, trace or imitate a logo.

B1. The tagline is exactly "Know where your money is waiting, and who can release it." wherever the Content prints it.
B2. Page 8: each stage carries only its name and its tag: no day values, no totals. If a figure appears on a stage, delete it.
B3. Gross current assets in days are one block. Never split them into work waiting for a bill and bills waiting for payment, and never add a figure that does.
B4. Page 13 is text only: no table, no figure.
B6. Never print a rating grade, a lender name or a borrowing limit. None is given in the Content.

### Booklet accuracy rules (apply to every page)
R1. **Claims about Crestline must be true today.** Make no claim about Crestline beyond the pasted text. No certification "in progress", no hosting region, no encryption claim, no client names, no "live in X days", and no present-tense claim that Crestline connects to the company's SAP or ERP. The pilot is one entity, one segment, read-only, 14 days, as the Content words it.
R2. **Every quote names the right speaker**, exactly as the Content credits it. Never move a quote to another person.
R3. **Ours versus the client's follows the product rule.** In cash-in steps, acknowledged, certified and collected are the client's acts; measuring, preparing, reviewing, submitting and invoicing are the company's own. Keep every OURS and CLIENT tag as given.
R4. **No "nothing went wrong" headlines.** Use only the headlines given; never soften or invert one.
R6. **Interest only at a rate the company discloses.** Print only the rate and interest figures the Content gives; never compute or label a rate as theirs.
R7. **Savings are scenarios.** Anything marked "with Crestline", "scenario" or "days less" is shown as a scenario, labelled "Scenario, not a forecast." Scenario rows overlap and are never added together; print no total the Content does not give. Data the Content marks as history only gets no scenario mark.
R8. **Peers and comparatives** appear only where the Content prints them, with the vintage it gives. Never build a scorecard of missed promises.
R9. **No other company's data.** Do not bring in any company, name, figure or design text from anywhere other than the Content, including from earlier booklets or your memory. No demo numbers beside the company's figures.
R10. **Address, names and titles** on the letter page and cover exactly as the reader table gives them.
R11. **"You said" only for the reader's own words.** Never print "you said", "in your own words" or any second-person credit over a quote by someone other than the reader. Every quotation on these pages is CRISIL Ratings' and is credited to CRISIL Ratings, as the Content gives it. Never print a note or flag on the page.

R12. **Never invent figures to fill a chart.** If the Content gives a stage, step or item without a value (for example the page 8 bill timeline), give it without a number. Never create an "illustrative split" of a total, and never add a sentence about one.
R13. **No signed numbers in print.** Write inflow / outflow, increase / decrease. No "+" or "-" in front of a figure.

R14. **Every problem chart shows the outcome with Crestline.** Beside the "today" figure, give the "with Crestline" result from the Content (labelled with the days and cash it releases, plus "scenario, not a forecast"). Use only the values the Content gives; if the Content gives no scenario value for a chart, show none (rule R12).

## 4. Before you finish, check every page

1. Count the pages: exactly 16, A4 portrait, in the order of the Content.
2. Read every headline against the Content: same words, same punctuation.
3. Pick every figure on every page and find it in the Content, character for character. Any figure you cannot find there, delete it.
4. Search the whole booklet for en dash and em dash characters (Unicode 2013 and 2014). There must be none.
5. Every chart: every item labelled, nothing taken from a figure that is not in the Content.
6. Every scenario carries "Scenario, not a forecast." Data marked "history only" or "no scenario mark" carries none. No scenario totals added.
7. Every footnote is on its page, with each bracketed tag replaced by its full caveat line.
8. No word was added: no extra taglines, no right-hand footer teasers beyond those the Content gives, no extra captions, no "Live in" line, no certification or hosting claim, no client names, no internal references, no printed negatives.
9. No HNB Engineers logo is drawn anywhere. The company is named in text only.
10. The QR code on page 15 (and on page 16) opens crestlineintelligence.com/book-a-demo.
11. Instruction words from the Content ("Data:", "Printed copy:", "Footnote:", notes about decisions and checks) are not printed anywhere.
12. The edition differs from the body copy only in the cover reader lines, the page 2 letter and the salutation. No "you said" sits over a quote.
