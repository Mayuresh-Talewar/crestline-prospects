Detailed rationale

Crisil Ratings has upgraded its ratings on the bank facilities of AIC Infrastructures Pvt Ltd (AIC) to ‘Crisil BBB+/Stable/Crisil A2’ from ‘Crisil BBB/Stable/Crisil A3+’.

 

The upgrade reflects the improvement in AIC’s overall credit profile. Revenue increased to Rs. 457 crores in fiscal 2025, from Rs. 367 crores in fiscal 2023, driven by higher execution of orders. In fiscal 2026, the company has reported revenue of Rs. 307 crores till December 2025 and is expected to report more than Rs. 550 crores for the full fiscal backed by orders in hand of around Rs. 1140 crores. The company was able to sustain its operating margins over 14%, leading to cash accruals of Rs. 45 crores expected in fiscal 2026. The financial risk profile is also comfortable, backed by strong networth and moderate debt levels. Liquidity remains adequate with sufficient accruals against debt repayment and bank limit utilization has reduced with enhancement in bank lines.

 

The ratings reflect the long-standing presence in the civil construction industry, the adequate revenue visibility and healthy debt protection metrices. These strengths are partially offset by the large working capital requirement, moderate capital structure and exposure to risks related to the tender-based business.

Analytical approach

Crisil Ratings has evaluated the standalone business and financial risk profiles of AIC.

 

Unsecured loan of Rs. 5 crores from the promoters is treated as debt.

Key Rating Drivers - Strengths 

Longstanding presence in civil construction industry: The promoter has been engaged in the civil construction business for over 26 years and specialises in aviation infrastructure. Longstanding track record of executing different airport infrastructure projects has given the promoter a strong understanding of market dynamics and enabled him to establish strong relationships with suppliers and customers. Backed by these factors, revenue rose to Rs 457 crore in fiscal 2025, from Rs 132 crore in fiscal 2021. In fiscal 2026, the company reported revenue of Rs. 307 crores till December 2025 and continued efforts towards bidding for profitable projects and focus of the government on infrastructure development should enable AIC to bag more orders going forward.

 

Healthy revenue visibility: Orders worth around Rs 1140 crore to be executed over the next 24-36 months, provide adequate revenue visibility over the medium term. With strong track record in aviation infrastructure which requires strong technical knowhow and capabilities, continued order flow from established customer base should support future orderbook.

 

Strong net worth and healthy debt protection metrices: Net worth is estimated to remain strong at around Rs 118 crore as on March 31, 2025, as against Rs 90 crore, a year earlier, and is expected to grow further, backed by steady accretion to reserve. The debt protection metrics were also comfortable with interest coverage ratio of 2.90 times and net cash accrual to total debt (NCA/TD) ratio of around 0.14 time for fiscal 2025. These metrices are expected to improve over the medium term with increased net worth and repayments of existing debt.

Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses 

Susceptibility to tender-based operations: Revenue is entirely dependent on the ability to win tenders and the timely execution of orders. Though the company has focused on airport infrastructure projects, it has started diversifying and taking up other niche projects. Delay in receipt of regulatory approvals could impact timelines of execution, and hence, sustained growth in revenue and operating margins will be monitored. Although the operating margins have been improving to around 14%, sustenance of the same in the medium term remain key monitorable.

 

Moderate capital structure: The capital structure is moderate, as reflected by gearing and total outside liabilities to adjusted networth (TOL/ANW) ratios of 2.08 times and 3.23 times, respectively, as on March 31, 2025 (1.58 times and 2.56 times, respectively, a year ago). It increased on account of increased debt to fund the improvement in the scale of operations. With increase in networth and internal cash flows, capital structure may improve and will be monitored.

 

Large working capital requirement: Gross current assets stood at 303 days as on March 31, 2025, with bulk of revenue booked in the last quarter of fiscal 2025. Receivables and Inventory were high at 125 days and 155 days respectively as on March 31,2025. The company maintains multiple deposits with customers, in the form of retention money, earnest deposits, security deposits and advances with suppliers, and holds a large inventory (primarily work in progress). The working capital cycle will continue to remain high over the medium term and management of same with increasing revenues will be monitored.

Liquidity Adequate

Bank limit utilisation is moderate at around 85% for the past twelve months ended December 2025. Cash accruals are expected to be over Rs 45 to 55 crore which are sufficient against term debt obligation of Rs 14 to 18 crore over the medium term. Current ratio is healthy at 1.33 times on March 31, 2025. Moderate cash balance including fixed deposits were around Rs. 86.46 crores as of March 31, 2025, mostly encumbered.

Outlook Stable

Crisil Ratings believes AIC’s business risk profile will continue to benefit from the experienced promoter and healthy order book over the medium term.

Rating sensitivity factors

Upward factors

  • Growth in revenue and sustenance of operating margin, leading to higher cash accruals.
  • Improvement in financial risk profile with TOL/ANW below 1.8 times and BLU below 80%

 

Downward factors

  • Decline in revenue or profitability, leading to net cash accrual below Rs 35 crore.
  • Any large debt funded capex or stretch in working capital, adversely affecting financial risk profile and liquidity

About the Company

Incorporated in 2014 in Mumbai and promoted by Mr Kritarth Raja, AIC undertakes aviation infrastructure projects and runway preservation activities. Projects include construction of runways, retaining walls and pavement roads, and expansion of taxiways. The company is also undertaking metro and road projects currently.
