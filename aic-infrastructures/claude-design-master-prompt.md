# Claude Design master prompt: AIC Infrastructures working-capital review (one booklet, one reader)

Paste everything below this line into Claude Design.

---

## 1. What you are building

A 16-page A4 portrait booklet titled "Working-capital review", printed and posted to the Board of Directors of AIC Infrastructures Private Limited. You build ONE booklet, for the one reader in the reader table.
It answers one question for the reader: what if Crestline were already running at AIC Infrastructures, how much money and how much time would it save?
Every word and every figure is supplied in the Content section at the end of this prompt. The Content carries what each page says, in page order. Do not add anything to it.

## 2. Reader

Build the single edition given in the reader table: the cover's "Prepared exclusively for" lines, the page 2 letter and the page 2 salutation come from that table. Body copy is written in a neutral voice. AIC Infrastructures has no logo in this booklet: its name is set as text wherever it appears.

## 3. Rules you must follow

1. Use ONLY the text and figures in the Content section. Do not write new copy, captions, taglines, teasers, pull quotes or headings.
2. Copy every figure exactly as written: same digits, same decimals, same currency sign, same unit. Do not round, re-total, convert or recompute anything.
3. Headlines exactly as given. A slash inside a headline marks where it breaks into two lines and is not printed.
4. Footnotes exactly as given, on the page they belong to.
5. Lines that start with ">" are printed copy. Lines that start with "Data" give the labelled values of the named chart, and those labels and values are shown. Other words that name a chart, a state or a step, such as "Position", "With Crestline", "Stages" and "Chart name", organise the content and are not printed unless a ">" line repeats them.
6. No internal references in printed text: no file names, ticket numbers, notes addressed to the team, or words describing how this prompt was made.
7. No printed negatives about the booklet itself. Print "Not published" or "Not public" only where the Content gives it as a value.
8. No em dashes and no en dashes anywhere. Use a comma, a full stop, "to" or a middle dot (·) as the Content does.
9. Ownership words stay as written: the client acknowledges, certifies and pays; AIC Infrastructures measures, prepares, reviews, submits and invoices. Do not swap them.
10. Do not show any company logo for AIC Infrastructures. Do not invent one.

B1. Charts carry the names the Crestline web app gives them, exactly as written in the Content ("The cash cycle · Where the money sits, and who is holding it" and "Process map · One bill, from work done to cash in the bank"). Show the values given under "Data" and nothing else. A stage or leg with no value stays without one.
B2. Every figure labelled "With Crestline" or "ten days less" or "thirty days less" is a scenario and is labelled "Scenario, not a forecast." wherever it appears. Scenario rows overlap and are never added together; print no total the Content does not give.
B3. Beside every "With Crestline" result, print exactly the two lines the Content gives for it about how the cash is released, word for word, never extended.

### Booklet accuracy rules (apply to every page)
A1. Claims about Crestline must be true today. Make no claim beyond the pasted text: no certification, no hosting region, no encryption claim, no client names of Crestline, no "live in X days", and no present-tense claim that Crestline connects to the company's ERP. The pilot is one to three projects, read-only, 14 days, as the Content words it.
A2. Every source is credited exactly as the Content credits it.
A3. Ours versus the client's follows the product rule: acknowledged, certified and collected are the client's acts; measuring, preparing, reviewing, submitting and invoicing are the company's own. Keep every owner tag as given.
A4. No "nothing went wrong" headlines. Use only the headlines given.
A5. Every value shown in a chart is exactly the value written in the Content, on one basis. Every value carries its label.
A6. Interest appears nowhere. No borrowing rate is published, so none is priced or implied.
A7. Savings are scenarios. Anything marked "With Crestline", "scenario" or "days less" carries "Scenario, not a forecast." Scenario rows overlap and are never added.
A8. Never bring in any other company, name, figure or text from anywhere other than the Content, including from earlier booklets or memory.
A9. Full address, names and titles on the letter page and cover exactly as the reader table gives them.
A10. "You said" is not used. Quotes, if any, are credited to the speaker as the Content gives it.
A11. Never invent figures. Process map stage estimates appear only as the Content gives them, each tagged "Estimate", with the basis line printed. Never create any other split of a total.
A12. No signed numbers in print. Write inflow / outflow, increase / decrease. No "+" or "-" in front of a figure.
A13. Never compute a figure that is not given in the Content.

## 4. Before you finish, check every page

1. Count the pages: exactly 16, in the order of the Content.
2. Read every headline against the Content: same words, same punctuation.
3. Pick every figure on every page and find it in the Content, character for character. Any figure you cannot find there, delete it.
4. Search the whole booklet for en dash and em dash characters. There must be none.
5. Every chart shows only the values given under "Data", each with its label.
6. Every scenario value carries "Scenario, not a forecast." No scenario totals are added.
7. Every footnote is on its page.
8. No word was added: no taglines, no teasers, no extra captions, no certification or hosting claim, no internal references, no printed negatives.
9. There is no logo for AIC Infrastructures; its name is text.
10. The page 15 booking address is crestlineintelligence.com/book-a-demo.
11. Instruction words from the Content ("Data", "Position", "Stages", "Chart name") are not printed unless a ">" line repeats them.
12. Page 13 carries no numbers in its text.
