# AIC Infrastructures: problems (AI-S23)

Source for every figure: CRISIL Ratings rationale of 13 February 2026, standalone, year to 31 March.

Respect first: operating income rose from Rs 284.17 Cr (FY24) to Rs 457.07 Cr (FY25), up 61% (Crestline's arithmetic); profit after tax from Rs 16.79 Cr to Rs 28.02 Cr; orders in hand of around Rs 1,140 Cr to be executed over the next 24 to 36 months; FY26 revenue Rs 307 Cr to December 2025, with more than Rs 550 Cr expected for the year (CRISIL's statement).

1. The wait to be paid. CRISIL records receivables at 125 days at 31 March 2025, with the bulk of FY25 revenue booked in the last quarter. One day of FY25 operating income is Rs 1.2522 Cr, so ten days is Rs 12.52 Cr and thirty days Rs 37.57 Cr, released once (scenario, not a forecast). The accounts cannot say which site, which bill, which client or which desk holds those days.
2. Work in progress. CRISIL records inventory, primarily work in progress, at 155 days, and gross current assets at 303 days (CRISIL's figure; the three are never added). Not priced: no cost base is published.
3. Money held at customers and suppliers. CRISIL names retention money, earnest deposits, security deposits and advances with suppliers. No amount is published.
