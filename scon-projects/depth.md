# SCON Projects: depth (story angle)

Respect first, then the question. Every figure below is in data.md with its document and basis (standalone, Rs crore, year to 31 March).

## Respect first
- Revenue Rs 445.50 Cr in FY22 to Rs 1,355.55 Cr in FY25: three times in three years, built on repeat industrial clients (CRISIL, 5 Feb 2026).
- CRISIL upgraded the company twice in 12 months: to A- (Feb 2025) then to A / Stable / A1 (5 Feb 2026), citing "timely completion of orders" and "steady order flow".
- Operating margin 9.4% to 15.5% and net cash accrual Rs 61 Cr to Rs 163 Cr (FY24 to FY25). Gearing 0.25x. Liquidity rated "Strong".
- Order book Rs 2,248 Cr as on 31 Oct 2025.
- Gross current asset days came down from 148 to 167 (three years to FY24) to 100 at 31 Mar 2025. A real discipline gain while revenue grew 58%.
- Safety and quality record on the company's own site: ISO 9001, 14001, 45001 and a long list of CIDC, PCERF, BAI and ICI awards.

## Where the question starts (the accounts stop here)
CRISIL itself names the open issue: working capital is "moderate" and "sustenance of the working capital management will remain monitorable". The downward trigger is "further stretch in the working capital cycle". What the accounts cannot show:
1. Which of the 100 gross-current-asset days is a bill waiting for the client's certification, which is retention, which is stock on site, which is margin money.
2. Where those days sit by project and by client. Rs 3.7 Cr a day of revenue (Computed, 1,355.55 / 365) means each day of waiting is a large sum, but the accounts give a single year-end number.
3. How that figure holds when the order book (Rs 2,248 Cr, 1.66x FY25 revenue) is executed at pace across Maharashtra (over 80% of revenue) and new states.
4. Non-fund-based limits are Rs 350 Cr of Rs 416.85 Cr: the company runs on bank guarantees. Every guarantee held against a delayed or retained bill is headroom not available for the next bid. CRISIL: the company "has to bid continuously, which requires increasing bank limit, especially non-fund-based limits" (Jun 2024).
5. Debtor, creditor and inventory days are not published, so the split is the thing no document answers.

## Candidate angles for step 2 (to be proven or dropped by the calc, not claimed here)
- "A hundred days, one number": the rating shows the total; which stage and which desk holds each day?
- Growth that must not stretch the cycle: the agency's own trigger, framed as a watch-point the company already tracks.
- Guarantees and retention: money trapped behind completed work.
- The company's own scale-up: the same discipline across more sites, one view.

## Cautions for copy
- Never say how the company runs its books or which system it uses: ERP NOT FOUND.
- Do not use "mid-size", "Rs 325 Cr" or "tight liquidity": all contradicted by CRISIL.
- Any interest figure needs a disclosed rate: none exists, so none is printed.
- GCA days are CRISIL's measure, not debtor days; do not relabel.
- The sensitive file is Garvis only; nothing in it goes into the booklet.
