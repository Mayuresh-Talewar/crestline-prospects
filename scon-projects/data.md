## Conflicts between sources
1. Revenue: dossier and gtm ~Rs 325 Cr vs CRISIL Rs 1,355.55 Cr (FY25). CRISIL wins.
2. FY22 operating income: CRISIL 445.50 (D2) vs CARE 444.55 (D3). FY22 PAT: CRISIL 29.17 vs CARE 28.36. FY22 interest coverage: CRISIL 8.28 (D2) vs 8.87 (D4 older version) vs CARE 7.78 / 8.12. Different agencies' definitions. Use CRISIL for the series; if FY22 is printed, name the agency.
3. FY23 income: provisional Rs 658.57 Cr, PAT 17.09 (D4, rationale of 2023) vs Rs 654.81 Cr, PAT 14.81 (D2, 14 Jun 2024). Use the later (D2).
4. Projects count: CARE Nov 2022 "325+"; CRISIL Jun 2024 "over 250"; CRISIL Feb 2026 "over 325". Agencies disagree; print only with name and date, or leave out.
5. GCA days: CARE FY22 262 days vs CRISIL "148-167 in three fiscals through FY24". Different definitions and years. Never put them on one trend line; the CRISIL 148-167 -> 100 pair is the only same-basis pair.
6. Registered/head address: Warje (company site) vs Kothrud (dossier via companydetails.in). Unresolved without MCA, which is out of scope.
7. Rating history dates: D1 history table lists "04-02-25 Crisil A-/Stable" and "14-06-24 Crisil BBB+/Stable", while D2 (14 Jun 2024) reaffirms BBB+/A2 and D1 says upgraded "from A-/A2+". So A- came on 4 Feb 2025 (upgrade from BBB+), A on 5 Feb 2026. Consistent; note only that the D2 amounts differ from D1's current ones because limits were enhanced.
