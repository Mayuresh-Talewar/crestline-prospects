# SCON Projects: problems (SC-2 story, respect first)

All figures: CRISIL Ratings rationale, Scon Projects Private Limited, 5 Feb 2026, standalone, Rs crore, years to 31 March, except the Computed lines (formula shown) and the scenario cash (calc/scon-savings-output.txt).

## Respect first
- Operating income Rs 859.89 Cr (FY24) to Rs 1,355.55 Cr (FY25); reported PAT Rs 53.97 Cr to Rs 152.67 Cr; operating margin 9.4% to 15.5% (CRISIL, 5 Feb 2026).
- Crisil A / Stable / Crisil A1 after the upgrade of 5 Feb 2026.
- Gross current assets days came down from 148 to 167 (three fiscals through FY24) to 100 as on 31 Mar 2025, while operating income grew 57.6% (1,355.55 / 859.89, Computed).

## Problem 1: one number, many queues (p5)
The rating carries 100 days as one company figure. CRISIL names what is inside: "moderate inventory, receivables including retentions, and other contractual current assets". Each belongs to a different desk, project and client; no split is published.
- Value of a day: Rs 1,355.55 Cr / 365 = Rs 3.7138 Cr (FY25).
- Scenario: 10 days less (100 to 90) = Rs 37.1 Cr released once; 30 days less (100 to 70) = Rs 111.4 Cr released once. Overlapping rows, never added. No interest (no rate published).
- What the accounts cannot say: which stage, which project, which client, since when.

## Problem 2: growth is the test (p6)
Order book Rs 2,248 Cr as on 31 Oct 2025, roughly 1.66 times FY25 operating income (dates differ). CRISIL: "The sustenance of the working capital management will remain monitorable", and a "further stretch in the working capital cycle", with total outside liabilities to adjusted net worth above 1.8 times, is a downward factor.
- At this scale one day is Rs 3.71 Cr, ten days Rs 37.1 Cr (scenario).
- What the accounts cannot say: how the 100 days hold as the order book executes, site by site.
