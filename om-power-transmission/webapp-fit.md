# Om Power Transmission Limited: webapp-fit.md (OP-S23)

Om Power Transmission is an EPC company (transmission line 52%, substation 22%, underground cabling 19%, operation and maintenance 7% of FY26 revenue, AR 2025-26 p.42). It bills the client in RA bills against measured work, so the product's own bill timeline applies directly.

## Screens that are true today and fit
| Crestline screen | What it answers for this company | Figure on the published accounts |
|---|---|---|
| Working Capital (cash-cycle diagram, Position and Simulate) | Where the money sits and who holds each leg | DIO 24.4, contract-asset days 19.5, DSO 179.9, DPO 129.8, cycle 74.5 |
| Working Capital (ranked levers) | Which move first | Ranked on cash released once, no interest priced |
| O2C | Which RA bills wait on whose desk | 179.9 days to be paid, 19.5 days to be billed |
| Client | Which client holds the days | Ageing from due date is published; client names are not attached to it |
| P2P and Vendors | Vendor timing against each vendor's own terms | 123.55 Cr payables; terms not published, so no target is drawn |
| Alerts and Tickets | Owner, date, re-measure, close on the number | Fixed product copy |
| Ask Crestline | A what-if inside the contract's bounds | "Ten days sooner" worked on the published figures |

## What the accounts cannot show (the product's job)
Which bill, which project, which desk, since when. How much of the 221.42 Cr is retention (it sits inside receivables and no separate balance is published, AR p.132). No client payment term or certification window is published, so the simulator floors are zero and no "to the stated term" scenario exists.

## Not fitted, and said so
Vendor terms (not published): vendor credit is shown, never priced. Interest: no single borrowing rate is published, none is priced. Process map: only the payment wait is anchored on a published figure; the stages before the invoice are estimates and are tagged.

## Ownership (product rule)
Ours: measure, prepare, review, submit, invoice. Client's: acknowledge, certify, pay. Joint measurement is both.

## ERP
No filing names the ERP (only "accounting software with audit trail from June 2025", note 42). Copy says "ERP". Pilot scope phrase: "one to three EPC projects".
