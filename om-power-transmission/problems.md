# Om Power Transmission Limited: problems.md (OP-S23)

Standalone, audited Annual Report 2025-26 (accounts dated 19 May 2026) unless stated. Rs Cr. Respect first: revenue rose 61% from 279.44 (FY25) to 449.16 (FY26), EBITDA 57.11, profit after tax 40.02 (up 81%), order book 621.28 at year end, shares listed on NSE and BSE on 17 April 2026 (AR p.12, p.109). Management says "a working capital cycle of around 75 days keeps capital productive across a growing order book" (AR p.27); Crestline's arithmetic on the same accounts gives 74.5, so the two agree.

## Problem 1: the wait to be paid grew faster than the work
Receivables went from 90.11 to 221.42 (FY25 to FY26) while revenue went from 279.44 to 449.16. Receivable days (Crestline's arithmetic: 221.42 / 449.16 x 365) moved from 117.7 to 179.9 (FY24 on the earlier filing: 139.6). Ageing from due date at 31 March 2026 (p.131): not due 176.11, under six months 30.27, six to twelve months 10.18, one to two years 7.99, two to three years 0.53, over three years 1.61. What the accounts cannot say: which bills, which projects, which desk, how much is retention (inside receivables, p.132).
With Crestline (scenario, not a forecast): ten days less releases 12.31 Cr once, thirty days 36.92 Cr.

## Problem 2: work done waits to become a bill
Contract assets (unbilled work in progress) rose from 6.68 to 24.05, from 8.7 to 19.5 days on revenue. The accounts say "increase in contract assets is primarily due to higher revenue recognition as compared to progress billing" (p.142). Measure, prepare, review and submit are the company's own steps; acknowledge and certify are the client's.
With Crestline: ten days less releases 12.31 Cr once. A thirty-day cut is below zero (only 19.5 days wait) and is not shown.

## Problem 3: operating cash turned
Cash from operations was an inflow of 3.53 (FY24), 16.87 (FY25 as restated in the 2025-26 report; 12.45 as first filed) and an outflow of 25.63 in FY26, against profit of 40.02. The cash flow statement (p.110) shows receivables absorbing 134.71, vendors funding 78.02 and inventories absorbing 8.44. Vendor credit stands at 129.8 days (payables 123.55); it is shown as a fact, never counted as a better position when higher.
With Crestline: one combined run of the three moves, ten days each, releases 31.13 Cr once and takes the cycle from 74.5 to 54.5 days. Project materials alone: 6.52 Cr at ten days.
