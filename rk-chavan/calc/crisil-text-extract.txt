Detailed Rationale
Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its ratings on the bank facilities of R K Chavan Infrastructure Private Limited (RKCIPL) at ‘Crisil BBB+/Stable/Crisil A2’.
The ratings continue to reflect the extensive experience of the promoters and established market position of RKCIPL in civil construction industry, adequate order book providing revenue visibility and healthy financial profile. These strengths are partially offset by large working capital requirement risks related to toll collection business and exposure to SPV setup for new HAM project. 
 | 
 | 
  | 
Analytical Approach
Crisil Ratings has moderately consolidated the business and financial risk profiles of RKCIPL and its special purpose vehicle (SPV), Bhoyegaon Gadchandur Roadways Pvt Ltd (BGRPL) and RKCIPL Karmala Tembhurni Highways Pvt Ltd This is because both the entities have common management and operational and financial linkages. Crisil Ratings has factored in the funding aid to the extent of support required and other cash flow.
Please refer to Annexure - List of entities consolidated, which captures the list of entities considered and their analytical treatment of consolidation.

 | 
  | 
Key Rating Drivers - Strengths 
Extensive experience of the promoters and established market position:  The promoters have more than two decades of experience in the roads construction industry that has enabled them to form an understanding of the market and industry. Resultantly, the company has been able to build an established market position on the back of timely execution of projects allotted by central and state government agencies such as NHAI, PWD, MORTH etc. The successful execution of projects in segments of roads, bridges and tunnels has helped the company to clock steady growth in revenue over the years, evident by compound annual growthy rate (CAGR) of nearly 20% during past four fiscals through fiscal 26. Revenue stood at around Rs. 700 crores in fiscal 2026 which had grown at 34% from Rs. 524 crores in fiscal 2025. 
Healthy order book providing revenue visibility: RKCIPL has healthy order book for EPC contracts of Rs 2595 crores  as of March 2026, to be executed over the next 24-36 months and toll contracts of around Rs. 625 crores which provides significant revenue visibility for the medium term. 
Healthy financial risk profile: Financial risk profile of the company is driven by estimated networth of more than Rs. 215 crores as on March 31, 2026. Geaing though increased has remained below 1 time as on March 31, 2026 against 0.59 times as on March 31, 2025, driven by increase in project specific dropline overdraft of Rs. 100 crores availed in fiscal 2026. Total outside liability to adjusted net worth is estimated to remain in the range of 1.9-2 times as on March 31, 2026. 
Interest coverage ratio is estimated to be around 4.7 times and net cash accruals to adjusted debt to be around 0.3 times for fiscal 2026, against 5.10 times and 0.45 times, respectively, for fiscal 2025. Sustenance of financial risk profile over the medium term will remain key monitorable.
 | 
 | 
  | 
Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses 
Working capital-intensive operations: Gross current assets estimated at around 250 days as on March 31, 2026 which increased from 187 days as on March 31, 2025, owing to higher debtors in the range of 100-140 days due to high revenue booking in Q4 and retenetion money. Inventory remains in the range of 70-90 days which majorky represents work in progress. Improvement in working capital cycle over the medium term will be closely monitored. 
Exposure to HAM project and toll collection business: RKCIPL has been awarded a HAM project under SPV- RKCIPL Karmala Tembhurni Highways Pvt Ltd. The project is at the nascent stages and concession agreement is yet to be executed. The project cost is estimated to be more than Rs. 800 crores and may require siginifcant investments from RKCIPL as well. Funding of the investment and timely completion of project with no cost overrun will remain key monitorable, as it can adversely impact the financial risk profile and liquidity position of RKCIPL. Further, the company has also entered toll collection business, which is yet to show track record of profitable operations and will also require significant working capital requirements. Funding of same and maintenance of profitability expectation amidst this also is a monitorable.
 | 
 | 
  | 
Liquidity Adequate 
Bank limit utilisation is moderate at around 80% for the past eleven months ended February 26. Cash accruals are expected to be over Rs 60 crore which is sufficient against term debt obligation of Rs 24-33 crore over the medium term. Cash and bank balance of around Rs. 25 crore as on March 31, 2026 further supports liquidity. 
 | 
 | 
  | 
Outlook Stable
Crisil Ratings believes RKCIPL will continue to benefit from the extensive experience of its promoters and adequate order book.

Rating sensitivity factors
Upward factors:
	Increase in revenue and sustenance of healthy profitability at above 10% leading to better cash accruals. 
	Efficient working capital cycle and improvement in financial risk profile. 
Downward factors:
	Decline in revenue or profitability, leading to cash accrual less than Rs 40 crore 
	Large debt-funded capital expenditure or increase in working capital or exposure to SPVs, leading to gearing more than 1.5 time

About the Group
Incorporated in 2011, RKCIPL is owned and managed by Rajkumar Chavan and his son Mr. Shubham Chavan. Based in Pune, Maharashtra, the company constructs roads, tunnels and bridges.
BGRPL is a hybrid annuity model SPV carrying out a project under Maharashtra Public Works Department for upgrade of the 2-lane Bhoyegaon-Gadchandur Road (SH-373), total length of 21 km (3rd call); and Gadchandur-Patan-Wani Road (SH-373), total length of 53 km under hybrid annuity package.
RKCIPL Karmala Tembhurni Highways Pvt Ltd is a SPV carrying out a project under NHAI for four Laning of to Karmala to Tembhurni section of NH-561 (PKG-||| of Ahmednagar to Tembhrni corridor ) From Ch.80.390 to Ch.139.750 in Maharashtra,

The revision in rating symbols for PPMLDs should not be construed as a change in the rating of the subject instrument. For details on Crisil Ratings' use of 'PP-MLD' please refer to the notes to Rating scale for Debt Instruments and Structured Finance Instruments at the following link: https://www.crisilratings.com/en/home/our-business/ratings/credit-ratings-scale.html
