## Disconfirm step (one search per headline)
- **Order book Rs 1,702.68 Cr (31 Dec 2024).** Searched Infomerics and the other six agencies for any later rationale: none newer than 23 Apr 2025 found. The figure cannot be refreshed and is 21 months old. **Conflict inside the same PR (p3):** it calls the book "3x of FY24 revenue" (36 months of FY24 revenue, calc) yet "to be executed in a period of 15-18 months", which implies a run rate of about Rs 1,135 to 1,362 Cr a year (calc), roughly double FY24. The FY24 and 9M FY25 revenue do not show that pace. Do not print an execution period or a "visibility" claim as fact; print the book, its date, and Infomerics' own words only.
- **Revenue and PAT.** Cross-read against the 6 Mar 2025 PR: same figures (567.63, 38.39). Heading differs (Standalone there, Consolidated here); approach and Annexure 4 say standalone.
- **Liquidity "cured".** The dossier says the PF-dues episode is cured. The upgrade PR itself (p4) records a fresh delay in Feb 2025 "on account of technical reasons" per auditors. Not cured on the record; stated as the auditors' view.
- **Leaders.** Site names three brothers; the PR names the same three. Neither gives titles.
- **ERP.** Search on ERP, Tally, SAP with the company name: zero results.
