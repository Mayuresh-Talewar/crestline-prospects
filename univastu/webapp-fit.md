# Univastu India: where Crestline fits (UV-S23)

Univastu India Limited is a Pune civil EPC company (metro, hospitals, sports, water, roads). Its Annual Report FY26 (consolidated, published August 2026) names the Quadra ERP as running "across all project sites" (Management System section). Crestline sits above Quadra, read-only. An adapter for Quadra does not exist yet; it is built first in the pilot (one to three EPC projects, read-only, 14 days). No page says Crestline reads Quadra today.

| Their situation (AR FY26, consolidated, 31 Mar 2026) | Crestline screen that answers it today |
|---|---|
| Unbilled revenue 66.36 Cr and net contract assets 87.47 Cr (153.8 Cr together; 230.7 days of FY26 revenue) | Project Overview waterfall and stage waits; Working Capital simulator (contract asset leg, "Built, waiting to be certified") |
| Current trade receivables 7.23 Cr to 80.63 Cr in a year | O2C screen and Client screen: which bill, which client, against that client's own terms |
| "Improving cash conversion and timely collections" (MD) | Ranked levers ("Do this first"), tickets, auditor that re-measures the figure |
| Who holds a bill | Ownership follows the act that ends the wait: measure, prepare, review, submit and invoice are Univastu's; acknowledge, certify and pay are the client's |
| Quadra ERP | Connector contract: adapter emits the universal event shape; nothing downstream changes |

Not used: P2P and vendor lenses (no vendor figure is published on a usable basis), ageing, any rating or interest view. Lenses with no public figure read "Not published".
