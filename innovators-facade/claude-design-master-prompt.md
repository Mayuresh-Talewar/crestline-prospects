# Claude Design master prompt: Innovators Facade Systems working-capital review (one booklet, per-reader editions)

Paste everything below this line into Claude Design. Upload the Crestline logo file. No Innovators logo file is supplied.

## 1. What you are building

A 16-page A4 portrait booklet titled "Working-capital review", printed and posted to named readers at Innovators Façade Systems Limited. You build ONE design and produce one edition per reader listed in the reader table (see "Reader editions" below).
It answers one question for the reader: what if Crestline were already running at Innovators Facade Systems, how much money and how much time would it save?
Every word and every figure is supplied in the Content section at the end of this prompt. The design is yours; this prompt carries content only.

## 2. Reader editions

Build ONE 16-page design. Then, for each row of the reader table in the Content section, produce one edition of it.
- An edition changes ONLY three things: the cover's "PREPARED EXCLUSIVELY FOR" lines (that reader's name and title), the page 2 letter (that reader's letter text and address block), and the page 2 salutation.
- Every other page, word, figure and chart is IDENTICAL across editions. Do not tailor any body page to a reader.
- Name each edition after its reader as given in the table (for example "Edition: <reader name>"). That label is for the file only and is never printed.
- Body copy is written in a neutral voice. Quotes are credited by name and title, as the Content gives them; never rewrite a credit into "you" for any edition.

## 3. Rules you must follow

1. Use ONLY the text and figures in the Content section. Do not write new copy, captions, taglines, teasers, pull quotes or headings. Where nothing is given, print nothing.
2. Copy every figure exactly as written: same digits, same decimals, same currency sign, same unit. Do not round, re-total, convert or recompute anything.
3. Headlines exactly as given in the page headings table, split into the two lines as shown. Never reword them.
4. Footnotes exactly as given, on the page they belong to.
5. How to read the Content: a line that starts with ">" is printed copy, word for word. In every other line, labels such as "Chart name:", "Data:", "With Crestline:", "Footnote:", "Footer:", "Panel:", "Label:", "Two views:" and any word in brackets are instructions to you and are never printed; the names, labels, values and sentences that follow them ARE the content of that page and are printed.
6. No internal references in printed text: no file names, ticket ids, notes addressed to the team, or instruction words.
7. Never print a negative about the booklet itself, such as what is missing, unknown or left out. Print "Not published" or "Not public" only where the Content gives it as copy.
8. No em dashes and no en dashes anywhere. Use a comma, a full stop, "to" or a middle dot (·) as the Content does.
9. Ownership words stay as written: the client acknowledges, certifies and pays; Innovators Facade Systems measures, prepares, reviews, submits and invoices. Do not swap them.
10. Logo: no Innovators logo file is supplied. Print the company name as text only and put no company mark in its place. The Crestline logo file is used as supplied and never redrawn, traced or imitated.

### Booklet accuracy rules (apply to every page, every edition)
A1. Claims about Crestline must be true today. Make no claim about Crestline beyond the supplied text. No certification, no hosting region, no encryption claim, no Crestline client names, no "live in X days", and no present-tense claim that Crestline connects to the company's ERP. The pilot is one entity, one segment, read-only, 14 days, as the Content words it.
A2. Every quote names the right speaker, exactly as the Content credits it.
A3. Ours versus the client's follows the product rule. In cash-in steps, acknowledged, certified and collected are the client's acts; measuring, preparing, reviewing, submitting and invoicing are the company's own. Keep every ownership tag as given.
A4. No "nothing went wrong" headlines. Use only the headlines given.
A5. Every value, label and unit shown for a chart is exactly the one the Content gives, and every value carries its label. Nothing is shown that the Content does not give.
A6. Interest only at a rate the company discloses. No rate is disclosed, so no interest or carry figure appears anywhere.
A7. Savings are scenarios. Anything marked "With Crestline", "scenario" or "days less" is shown as a scenario, not as a reported figure, with the words "Scenario, not a forecast." Scenario rows overlap and are never added together; print no total the Content does not give. Where the Content says "History only", no scenario value is shown for that chart.
A8. Peers and comparatives appear only where the Content prints them. None are given.
A9. No other company's data. Do not bring in any company, name, figure or text from anywhere other than the Content, including from earlier booklets or your memory. No demonstration figures beside the company's figures.
A10. Full address and correct names and titles on the letter page and cover, exactly as the reader table gives them, per edition.
A11. "You said" only for the reader's own words. Never print "you said" or "in your own words" over words by anyone but that edition's reader.
A12. Never invent figures to fill a chart. If the Content gives a stage or item without a value, print it without a number. The process map values are the ones given, each with its Published or Estimate tag.
A13. No signed numbers in print. Write inflow and outflow, increase and decrease. No "+" or "-" in front of a figure.
A14. Every chart that has a "With Crestline" result in the Content shows that result with its label, its days and its cash, and the words "Scenario, not a forecast." Use only the values the Content gives; if the Content gives none, show none.

B1. Name each chart exactly as the Content names it.
B2. Every "With Crestline" result is printed together with the two lines the Content gives for it, exactly as written, and never with other lines.
B3. Process map estimates: print the Published or Estimate tag with each stage value, print the basis line exactly as given, and never attach a cash figure to an Estimate.
B4. Where the Content says "Not published", "Not public" or gives no value, print exactly that and no figure of your own.
B5. A scenario value is never shown as a reported figure. The one combined result is one scenario run, not a sum of the rows around it.

## 4. Before you finish, check every page of every edition

1. Count the pages: exactly 16, A4 portrait, in the order of the Content.
2. Read every headline against the page headings table: same words, same punctuation, two lines as shown.
3. Pick every figure on every page and find it in the Content, character for character. Any figure you cannot find there, delete it.
4. Search the whole booklet for en dash and em dash characters (Unicode 2013 and 2014). There must be none.
5. Every chart: its name, labels and values are exactly the Content's, and nothing comes from a figure that is not in the Content.
6. Every scenario value carries "Scenario, not a forecast." Charts marked "History only" carry none. No scenario totals added.
7. Every footnote is on its page.
8. No word was added: no taglines, no teasers, no extra captions, no "Live in" line, no certification or hosting claim, no client names beyond those the Content prints, no internal references, no printed negatives.
9. No company logo is shown for Innovators Facade Systems. Nothing is redrawn.
10. The QR code on page 15 (and on page 16) opens crestlineintelligence.com/book-a-demo.
11. Instruction words from the Content (labels listed in rule 5, notes about decisions and checks) are not printed anywhere.
12. Editions: compare each edition with the first. Only the cover reader lines, the page 2 letter and the salutation differ.
