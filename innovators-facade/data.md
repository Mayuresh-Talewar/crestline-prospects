## Day measures: "Crestline's arithmetic on the published accounts" (closing balance / base x 365)
Formulas: DSO = trade receivables / revenue from operations x 365 (the company's own Debtors Turnover = Sales/trade receivables, 5.43x FY26 and 4.05x FY25 on closing balance, AR26 p68, equals this). Contract-asset days = unbilled revenue / revenue x 365. DIO = inventory / materials consumed x 365. DPO = trade payables / purchases of materials x 365 (the company's stated payables ratio uses Purchases, AR26 p102). CCC = DIO + contract-asset days + DSO - DPO.
| | FY23 | FY24 | FY25 | FY26 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DSO | 69.6 | 62.5 | 90.2 | 67.2 |
| Contract-asset days | 37.1 | 29.8 | 37.7 | 68.0 |
| DIO (on materials consumed) | 311.9 | 229.6 | 182.7 | 271.1 |
| DPO (on purchases) | 162.3 | 175.4 | 111.1 | 152.8 |
| CCC | 256.3 | 146.5 | 199.6 | 253.5 |
Caveats: FY23 is on AR24's own revenue and has not been re-stated in a later AR; FY24 figures use AR25's regrouped revenue (21,548.09). Trade payables include non-material vendors (installation, fabrication) while DPO base is materials purchases only; this is the company's own stated pairing. Customer advances (Rs 64.90 Cr at FY26) are not credited anywhere in the CCC: unbilled revenue is already net of Rs 22.56 Cr of them. Value of one day: revenue/day FY26 = Rs 62.33 lakh; materials consumed/day = 32.45 lakh; purchases/day = 41.73 lakh.
Other published ratios: current ratio 1.35 (FY26) vs 1.69 (FY25); debt-equity 0.29 vs 0.42; inventory turnover 2.17x vs 2.49x (on AVERAGE inventory); receivables turnover 4.71x vs 4.83x (on AVERAGE) (AR26 p102). Average-basis ratios are not mixed with the closing-balance days above.
