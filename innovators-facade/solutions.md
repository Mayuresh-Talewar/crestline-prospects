# Solutions: Innovators Facade Systems (IF-S23)

True of the product today, once connected, read only. The change is made by the company's team. Every figure is from calc\savings-output.txt; rows overlap and are never added; the combined figure is ONE simulate run.

| Problem | What Crestline does once connected | Scenario (calc) |
|---|---|---|
| Built, waiting to be certified | Rebuilds each bill from its own dates, shows the wait to be measured, submitted and certified and whose move ends it; raises the longest waits as tickets | 10 days: Rs 6.23 Cr. 30 days: Rs 18.70 Cr |
| Billed, waiting to be paid | Ages each unpaid invoice against its own due date, ranks by money x days past term, names client and desk, re-measures until paid | 10 days: Rs 6.23 Cr. 30 days: Rs 18.70 Cr |
| Project materials held | Replays stock movements on posting dates and shows what has sat on site unissued by site and value | 10 days: Rs 3.25 Cr. 30 days: Rs 9.74 Cr |
| The three together (one run) | Prices every move in one currency, ranks them, stops at the contract's own terms | 10 days each: Rs 15.71 Cr, cycle 253.5 to 223.5 days. 30 days each: Rs 47.13 Cr, cycle 253.5 to 163.5 days |
| Vendor credit | Judges each payment against that vendor's own terms; never counts paying late as a saving | Not priced: no terms published |

Rule 13 marks come from the calc values only. Ownership: measure, prepare, review, submit and invoice are ours; acknowledge, certify and pay are the client's, chased and never "fixed".
