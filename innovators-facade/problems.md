# Problems: Innovators Facade Systems (IF-S23)

Respect first. Innovators has built facades for 27 years (Annual Report for the year ended 31 March 2026, p10), listed on BSE SME in 2018. In FY26 it booked new orders of Rs 571.79 Cr, up 134.05% on the year before, to reach an order book of Rs 741.02 Cr at 31 March 2026, an all time high (company press release, 30 May 2026, p2). Revenue was Rs 227.52 Cr (up 2.8%, arithmetic on p79), profit after tax Rs 13.46 Cr, EBITDA margin 13.85% (p68), cash generated from operations Rs 41.82 Cr (p80). Execution is expected over two to three years.

All days: Crestline's arithmetic on the published accounts, standalone, closing balance / base x 365, FY26, one basis. Base: revenue 22,751.51 lakh for receivables and unbilled; materials consumed 11,844.29 lakh for stock; purchases 15,229.69 lakh for vendor credit.

## 1. Built, waiting to be certified
Unbilled revenue went from Rs 22.86 Cr (FY25) to Rs 42.39 Cr (FY26): 37.7 days of revenue to 68.0 (AR26 p78, p92). The accounts cannot say which tower, which bill, which desk or since when. The company's press release names design approval timelines, site readiness and mobilisation as the cause of the earlier half-year delay; a certification wait sits between the company's measurement and the client's act, and ends in the client's certificate.
Scenario: 10 days less is Rs 6.23 Cr released once; 30 days less Rs 18.70 Cr (62.33 lakh a day of revenue).

## 2. Stock, materials held for projects
Inventories rose from Rs 54.11 Cr to Rs 87.97 Cr: 182.7 days of materials consumed to 271.1, as the order book grew and sites mobilised (p78, p94). The note counts material at site. Cash is paid out for material that has not yet gone into the work.
Scenario: 10 days less is Rs 3.25 Cr released once; 30 days less Rs 9.74 Cr (32.45 lakh a day of materials).

## 3. Billed, waiting to be paid, and the year-to-year swing
Receivables 67.2 days (FY25 90.2), Rs 41.91 Cr, 72.2% of it not yet due from the due date (p92). Cash generated from operations ran Rs 17.76 Cr, 35.46 Cr, 4.74 Cr, 41.82 Cr over FY23 to FY26, and the cash cycle 256.3, 146.5, 199.6, 253.5 days. Vendor credit (152.8 days, Rs 63.76 Cr of trade payables) funded part of FY26; it is shown, never counted as better when higher, and not priced.
Scenario: 10 days less is Rs 6.23 Cr; 30 days less Rs 18.70 Cr. Collection is the client's act.
