# Innovators Facade Systems: story angle (IF-1)

Respect first. Innovators has built facades on some of Mumbai's tallest towers for 27 years, listed in 2018, and in FY2025-26 booked Rs 571.79 Cr of new orders (up 134%) to reach a record order book of Rs 741.02 Cr at 31 March 2026 (press release, 30 May 2026). Bank guarantees outstanding stood at Rs 159.68 Cr (AR 2025-26, Note 30).

The question where bills wait. Every rupee on the books sits in three places that a ledger shows only as year-end balances:
- Built, waiting to be certified: unbilled revenue went from Rs 22.86 Cr to Rs 42.39 Cr in one year, 68.0 days of revenue against 37.7 (arithmetic on the AR 2025-26 accounts).
- Billed, waiting to be paid: receivables are 67.2 days against 90.2 the year before; 72% of the Rs 41.91 Cr is not yet due, Rs 1.67 Cr has crossed one year.
- Material bought, waiting to be fitted: stock rose from Rs 54.11 Cr to Rs 87.97 Cr, 271.1 days of materials consumed against 182.7, as the order book doubled and sites mobilised.
Cash from operations swung Rs 35.46 Cr, Rs 4.74 Cr, Rs 41.82 Cr over FY24 to FY26, and FY26 was funded in part by Rs 64.90 Cr of client advances and Rs 63.76 Cr of payables. The accounts say how much; they cannot say which tower, which bill, which desk, since when. That is the one question Crestline asks: where is the money trapped, for how long, why, who can release it.

Angles for the booklet (all from the company's own filings): the swing between design approval, site readiness and mobilisation named in the press release as the cause of the H1 delay; the 2 to 3 year execution window on the new order book; retention Rs 11.24 Cr held on contracts in progress; what a 10-day or 30-day move in the certification leg is worth at Rs 62.33 lakh of revenue a day (calc to be run in step 2). Never say vendors should be paid later.
