# BSR Infratech: story angle

Respect first (all ICRA 03 Aug 2026): revenue Rs 775.2 Cr (FY21) to Rs 2,682.2 Cr (FY26), 28.2% CAGR, 60% in FY26 alone; PAT Rs 165.2 Cr; interest cover 5.4x; outlook moved to Positive; order book Rs 11,098 Cr after over Rs 6,000 Cr of orders from APCRDA and ADCL; moved ERP to SAP S/4 HANA RISE (own site). Over two decades executing for state and central agencies.

The question: a business that grew 3.5x in five years holds Rs 1,222 Cr in gross debtors at March 2026 (up from Rs 728 Cr), about 166 days of operating income, after heavy Q4 billing (ICRA's own reason). Collections then pulled it to Rs 989.8 Cr by 30 June. Where in the bill's life do those weeks sit: measurement, certification by the authority, or release of payment, and on which project and desk? The accounts show the balance, not the stage. With a 35% nascent order book and Rs 707 Cr of mobilisation advances to be recovered through running bills in FY27, the bill-by-bill view matters more as volume ramps.
