# Atmastco: webapp fit (AT-S23)

Fit: LIGHT, EPC part only. Services and EPC were 23.9% of FY26 net turnover (₹69.99 Cr of ₹292.62 Cr), 15.6% in FY25 (audited results, 26 May 2026, segment annexure). Fabrication plant stock is outside Crestline and is never priced.

| Retention (O2C drill-down) | which retained rupee is due and since when | ₹82.54 Cr retention money at 31 Mar 2025 (Acuite) |

| Ask Crestline, Alerts and Tickets | question, owner, date, re-measure | SAIL LOI ₹235.00 Cr, 19 Feb 2026 |

ERP: Atmastco's Annual Report FY25 (page 62) says an SAP implementation commenced in June 2024. Crestline "reads it, read-only, alongside it", once connected. No go-live is claimed.
Not modelled: plant inventory (no inventory days anywhere), fabrication segment, rating and lender matters.
