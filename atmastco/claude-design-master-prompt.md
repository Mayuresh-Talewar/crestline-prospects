# Claude Design master prompt: Atmastco working-capital review (one booklet, per-reader editions)

Paste everything below this line into Claude Design. Upload the Crestline logo file and the Atmastco logo file before you send it.

---

## 1. What you are building

A 16-page A4 portrait booklet titled "Working-capital review", printed and posted to named readers at Atmastco Limited. You build ONE booklet and produce one edition per reader listed in the reader table (see "Reader editions" below).
It answers one question for the reader: what if Crestline were already running at Atmastco, how much money and how much time would it save?
Every word and every figure is supplied in the Content section at the end of this prompt. This prompt carries content only. All design choices are yours.
Logos: the Crestline logo file and the Atmastco logo file, both used exactly as supplied and never redrawn.

## 2. Reader editions

Build ONE 16-page booklet. Then, for each row of the reader table in the Content section, produce one edition of it.
- An edition changes ONLY three things: the cover's "PREPARED EXCLUSIVELY FOR" lines (that reader's name and title), the page 2 letter (that reader's letter text and address block), and the page 2 salutation.
- Every other page, word and figure is IDENTICAL across editions. Do not tailor any body page to a reader.
- Name each edition after its reader as given in the table (for example "Edition: <reader name>"). That label is for the file only and is never printed.
- Body copy is written in a neutral voice. Quotes are credited by name and title, as the Content gives them; never rewrite a credit into "you" for any edition.

## 3. Rules you must follow

1. Use ONLY the text and figures in the Content section. Do not write new copy, captions, taglines, teasers, pull quotes or headings. If a line has no text, leave it empty.
2. Copy every figure exactly as written: same digits, same decimals, same currency sign, same unit. Do not round, re-total, convert or recompute anything.
3. Headlines exactly as given, never reworded.
4. Footnotes exactly as given, on the page they belong to.
5. Text on a line that begins with ">" is printed copy. Lines that begin with "Data:" or "Data," give the labels and values of a chart or list as content. Other plain lines in the Content are instructions to you and are never printed.
6. No internal references in printed text: no ticket ids, file names, reviewer notes, status markers, or any note addressed to the team. A source is printed only in the words the Content gives for it.
7. No printed negatives about the booklet itself: never print what is missing, unchecked or left out ("we could not find", "TBD", "placeholder"). Where a line is empty, leave it empty. Print a "not published" statement only where the Content gives it as printed copy.
8. No em dashes and no en dashes anywhere. Use a comma, a full stop, "to" or a middle dot (·) as the Content does.
9. Ownership words stay as written: the client certifies and pays; Atmastco measures, prepares, reviews, submits and invoices. Do not swap them.
10. The Atmastco logo appears only as the uploaded file, used as supplied. Never redraw, trace or imitate it. Without the file, show the reader's name only.

### Accuracy rules (apply to every page, every edition)
R1. Claims about Crestline must be true today. Make no claim about Crestline beyond the pasted text. No certification, no hosting region, no encryption claim, no client names, no "live in X days", and no present-tense claim that Crestline connects to Atmastco's SAP or ERP. The pilot is one entity, one segment, read-only, 14 days, as the Content words it.
R2. Every quote names the right speaker, exactly as the Content credits it. Never move a quote to another person.
R3. Ours versus the client's follows the product rule. In cash-in steps, acknowledged, certified and collected are the client's acts; measuring, preparing, reviewing, submitting and invoicing are the company's own. Keep every ownership tag as given.
R4. No "nothing went wrong" headlines. Use only the headlines given; never soften or invert one.
R5. Every value shown is exactly the value the Content gives for it, with its label. Never compute a figure that is not given in the Content.
R6. No interest is priced anywhere. Print no rate and no interest figure.
R7. Savings are scenarios. Any value described as "ten days less", "thirty days less", "with Crestline" or "scenario" is shown as a scenario, labelled "Scenario, not a forecast." Scenario values overlap and are never added together; print no total the Content does not give.
R8. Comparatives appear only where the Content prints them, with the date it gives. Never build a scorecard of missed promises.
R9. No other company's data. Do not bring in any company, name, figure or text from anywhere other than the Content, including from earlier booklets or your memory. No demo numbers beside Atmastco's figures.
R10. Full address, names and titles on the letter page and cover are exactly as the reader table gives them, per edition.
R11. "You said" only for the reader's own words. Never print "you said", "in your own words" or any second-person credit over a quote by someone other than that edition's reader. Use the speaker's name and title or the company's name as the Content gives it.
R12. Never invent figures. Where the Content gives a stage or step without a value (the page 8 timeline), show it without a number. Never create a split of a total.
R13. No signed numbers in print. Write inflow / outflow, increase / decrease. No "+" or "-" in front of a figure.
R14. No single days figure for any wait is used. Only the value of a day, and ten and thirty day steps on it, as the Content gives them.

## 4. Before you finish, check every page of every edition

1. Count the pages: exactly 16, in the order of the Content.
2. Read every headline against the Content: same words, same punctuation.
3. Pick every figure on every page and find it in the Content, character for character. Any figure you cannot find there, delete it.
4. Search the whole booklet for en dash and em dash characters. There must be none.
5. Every value shown is labelled as the Content labels it. Scenario values carry "Scenario, not a forecast." and are never added.
6. Every footnote is on its page.
7. No word was added: no taglines, no extra captions, no "Live in" line, no certification or hosting claim, no client names beyond those in the Content, no internal references, no printed negatives.
8. The Atmastco logo is the uploaded file or absent. Nothing redrawn.
9. Page 13 carries no numbers and page 8 stages carry only their names and ownership tags.
10. The QR code on pages 15 and 16 opens crestlineintelligence.com/book-a-demo.
11. Instruction lines from the Content ("Data:" lines are content; other plain lines are not printed) are handled as rule 5 says.
12. Editions: compare each edition with the first. Only the cover reader lines, the page 2 letter and the salutation differ. No "you said" sits over a quote that is not that edition's reader's own.
