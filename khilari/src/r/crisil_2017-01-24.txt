Detailed Rationale
CRISIL has assigned its 'CRISIL BBB-/Stable/CRISIL A3' ratings to the bank loan facilities of Khilari
Infrastructure
Pvt Ltd (KIPL).
 
The rating reflects extensive experience of the promoters in the industry and healthy order book in hand. The
rating
also factors in moderate financial risk profile of the company. These rating strengths are partially mitigated
by
moderate scale of operations, average profitability levels and working capital intensity in operations.
Key Rating Drivers & Detailed Description
Strengths
* Extensive experience of the promoters in the industry: Mr S S Khilari has been in this business for more
than
35 years, and has established healthy relationships with municipal bodies and a strong track record of
executing
orders in time, which will benefit the company's business risk profile.
 
* Healthy order book in hand: As of January 2016, the company had orders of around Rs 1400 crore to
be
executed in 2-3 years, which provide healthy revenue visibility for the medium term.
 
* Moderate financial risk profile: The financial risk profile is driven by comfortable networth of Rs 21.2 crore
and
gearing of 1.4 times as on March 31, 2016, and adequate net cash accrual to adjusted debt ratio of 0.29 time
for
fiscal 2016.
 
Weaknesses
* Moderate scale of operations & average profitability margin: In fiscal 2016, the company reported revenues
of
Rs. 223.9 crore as against Rs. 107.8 crore during fiscal 2015. The operating margins of the company declined to 4.8
percent in fiscal 2016 as against 7.0 percent in fiscal 2015 due to intense competition and fragmented nature
of
operations.
 
* Working Capital Intensive operations: The GCA days of the company was at 114 days in fiscal 2016 backed by
moderate debtors of 43 days and high security deposits and retention money. Sustenance of efficient working capital
management backed by access to incremental funding given aggressive growth appetite will remain a key sensitivity
factor.
Outlook: Stable
CRISIL believes KIPL will benefit from its promoters' extensive industry experience. The outlook may be revised
to
'Positive' if revenue and cash accrual increase more than expected, leading to better liquidity. The outlook may
be
revised to 'Negative' if decline in accrual leads to a stretch in working capital cycle, or if sizeable, debt-funded capital
expenditure weakens the financial risk profile.
