RATING ACTION / OUTLOOK 
BWR has considered the audited financial statements upto FY2020, Management certified
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
provisional financials for H1FY2021, Projected financials upto FY22 and publicly available
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
information and clarifications provided by the Company’s  management. 
 
The rating reflects the moderate improvement in performance, long track record of promoters in
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
  
the waste management industry, and established relations with customers, who mostly are
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
state-level/municipal entities. However, the rating remains constrained by KIPL’s moderate scale
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
of business, dependence on the municipalities for proper waste disposal and management, and
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
existing competition in the market.

Credit  Strengths:  
Experienced management

Technical expertise 
The Company has acquired necessary technical expertise in the last 25 years to execute larger projects.

COMPANY PROFILE 
Khilari Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd. (KIPL or the ‘Company’) was initially established as a
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
proprietorship concern in the year 1998 under the name ‘Khilari S. S’. Later, in the year 2005,
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
the firm was converted into a private limited company under the name Ask Infrastructure Pvt.
 
 
 
 
  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Ltd. Subsequently, in the same year the name of the Company was changed to Khilari
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd. The administrative office of the Company is located in CBD Belapur,
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
  
 
 
 
 
Navi Mumbai. KIPL is a major player in construction of landfill for sanitary & hazardous
 
 
 
  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
industrial waste with closure, operation & maintenance of dumping ground, STP and all types
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
UGD sewer lines. The Company is engaged in providing EPC work for Sewage Treatment Plant,
 
 
 
 
  
  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
O & M of Sewage Treatment Plant, Scientific closure of Dump yard as per MSW Rule-2000,
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Hazardous waste Management. The Company also has started a New Project under guidance of
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
  
 
 
 
 
 
Navi Mumbai Municipal Corporation to prepare compost from Waste/Garbage. 
KIPL is an ISO 9001: 2015, certified & registered as a Class ‘A’ with Office of the
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Engineer-In-Chief PWD, Chhattisgarh and also registered as Class-1 (Civil) by Maharashtra
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Jeevan Pradhikaran. 
Mr. Sahebrao Sukhdeo Khilari, Chief Executive Officer of the Company, has three decades of
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
experience in this business.
