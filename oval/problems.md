# OVAL Projects Engineering Ltd: working-capital problems (OV-3)

7 Oct 2026. Every Rs Cr figure is converted from the filings' Rs lakh by `calc/oval-savings.ts` (output `calc/oval-savings.output.txt`, section 7 unless noted). Pages and grades: `data.md`. Bases: **FY25 and FY26 are standalone (AR26, Annual Report 2025-26, auditor's report 29 May 2026, published Sep 2026); FY24 is restated consolidated (RHP, 21 Aug 2025)**. A FY24 figure is never printed as the same series without that note.
Scope: EPC works only (`webapp-fit.md`). Trading (20.2% of FY26 revenue) is outside it.

## 1. Profit grew three years running; cash from operations went the other way

| | FY24 (cons.) | FY25 | FY26 | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PAT | Rs 4.40 Cr | Rs 9.34 Cr | Rs 14.81 Cr | RHP F-6; AR26 p151 |
| Cash from operating activities | Rs -1.13 Cr | Rs -15.75 Cr | **Rs -29.96 Cr** | RHP F-7; AR26 p153-154 |

- FY25 and FY26 together: operations used Rs 45.71 Cr while reporting Rs 24.15 Cr of profit.
- The gap was filled from outside: financing inflow Rs 49.97 Cr in FY26 (AR26 p154), including the IPO (Rs 46.74 Cr gross, Sep 2025).
- The company names it itself: "a net cash outflow from operating activities of 2,995.93 lakh, principally reflecting the build-up of inventories and work in progress and the reduction in trade payables" (AR26 p84), and calls cash conversion "a primary management objective" (AR26 p93).
- Respectful framing: this is what fast growth on government EPC work costs in cash. The question is which project and which stage absorbed it.

## 2. Half the receivable has not been billed yet

| | FY25 | FY26 | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trade receivables | Rs 43.07 Cr | Rs 35.07 Cr | AR26 p168 |
| of which unbilled | none shown | **Rs 16.86 Cr (48.1%)** | AR26 p191-192 ageing |
| Work in progress (inside inventory, not in receivables) | Rs 27.00 Cr | Rs 34.11 Cr | AR26 p167 |
| Other LD & withheld (includes retention held by clients) | Rs 9.15 Cr | Rs 10.50 Cr | AR26 p166 |

- AR26 reads the Rs 7.99 Cr fall in receivables as "materially improved collection performance" (AR26 p103). The FY26 ageing adds an unbilled column the FY25 ageing did not have, so the fall is not like-for-like.
- Billed and unbilled, the receivable is 40.2 days (unbilled) plus 43.4 days (billed) of FY26 revenue from operations; on works revenue alone, 51.7 plus 55.8 days (calc section 2).
- Billing is OVAL's own act; certification and payment are the client's. Unbilled work is the part of the receivable OVAL can move on its own.
- The "not due" column is empty in both years and retention has no ageing (AR26 p166, p191).

## 3. Cash went out ahead of the work: stock, vendor advances, and payables settled early

| | FY24 (cons.) | FY25 | FY26 | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Inventory incl. WIP | Rs 39.88 Cr | Rs 43.69 Cr | Rs 59.07 Cr (+35.2%) | AR26 p149-150 |
| Advances to vendors and others | NA | Rs 11.09 Cr | Rs 29.10 Cr (+162.3%) | AR26 p168 |
| Trade payables, current | Rs 27.63 Cr | Rs 34.06 Cr | Rs 12.94 Cr | AR26 p149, p163 |
| Trade payables, long term | nil | nil | Rs 6.85 Cr (34.6% of trade payables) | AR26 p162 Note 7.10 |
| Net cycle (Note 37, turnover-implied) | | 93 days | 97 days | AR26 p104 |

- Revenue grew 49.7%; vendor advances grew 162.3% and stock 35.2%.
- Payable days fell from 148 to 105 (AR26 p104). The company says it "deliberately settled vendor obligations ... in exchange for improved commercial terms" (AR26 p105). Part of what remains owed has moved to long-term trade payables, a new line in FY26.
- What the filings do not say: whether those settlements and advances were made on or before each vendor's due date, and what the "improved commercial terms" were worth against the cash they used. Crestline never treats paying vendors later as a saving; the question is only whether money went out earlier than the contract needed.

## 4. The order book is 5.2 times revenue, and the limits that carry it are mostly in use

| | FY25 | FY26 | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Order book | | Rs 796 Cr total contract value, 30 Apr 2026 (5.2x FY26 revenue) | AR26 p87 |
| Bank guarantees outstanding / BG limit | Rs 43.06 Cr | Rs 59.85 Cr of Rs 75.00 Cr = **79.8%**; headroom Rs 15.15 Cr | AR26 p174 Note 33, p162 |
| Cash credit drawn / limit (year end) | | Rs 23.53 Cr of Rs 26.50 Cr = 88.8% | AR26 p162 |
| Total sanctioned limits | | Rs 104.72 Cr (ICICI, Indian Bank, Yes Bank, Bank of Baroda) | AR26 p162 |
| Revenue from government-controlled entities | FY23 91.06%, FY24 77.73%, FY25 90.17% | not disclosed | RHP PDF p37 |

- The company's own words: bank-guarantee capacity "is the binding constraint on how much public sector work a contractor may hold at any time" (AR26 p106). Its first claim on capital is "working capital to support execution of the existing order book" (AR26 p89).
- With about 90% of revenue from government-controlled clients, the payment clock is the client's. What OVAL controls is how fast work becomes a bill and a bill becomes a complete claim.
- Year-end cash credit is a single date; drawings through the year are not disclosed.

## 5. Two records of the same money do not agree (Note 40, stated once, neutrally)

The quarterly stock and book-debt statements sent to the banks were above the books in each month of FY26, by between Rs 0.42 Cr and Rs 8.81 Cr; management attributes it to "reconciliation of accounts with customers and stock verification" (AR26 p177). It is a reconciliation point: two records of the same money that do not agree, and an integrated accounting system is "in progress" (AR26 p97).

## What their books cannot tell them

- **Which project, which bill.** One receivable ageing for the whole company; no project or invoice split (AR26 p191).
- **Which stage.** How long work sits executed before it is measured, measured before it is billed, billed before it is certified, certified before it is paid. Unbilled is one number.
- **Whose desk.** Who prepares, submits and follows up each bill; who released each vendor advance.
- **Since when.** No "not due" split and no retention ageing; FY25 and FY26 ageing use different classes.
- **Against which terms.** No client payment term or certification window is disclosed, and no vendor due dates (only that 10-15% of subcontractor payments is held as retention, RHP PDF p105).
