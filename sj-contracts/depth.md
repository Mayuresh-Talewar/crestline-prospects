# SJ Contracts: story angle (SJ-1)

Respect first. A Pune builder that started in 1988 as a proprietorship (CRISIL), has kept the same A-/Stable and A2+ rating since January 2021, and grew operating income from Rs 610.18 cr (FY23) to Rs 963.40 cr (FY24), up 57.9%, while PAT rose from Rs 17.86 cr to Rs 41.16 cr (CRISIL, 15 Apr 2025, standalone). Order book Rs 2,484 cr at 31 Mar 2025, 12 to 36 months of work. Net worth Rs 184.5 cr (FY24). It builds for Godrej, Kolte-Patil, VTP, Serum, Volkswagen India and the company's site lists safety awards back to 2016. Say that plainly and early.

Then the question, framed as a question and never as a flaw: when work doubles in a year, where do bills wait?
- The growth is on paper in the rating: rated limits went Rs 115 cr (2021) to Rs 200 cr (Feb 2024) to Rs 242 cr to Rs 335.1 cr (Apr 2025). Of that, Rs 235.7 cr is guarantees and proposed non-fund limits (the money lenders stand behind on the company's behalf), Rs 99.4 cr is fund-based.
- CRISIL itself names the working-capital cycle as the upgrade and downgrade trigger in both 2024 and 2025 ("sharp improvement" / "sharp stretch"). The rating agency is asking the same question.
- Non-fund-based limits were used 88% for the 14 months to Nov 2023 (CRISIL Feb 2024): when guarantees run hot, the bills behind them matter.
- The accounts say how much, never which site, which bill, which desk, since when. About 80% of revenue is real estate, so a handful of developer sites decide the month. That is the gap Crestline fills: sites, bills, desks, days.
- Margin was volatile 7.7% to 9.9% over three years (CRISIL); a day of delay costs more when margin is thin.

What cannot be said: any days figure, any WC cycle, any ERP, any claim of delay at SJ. Debtor and creditor days are NOT FOUND. Illustrations must be Crestline scenarios on Rs 963.40 cr (per-day value only if the calc states basis: FY24, standalone) and labelled so. Do not claim "heavy unbilled WIP"; the filings do not say it.

Opening line candidate: "A builder whose order book is 2.6 times its last full year, and who asks a bank for Rs 335 cr of room to carry it." Check the ratio wording against data.md before use.
