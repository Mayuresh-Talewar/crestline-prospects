# RB Ghodke Infrastructure: where Crestline fits (true today)

Source of every RB Ghodke figure: Acuite rating rationale, 3 Nov 2025, standalone (see data.md). No ERP is named anywhere, so the booklet says "ERP". The adapter does not exist until the pilot builds it: nothing below is claimed as connected.

## Screens that answer the RB story (project scope, from the app's navigation)
| Question the Chairman has | Screen | What it does today |
|---|---|---|
| Where does a bill wait between work done and cash? | O2C (Cash In) | Rebuilds each RA bill from its own dates; shows each stage, how long, whose move ends the wait |
| Which client, and why? | Client (under O2C) | One client's certification and payment behaviour, whose delay it is |
| What is ten days worth? | Working Capital: cycle simulator | Moves the receivable leg at a measured daily rate; sliders stop at the contract's own terms |
| What first? | Working Capital: ranked levers | Every move priced in cash released once, ranked |
| Are suppliers paid on their due date? | P2P (Cash Out) and Vendors | Payment timing against each vendor's own terms; early payment is shown as cash leaving, paying late is never a saving |
| Who is told and did it move? | Alerts & Tickets, Performance | Finding, owner, due date, auditor re-measures the same figure; closed only when the number moves |
| Ask in plain words | Ask Crestline | Retrieves from the screen's own facts; code does the arithmetic, never the model |

## Ownership (product rule)
Ours (the company): measure with the client, prepare, review, submit, invoice. The client's: acknowledge, certify, pay. A wait is owned by whoever makes the act that ends it. The 69 debtor days therefore mix both: Crestline separates them once connected.

## Limits to state
- Read-only; one entity, one segment, 14 days; adapter built first.
- Step waits are not public: page 8 draws stages with no days.
- No interest priced: no borrowing rate is published.
- Paying vendors later is never shown as releasing cash (creditor days 12 stay as they are).
- No ISO, hosting or encryption claim.
