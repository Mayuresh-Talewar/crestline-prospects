# RB Ghodke Infrastructure: problems (RB-2 story)

All figures: Acuite Ratings & Research, rating rationale dated 3 Nov 2025, standalone, FY ends 31 March. Page numbers are the document's own.

## Respect first
Civil work for the Maharashtra government for more than three decades (p3). Revenue Rs 120.16 Cr in FY24 to Rs 313.50 Cr in FY25 (p3, p5), profit after tax Rs 7.28 Cr to Rs 22.26 Cr (p5), operating margin 14.40% in FY25 (p3), about Rs 187 Cr of revenue in H1 FY26 (p3), order book about Rs 1,014 Cr at 30 Sep 2025 (p1, p3). Acuite upgraded the ratings to BBB+ / Stable and A2 on 3 Nov 2025 (p1).

## Problem 1: the wait to be paid went from 7 days to 69 while revenue grew 2.6 times
- Debtor days 69 in FY25, 7 in FY24 (Acuite, 3 Nov 2025, p3). Revenue 313.50 against 120.16 (p3).
- Acuite also notes that most of the retention money is due to be received within 3 months (p3).
- Not visible in the accounts: which bills, at which desk, for how many days, and how much of the 69 is retention that is simply scheduled.

## Problem 2: suppliers are paid in 12 days, bills wait 69
- Creditor days 12 in FY25, 23 in FY24; Acuite: the company opts for advance payment to avail cash discount from suppliers (p3). A choice, not a fault.
- Not visible in the accounts: payment by payment, whether each early payment earned its discount, against each vendor's own due date. Paying vendors later is never presented as a saving.

## Problem 3: the order book multiplies every day of waiting
- Order book about Rs 1,014 Cr at 30 Sep 2025, 3.23 times FY25 revenue (Acuite p1, p3). Gross current asset days 102 in FY25, 91 in FY24 (p3).
- At the FY25 revenue rate, one day is Rs 0.8589 Cr (313.50 / 365). Ten fewer debtor days is Rs 8.59 Cr, thirty is Rs 25.77 Cr (calc/rb-savings-output.txt). Scenarios, not forecasts; no interest priced because no rate is published.
