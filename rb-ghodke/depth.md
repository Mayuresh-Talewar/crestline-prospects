# RB Ghodke Infrastructure: story angle (depth)

All figures: Acuite rationale 3 Nov 2025, standalone, see data.md. Nothing below is from the company's own accounts.

## Respect first
- Founded 1986 as a proprietorship, a private limited since 2021. More than three decades of civil work for the Maharashtra government: canals, dams, barrages, roads, bridges, water and drainage.
- Revenue Rs 120.16 Cr (FY24) to Rs 313.50 Cr (FY25), about 2.6 times in one year, with profit after tax up from Rs 7.28 Cr to Rs 22.26 Cr and interest cover holding at 6.30 times. Order book ~Rs 1,014 Cr at 30 Sep 2025, three years of work ahead.
- Acuite upgraded the firm from BBB to BBB+ and A3+ to A2 in November 2025, and called the management track record established.
- Bank limits are used moderately (~50-70% of fund-based). This is a firm that grew fast and kept its credit.

## Then the question: where do the bills wait?
- Debtor days went from 7 to 69 between FY24 and FY25, which Acuite ties to "pending recoveries with PWD Parbhani". Gross current assets went from 91 to 102 days.
- Inventory fell from 47 to 9 days and creditors from 23 to 12 days: the firm pays suppliers fast (advance payment for cash discount on concrete roads) while the state pays it slower. Computed cycle (debtors + inventory - creditors): 31 days to 66 days.
- The accounts cannot say which bill, at which desk (measurement, certification, PWD approval, treasury), for how many days, or how much of the 69 is retention that is simply scheduled. That is the gap Crestline fills: the stage where each bill is waiting, in rupees and days.
- Debt rose from Rs 22.07 Cr to Rs 108.63 Cr in one year (machinery capex), so each day of receivable now sits against borrowed money. Do not print an interest cost: no rate is disclosed.

## Cautions for copy
- 69 vs 7 days is a jump, not a chronic delay; frame it as growth overtaking the bill-chasing routine, with one named client (PWD Parbhani) per the agency, not "the government pays late".
- Unlisted: no earnings call, no management quotes exist. No quote in the booklet.
- Readers: the Chairman is the only verified leader. No CFO name is available.
