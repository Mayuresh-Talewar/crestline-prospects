## Auditor matters (six emphasis-of-matter items; opinion unmodified)
a) Note 44: turnover and costs of joint-venture entities are booked in the company's accounts although projects are executed by the JV partners under the company's supervision; project bank accounts are controlled by the company and partners are authorised to raise and sign invoices. 71% of FY26 revenue is "Contract Receipt Joint Ventures" (38,536.76).
b) Note 32: DGGI (GST Intelligence) search and seizure at the Mumbai office on 6 Jan 2021 under CGST s.67. The company challenged it by writ petition in the Gujarat High Court; sub judice. The auditor is "unable to comment on financial liabilities". Rs 108.40 Lakh deposited under protest; no provision. In FY21 contractual revenue and matching subcontract costs were reversed; the disputed receipts and payments remain in other current liabilities (3,285.55) and loans and advances (3,178.66, Note 10), "in process of resolving the disputes".
c) Note 40: trade payables, receivables, advances received and given, GST and income-tax balances are "subject to reconciliation and confirmation".
d) Note 33: expected credit loss provision of 2,769.36 booked in an earlier year against parties in litigation or arbitration, adjusted directly against general reserves (not through profit and loss); 162.93 recovered in FY26. Receivables ECL stands at 2,760.95; advances ECL 3,225.19 (FY25 3,388.11).
e) Note 35: income tax assets (net) 2,068.82; appeal orders in the company's favour, giving-effect orders pending.
f) The accounting software's audit trail (edit log) was not activated throughout the year for all relevant transactions.
Also: Note 37 contingent liabilities, income tax demands 1,699.43 (assessment years 2009-10 to 2012-13, before CIT(A) Mumbai). Note 39: company claims of 2,116.73 under arbitration.
