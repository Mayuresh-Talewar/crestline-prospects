# Niraj Cement Structurals: problems (NC-S23)

All figures standalone, Annual Report FY26 (accounts signed 21 May 2026) unless stated; FY24 from Annual Report FY25.

Respect first: revenue from operations Rs 470.22 Cr (FY24), Rs 506.72 Cr (FY25), Rs 540.44 Cr (FY26); profit after tax Rs 21.60 Cr in FY26. A listed civil EPC contractor that has grown three years running.

## 1. The wait to be paid is one number for the whole company
Trade receivables were Rs 70.76 Cr at 31 March 2026 (Note 8, closing). The company's own trade receivables turnover ratio is 7.76 in FY26 and 8.11 in FY25 (Note 47), which is 47.0 and 45.0 days (365 / ratio, average balance). The accounts cannot say which bill waits at which step, or since when. Ownership: acknowledgement, certification and payment are the client's; measure, prepare, review, submit, invoice are Niraj's.

## 2. Thirteen joint ventures, one total
Rs 385.37 Cr of Rs 540.44 Cr (71%) came through 13 joint ventures (Notes 20 and 31, FY26). Each runs its own bills against its own client. The question: across 13 joint ventures, where does each bill wait and whose desk is it on.

## 3. What a day is worth
One day of revenue is Rs 1.48 Cr (540.44 / 365). Ten days on the wait to be paid is Rs 14.81 Cr; thirty days is Rs 44.42 Cr, released once. Scenarios, not additive, no interest (no rate disclosed). Client delay is chased, not "fixed".
