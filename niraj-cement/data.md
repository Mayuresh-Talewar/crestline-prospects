# Niraj Cement Structurals Limited: data (step 1, NC-1, 2026-10-09)

## VERDICT: LIGHT
Reasons: listed, FY2025-26 annual report filed (accounts approved 21 May 2026), and the receivables ageing is published and is a strong working-capital story (55% of trade receivables are over six months old). But: no rating rationale exists (the Infomerics rating is a bare letter on a Rs 5 Cr PROPOSED facility), no order book, no ERP, no disclosed interest rate, no earnings call or investor deck found, and the accounts carry six auditor emphasis-of-matter items, so what can print is narrow and must be framed with care. Do not build on "rated limits", "order book" or "ERP": none are in the filings.

Basis for every figure: STANDALONE, Ind AS, Rs Lakh as printed; Rs Cr = Lakh / 100.
