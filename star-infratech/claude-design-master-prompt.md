## 3. Rules you must follow

1. Use ONLY the text and figures in the Content section. Do not write new copy, captions, taglines, teasers, pull quotes or headings. Where a line has no text, leave it empty.
2. Copy every figure exactly as written: same digits, same decimals, same currency sign, same unit. Do not round, re-total, convert or recompute anything.
3. Headlines exactly as given, split into two lines at a natural break. Never reword them.
4. Footnotes exactly as given, on the page they belong to.
5. Lines that begin with ">" are printed copy, exactly as written (without the ">"). Text inside quotation marks in the Content is printed copy. A line that begins "Data:" gives the labels and values of the chart named in the line before it; print every label with its value. Other plain lines (for example "Chart:", "Text only", "Never compute a figure that is not given here") are instructions and are never printed.
6. No internal references in printed text: no ticket ids, file names, checker notes, status words used as markers, or any note addressed to a team. A source is printed only in the words the Content gives for it.
7. No printed negatives about the booklet itself: never print what is missing, unknown, unchecked or left out ("we could not find", "TBD", "placeholder"). Print "Not public" or "Not published" only where the Content gives it as printed copy.
8. No em dashes and no en dashes anywhere. Use a comma, a full stop, "to" or a middle dot (·) as the Content does.
9. Ownership words stay as written: the client certifies and pays; Star Infratech measures, prepares, reviews, submits and invoices. Do not swap them.
10. No company logo is supplied. Show the reader's name only on the cover, and no company mark.
