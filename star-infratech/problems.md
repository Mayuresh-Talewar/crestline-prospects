# Star Infratech: problems (ST-S23)

Respect first: a Karnataka government contractor since 1991; operating income Rs 1,154.2 Cr (FY24, ICRA 10 Jul 2026 p2) to Rs 1,384.5 Cr (FY25) to Rs 1,525.4 Cr (FY26, provisional); PAT Rs 69.8 Cr (FY26, provisional); order book Rs 6,347.8 Cr at 31 Mar 2025 (ICRA 22 Jul 2025 pp1-2).

1. Where each bill waits, as the book executes. ICRA states working-capital intensity fell from 38% (FY24) to 12% (FY25) on a significant improvement in the receivable cycle and a sizeable reduction in sub-contractor advances (22 Jul 2025 p1). That is a result, not a map: which department, which stage, how long, since when is not in any public document. No days are published, so none are quoted or converted.
2. What a day is worth. With no day count, the only honest price is the value of a day: 1,525.4 / 365 = Rs 4.1792 Cr; ten days Rs 41.79 Cr, thirty days Rs 125.38 Cr, released once (calc\st-savings-output.txt). It says nothing about an actual Star Infratech wait.
3. Not visible from outside: client terms, ageing, retention, contract assets, vendor terms, payables. All NOT FOUND; every lever is listed unpriced in the lever table.

Cannot be said from the accounts: that any bill is late, that any wait is long, or any rate of interest.
