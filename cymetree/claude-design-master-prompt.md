Upload the Crestline logo file and the Cymetree Projects logo file before you send this.

## 1. What you are building

A 16-page A4 portrait booklet titled "Working-capital review", printed and posted to the partners of Cymetree Projects LLP. You build ONE design and produce the one edition in the reader table (see "Reader edition" below).
It answers one question for the reader: what if Crestline were already running at Cymetree Projects, how much money and how much time would it save?
Every word and every figure is supplied in the Content section at the end of this prompt.

## 2. Reader edition

There is ONE edition, for the reader in the reader table in the Content section.
- The edition uses the cover's "PREPARED EXCLUSIVELY FOR" lines, the page 2 address block, salutation and letter exactly as the reader table gives them.
- The label of the edition is for the file only and is never printed.
- Body copy is written in a neutral voice. Quotes are credited by name, as the Content gives them.

## 3. Rules you must follow

1. Use ONLY the text and figures in the Content section. Do not write new copy, captions, taglines, teasers, pull quotes or headings. If a place has no text, leave it empty.
2. Copy every figure exactly as written: same digits, same decimals, same currency sign, same unit. Do not round, re-total, convert or recompute anything.
3. Headlines exactly as given for each page, split into two lines as shown. Never reword them.
4. Footnotes exactly as given, on the page they belong to.
5. Text inside quotation marks in the Content is printed copy. Words outside quotation marks (for example "Data:", word counts in brackets, notes such as "No numbers on this page") are instructions to you and are never printed.
6. No internal references in printed text. Reference codes, file names, tags in brackets, checker notes, status words used as markers, and any note addressed to the team are never printed. A source is printed only in the words the Content gives for it.
7. No printed negatives about the booklet itself: never print what is missing, unknown, unchecked or left out ("not available", "we could not find", "to come"). Where a place is empty, leave the space empty. Print a "not public" line only where the Content gives it as quoted copy.
8. No em dashes and no en dashes anywhere. Use a comma, a full stop, "to" or a middle dot (·) as the Content does.
9. Ownership words stay as written: the client certifies and pays; Cymetree Projects measures, prepares, submits and escalates. Do not swap them.
10. The Cymetree Projects logo is the supplied file, used as supplied. Never redraw, trace, recolour or imitate it.

### Booklet accuracy rules (apply to every page)
R1. **Claims about Crestline must be true today.** Make no claim about Crestline beyond the pasted text. No certification "in progress", no hosting region, no encryption claim, no client names, no "live in X days", and no present-tense claim that Crestline connects to the company's ERP. The pilot is one entity, one segment, read-only, 14 days, as the Content words it.
R2. **Every quote names the right speaker**, exactly as the Content credits it. Never move a quote to another person.
R3. **Ours versus the client's follows the product rule.** In cash-in steps, acknowledged, certified and collected are the client's acts; measuring, preparing, reviewing, submitting and invoicing are the company's own. Keep every OURS and CLIENT tag as given.
R4. **No "nothing went wrong" headlines.** Use only the headlines given; never soften or invert one.
R6. **Interest only at a rate the company discloses.** Print only the rate and interest figures the Content gives; never compute or label a rate as theirs.
R7. **Savings are scenarios.** Anything marked "with Crestline", "scenario" or "days less" is shown as a scenario, labelled "Scenario, not a forecast." Scenario rows overlap and are never added together; print no total the Content does not give. Data the Content marks as history only gets no scenario mark.
R8. **Peers and comparatives** appear only where the Content prints them, with the vintage it gives. Never build a scorecard of missed promises.
R9. **No other company's data.** Do not bring in any company, name, figure or design text from anywhere other than the Content, including from earlier booklets or your memory. No demo numbers beside the company's figures.
R10. **Full address and correct names and titles** on the letter page and cover, exactly as the reader table gives them.
R11. **"You said" only for the reader's own words.** Never print "you said", "in your own words" or any second-person credit over a quote by someone other than the reader. Use the speaker's name or the company's name as the Content gives it. Never print a note or flag on the page.

R12. **Never invent figures to fill a chart.** If the Content gives a stage, step or item without a value (for example the page 8 bill timeline), give it without a number. Never create an "illustrative split" of a total, and never add a sentence about one.
R13. **No signed numbers in print.** Write inflow / outflow, increase / decrease. No "+" or "-" in front of a figure.

R14. **Every problem chart shows the outcome with Crestline.** Beside the "today" figure, give the "with Crestline" result from the Content (labelled with the days and cash it releases, plus "scenario, not a forecast"). Use only the values the Content gives; if the Content gives no scenario value for a chart, show none (rule A12).

B1. **Every figure on the page is given in the Content.** The value of one day, the ten and thirty day figures, and the two company results are the only calculated figures; print no other number, ratio or total. The one published figure "About 54%" on page 6 is quoted from CRISIL, not calculated.
B2. **No figure for today's days.** Give only the scenario values the Content gives. Never give the days a wait lasts today.
B3. **Page 8 stages carry only a name and a tag.** Page 13 is text only. The CRISIL estimate on page 3 is text only and is never used in a calculation.


## 4. Before you finish, check every page

1. Count the pages: exactly 16, A4 portrait, in the order of the Content.
2. Read every headline against the Content: same words, same punctuation, same two-line split.
3. Pick every figure on every page and find it in the Content, character for character. Any figure you cannot find there, delete it.
4. Search the whole booklet for en dash and em dash characters (Unicode 2013 and 2014). There must be none.
5. Every chart: every item labelled, nothing taken from a figure that is not in the Content.
6. Every scenario carries "Scenario, not a forecast." No scenario totals added.
7. Every footnote is on its page, as given.
8. No word was added: no taglines, no right-hand footer teasers, no extra captions, no "Live in" line, no certification or hosting claim, no client names, no reference codes, no printed negatives beyond the quoted copy.
9. The Cymetree Projects logo is the supplied file. Nothing redrawn.
10. The QR code on page 15 (and on page 16) opens crestlineintelligence.com/book-a-demo.
11. Instruction words from the Content (word counts in brackets, "Data:", notes about decisions and checks) are not printed anywhere.
12. Page 8 shows the eleven stages with only a name and a tag. Page 13 has no figures.
