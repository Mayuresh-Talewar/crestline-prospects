# Cymetree Projects: story angle (respect first, then where bills wait)

## Respect first
- A Pune firm that began as an LLP in December 2021, started with specialised healthcare equipment supply, and by FY25 had operating income of Rs 154.50 crore, with PAT of Rs 17.97 crore (CRISIL, 31 Jul 2026). FY24 was Rs 69.76 crore; FY23 Rs 4.8 crore.
- CRISIL's own estimate puts FY26 at Rs 340 - 345 crore, and the order book at Rs 846 crore (May 2026), to execute over 12 - 24 months. CRISIL names "extensive industry experience of the partners" and "healthy order book providing revenue visibility" as strengths.
- Work is in hospitals, medical colleges and institutional buildings, turnkey, with medical equipment supply and installation; the site says state government clients across Maharashtra.
- Credit: first rating, CRISIL BBB-/Stable and A3, limits Rs 102 crore, interest cover 28.94 times in FY25 (CRISIL).

## Then the question: where do the bills wait?
- CRISIL says gross current assets run at 130 - 150 days over three fiscals and debtor days at 120 - 145, because "it is required to extend long credit period in line with the industry standards." (ranges, not a single year.)
- The growth is the pressure: "With increase in revenues, the working capital requirements shall increase and management of same will remain key monitorable." A downward trigger is a stretch in the working capital cycle.
- Two orders are 80% of the book, so one slow certification on one site moves a large share of what is owed. CRISIL: "Any major delays in order execution or delay in receivables may impact on the firm's credit profile."
- Cash balance was Rs 0.47 crore at March 2026 and the promoters "may extend support in the form of unsecured loans". Bank limit use was about 54%.
- The accounts say how many days on average. They cannot say which bill, at which site, at which desk (measurement, certification, client department payment) and since when. A company that tripled in a year rarely has a single place where a bill goes to wait.

## Do not say
No ERP claim. No saving figure from ranges alone. Not "Rs 340 crore revenue" as a filed result; say "CRISIL's FY26 estimate". Never pair FY25 table ratios with FY26 text ratios. Do not mention the limited track record as a fault; say the firm is young and quick.
