# Claude Design master prompt: Rajkamal Builders Infrastructure working-capital review (one booklet)

## 1. What you are building

A 16-page A4 portrait booklet titled "Working-capital review", printed and posted to the named reader at Rajkamal Builders Infrastructure Private Limited. You build ONE design and produce one edition for the reader in the reader table.
It answers one question for the reader: what if Crestline were already running at Rajkamal Builders Infrastructure, how much money and how much time would it save?
Every word and every figure is supplied in the Content section at the end of this prompt. Your job is the layout of that content. The design is yours; this prompt carries content only.

## 2. Reader editions

Build ONE 16-page design, then the edition for the row of the reader table in the Content section.
- An edition changes ONLY three things: the cover's "PREPARED EXCLUSIVELY FOR" lines, the page 2 letter and address block, and the page 2 salutation.
- Name the edition after its reader as given in the table. That label is for the file only and is never printed.
- Body copy is written in a neutral voice.

## 3. Rules you must follow

1. Use ONLY the text and figures in the Content section. Do not write new copy, captions, taglines, teasers, pull quotes or headings.
2. Copy every figure exactly as written: same digits, same decimals, same currency sign, same unit. Do not round, re-total, convert or recompute anything.
3. Headlines exactly as given, never reworded.
4. Footnotes and source lines exactly as given, on the page they belong to.
5. Lines starting with ">" are printed copy. Lines that do not start with ">" (such as "Data:", "Chart name:", lists under "Data", and notes) are instructions to you and are never printed as written; a "Data" list gives the labels and values to show.
6. No internal references in printed text: no file names, no ids, no checker notes, and no note addressed to the team.
7. No printed negatives about the booklet itself. Where a value is not public, print the words "Not public" or "Not published" only where the Content gives them.
8. No em dashes and no en dashes anywhere. Use a comma, a full stop, "to" or a middle dot (·) as the Content does.
9. Ownership words stay as written: the client acknowledges, certifies and pays; Rajkamal Builders Infrastructure measures, prepares, reviews, submits and invoices.
10. The Crestline logo is used as supplied and never redrawn. Nothing stands in for a Rajkamal Builders Infrastructure logo.

### Booklet accuracy rules (apply to every page)
A1. Claims about Crestline must be true today. Make no claim about Crestline beyond the supplied text. No certification, no hosting region, no encryption claim, no client names, no "live in X days", and no present-tense claim that Crestline connects to the company's ERP. The pilot is one entity, one segment, read-only, 14 days, as the Content words it.
A2. Quotes are not used in this booklet. Do not add any.
A3. Ours versus the client's follows the product rule, and every ownership tag stays as given.
A4. No headline is softened or inverted.
A5. Every value printed exactly as given, each with its label. Nothing is estimated or drawn from a figure that is not in the Content.
A6. Interest is not priced anywhere. Print no interest or rate figure.
A7. Anything marked "scenario", "with Crestline", "days less" or "released once" is labelled "Scenario, not a forecast." and is never presented as a reported figure. Scenario rows overlap and are never added together; print no total the Content does not give.
A8. No peers or comparatives.
A9. No other company's data. Do not bring in any company, name, figure or text from anywhere other than the Content. No demo numbers.
A10. Full address and correct names and titles on the letter page and cover, exactly as the reader table gives them.
A11. "You said" is never used.
A12. Never invent figures to fill a chart. The stages on page 8 carry no numbers at all; each reads "Not public". Never create a split of a total.
A13. No signed numbers in print: no "+" or "-" in front of a figure. Write increase / decrease in words.
A14. Every scenario result sits with its two lines on how Crestline would release it, exactly as the Content gives them.

B1. Every figure for Rajkamal Builders Infrastructure is combined across four group entities. Wherever a figure prints, the words "combined" and "four group entities" print with it or with its source line, as the Content gives them.
B2. Print no rating grade and no reason for the movement in income or in the operating cycle. Print figures and the question only.
B3. Page 13 carries no numbers and no figures: text only.
B4. Page 8 holds nothing but the Process map and its lines; the Process map is the whole of its content, and each stage carries only its name, its ownership tag and the words "Not public".
B5. Never compute a figure that is not given in the Content.

## 4. Before you finish, check every page

1. Count the pages: exactly 16, A4 portrait, in the order of the Content.
2. Read every headline against the Content: same words, same punctuation.
3. Pick every figure on every page and find it in the Content, character for character. Any figure you cannot find there, delete it.
4. Search the whole booklet for en dash and em dash characters. There must be none.
5. Every scenario is labelled "Scenario, not a forecast." and no scenario total was added.
6. Every footnote and source line is on its page.
7. No word was added: no taglines, no extra captions, no certification or hosting claim, no client names, no internal references.
8. Page 13 has no numbers. Page 8 stages carry no day counts.
9. The QR code on pages 15 and 16 opens crestlineintelligence.com/book-a-demo.
10. Lines that do not start with ">" are not printed as written.
