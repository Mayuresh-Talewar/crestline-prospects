# Depth: story angle (Rajkamal Builders Infrastructure)

Respect first: in business since 1979, "AA" class Government of Gujarat contractor, IVR A-/Stable and A2+ reaffirmed 29-Sep-2025, limits raised to Rs 519.85 crore, order book Rs 1,977.38 crore (4.07x FY25 combined income), no bank guarantee ever invoked, gearing 0.35x, interest cover 2.27x to 4.13x, EBITDA margin 7.22% to 13.76% on a richer STP/WTP mix (about Rs 278 crore of Rs 671 crore certified work).

The question: Infomerics says execution slowed because land possession and drawing approvals did not arrive; income fell from Rs 537.16 to 485.32 crore, and the operating cycle moved from 103 to 137 days while the order book grew. Infomerics lists a shorter operating cycle as an upward rating factor and a longer one as a downward factor. Where in those 137 days does each bill wait (measurement, certification, the department's desk, retention), and which of the four group entities holds it? The accounts are combined across four entities, so no single ledger shows it. Frame as visibility, never fault. The agency's 30-60 day collection range is a generalisation, not the company's own measure; do not use it as one.

Source: Infomerics press release 29-Sep-2025 (pp.1-7).
