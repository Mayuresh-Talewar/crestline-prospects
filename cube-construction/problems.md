# Problems, on the ruled story (CB-S23)

Respect first: operating income rose from Rs 520.79 Cr (FY23) to Rs 712.18 Cr (FY24), up 37%; order book Rs 1,522 Cr at 31 Dec 2024, 18 to 24 months of work (CRISIL, 2 May 2025, consolidated, adjusted by CRISIL).

1. Receivables run long. CRISIL estimates receivables at nearly 130 days at 31 Mar 2025, with payments realised in 130 to 150 days (FY25 estimate, basis not stated). Which bill, which step, which desk is not visible in any public account.
2. Retention sits inside that. CRISIL: "A large portion of receivables pertains to retention money." No balance or release date is published.
3. Year-end bunching. CRISIL: the company books bulk of its revenue in March, so receivables are high at the year end. Gross current assets 260 to 270 days, inventory about 115 days (FY25 estimates).
Question for the Board: where does each bill wait for those days?
