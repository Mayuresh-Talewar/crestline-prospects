# Garuda Construction and Engineering Limited: data (research step GA-1, 2026-10-09)

## VERDICT: FULL
Listed, filings are complete, FY26 audited accounts are out (AGM 30 Sep 2026), and the books are on Tally (auditor, stated). The working-capital story is real and large on one basis: trade receivables 165 days of revenue at 31 Mar 2026 (417 / 293 / 165 over FY24 / FY25 / FY26), plus contract assets (unbilled) of Rs 218.91 Cr. Revenue grew 2.4x in FY26.
Conditions for step 2 (see sensitive-garvis.md, not for the booklet):
1. 91.8% of FY26 trade receivables (Rs 220.63 Cr of Rs 240.41 Cr) are owed by related parties (promoter group and subsidiaries). The "who pays" side of O2C is largely group entities. The brochure must not say this; Garvis decides the framing.
2. Use STANDALONE as the primary basis throughout (rating, auditor, ratios are standalone). Consolidated differs by under 1% on revenue and PAT.
3. Q1 FY27 results (Aug 2026) were seen only in news. Primary filing NOT read, so none of it prints.
4. Order book has three dated figures that do not reconcile (see Conflicts). Pick one and print its date.

1. Revenue FY25: dossier 226.76 vs filings 225.03. Resolved: the dossier took total income.
2. Order book: 3,461 (27 Oct 2025, company deck) vs 4,876.78 (5 Nov 2025, NSE intimation) vs 4,336.72 (31 Dec 2025, rater). The first two reconcile (3,461 + 1,416 is 4,877). The rater's later figure is lower and its basis is not stated; difference NOT explained. Print one with its date only.

4. Registered office: AR26 lists "201, A Wing, Fortune 2000"; the Aug 2026 NSE letterhead reads "201, 2nd Floor, Fortune 2000". Use the annual report wording.
5. Promoter holding: 51.7% of total capital per the Aug 2026 pledge filing; 67.56% on a data site for Mar 2026. Not resolved. Not printed.
