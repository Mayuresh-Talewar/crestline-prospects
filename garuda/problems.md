# Garuda: problems (GA-2 story: contract assets, never debtors)

Basis: standalone, Rs crore, from the company's own Annual Reports. "AR26" = Annual Report 2025-26 (audit report 18 May 2026, submitted to the exchanges 5 September 2026). "AR25" = Annual Report 2024-25 (audit report 30 May 2025). Pages are printed page numbers.

## Respect first
Revenue from operations went from Rs 154.18 Cr (FY24, AR25 p.119) to Rs 225.03 Cr (FY25) to Rs 530.72 Cr (FY26, AR26 p.112). Profit after tax went from Rs 36.44 Cr to Rs 49.80 Cr to Rs 122.54 Cr (same pages). The company did this with 97 employees including the managing director at 31 March 2026 (AR26 p.53). That is the achievement. Everything below is about the cash that growth like this has to carry.

## Problem 1: work done is accumulating faster than it becomes a certified bill
- Contract assets (revenue in excess of billing) were Rs 96.13 Cr at 31 March 2025 and Rs 218.91 Cr at 31 March 2026 (AR26 p.131, Contract Balances; also p.124, Note 10).
- On FY26 revenue per day (Rs 530.72 Cr / 365 = Rs 1.454 Cr), the 31 March 2026 balance is 150.6 days. Crestline analytical measure, not an accounting standard.
- Why it matters: a bill cannot be raised until the work is measured and certified. Ten days off this wait is Rs 14.54 Cr of cash, released once. Thirty days is Rs 43.62 Cr. Scenario, not a forecast; the rows overlap and are not added. No interest is priced.

## Problem 2: the accounts cannot say which bill, which step, whose desk
- The Annual Report gives one balance per year-end. It does not say which running bills make it up, how long each has waited, or whether the wait is the company's desk (measure, prepare, review, submit, invoice) or the client's (acknowledge, certify).
- That is not a gap in the accounts; it is what accounts are for. The dates sit in Tally, one entry at a time. Crestline would read them as a queue. Step waits are not public and are not drawn.

## Problem 3: the book is large, and every rupee of it passes through the same wait
- The company's own presentation states an order book of Rs 3,461 Cr at 27 October 2025 (company presentation dated 28 October 2025).
- One day of FY26 revenue is Rs 1.454 Cr. As more of that book is executed, the same wait applies to more work. Shortening it by a day is worth more each year than the year before, if the book is executed. This is a direction, not a forecast, and it is not a calc input.

Not used (GA-2): any receivable or debtor measure, related-party items, pledge, exchange fines, disputed tax, rating operating-cycle and creditor days, the deposit and its rate, issue-proceeds table, other order book figures, the dossier's 226.76 and PACE claim.
