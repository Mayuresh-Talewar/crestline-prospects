## ONE-BASIS DAYS (Crestline's arithmetic on the published accounts), standalone, closing balance / flow x 365
Same method reproduces management's own numbers: receivables 82, 50, 78, 103 days (AR26 p20 [36]) and payables 71 to 57 days. Checked against the AR's ratio note 45 (AR26 p200 [259]): receivable turnover 4.37, inventory turnover 3.25, payables turnover 6.36 all reproduced to two decimals from the bases below (average balances there).
- DSO = trade receivables net / revenue from operations x 365.
- DIO = inventories / (material consumed + change in inventories) x 365. (Note 45 "cost of goods sold" base, confirmed by 3.25x.)
- DPO = trade payables / (that cost + construction and other project expenses) x 365. (Note 45 base, confirmed by 6.36x.)
- Contract-asset days = "Amount due from customers (unbilled)" net of ECL / revenue from operations x 365.
