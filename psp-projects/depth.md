# PSP Projects: story angle (depth.md)

All figures standalone, FY26 audited (AR 2025-26), as in data.md. Respect first, then the question of where bills wait.

## Respect first
- Over two decades of building: 256 projects completed, 94 running, order book Rs 13,447 Cr at 31 Mar 2026, up 85% in a year, after Rs 10,925 Cr of new orders (AR26 pp14, 16).
- Revenue grew 21.1% to Rs 2,989.45 Cr. Operating cash flow turned from Rs 54.52 Cr to Rs 337.41 Cr, and cash rose to Rs 257.98 Cr (AR26 p145).
- Surat Diamond Bourse, 68 lakh sq ft on one basement, was delivered by this company. The CFO's own letter calls this "an inflection point".

## The question of where bills wait (what the accounts show, what they cannot show)
1. Receivables took longer: debtor days 49.7 (FY24), 78.1, 102.6 (FY26); trade receivables Rs 335 Cr to Rs 840 Cr in two years. Management's own table says 50, 78, 103. 54% is not yet due; Rs 125.61 Cr is more than a year past due.
2. Built, waiting to be certified: unbilled revenue Rs 400.35 Cr net (48.9 days of revenue), down from Rs 514.74 Cr (76.1 days). Retention Rs 211.00 Cr, released "at the end of the project".
3. Stock: Rs 343.75 Cr, 116 days of material cost, 146 a year ago.
4. Vendors: 56.6 days on payables, 82.7 days if the Rs 184.58 Cr of bank-funded supplier invoices is counted. Vendor terms: 30-60 days outside the scheme, 30-180 inside. Paying vendors later is never the answer; the question is which bill waits on which desk.
5. The company has said what it wants: working capital "within 60 days" and receivable days "around 60 to 70" (CFO Hetal Patel, 30 Apr 2026). The gap between 102.6 and that target is about 33 to 43 days on Rs 8.19 Cr of revenue a day.

## What the accounts cannot tell them (Crestline's question)
Which site, which bill, which client desk, since when. The accounts give a year-end balance; a bill's eleven stages, who holds it and for how many days is in the ERP (the AR says SAP-enabled systems), not in the filing.

## Concentration to handle with care
One customer group is 30.8% of revenue and 28.8% of receivables, and about 67% of the order book is one group (see sensitive file; do not name in the booklet without Garvis).
