# PSP Projects: the problems, respect first (ticket PS-S23)

Basis: standalone, audited, AR 2025-26 (auditor reports 30 Apr 2026), AR 2024-25 and AR 2023-24 for the series. Rs Cr. Days are Crestline's arithmetic on the published accounts (balance / flow x 365).

## Respect first
Revenue from operations Rs 2,989.45 Cr in FY26 (up 21.1% from 2,468.28), EBITDA 179.50, PAT 52.29, order book 13,447 Cr at 31 Mar 2026 after 10,925 Cr of new orders (AR26 pp 37 and 203). Cash from operations turned from 54.52 Cr to 337.41 Cr (p204). The cash cycle already shortened from 229.7 to 210.8 days and unbilled days from 76.1 to 48.9.

## Problem 1. Receivables took longer, against management's own target
Receivable days 82.3 (FY23), 49.7, 78.1, 102.6 (FY26); trade receivables Rs 335.10 Cr to 840.30 Cr in two years. Management states 82, 50, 78, 103 (AR26 p36). The CFO, Mrs. Hetal Patel, on the 30 Apr 2026 call: "we will be maintaining around 60 to 70 days of trade receivable days" (p13). Fair reading from the report itself: 472.98 Cr of the 876.38 Cr gross was not yet due, and management ties the rise to revenue recognised in the final month (p36). Rs 403.41 Cr was past its due date, of which 125.61 Cr more than a year. The accounts cannot say which bills, which clients' desks, since when.
Question: which bills carry the 32.6 days between today and 70.

## Problem 2. Built, waiting to be billed and certified
Unbilled work Rs 400.35 Cr net (48.9 days), from 514.74 Cr (76.1 days). Already improving; the open question is which bills still wait and at which step (measure, prepare, review, submit are the company's; acknowledge and certify are the client's). No certification window is published.

## Problem 3. Project materials and the balances with no clock
Stock Rs 343.75 Cr, 116.0 days of material cost (146.1 a year earlier). Retention Rs 211.00 Cr, released at the end of each project per contract, sometimes replaced by bank guarantees, no release time stated. Vendor credit 56.6 days: shown, never priced, because paying later is never a saving.
