# Yash Construction Co: problems (YC-S23, 9 Oct 2026)

All figures: Acuite rating rationale, 29 Jun 2026. FY26 is provisional. Respect first: an unexecuted order book of Rs 1,543.19 Cr (as on 11 Jun 2026), operating margin up from 10.60% (FY25) to 12.51% (FY26 provisional), PAT Rs 41.12 Cr (FY26 provisional), and debtor days already down from 70 (FY25) to 64 (FY26 provisional). Revenue was Rs 620.50 Cr in FY25 and Rs 562.18 Cr in FY26 (provisional); printed as figures only, with no cause stated.

## 1. The wait is one number, and a bill's life has many steps
Debtor days of 64 is the average for a year. It cannot say which bill, which client department, which step (measurement, bill preparation, review, submission, acknowledgement, certification, payment), or since when. Crestline's question: where each bill waits, and whose move ends the wait.

## 2. The book still to be executed will add bills to that wait
Rs 1,543.19 Cr is to be executed (11 Jun 2026). Every rupee of it will pass through the same stages. One day of FY26 (provisional) revenue is Rs 562.18 Cr / 365 = Rs 1.5402 Cr, so ten days on the receivable leg is Rs 15.40 Cr and thirty days is Rs 46.21 Cr (scenarios, calc/yc-savings-output.txt).

## 3. A figure needs a name and a date on it
The rating record gives no breakdown by client, site or desk. A finding is only useful when it names the bills, the owner and a due date, and when the same figure is re-measured afterwards. That is the ticket and auditor flow.
