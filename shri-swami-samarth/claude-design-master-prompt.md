# Claude Design master prompt: Shri Swami Samarth Engineers working-capital review (one booklet, per-reader editions)

Paste everything below this line into Claude Design. Upload the Crestline logo file before you send it.

---

## 1. What you are building

A 16-page A4 portrait booklet titled "Working-capital review", printed and posted to named readers at Shri Swami Samarth Engineers Limited. You build ONE design and produce one edition per reader listed in the reader table (see "Reader editions" below).
It answers one question for the reader: what if Crestline were already running at Shri Swami Samarth Engineers, how much money and how much time would it save?
Every word and every figure is supplied in the Content section at the end of this prompt. Your job is layout, type and charts, on your own canvas.

## 2. Reader editions

Build ONE 16-page design. Then, for each row of the reader table in the Content section, produce one edition of it.
- An edition changes ONLY three things: the cover's "PREPARED EXCLUSIVELY FOR" lines (that reader's name and title), the page 2 letter (that reader's letter text and address block), and the page 2 salutation.
- Every other page, word, figure, chart and position is IDENTICAL across editions. Do not tailor any body page to a reader.
- Name each edition after its reader as given in the table (for example the word Edition followed by the reader's name). That label is for the file only and is never printed.
- Body copy is written in a neutral voice. Quotes are credited by name and title, as the Content gives them; never rewrite a credit into "you" for any edition.

## 3. Rules you must follow

1. Use ONLY the text and figures in the Content section. Do not write new copy, captions, taglines, teasers, pull quotes or headings. If a field has no text, leave it empty.
2. Copy every figure exactly as written: same digits, same decimals, same currency sign, same unit. Do not round, re-total, convert or recompute anything.
3. Headlines exactly as given in the headline table, split into two lines as shown. Never reword them.
4. Footnotes exactly as given, on the page they belong to.
5. Text inside quotation marks in the Content is printed copy. Words outside quotation marks (for example "Chart:", "Lead:", "Tiles", instructions about scale, and notes about decisions) are instructions to you and are never printed.
6. No working notes in printed text. Reference codes, file names, tags in brackets, status words used as markers, and any note addressed to the team are never printed. A source is printed only in the words the Content gives for it.
7. No printed negatives about the booklet itself: never print what is missing, unknown, unchecked or left out ("not available", "we could not find", "to be decided", and so on). Where a field is empty, leave the space empty. Print a "not published" or "not public" line only where the Content gives it as quoted copy.
8. No long dashes (Unicode 2013 and 2014) and no dash used as punctuation anywhere. Use a comma, a full stop, "to" or a middle dot (·) as the Content does.
9. Ownership words stay as written: the client acknowledges, certifies and pays; Shri Swami Samarth Engineers measures, prepares, reviews, submits and invoices. Do not swap them.
10. Never draw, trace or imitate a logo for Shri Swami Samarth Engineers. Its name is set as text. Only the Crestline logo file you were given may be used.

### Added rules for this booklet
B1. The company name prints as text on the cover and in the letter. No company logo appears anywhere.
B2. Page 8: each stage tile carries ONLY its name and its tag. No days, no bar lengths, no shares, no example waits of any kind. Do not invent step waits, not even as examples. There is no card above the timeline.
B3. Page 13 is text only. No table, no chart, no example or computed figures. No numbers on this page.
B4. On every page with a chart, tiles or a timeline: never compute a figure that is not given in the Content. Example figures count as figures.
B5. A lens or card with no public figure reads exactly as the Content words it, with its reason. Add nothing to it.
B6. Never print a rating grade, a limit, or the name of any rating agency other than Acuite.
B7. Scenario bars and all chart bars start at zero and share one scale per chart.
B8. No interest figure is printed anywhere. FY25 and FY26 are the only years that appear.
B9. Never print a type size or any other design measurement in the booklet.

### Booklet accuracy rules (apply to every page, every edition)
C1. **Claims about Crestline must be true today.** Make no claim about Crestline beyond the pasted text. No certification "in progress", no hosting region, no encryption claim, no client names, no "live in X days", and no present-tense claim that Crestline connects to the company's accounting system. The pilot is one entity, one segment, read-only, 14 days, as the Content words it.
C2. **Every quote names the right speaker**, exactly as the Content credits it. Never move a quote to another person.
C3. **Ours versus the client's follows the product rule.** In cash-in steps, acknowledged, certified and collected are the client's acts; measuring, preparing, reviewing, submitting and invoicing are the company's own. Keep every company and client tag as given.
C4. **No "nothing went wrong" headlines.** Use only the headlines given; never soften or invert one.
C5. **Charts drawn to scale.** Every bar, segment, column and point sits within 2% of its own value on ONE shared scale per chart, starting at zero. Every bar shows its value label.
C6. **Interest only at a rate the company discloses.** No rate is disclosed, so none is printed or computed.
C7. **Savings are scenarios.** Anything marked "scenario", "sooner" or "dashed" is drawn dashed and hollow with "Scenario, not a forecast." beside it. Scenario rows overlap and are never added together; print no total the Content does not give. Charts the Content marks as history only get no scenario mark.
C8. **Peers and comparatives** appear only where the Content prints them. None are printed here.
C9. **No other company's data.** Do not bring in any company, name, figure or design text from anywhere other than the Content, including from earlier booklets or your memory. No demo numbers beside the company's figures.
C10. **Full address and correct names and titles** on the letter page and cover, exactly as the reader table gives them, per edition.
C11. **"You said" only for the reader's own words.** Never print "you said", "in your own words" or any second-person credit over a quote by someone other than that edition's reader. Never print a note or flag on the page.
C12. **Never invent figures to fill a chart.** If the Content gives a stage, step or bar without a value (for example the page 8 bill timeline), draw it without a number. Never create an "illustrative split" of a total, and never add a sentence about one.
C13. **No signed numbers in print.** Write inflow / outflow, increase / decrease. No "+" or "-" in front of a figure. The telephone number is copied as given.
C14. **Every problem chart shows the outcome with Crestline.** Beside the "today" figure, draw the scenario result from the Content (dashed outline, same scale, labelled with the days and cash it releases, plus "Scenario, not a forecast."). Use only the values the Content gives.

## 4. Before you finish, check every page of every edition

1. Count the pages: exactly 16, A4 portrait, in the order of the Content.
2. Read every headline against the headline table: same words, same punctuation, line 2 in gold.
3. Pick every figure on every page and find it in the Content, character for character. Any figure you cannot find there, delete it.
4. Search the whole booklet for long dash characters (Unicode 2013 and 2014). There must be none.
5. Every chart: one scale per chart starting at zero, each bar within 2% of its value, every bar labelled, nothing drawn from a figure that is not in the Content.
6. Every scenario mark is dashed and hollow and carries "Scenario, not a forecast." Charts marked "history only" carry none. No scenario totals added.
7. Every footnote is on its page, word for word.
8. No word was added: no taglines, no right-hand footer teasers, no extra captions, no "Live in" line, no certification or hosting claim, no client names, no working notes, no printed negatives. Page 8 has no days on any tile. Page 13 has no digits in its body text.
9. No company logo anywhere. Only the Crestline logo file you were given is used.
10. The QR code on page 15 (and on page 16) opens crestlineintelligence.com/book-a-demo.
11. Instruction words from the Content ("Chart:", "Lead:", notes about decisions and checks) are not printed anywhere.
12. Editions: compare each edition with the first. Only the cover reader lines, the page 2 address block, the page 2 salutation and the page 2 letter differ. No "you said" sits over a quote that is not that edition's reader's own.
