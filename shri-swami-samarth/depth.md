# Shri Swami Samarth Engineers: story angle (respect first, then where bills wait)

All figures are in data.md with source, date and basis. Nothing here implies access to the company's ERP.

## Respect first (what the record shows, in the rating agencies' own words)
- A Pune contractor that scaled fast: FY23 381.76 Cr and FY24 519.72 Cr (CRISIL, 16 Oct 2024), and FY26 operating income of 630.37 Cr with PAT 56.28 Cr (Acuite, 6 Jul 2026). Acuite calls the growth "~46 percent" on FY25.
- Self-execution with its own fleet: operating margin 18% in FY26 (Acuite), with roads at ~78% of revenue and irrigation ~20%.
- The company has pulled its own balance sheet forward: net worth 236.85 Cr against 151.03 Cr a year earlier; gearing 0.86x against 1.35x; promoters put in 29.52 Cr of equity in FY26 (Acuite).
- Working capital moved the right way in FY26: GCA 132 days (169), debtors 65 (81), inventory 32 (63), creditors 65 (78). Acuite calls it "moderately intensive" and expects it to stay near that level.
- Order book ~1,181.27 Cr at 31 May 2026 (Acuite).
- Rating story across agencies: CRISIL BBB+/Stable (Oct 2024) then Acuite A-/A2+ (Jul 2026), on bank limits that grew from 36 to 196 Cr (CRISIL) and to 81 Cr (Acuite).
Respect line for the letter: a firm that builds NHAI and irrigation work with its own machines and has put its own equity behind the growth. Print only what the record says; do not mention any agency that did not rate it well (that is Garvis only).

## Then the question: where do bills wait?
The rationales stop at "receivables 65 days". They cannot say:
- how many of those 65 days sit before the bill (measurement, joint inspection, certification) and how many after (the authority's own payment queue);
- which site or authority, since NHAI roads and state irrigation bill differently (Infomerics 19 Mar 2024: roads monthly with inspection within 15 days; irrigation on milestones, paid in 1 to 2 months);
- who in the company owns each wait.
Acuite adds a second place money sits that a debtor figure hides: "other current assets (majorly consisting of advances to suppliers)" inside GCA of 132 days, and 42.46 Cr of interest-bearing loans to group companies at 31 Mar 2026. Do not print any of these as a problem the company has; they are figures the agency named and the question is which desk could see them by the day.

Candidate frame (to be sized in step 2 from FY26 Acuite figures only): the same 65-day receivable seen by authority and by stage, instead of as one number. Never combine with FY25 days or with Infomerics FY23 days.
