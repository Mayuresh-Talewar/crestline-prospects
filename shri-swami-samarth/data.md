## Conflicts between sources
1. Revenue FY24 vs FY25: CRISIL/CARE FY24 519.72/519.61 against Acuite FY25 432.42 (a fall of about 17%, not computed as a trend). Acuite gives no FY24 figure; no source explains it (restatement, JV share or definition?). Do not print an FY24 to FY26 growth line without a footnote; safest to print FY25 to FY26 (432.42 to 630.37, Acuite's own "~46 percent") and FY23 to FY24 separately.
2. FY22/FY23 totals differ by a few lakh between Infomerics (127.11/381.71) and CRISIL/CARE (127.73/381.76). Use CRISIL for the pair FY23/FY24.
3. Year established: 1994 (Infomerics), 1998 (company site /about/, Acuite), 1999 (CRISIL, CARE, Brickwork), 2000 (company site home page). Incorporation 2019 is consistent. Print "incorporated 2019 from a partnership firm" and no founding year.
4. Registered office: company site says Mumbai (Nariman Point); aggregator CIN prefix PN suggests Pune; CRISIL says "Based in Pune". Site pages carry the page title "Jai Hind Sugar", i.e. site template shared with another business. Confirm before printing a registered address; print the Pune corporate office only if the letter needs one.
5. Acuite says "Status of non-cooperation with previous CRA: None"; CRISIL (Oct 2024), CARE (Jul 2024) and Infomerics (May 2025) each record non-cooperation by Brickwork and, for Infomerics, itself.
6. Dossier vs rationale: see Dossier check.
7. Acuite 6 Jul 2026 net worth increase explanations: no conflict, listed for completeness of equity infusions.
