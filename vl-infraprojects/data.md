## BASIS (use this one everywhere)
STANDALONE, audited, V.L. Infraprojects Limited Annual Report 2025-26 (auditor Pary & Co, report dated 29 May 2026, AGM 29 Sep 2026), Rs lakh converted to Rs Cr. Per-day = annual / 365. Reason: the company states NO day-count; it publishes turnover in "times" only (Note 23). 365 is Crestline's arithmetic and ties to CRISIL's own 212-day FY25 GCA (below). Year-ends are closing balances (not averages), because the company's own average-based turnover cannot be turned into a year-end day figure.
