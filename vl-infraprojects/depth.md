# V.L. Infraprojects: story angle (figures from data.md only)

## Respect first
A Rs 150 Cr water EPC that tripled in three years (revenue from operations Rs 45.56 Cr FY23 to Rs 149.74 Cr FY26), listed on NSE Emerge in July 2024, and reduced its top-five client share from 80.50% to 43.12% and Gujarat from 89.74% to 67.16% of revenue. Its own MD letter (Annual Report 2025-26) calls working capital "our single clearest operational priority for FY27" and tells shareholders to hold the company to it. That candour is the opening.

## The question: where do the bills wait?
1. Built, not yet billed: unbilled revenue Rs 49.54 Cr at 31 Mar 2026, about 120.8 days of revenue (Rs 40.02 Cr, 120.7 days a year earlier), nearly half of total assets. It is larger than all trade receivables (Rs 23.12 Cr, 56.4 days) and larger than the whole net worth (Rs 50.33 Cr). The accounts cannot say which projects, which certificate, or since when.
2. Billed, waiting for payment: 56.4 days (53.0 a year earlier); 98% inside six months of due date, Rs 0.40 Cr disputed.
3. Stock: inventory Rs 9.18 Cr, 59.4 days of materials (19.9 a year earlier), as subcontracting grew to 43.4% of revenue.
4. Retention both ways: Rs 7.68 Cr receivable from clients, Rs 10.83 Cr held back from vendors; release dates not published.
5. Cash: operating cash flow -Rs 15.39 Cr (FY25) and -Rs 4.10 Cr (FY26) while profit was Rs 7.02 Cr and Rs 8.42 Cr; short-term borrowings up from Rs 17.92 Cr to Rs 26.51 Cr. CRISIL's measure, gross current assets 212.5 days (FY25) and 218.1 days (FY26), against its 180-day upgrade trigger.

## What the accounts cannot tell them (Crestline's gap)
Site, bill, desk and since when; the client's certification window against the actual wait; which of the 18 projects holds the unbilled Rs 49.54 Cr; JV-level view across H.M. Electro Mech projects. Do not imply their ERP or data.

## Lines to avoid
No claim they are in trouble; no ERP claim; no rating-upgrade promise; savings only as scenarios; no rate-based interest figure (no disclosed rate).
