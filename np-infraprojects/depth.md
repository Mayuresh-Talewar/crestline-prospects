# NP Infraprojects: story angle (depth)

Respect first. Everything below comes from the CARE Ratings press release of 22 Sep 2025 (standalone; FY25 unaudited). Nothing from the CRISIL rationale of the Pune namesake.

## What to respect
- A company incorporated in November 2018 that reached total operating income of Rs 242.39 Cr in FY25 from Rs 102.62 Cr in FY21, about 24% a year (CARE p1). Growth came from executing subcontracted projects.
- Work done for RVNL, NHAI and KEC International (CARE p2).
- Margins up: PBILDT 14.57% in FY25 from 11.36% in FY24; PAT Rs 15.93 Cr from Rs 7.98 Cr; gearing down to 1.57x from 1.92x.
- A Rs 1,367 Cr order book as on July 2025, 5.65x the year's revenue (CARE p1).

## The question (where bills wait)
- CARE puts gross current asset days at 282 in FY25 and names the cause: margin money, security deposits and retention locked with clients. The same document calls the reported operating cycle moderate at 42 days. Two measures, never netted; the story is that the cycle looks short while a large share of money sits in assets that do not move.
- So the angle is not "slow invoicing". It is: which of these balances (retention, deposits, margin money) is held on which project, with which client, released against which document, and since when. The accounts show a single total; they cannot show the site, the bill or the desk.
- With about Rs 1,000 Cr of newer orders not yet executing at pace, each project that does start adds its own retention and deposits. Seeing them per project, early, is the Crestline question.
- What the filing cannot tell: debtor, creditor and inventory days, retention split by client, ageing. All NOT FOUND.
