# NP Infraprojects: problems (step 2)

Entity: NP Infraprojects India Private Limited (NIPL), standalone. Source for every figure: CARE Ratings Limited press release, 22 September 2025. FY24 audited, FY25 unaudited. Story per README NP-2. Respect first, then the question.

## Problem 1. Growth, and the second number
Respect: total operating income Rs 220.63 Cr (FY24) to Rs 242.39 Cr (FY25, unaudited), Rs 102.62 Cr in FY21, about 24% a year (CARE, 22 Sep 2025). Operating margin (PBILDT) 11.36% to 14.57%; profit after tax Rs 7.98 Cr to Rs 15.93 Cr.
Problem: gross current asset days rose to 282 in FY25, which CARE ties to margin money requirements, security deposits and retention amounts locked with clients (CARE, 22 Sep 2025, p1).
What the accounts cannot tell: which project, which client, which document, since when. No split of the 282 is published.

## Problem 2. What a day is worth
One day of FY25 total operating income = 242.39 / 365 = Rs 0.664 Cr (FY25 unaudited). Ten days on the 282 = Rs 6.64 Cr, thirty = Rs 19.92 Cr, once (calc/np-savings-output.txt). Scenario, not a forecast. No interest: no rate published.
What the accounts cannot tell: which of those days belong to NP's own paperwork and which end with the client's act.

## Problem 3. Order book, project by project
Order book Rs 1,367 Cr as on July 2025 (CARE, 22 Sep 2025, p1). Each project can carry its own retention, deposits and margin money; seeing them per project, early, is the question. The accounts show one order-book figure and one GCA-days figure.
Deliberately not used: CARE's execution-pace comment and any rating wording (Garvis only).
