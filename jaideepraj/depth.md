# Jaideepraj Infrastructure - depth.md (story angle)

Respect first. A promoter-led water-supply EPC firm, with promoters of more than two decades in civil construction (Infomerics, 21 Feb 2025), took revenue from Rs 240.30 Cr to Rs 401.83 Cr in FY24 (+67%), lifted EBITDA margin from 9.12% to 11.93%, and cut gearing from 2.45x to 1.14x by prepaying loans. The agency calls the order book strong: Rs 1,229.48 Cr at 31 Dec 2024, 3.06x of FY24 revenue, from government and public-sector clients, on contracts of 18-24 months.

Then the question of where bills wait. A book three times the year's revenue is a long run of milestone bills to measure, certify and collect. At 31 Mar 2024 cash stood at Rs 2.79 Cr and the current ratio at 1.14x; fund-based limit use averaged 71% over the 12 months to Jan 2025. The agency names "stretch in the working capital cycle" as a downward factor. The accounts do not show which bill is waiting, at which desk, or since when. That is the gap a bill-level view of time and money fills.

No day figure for this company exists in a filing, so none may be printed. Any per-day value in Step 2 is a computed figure on FY24 revenue (Rs 401.83 Cr), with its basis stated.
