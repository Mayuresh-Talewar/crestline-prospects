# Jaideepraj Infrastructure: problems (respect first)

All figures: Infomerics Ratings press release, 21 Feb 2025, standalone, audited. FY24 = year to 31 Mar 2024.

## Respect first
Revenue rose from Rs 240.30 Cr (FY23) to Rs 401.83 Cr (FY24), up 67% (pp 2, 4). EBITDA was Rs 47.94 Cr, a margin of 11.93%, and PAT Rs 29.86 Cr (p4). The order book was Rs 1,229.48 Cr at 31 Dec 2024, 3.06 times FY24 revenue (p2).

## Problem 1: a book three times the year's revenue is a long run of bills
Each running bill passes steps owned by different desks: measure, prepare, review, submit and invoice are the company's; acknowledge, certify and pay are the client's. Published accounts show totals, not which bill waits at which step or since when. No days are published for any wait.

## Problem 2: the value of a day is large, and unmeasured
One day of FY24 revenue is Rs 401.83 Cr / 365 = Rs 1.10 Cr (rounded; unrounded 1.100904). Ten days on one wait is Rs 11.01 Cr and thirty days is Rs 33.03 Cr (calc output). These are scenarios on published figures. No interest is priced; no borrowing rate is published.

## Problem 3 (optional, not used as a figure): the accounts cannot say whose move ends each wait
Without the dates in the ERP, a wait on certification (client) cannot be told from a wait on submission (company). That split is what makes a delay an action list.
