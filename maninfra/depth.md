# Man Infraconstruction: depth (MI-1)

Document and page for every quote; speaker confirmed on the page image. Basis: STANDALONE = EPC (see data.md section 0).

## 1. Management on EPC working capital and collections
**NOT FOUND.** In the Q4 FY25 and Q4 FY26 calls, the Q1 FY27 analyst meet and the presentations, management says nothing about EPC receivables, certification, retention, vendor payments or EPC working capital. Collections it quotes are real-estate buyer collections: "sales of approximately INR1,800 crores, along with collections of approximately INR990 crores" for FY26 (Manan Shah, Managing Director, Q4 FY26 call 14 May 2026, p.4), "collection approximately INR279 crores" in Q4. That is group real estate, not EPC.
The only working-capital wording in the filings is a risk-table line in the Annual Report: under Liquidity Risk, "Strong working capital management practices" and "EPC contracts often provide mobilization advances to support upfront funding needs" (AR FY2025-26, MD&A p.99 [PDF 121]). No figure.

## 2. EPC growth: exact words
- "Moving to our next engine of growth, the EPC business. The current EPC order book stands at about INR392 crores, which shall be executed over the next 3 to 4 years. Our upcoming development constitute a construction area of about 1 crore square feet. About 50% of this will add to the EPC order book once the pipeline is launched in FY27." Manan Shah, MD, Q4 FY26 call, 14 May 2026, p.3 (prepared remarks, opened by him on p.1).
- Asked if the company is moving away from ports: "we don't want the company's growth to depend just on the ports ... we've been focused more towards the luxury residential segment." Manan Shah, same call, p.8. And: "real estate would definitely be the heavy lifter ... the intention is definitely to grow more towards real estate" (p.9).
- "our current in-house EPC portfolio is nearly 1 crore square feet. So that either comes in the form of savings to the company where the company self-executes the project or where there are partners, we tend to give the EPC contract to the Man Infra." Manan Shah, p.9.
- Q4 FY25 call, 20 May 2025, p.9, Manan Shah: "regarding working for the other developers, no, we would like to focus and use all our capex onto the own real estate side of it. But regarding the infrastructure projects, we are definitely keen." p.13: "Rs 500 crores is purely the outside EPC."
- Analyst meet, 21 Aug 2026, p.6, Manan Shah (MD): "we are almost on the final verge of negotiations for one of the significantly large order for EPC. It's under discussion and hopefully in the next two quarters, we would be announcing some great numbers on the EPC side ... we are very strong on the port side. It's almost on the verge to get completed ... a 9 to 10,000 crore of future order book of our own residential and commercial portfolio."
- Reading: EPC is being run down as a third-party business (revenue -70% from FY23, order book -69%), and is repositioned as the in-house builder for the group's real estate. A "large EPC order" is under negotiation but unsigned.

## 3. Real-estate targets (not EPC; context only)
- FY27 and FY28: "combined sales target ... of over INR5,000 crores" (Q4 FY26 call p.3). FY27 alone: "we target nothing less than INR2,500 crores for this upcoming year" (Manan Shah, p.5). This is a real-estate presales target, not revenue. The dossier's ">Rs 2,500 Cr sales FY27" is correct in words, wrong if read as revenue.
- Vision 2031: portfolio GDV Rs 18,125+ Cr to Rs 35,000+ Cr by 2031 (IP Q1FY27 slides 39-41). Rs 5,500-6,500 Cr GDV of launches in FY27, 1 million sq ft (call p.6).
- Revenue recognition "nearly around 35% to 40% of growth" (Manan Shah, p.6).

## 4. Debt-free wording
- "Consolidated debt remained extremely low at approximately INR58 crores, thereby maintaining MICL's net debt-free position." Manan Shah, Q4 FY26 call p.4. Not "debt-free". Standalone has nil borrowings (AR26 p.108).
- "we do not like taking debt ... the 78 crores ... majority of that is a contribution from partners in the form of debt. It is not an actual borrowing" (analyst meet p.5) and "the intention is just to remain debt free" (p.9).

## 5. Track record (internal, from the company's own documents)
- "20/20 projects completed before schedule, 6-30 months early across all projects" and "~90% inventory sold prior to OC" (IP Q1FY27 slides 9-10). Real-estate delivery record, not EPC billing record.
- Aaradhya Avaan 38-storey members tower delivered in under 2.5 years (call p.2). Port record: 8 ports (AR26 p.97 [119]); BMCT Terminal 4, Rs 1,800+ Cr, 95%+ executed at Jun-26.
- Own scale: "60+ million sq. ft. of construction, 60+ projects, 20,000+ units, in-house engineering strength of 500+ engineers" (AR26 p.97); group over 850 people (p.99).
- Cost control language only: "tight project monitoring and cost control" (AR26 p.98). No published cash-conversion, DSO or collection-days target for EPC.

## 6. Order book live-check
- BMCT (PSA, Nhava Sheva) is the only large external order: Rs 1,800+ Cr, 2022-2026, 95%+ executed (IP Q1FY27 slide 27). It is near completion, so the external order book is running off.
- 30 Jun 2026 book Rs 305 Cr: infra 61% (about Rs 186 Cr), residential 39% (PMC fees and Artek Park EPC) (slide 28). Jun-26 is lower than Mar-26's 392.
- The Rs 9,000-10,000 Cr "future order book" is the group's own pipeline, not contracted orders (analyst meet p.6; Q4 FY25 call p.13 said about Rs 5,000 Cr and 10% PMC fee).

## 8. Fit read for MI-2 (analysis, not claim)
- Standalone EPC receivables are Rs 37.9 Cr, payables Rs 18.7 Cr, retention Rs 3.6 Cr, contract assets Rs 11.6 Cr. Ageing is 84.5% under 6 months. Little trapped money, no debt, so no interest saving and no distress story.
- Rs 6.4 Cr (16.9%) of receivables is from group associates; Rs 821 Cr of standalone cash is lent to group real-estate entities (outside EPC scope).
- Honest fit is thin for EPC-only scope: small, shrinking book, small balances. The real money-and-time question sits in real estate and group funding, which Crestline does not model.
