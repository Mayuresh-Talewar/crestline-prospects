Company Profile

A Legacy of Excellence

Who We Are

Shubham EPC Private Limited is a leading EPC company based in Pune since 1997. Our presence has expanded across India. We primarily offer turnkey solutions to the Industrial, Institutional, and Public sectors, covering everything from conceptual design to the final project handover.

Crafting excellence is our forte, and we take immense pride in our prowess in project execution. Our scope of work covers Civil Construction Projects, Mechanical, Electrical, and Plumbing (MEP) works, Interior fit outs, Healthcare, Life sciences and Laboratory Construction.

We have unique capabilities and have successfully completed numerous projects in the above segments, including installation, testing, and commissioning of engineering as well as medical equipment. With our meticulous planning strategies, project execution skills, quality construction practices, and timely services, we have become one of the best solution providers for the Private sector and Public sector.

Renowned for our unwavering commitment to excellence, we consistently deliver comprehensive end-to-end solutions that set the benchmark for quality. With our focus on creating sustainable landmarks nationwide, we have emerged as the premier choice in the EPC industry.

"At Shubham EPC, we are committed to contributing to Nation building by delivering transformative projects that reflect our aspiration of 'Building Future'."
What We Do
We execute each project with engineering excellence, whether it involves constructing resilient infrastructures, pioneering healthcare facilities, or providing comprehensive MEP services, all under our visionary Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) framework.
Moving Forward
Driven by innovation, collaboration, and continuous growth, our journey has been marked by remarkable achievements and rooted in a steadfast commitment to excellence. As we move forward, our focus remains on reaching new milestones, shaping a more impactful future, and delivering sustainable solutions through smarter, safer, and environmentally conscious construction for future generations.
