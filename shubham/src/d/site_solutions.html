Our Verticals

What We Do

Building

Shubham EPC is a leading EPC company in India, offering reliable and innovative solutions in the industrial, public, and institutional sectors for EPC construction and project management. Our expertise spans across various domains, making us one of the top EPC companies in Pune, Maharashtra and India.

Healthcare & Life Sciences

Our offerings include Modular and Hybrid Operation Theatres, Intensive Care Units, Central Sterile Supply Departments, Medical Gas Distribution Systems, Oxygen Generation Plants, Pneumatic Tube Systems, and Biomedical Waste Systems. We also offer various system elements such as intelligent theatre control panels and OT integration.

MEP

Shubham EPC Pvt. Ltd., a leading MEP service provider with over 26 years of experience, offers a one-stop solution for all MEP services nationwide. We provide sustainable MEP solutions for various industries, including Commercial, Residential, Industrial, Institutional, Hospitals, and Infrastructure.

Data Centre

We provide comprehensive EPC solutions for mission-critical Data Centre, including civil, structural, and advanced MEP works. Our proven track record includes prestigious projects like NMDC and STT GDC, ensuring high reliability and safety standards.
