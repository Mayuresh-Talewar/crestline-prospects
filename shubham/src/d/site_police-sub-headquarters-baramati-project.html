Police Sub Headquarters, Baramati

Public Sector Project

Project Details

Client:Government of Maharashtra, Police Department
Location:Baramati, Pune District, Maharashtra
Sector:Government / Public Sector
Scope:Civil and Structural Works
Status:Completed

Project Overview

Shubham EPC built the Police Sub Headquarters at Baramati, a critical law enforcement infrastructure project for the Government of Maharashtra. The complex houses administrative offices, control rooms, barracks, and support facilities.

The project was delivered with adherence to government construction standards while ensuring the complex meets all operational requirements of a modern police sub-headquarters within the approved budget and schedule.
