Our Expertise

Healthcare Infrastructure Construction

Shubham EPC offers a comprehensive range of solutions to enhance Healthcare. We provide a complete suite of services, from conceptualization and design to the execution of life sciences & laboratory construction, along with annual maintenance contracts. specialized healthcare construction

The team's expertise in procurement, installation, testing, and commissioning of medical equipment has been instrumental in the success of numerous Healthcare projects. With our innovative approach, we have already established a strong presence across the nation in this segment.

Our offerings encompass Modular and Hybrid Operation Theatres, Intensive Care Units, Central Sterile Supply Departments, Medical Gas Distribution Systems, Oxygen Generation Plants, Pneumatic Tube Systems, and Biomedical Waste Systems. Additionally, we provide various system elements such as intelligent theatre control panels, OT integration, surgical scrub sinks, and more.
