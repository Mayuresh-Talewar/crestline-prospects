Our Leaders

Visionary Leadership Driving Excellence

At Shubham EPC, our leadership team comprises industry veterans with decades of experience in Engineering, Procurement, and Construction. Their visionary guidance and unwavering commitment to excellence are the driving forces behind our rapid growth and reputation for delivering quality projects.

Mr. Vinay Badera

Founder & Managing Director

Mr. Vinay Badera is the visionary Founder and Managing Director of Shubham EPC Pvt. Ltd. With his profound expertise and strategic foresight, he has steered the company from its inception to becoming a leading name in the EPC sector. His leadership is characterized by a deep commitment to quality, innovation, and sustainable growth.

Under his guidance, the company has successfully executed complex projects across diverser sectors, earning the trust of clients nationwide. He believes in empowering teams and fostering a culture of excellence and integrity.

Mrs. Archana Badera

Director

Mrs. Archana Badera plays a pivotal role in the strategic direction and operational excellence of Shubham EPC. Her focus on organizational development and human resources has been instrumental in building a strong, cohesive team that shares the company's core values.

She brings a compassionate yet results-oriented approach to leadership, ensuring that the company not only achieves its business goals but also maintains a positive and inclusive work environment.

CA Mangesh Katariya

Director

CA Mangesh Katariya brings financial acumen and strategic planning expertise to the Board of Directors. As a Chartered Accountant, his deep understanding of financial management, compliance, and corporate governance ensures the company's financial health and stability.

His insights are crucial for long-term planning and investment decisions, enabling Shubham EPC to pursue ambitious projects with confidence and fiscal responsibility.

Mr. Karan Shah

Director

Mr. Karan Shah is a dynamic leader who brings fresh perspectives and energy to the directorship. His focus on modern construction technologies and project management methodologies helps the company stay ahead of industry trends.

Committed to innovation and efficiency, he oversees key project initiatives, ensuring timely delivery without compromising on the high standards of quality that Shubham EPC is known for.

Mr. Vipul Patel

Director

Mr. Vipul Patel joined Shubham EPC Pvt. Ltd. as a Director in 2026, marking a strategic milestone in our growth journey. He holds extensive experience in Civil Construction (9+ years) and Electrical Works (24+ years), bringing strong expertise in project planning, procurement, budgeting, and site execution.

With a hands-on approach, Mr. Patel independently oversees purchasing, site-level financial management, and critical operational decisions. His commitment to quality and timely delivery has contributed significantly to the successful execution of numerous infrastructure projects.

Recognized for his leadership and problem-solving abilities, Mr. Patel consistently drives project efficiency and organizational growth, while remaining actively involved in social initiatives and community service.
