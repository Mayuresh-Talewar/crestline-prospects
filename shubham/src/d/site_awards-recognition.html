Awards & Recognition

Celebrating Excellence & Innovation

Shubham EPC proudly celebrates a significant milestone with our NMDC-02 and NMDC-03 Data Centre projects in Mumbai receiving the Best Safety Excellence Award. This recognition reinforces our steadfast commitment to safe, efficient, and high-quality project execution across every site.

At the Credai Pune Metro Safety Awards 2024-25, Shubham EPC earned the Silver Award in Category-II (1 to 5 lakh sq. ft. built-up area) for outstanding Safety Management practices. This achievement reflects our continuous focus on building a culture where safety, compliance, and discipline define everyday work.

Adding to these accomplishments, our STT DC-05 project has been honoured with the Best Facility Award for Innovation. This award highlights our innovative approach and dedication to developing and maintaining best-in-class labour camp facilities that enhance comfort, efficiency, and well-being.
