Casa Venero

Ongoing Projects

Project Details

Client: Emaar India
Location: Alibag, Maharashtra
Sector: Real Estate / Luxury Residential
Total Area: 99,500 Sq. m (approx. 24.5 acres)
Scope: Civil & Finishing Works

Overview

Casa Venero, nestled in the serene coastal town of Alibag, offers a haven of pure leisure for those seeking tranquillity and refined living. Spread across 99,500 sq. m (9 hectares 95 ares) of lush, verdant landscape, this exclusive development presents a limited collection of finely crafted villas designed for true weekend indulgence.

Emaar India's first project in Maharashtra, Casa Venero is an ultra-luxury villa development that harmonizes with nature. Shubham EPC is proud to be associated with this prestigious project, executing high-quality Civil and Finishing works to bring this vision of leisure and luxury to life.
