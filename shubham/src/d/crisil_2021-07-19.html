Detailed Rationale

CRISIL Ratings has reaffirmed its ratings on the bank facilities of Shubham EPC Private Limited (SEPL) and has subsequently withdrawn its ratings at the company's request and on receipt of no objection certificate from the banker. The withdrawal is in line with CRISIL Ratings’ policy on withdrawal of ratings.

About the Company

SEPL was incorporated in 2009 and is promoted by Mr Vinay Badera and Ms Archana Badera. The company undertakes civil construction works related to industrial, commercial, and residential buildings. It is headquartered in Pune, Maharashtra, and is a Class I contractor registered with the Maharashtra Public Works Department and the Central Public Works Department.
