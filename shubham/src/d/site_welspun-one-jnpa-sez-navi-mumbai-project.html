Project Details

Location: JNPA SEZ, Navi Mumbai
Sector: Industrial / Warehousing
Architect: Venkataramanan Associates

Overview

Shubham EPC is proud to execute the Welspun One Warehouse Development Project at JNPA SEZ, Navi Mumbai. This landmark project will serve as a modern logistics hub designed for seamless connectivity, efficient operations, and future-ready warehousing.

With support from leading consultants-Venkataramanan Associates (Architect), WWP (Structural), Gleeds (Cost & Tender), and Grune Design (MEP)-the project reflects our commitment to precision, quality, and timely delivery. This development strengthens regional logistics infrastructure while reinforcing Shubham EPC's expertise in building high-performance industrial facilities.
