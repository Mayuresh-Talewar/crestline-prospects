Detailed Rationale

Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its ‘Crisil BBB+/Stable/Crisil A2’ ratings on the bank facilities of Shubham EPC Private Limited (SEPL)

 

The rating continues to reflect the extensive experience of the promoters in the civil construction industry, the healthy order book of SEPL providing medium-term revenue visibility and its comfortable financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by exposure to risks inherent in tender-based business amid competition, along with large working capital requirement.

Analytical Approach

Crisil Ratings has evaluated the standalone business and financial risk profiles of SEPL

Key Rating Drivers & Detailed Description

Strengths:

  • Extensive experience of the promoters: The two-decade-long experience of the promoters has given them a strong understanding of the market dynamics and enabled them to establish healthy relationships with suppliers and varied customers. Company has among few prominent players in healthcare infra segment. Business is thus aided by the company diversifying its revenue stream owing to work orders from the healthcare, data centre and MEP segments. Customers include Public Works Departments, other government and semi-government bodies and corporates.

 

  • Healthy order book providing medium term revenue visibility: SEPL has an order book of above Rs 1400 crore, to be executed in the next 24-30 months, providing revenue visibility over the medium term. The order book is diversified owing to orders from Public Works Departments, government bodies and private parties for healthcare and data center project. Sustenance of improved revenue and maintenance of healthy orderbook backed by smooth execution remains critical.

 

  • Comfortable financial risk profile: Capital structure is moderate on account of lower reliance on external funds as reflected in gearing of 0.61 time as on March 31, 2025. Debt protection metrics were adequate, indicated by interest coverage and net cash accrual to total debt ratios of 5.82 times and 0.52 times, respectively, for fiscal 2025. With improving operating performance and net cash accruals, the financial risk profile is expected to remain comfortable over medium term as well.

 

Weaknesses:

  • Susceptibility to tender-based operations amid competition: Most of the company’s orders are derived from Maharashtra. Revenue and profitability depend entirely on the ability to win tenders. Also, entities in this segment (more specifically in civil infra) face intense competition, thus requiring them to bid aggressively to procure contracts, which restricts the operating margin to a moderate level. Same is mitigated with company diversifying its operations in healthcare and data centre segment. Also, given the cyclicality inherent in the construction industry, the ability to maintain profitability through operating efficiency becomes critical.

 

  • Large working capital requirement: Gross current assets have remained high in the range of 155-200 days over the three fiscals through March-25, driven by moderately high receivables, inventory and other current assets pertaining to contractual requirement towards work orders. The working capital requirement is funded through credit from suppliers and short-term bank debt. Execution of large orders may lead to high incremental working capital requirement. Efficient working capital management and better liquidity position would be critical over the near-to-medium term.

Liquidity: Adequate

Bank utilization is moderate at 80 percent for the past twelve months ended April 2025.  Cash accrual are expected to be around Rs 40 crore which are sufficient against term debt obligation of Rs 5-8 crore over the medium term. In addition, it will be act as cushion to the liquidity of the company.

Outlook: Stable

Crisil Ratings believes that SEPL will continue to benefit from the extensive experience of its promoters, established relationships with clients and healthy order book.

Rating sensitivity factors

Upward factors:

  • Sustained increase in revenue and operating margin sustenance leading to cash accrual of Rs.50 crore or above.
  • Maintenance of healthy order book on continuous basis
  • Sustenance of the healthy financial risk profile and liquidity

 

Downward factors:

  • Decline in revenue leading to net cash accrual of less than Rs 30 crore.
  • Large debt-funded capital expenditure, substantial increase in the working capital requirement or limited cash flow weakening liquidity and the financial risk profile.

About the Company

SEPL was initially founded in 1997 as a proprietorship entity and was reconstituted as a private limited company in 2010. Headquartered in Pune, the company undertakes civil construction works related to industrial, commercial and residential buildings. In October 2020, it changed its name from Shubham Civil Projects Pvt Ltd to the current name because it has now added MEP and healthcare work to its portfolio, whereas earlier it was only into civil works. Mr Vinay Badera and Ms Archana Badera are the promoters of the company.
