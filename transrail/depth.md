## 3. Any working-capital target
- Stated target for working-capital days: below 80 (27 May 2026, aspiration), restated as below 81 (7 Aug 2026). No dated commitment.
- Stated target for net debt to EBITDA: 0.33 by year end FY27 ("our stated direction").
- No target for receivable days, contract-asset days, retention release, or inventory days found in any document read (searched AR, deck, both transcripts for "target" and "days").
- The definition of "working capital days" is not printed in any document read; the Chairman's letter calls it "Net Working Capital". Do not print a computed figure beside 81 (own build is about 51.8 days on a different basis, data.md).
