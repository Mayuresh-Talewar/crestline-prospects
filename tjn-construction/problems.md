# TJN Construction: problems.md (two questions the published record cannot answer)

No figure appears here. Both are framed as questions, with respect: the published record carries no financial figures for TJN Construction, which is itself the reason a live picture from its own ERP matters.

## Problem 1: Where does a buried stage wait before it becomes a bill?
Ground engineering is finished underground and cannot be re-inspected, so each stage must be measured and agreed before it is billed. The published record cannot say which sites hold finished work that is not yet measured, checked or submitted, how long it sits, or whose desk it is on. Crestline would read the dates the ERP already records for joint measurement, check, preparation, review and submission, and name the desk for each wait.
Chart on the page: outline of where a bill can wait (page 5), no values.

## Problem 2: After the bill is submitted, whose move ends each wait?
Acknowledgement, certification and payment belong to the client; invoicing belongs to TJN Construction. The published record cannot split a delay between the two, by client or by site, or show how much retention is still held. Crestline would rebuild each bill from its dates and rank the waits by money held and days waited, with client delay never counted as the company's.
