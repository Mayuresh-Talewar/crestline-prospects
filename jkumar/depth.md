## 3. Working capital target

Latest, in the company's own words: "100 to 110, not more than that" (Nalin Gupta, T127 p17, 7 Aug 2026); "if it's anything, it will be like 110" (Kamal Gupta, T127 p11). This is stated as intent ("we intend to keep"), not a commitment, and the definition of "working capital days" is never given (data.md section 7). Earlier anchors, each re-set after delivery beat it: "around 120 to 125" (Jul 2025), "115 to 120" (Feb 2026). Q1 FY27 stands at 103 days.

No receivable-day, contract-asset or retention target was found in any document read.

## 4. Disconfirm step (one test per headline)

| Headline | Test designed to break it | Result |
|---|---|---|
| "Working capital improved 112 to 99 days" | Read the components on the balance sheet | Receivables did not move (Rs 1,488.56 to 1,485.29 Cr). The gain came from inventory (Rs 503.48 to 329.65 Cr; company inventory days 70 to 58) and a larger mobilisation advance (Rs 621.02 to 706.09 Cr). So "tightened collections" (Q3) is not visible in the receivable line |
| "Collections tightened" (Q3, Q5) | Receivable ageing from due date, AR26 PDF p97 | Opposite signal: overdue share 17.2% to 27.5%; 6 to 12 months overdue Rs 12.65 to 141.15 Cr (11.2x); over 1 year overdue fell slightly (Rs 88.50 to 81.61 Cr). A block of bills went past due in H2 FY26 |
| "OCF Rs 1,128 Cr from Rs 376 Cr" | Read every cash flow line, AR26 PDF p90 | Includes Rs 283.97 Cr of released bank deposits and Rs 176.51 Cr of new capex creditors. Without both, standalone OCF is Rs 655.97 Cr, x1.75 not x2.97 (Computed, our adjustment) |
| "Debtor days 96 to 95" | Rebuild from note 42's own ratio | FY26 95.3 days ties; FY25 note 42 gives 85.9 days on average basis, the MD&A says 96 (closing basis). Two bases inside one report |
| "WC days FY24 = 105" (decks) | Compare with the FY24 annual report | AR24 PDF p32 says 123 days for FY24. Same company, same year, two figures. Not reconciled anywhere |
| "Healthy cash balance" | Compare the two statements in AR26 | MD&A: "healthy cash balance of ₹235 crore" (AR26 PDF p30, printed 55); letter: "net cash of ₹264 crore" (printed 13); standalone cash and equivalents Rs 203.10 Cr (AR26 PDF p96). Different measures, none defined |
| "Total mobilisation advance 470" (Nalin Gupta, T127 p13) | Balance sheet | Rs 706.09 Cr at 31 Mar 2026 (AR26 PDF p103). 470 is the interest-bearing part only |
