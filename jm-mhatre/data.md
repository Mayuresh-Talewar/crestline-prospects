## FEASIBILITY VERDICT: LIGHT

Blunt version. Enough primary material exists to fill a booklet's figures, but only a thin one:

- **Yes:** rating documents from six agencies (CARE, CRISIL, India Ratings, Infomerics, Brickwork, Acuite/SMERA) from 2016 to June 2026; ICRA has none, plus two annual returns (Form MGT-7, FY24 and FY25) and an AGM notice that the company itself publishes on jmminfra.com. Revenue, EBITDA and PAT run FY18 to FY25. The working-capital days the rationales print run FY16 to FY23. Order book, client mix, rated limits by lender and the board are all on primary paper.
- **No:** working-capital days stop at FY23 (the last cooperative rationale with day counts is Infomerics, 28 Mar 2024). FY24 and FY25 have P&L only. No interest rate is disclosed in any document, so the booklet can carry **no interest-saving figure** (README basis rule). No CFO exists in the filings: the only key managerial person is the Company Secretary.
- **Before Garvis commits:** the rating file is dominated by sensitive items S1 to S4 (see sensitive-garvis.md). Read them before deciding to proceed at all. They do not block the data; they may block the outreach.

FULL would need FY24/FY25 debtor, inventory and creditor days and a disclosed borrowing rate. Neither exists publicly.
