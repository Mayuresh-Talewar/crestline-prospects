Please note that the rating(s) are based on best available information with the credit rating agency: the entity whose debt is being published via this rating rationale did not provide the requisite information needed to conduct the rating exercise or did not provide the No Default Statements (NDS) for the last three months and is therefore classified as 'non cooperative'.

Non cooperation by Issuer

CRISIL Ratings has been consistently following up with J. M. Mhatre Infra Private Limited (JMMIPL) for obtaining information through letters and emails dated September 25, 2023, September 27, 2023  and September 29, 2023 among others, apart from telephonic communication. However, the issuer has remained non cooperative. The entity has also not provided No Default Statement NDS for the last three months.

 

‘The investors, lenders and all other market participants should exercise due caution with reference to the rating assigned/reviewed with the suffix 'ISSUER NOT COOPERATING' as the rating is arrived at without any management interaction and is based on best available or limited or dated information on the company. Such non co-operation by a rated entity may be a result of deterioration in its credit risk profile. These ratings with 'ISSUER NOT COOPERATING' suffix lack a forward looking component

Detailed Rationale

Despite repeated attempts to engage with the management, CRISIL Ratings failed to receive any information on either the financial performance or strategic intent of JMMIPL and has also not received No Default Statement from the client, which restricts CRISIL Ratings’ ability to take a forward-looking view on the entity's credit quality. CRISIL Ratings believes that rating action on JMMIPL is consistent with ‘Assessing Information Adequacy Risk’. Based on the last available information, the ratings on bank facilities of JMMIPL has been migrated to ‘CRISIL A4+ Issuer Not Cooperating’.

 

CRISIL Ratings has withdrawn its rating on the bank facilities of JMMIPL on the request of the company and after receiving no objection certificate from the bank. The rating action is in-line with CRISIL Rating's policy on withdrawal of its rating on bank loan facilities.

About the Company

JMMIPL was initially set up as a partnership firm M/s. J.M. Mhatre in 1985 by members of the Mhatre family of Panvel (Maharashtra). It was reconstituted and incorporated as a private limited company in January 2010. The company carries out civil construction works such as earthwork for roads and railway lines, construction of roads and highways, canals, bridges, railway over bridges, wastewater treatment plants, railway stations, special marine structures and ports, etc. It is a class 1A contractor and specializes in complex bridges and construction of national highways , ports and water supply systems. carries out works for Public Works Department (PWD), City & Industrial Development Corporation of Maharashtra Limited (CIDCO), J.N.P.T, NHAI, Central Railways and other government entities.

Status of noncooperation with previous CRA:

The company has not co-operated with India ratings and research private limited and Brickworks Ratings India Private Limited which has classified the company as Issuer non-cooperative vide their PR dated 25th September 2018 and 7th September 2023 respectively. Reason provided by India ratings and research private limited and Brickworks Ratings India Private Limited is non furnishing of Information required to carry rating exercise.
