# LCC Projects Limited: depth.md (story angle)

Figures: restated consolidated, FY26, 365-day closing-balance basis, from the Prospectus of 11 Sep 2026 (see data.md for pages). Days are Crestline's arithmetic on the published accounts.

## Respect first
- Two decades from a Gujarat partnership firm (2004) to a listed EPC house: Rs 2,439 Cr to Rs 3,600 Cr revenue in two years, PAT Rs 122 Cr to Rs 286 Cr, order book Rs 7,953 Cr across 103 projects.
- 89% of revenue is from government departments. Majority of the order book is state water schemes; CARE rates the bank lines A; Stable (reaffirmed 8 Jul 2026) and cites low counterparty risk.
- Operating cash from operations turned from Rs 77 Cr (FY24) to Rs 245 Cr before tax in FY26, and the cash and bank balances rose to Rs 460 Cr.

## Then the question: where do the bills wait?
1. Work done, not yet billed: unbilled revenue Rs 535 Cr = 54 days of revenue, on top of Rs 456 Cr of receivables = 46 days. A bill is in one of those two rooms for about 100 days of revenue. CARE names year-end billing and trial runs on water supply projects as the cause. The accounts cannot say which site, which bill or which desk.
2. Debtor days went 23 to 31 to 46 in two years; receivables rose Rs 206 Cr in FY26 alone (4,558.21 less 2,501.66 mn) while revenue rose 23%. Nearly all of it is under 6 months, so the wait is in the process of certifying and clearing, not in old disputes.
3. Stock on site: Rs 248 Cr, 65 days of material consumed (36 days in FY24). Pipes dominate: one supplier is 35% of material bought.
4. Cycle: stock + unbilled + debtors less vendor credit = about 133 days; vendor credit covers only 32 days of total construction cost. The company itself reports working capital days of 67 (current assets less current liabilities over revenue).
5. Funding: Rs 720 Cr of the Rs 861 Cr total debt is working-capital borrowing; fund-based limits used 50 to 60%. The rate is a range (7.05% to 10.70%), so no interest figure is printed.
6. 103 projects, 12 states, 12 to 36 month contracts, retention of 5 to 10% held to the end of the defect period: a bill's position across that spread is exactly what an ERP records as what happened, not how long each step took.

## What the accounts cannot tell them
Which of 103 projects holds the unbilled Rs 535 Cr, how long each bill sat at measurement, certification, and client payment, whose desk, and since when. SAP is being implemented (Prospectus): the layer above it that times each step is the Crestline question.

## Do not say
No S/4HANA RISE or Highbar (unsupported). No interest saving figure. No retention balance. No cash-cycle target the company has not stated.
