# LCC Projects Limited: problems.md (LC-S23, 2026-10-12)

Respect first. LCC Projects took revenue from ₹2,438.91 Cr (FY24) to ₹3,600.25 Cr (FY26), profit after tax from ₹122.00 Cr to ₹286.44 Cr, and holds an order book of ₹7,953.18 Cr across 103 projects (Prospectus dated 11 Sep 2026, restated consolidated, pages 32, 33, 261). Cash generated from operations before tax went from ₹77.07 Cr to ₹244.74 Cr (page 262).

## 1. Receivable days went from 23.4 to 46.2 in two years
- Trade receivables ₹156.62 Cr (31 Mar 2024), ₹250.17 Cr, ₹455.82 Cr (31 Mar 2026), while revenue grew 1.48 times and receivables 2.91 times (Crestline's arithmetic, page 260 and 261).
- Almost all of it is under 6 months from the bill's due date (₹452.85 Cr), with ₹8.61 Cr at 6 months to 1 year and nothing older (Note 11.4, page 298). The wait is in the process of certifying and clearing, not in old disputes.
- The company states a customer credit period of 45 to 90 days (page 308). 46.2 days sits inside it, so collection is not priced: receivables already sit within the credit clients are given and a bill is never moved before its own due date.
- The accounts cannot say which of 103 projects, which bill or which desk holds the days. That is the Crestline question.

## 2. A bill waits 54.2 days of revenue before it is billed
- Unbilled revenue ₹534.83 Cr at 31 Mar 2026 (₹461.74 Cr, ₹324.62 Cr in the two years before; Note 15, page 291). Contract asset days 48.6, 57.8, 54.2 (Crestline's arithmetic).
- Together with receivables, ₹990.65 Cr and 100.4 days of revenue are in the two rooms.
- No certification window is published, so collection is not priced: receivables already sit within the credit clients are given and a bill is never moved before its own due date. Measuring, preparing and submitting the bill are LCC Projects' own; certifying is the client's.

## 3. Operating profit does not become cash at the same speed
- Operating profit before working capital changes ₹481.73 Cr against cash generated from operations ₹244.74 Cr in FY26; ₹236.99 Cr stayed in working capital (page 262, Crestline's arithmetic). The company's own working capital days went 31, 52, 67 (page 32).
- Stock days on material consumed also grew, 35.7, 54.6, 65.3; vendor credit covers 32.3 days of construction expenses.
- Not raised: vendor credit (paying later is never cash released), retention (mixed with deposits), interest (no single borrowing rate is published).
