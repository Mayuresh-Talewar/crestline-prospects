# Claude Design master prompt: Dineshchandra R. Agrawal Infracon working-capital review (one booklet)

Paste everything below this line into Claude Design. Upload the Crestline logo file before you send it.

## 1. What you are building

A 16-page A4 portrait booklet titled "Working-capital review", printed and posted to a named reader at Dineshchandra R. Agrawal Infracon Private Limited. You build ONE booklet and produce the one edition listed in the reader table.
It answers one question for the reader: what if Crestline were already running at Dineshchandra R. Agrawal Infracon, how much money and how much time would it save?
Every word and every figure is supplied in the Content section at the end of this prompt. This prompt carries content only. The design is yours.

## 2. The edition

Produce the one edition in the reader table. It changes only three things: the cover's "PREPARED EXCLUSIVELY FOR" lines, the page 2 letter and address block, and the page 2 salutation. Name the edition after its reader as given in the table ("Edition: <reader name>"). That label is for the file only and is never printed.
Body copy is written in a neutral voice. Quotes, where any appear, are credited by name and title as the Content gives them.

## 3. Rules you must follow

1. Use ONLY the text and figures in the Content section. Do not write new copy, captions, taglines, teasers, pull quotes or headings. Where a line has no text, leave it empty.
2. Copy every figure exactly as written: same digits, same decimals, same currency sign, same unit. Do not round, re-total, convert or recompute anything.
3. Headlines exactly as given, never reworded.
4. Footnotes exactly as given, on the page they belong to.
5. Lines starting with ">" in the Content are printed copy. Words outside those lines (for example "Data:", "Chart name:", "Closer:", "Body:", and "HEADLINE:" or "KICKER:" as labels) tell you what a line is and are never printed; print the text after the label.
6. No internal references in printed text: no file names, reference codes or notes addressed to the team. A source is printed only in the words the Content gives for it.
7. Print "Not published" and "Not public" only where the Content gives that wording. Never add your own notes about what is missing, unknown or unchecked.
8. No em dashes and no en dashes anywhere. Use a comma, a full stop, "to" or a middle dot as the Content does.
9. Ownership words stay as written: the client acknowledges, certifies and pays; Dineshchandra R. Agrawal Infracon measures, prepares, reviews, submits and invoices. Do not swap them.
10. Use the Crestline logo file as supplied, never redrawn. No logo of Dineshchandra R. Agrawal Infracon is supplied; show the reader's name and title only, with no company mark.
B1. Charts are named exactly as the Content names them, and carry the values the Content gives under "Data:", each with its label. Nothing is added to a chart.
B2. Every "With Crestline" value and every ten-day value is a scenario, and is shown as one, labelled "Scenario, not a forecast." Where the Content gives no scenario value for a chart, show none.
B3. Each "HOW CRESTLINE WOULD ..." pair of lines is printed beside the result it explains, exactly as given, two lines each.

### Booklet accuracy rules (apply to every page)
A1. Claims about Crestline must be true today. Make no claim about Crestline beyond the pasted text. No certification, hosting region or encryption claim, no client names, no "live in X days", and no present-tense claim that Crestline connects to the company's ERP. The pilot is one entity, one segment, read-only, 14 days, as the Content words it.
A2. Every quote names the right speaker, exactly as the Content credits it.
A3. Ours versus the client's follows the product rule. Acknowledged, certified and paid are the client's acts; measuring, preparing, reviewing, submitting and invoicing are the company's own. Keep every ownership tag as given.
A4. Use only the headlines given; never soften or invert one.
A5. Print every value exactly as the Content gives it, and give each value its label. Never compute a figure that is not given.
A6. Interest only at a rate the company discloses. No rate is disclosed, so no interest figure appears anywhere.
A7. Savings are scenarios. Anything marked "With Crestline", "scenario" or "days less" is labelled "Scenario, not a forecast." Scenario rows overlap and are never added together; print no total the Content does not give.
A8. Comparatives appear only where the Content prints them. Never build a scorecard of missed promises.
A9. No other company's data. Do not bring in any company, name, figure or text from anywhere other than the Content, including from earlier booklets or your memory. No demonstration figures beside the company's figures.
A10. Full address, correct names and correct titles on the letter page and cover, exactly as the reader table gives them.
A11. "You said" only for the reader's own words. Never credit a quote to the reader that someone else spoke.
A12. Never invent figures. Where the Content gives a stage or line without a value, print it without a number. Never create a split of a total.
A13. No signed numbers in print. Write inflow and outflow, increase and decrease, never "+" or "-" in front of a figure.
A14. Every chart that has a scenario shows the "With Crestline" value from the Content beside the "today" value, labelled with the days and the cash it releases and "Scenario, not a forecast." Use only the values the Content gives.
