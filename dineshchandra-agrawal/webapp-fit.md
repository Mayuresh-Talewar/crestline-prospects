# DRAIPL: where Crestline fits (DA-S23)
Sits above the company's ERP (system not named in any public source, so the pack prints "ERP"). Read-only; the company's own team makes every change.
- O2C (cash in): bill timeline, certification and collection waits, client screen. Fits the 38-day collection and the bill journey on page 8. Owners: measure, prepare, review, submit and invoice are ours; acknowledge, certify and pay are the client's.
- Working capital: cash-cycle diagram and simulator. Public data supports only two legs (stock 20, collection 38), so the diagram is partial and drawn without a total.
- P2P and vendors: advances to subcontractors and suppliers are named by CARE, with no balance, creditor days or terms. Lens shown as not published.
- Tickets and auditor: the 38-day finding becomes a ticket with an owner and date, re-measured until it moves.
- Not modelled: the HAM, BOT, toll and transmission asset businesses; pilot is one to three EPC projects.
