# CS Construction India: story angle (respect first, then where bills wait)

## Respect first
- A Nagpur family firm that started in 1983 with bore-driven piling for railway bridges and now builds high-level bridges, ROBs, RUBs and flyovers on EPC terms. Company's own profile: "90+ projects in the last four decades", 38 under execution at 31 Mar 2025. Contractor to Indian Railways.
- CRISIL's independent reading: operating income Rs 103.10 Cr (FY22), Rs 186.23 Cr (FY23), about Rs 319 Cr (FY24); interest cover 3.74x, 5.39x, then above 10x; debt to net worth 1.24x then 1.00x; first-time BBB/Stable on 8 Aug 2024. Praised: promoter experience (four decades), order book visibility, comfortable financial profile.
- Order book Rs 1,808.9 Cr at 31 Mar 2025 (company statement), against Rs 1,251 Cr in CRISIL's August 2024 view. 230+ people, 8+ of them in finance and accounts: a lean desk for 38 live sites.

## Then the question of where bills wait (only what the sources say)
- CRISIL names the pressure itself: working capital requirement is high because of deposits with customers for bidding, security and retention, and it rises with revenue. A stretch in the working-capital cycle is a downward rating trigger. Money sits with clients as deposits and retention, not only as unpaid bills.
- Clients are public bodies (Railways, PWD, MRIDCL, DFCCIL, municipal): CRISIL says entire revenue is from tender-based orders.
- Concentration: top 4 orders are 46% of the book. CRISIL: "any major delays in order execution or delay in receivables may impact company's credit profile".
- Bank limit use about 64% on a Rs 30 Cr cash credit; unencumbered cash Rs 6 Cr at 31 Mar 2024.
- Company's own site (/overview/): bank guarantees and surety bonds from nationalised banks need collateral, leaving "operational capital unnecessarily tied up". Written in a passage addressed to a trade-finance firm; the company's own view.
- The question: of the rupees held in bid deposits, retention and certified-but-unpaid bills across 38 sites, which bill, which site and which desk is each waiting on, and since when? The accounts cannot say.

## Cautions for the writer
- Revenue: CRISIL's basis for FY22 to FY24. The company's FY25 bar (Rs 388.06 Cr) is on a different basis: never put it beside CRISIL's numbers.
- No days figures exist. Do not invent a DSO. Do not imply their ERP.
- Describe bridge and railway-led EPC; "roads" is in CRISIL's wording but the company stresses bridges.
