## 2. The five problems

### P1. Work done, not yet billed: ₹1,313.5 Cr
- Contract assets ₹1,313.5 Cr (current 1,068.34 + non-current 245.15), 31 Mar 2026. **V** [R p.10].
- 182.8 days of revenue. **C** = 1,313.49 / 2,622.72 x 365.
- Trend, days **C** (contract assets / revenue x 365): FY23 187.5 · FY24 232.2 · FY25 178.7 · FY26 182.8. Inputs **V** [IP slide 28; R p.10].
- Non-current ₹245.15 Cr is new in FY26 (nil FY25); reason NOT FOUND. **V** [R p.10].
- Accounts cannot say: which RA bill / work order, how long it sat at measurement vs certification vs submission, whose desk, why ₹245 Cr moved to non-current. Contract-asset ageing NOT FOUND.
- Chart: **stage timeline** (work done → measured → certified → billed → paid) with the unbilled span thick; days from the ERP are blank dashed boxes.

### P2. Bills raised, not yet paid: ₹1,176.8 Cr, growing 3.6x faster than sales
- Trade receivables incl. non-current ₹1,176.8 Cr (1,085.00 + 91.81). **V** [R p.10]. Terms "generally 45 to 90 days". **V** [AR26 PDF p.158].
- Trend ₹ **C**: FY23 445 · FY24 634 · FY25 1,139 · FY26 1,177 (current + non-current, IP slide 28). Revenue 1,799 → 2,623. Receivables +164%, revenue +46% (1,177/445; 2,623/1,799).
- Days **C**: FY23 90.3 · FY24 119.8 · FY25 177.0 · FY26 163.8. Combined with contract assets: 277.8 · 352.0 · 355.7 · 346.6.
- Receivables + contract assets = ₹2,490 Cr, 62.7% of total assets (auditor KAM). **V** [AR26 PDF p.141, consolidated KAM; **SALES-10 fix:** the auditor's own base is ₹2,460.3 Cr net of ECL and excludes the qualified and EoM items; 62.73% is the exact figure; our ₹2,490 Cr is the results-table sum, label it "Crestline sum"].
- Caveat to print: Capacite's policy files retention and unbilled-awaiting-certification inside receivables [AR26 p.148], so days overstate versus peers.
- Accounts cannot say: split of retention vs certified-awaiting-payment vs disputed; by client, public vs private, site.
- Chart: **indexed trend lines**, FY23 = 100: revenue 146, receivables 264, labelled at the end. One image, one idea.

### P3. The old money is getting older
- Over six months: ₹128.2 Cr (10.8%) vs ₹90.4 Cr (7.9%). **C** from ageing table **V** [AR26 PDF p.158]. 1-2 years: 17.93 → 45.69. 2-3 years: 6.23 → 14.56. Over 3 years flat 24.0. Disputed within it ₹41.21 Cr.
- Auditor: Emphasis of Matter ₹54.93 Cr long-outstanding receivables, contract assets and other exposures (NCLT, High Court, RERA); FY25 ₹63.62 Cr. **V** [R p.5; AR25]. Qualified opinion on ₹11.56 Cr (one party) running since Q2 FY24. **V** [R p.5, p.14]. **SALES-10: a Qualified Opinion (Basis: INR 1,155.93 lakhs, one party) AND an Emphasis of Matter (INR 5,492.76 lakhs) sit in the SAME report, standalone and consolidated; they are not alternatives. The party was re-admitted into CIRP (insolvency) in FY26 (Note 53), so "insolvent counterparty" is fair for the 11.56 Cr, not for the 54.93 Cr.** See section SALES-10. Write as "flagged by the auditor", never as fault.
- Allowance ₹9.78 Cr closing; ₹57.93 Cr charged and ₹57.45 Cr written off in FY26. **V** [AR26 PDF p.158].
- Accounts cannot say: which clients and projects the aged bills belong to, since which date each stalled, who is chasing.
- Chart: **ageing stack**, two columns (Mar 2025, Mar 2026), 1-2 yr band highlighted.

### P4. Growth has been funded by working capital, then by borrowing
- Cash absorbed by working capital, FY23 to FY26: 301 + 467 + 409 + 199 = **₹1,376 Cr** against operating profit before working-capital changes 407 + 435 + 503 + 494 = ₹1,839 Cr, so 75%. **C**, inputs **V** [IP slide 29]. Operating cash FY23-26: 88, (39), 52, 223 (reported, rounded).
- Borrowings 326 (Mar 24) → 417 (Mar 25) → 472 (Mar 26). **V** [IP slide 28; R p.11]. Rose in FY26. Net debt eased only because cash rose 94 → 164 (pack s.1, s.2). Limits "fully tied up" **V** [T p.2].
- Finance cost % revenue **C**: FY23 4.95 · FY24 4.97 · FY25 3.97 · FY26 3.68 (89, 96, 93.35, 96.41 over revenue). Rate down 12.65% → 9.65% in 15 months **V** [T p.7; IP slide 5].
- Peers FY26 **C** (pack s.5): B.L. Kashyap 3.15%, PSP 1.44%, Ahluwalia 1.11%. Directional only.
- 10 days of DSO = ₹71.9 Cr; carry at the company's 9.65% about ₹6.9 Cr a year. **C** (pack s.2).
- Credit to say: FY26 is the best year, operating cash ₹223 Cr, interest down. Accounts cannot say: which projects absorbed the ₹1,376 Cr.
- Chart: **waterfall**, FY23-26 cumulative: operating profit before WC 1,839 → working capital (1,376) → tax (136) → operating cash about 324 (reported sum; rounding stated).

### P5. The vendor side: one average hides every vendor
- Trade payables ₹909.92 Cr (MSME ₹45.66 Cr + other ₹864.26 Cr). **V** [R p.11]. Series 634 → 818 → 896 → 910 **V** [IP slide 28].
- DPO about 179 days FY26 (202 FY25) **C**, pack s.2; cash cycle 224.5 vs 203.0 days **C**. Company basis not disclosed, so label "Crestline arithmetic".
- Peers pay faster: PSP 62, Ahluwalia 83, B.L. Kashyap 85 days **C** (pack s.5).
- Accounts cannot say: payable ageing against each vendor's own due date, early vs late, MSME 45-day exposure by vendor, whether stretching vendors is a choice or a consequence. Rule 1: never present higher DPO as good.
- Risk: reads as "you pay late". Frame as "an average of 179 days across every vendor is one number for hundreds of different dates".
- Chart: **peer bar** (days to pay vendors) or dumbbell (terms vs actual) as a blank frame for the ERP version. Use only if a 6th problem slot is cut; otherwise keep to the 4 above plus this as the closing "other half".

Ranking for a chairman (impact / effort to print): P2 trend (high/low) > P1 unbilled (high/low) > P3 ageing (high/low) > P4 waterfall (high/medium) > P5 (medium/low, sensitive).
