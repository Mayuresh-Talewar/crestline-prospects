It is a "Strictly Private & Confidential" file that Crestline will post to one person: Mr. Rohit Katyal, Executive Chairman of Capacite Infraprojects Limited, an Indian listed building-construction (EPC) company.

WHY IT EXISTS
Mr. Katyal replied to our email on 27 September saying he was available for a video call, then went quiet after we sent times. This file has to make him open it, understand each page in a few seconds, trust it, and book the call. He runs finance, commercial and accounts at Capacite himself.

THE STORY
It is Capacite's own story, told from its published documents: who they are, what they have built, where the cash is waiting, what the accounts cannot show. Crestline appears only at the end, as the answer. Respectful throughout: growth and the order book are the achievement; the waiting cash is the cost of that growth, not a failing.

WHAT CRESTLINE IS
Crestline sits above a construction company's ERP. It only reads the ERP, never changes it, and shows where the company's cash is waiting, for how long, why, and whose desk is holding it. In all copy say "your ERP". Never name a software product.
