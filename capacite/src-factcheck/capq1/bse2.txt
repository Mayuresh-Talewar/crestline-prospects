Key Highlights

@@PAGE 5
KEY COMPANY UPDATES
Operational Highlights:
•
Execution during the first half of the quarter was impacted due to workmen shortage. The workmen situation has since normalized, with 
increasing on-ground workforce.
•
The Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation (BMC) suspended all water supply connections to construction sites across Mumbai effective June 17, 
2026, to address water shortage. While the Company sourced water through alternate means, execution in the Mumbai region was partially 
impacted.
•
Commodity price volatility, to the extent not reflected in the WPI index, exerted pressure on margins. The Company has taken an additional 
provision of ~₹ 10 crores in the current quarter.
Order Book & Pipeline
•
FY27 order inflow target: ₹4,500 – ₹5,000 crores.
•
Orders booked so far in FY27: ₹1,071 crores.
•
Supported by a strong pipeline of quality bids, the Company remains confident of achieving its FY27 order inflow target.
Asset Monetization:
•
The Company realized an aggregate of ₹ 6.5 crores during Q1 FY27 from disposal of non-core assets (properties). 
•
Target realization from non-core asset sales in FY27: ₹50 crores.
The Company continues to strengthen execution capabilities, supported by normalized labour availability and proactive measures to mitigate 
external challenges. With a robust bid pipeline and disciplined asset monetization strategy, we remain confident of delivering on our FY27 growth 
and profitability objectives.
