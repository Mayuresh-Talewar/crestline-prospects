## Summary
Four of the five groups verify against primary documents. Three edits are needed: (1) p6 "no client failed and no site stopped / Nothing went wrong" has no source and the filings contradict it in part; (2) p3/p13 "promised" should read "expects at least" for the 25-30 days; (3) the March ratio is 0.11x on the Q1 deck but 0.10x on the Q4 call, so keep 0.11 and footnote it. Fix capacite-pack-data.md section 0: 152 days is in AR26 PDF p94.

## Method
Counts and figures were read from the PDFs by script (pymupdf text search for each claim, page printed with the hit); the debt slide was read as an image (S2 PDF p8). Disconfirming check done on each key figure: a secondary summary said "40-day" reduction (an analyst's phrase on the call), the CEO's own words give 43; a pack-data note said 152 is in no primary document, AR26 p94 contradicts it.
