# Vascon Engineers: EPC working-capital problems (VS-4)

Scope: EPC contracting only (96.6% of FY26 consolidated revenue, segment note AR26 PDF p124). Real estate is not modelled by Crestline and is out of every problem below. Figures are from `data.md` and `solutions.md`; page references are those files'. Printed footnotes say "Crestline arithmetic on the published figures". Per day = Rs 948.53 Cr / 365 = Rs 2.5987 Cr. depth.md not present: {depth:peers and public proofs pending VS-3}.

Sources by date: AR26 (FY26 annual report), AR25, AR24, AR23 as cited in data.md; Q4T26 call 13 May 2026; Q1T27 call 14 Aug 2026; Q1D27 deck (position at 30 Jun 2026).

## P1. Work is done faster than it becomes a bill (unbilled, the largest balance)
- Trend (standalone, net amount due from customers): Rs 300.64 Cr (Mar-24), 467.66 (Mar-25), 563.51 (Mar-26), +87.4% (AR24 PDF p87; AR25 PDF p111; AR26 PDF p59).
- In days of revenue: 143.7 (FY24, revenue 763.40) to 216.8 (FY26, revenue 948.53). Consolidated revenue fell in FY26 (1,077.41 to 948.53, AR26 PDF p87) while the balance rose.
- Gross unbilled Rs 690.91 Cr less related advances Rs 127.40 Cr (AR26 PDF p59).
- His words: projects "at the fag end of the completion" where "final bill, certification takes its own sweet time" (Somnath Biswas, CFO, Q1T27 p8).
- Strength: Strong (three annual reports and one call agree).
- Accounts cannot tell: which bills, which site, measured or prepared or certified, since when, whose desk.

## P2. Retention grows faster than the work it is held on
- Retention accrued, not due (standalone): 45.26 (Mar-22), 61.67, 76.76, 84.72, 107.18 (Mar-26), +136.8% (AR23 PDF p92; AR24 PDF p88; AR26 PDF p60).
- From FY23, retention +73.8% against standalone revenue +23.6% (767.47 to 948.53). Retention days 29.3 to 41.2. It is 45.0% of the net receivable.
- Release is the client's act (a certificate), so this is chase-and-evidence work.
- Accounts cannot tell: which retention has met its defect-liability milestone and is simply not released.

## P3. Billed debtors are inside terms; the trapped cash is not collection
- Stated credit period on EPC contracts: 60 days (AR26 PDF p60).
- Billed debtors without retention: 238.10 - 107.18 = Rs 130.92 Cr = 50.4 days, inside 60. Consolidated receivables including retention Rs 238.30 Cr = 91.7 days.
- So the days beyond terms sit in unbilled work and retention. Chasing collections alone would not move them.
- Disconfirming note: gross debtors over three years old are 7,555.80 lakh (45.2%, AR26 PDF p61), including 3,168.05 lakh credit-impaired. That is old, partly provided-for debt and a separate matter from the cycle. The note's ageing basis is ambiguous (due date or transaction date), so never call it "overdue".

## P4. Cash from operations turned negative; net debt is rising
- Cash generated from operations (consolidated): 114.17 (FY23), 16.21 (FY24 original), 18.25 (FY25), -103.18 (FY26). Working capital absorbed (computed): -9.92, -84.09, -79.42, -157.30 against operating profit before WC of 54.13 in FY26 (AR26 PDF p88; AR25 p162; AR24 p133; AR23 p137). FY24 is the original AR24 vintage, restated in AR25.
- Net debt on the company's own table: Rs 16.62 Cr (Mar-25), 91.41 (Mar-26), 151.74 (30 Jun 2026), +60.33 Cr in one quarter (Q1D27 p8). Total debt 201.90 to 338.76.
- Return on equity 4.36%, return on capital employed 6.11% (standalone, Note 34, AR26 PDF p79).
- CFO's own words: the net working capital cycle "has been increased from 45 days to almost 65 to 70 days" (Somnath Biswas, CFO, Q1T27 p6-7, 14 Aug 2026), tied to two government projects, Supaul and Sindhudurg (Dr Santosh Sundararajan, Group CEO, Q1T27 p3, p10). Vascon publishes no formula for this cycle (NOT FOUND in AR22-AR26 and the decks), so the figure stays his description.
- Q3 FY26 note: PAT fell on a base holding a Rs 74 Cr exceptional gain; PBT before exceptionals fell 42% and the company names client delay. Not a margin story; the 88% is never printed.

## P5. Payables run long and a share sits on supplier finance (visibility only)
- Trade payables over one year: 150.80 / 641.35 = 23.5% (standalone, ageing "from the date of transactions", AR26 PDF p66).
- Supplier finance outstanding Rs 104.33 Cr (Rs 29.40 Cr a year earlier), 16.3% of trade payables, terms "30-180days" (AR26 PDF p66). MSME interest accrued and unpaid Rs 2.44 Cr (Note 35).
- No saving is claimed. Paying later is never a saving. Value is visibility and MSME compliance.

## What the accounts cannot tell them
Which sites hold the 65 to 70 days; which bill sits at which stage and since when; which part is Vascon's desk (measure, prepare, submit, invoice) and which is the client's (certify, pay, release); which retention has met its milestone; which payables sit on supplier finance. SAP records the dates but the audit trail is not enabled (AR26 PDF p44), so event dates are read as recorded, and the pilot says so.

1. P1 unbilled: largest balance, partly Vascon's own desks. 2. P2 retention: smaller, the client's act. 3. P4 as the consequence frame for the Chairman. 4. P3 as framing (where not to look). 5. P5 visibility only.
