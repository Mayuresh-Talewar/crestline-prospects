> Per day: ₹948.53 Cr (FY26 revenue from operations, consolidated) / 365 = ₹2.60 Cr a day. EPC is 96.6% of that revenue.
> Cash released = days x ₹2.60 Cr. Interest = cash x 9.65% to 10.95% (cash-credit range, Annual Report 2025-26, consolidated, PDF p103), saved only if the cash repays borrowing.
