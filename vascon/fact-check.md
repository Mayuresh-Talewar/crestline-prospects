## C. ERP, primary evidence

Statutory auditor's report, AR26 PDF p44 (printed p84), under "report on other legal requirements" and repeated for the consolidated statements (PDF p84): "The audit trail feature was not enabled at the application level for the Enterprise Resource Planning Software (SAP) to log all relevant transactions recorded in the software." and "The audit trail feature was not enabled at the database level for the below mentioned accounting software to log any direct data changes, used for maintenance of all relevant accounting records by the Company: a) Enterprise Resource Planning Software (SAP) and b) Human Resource Management System (Ellisys by Ascent Software)." Grade: Verified (company filing, image read). It also records the control gap: the audit-trail edit log is not enabled at the SAP application and database level, which bears on how far transaction timestamps from this SAP can be trusted for a time-based product (a point for VS-2, not for copy). Version: NOT FOUND in filings. Vendor: SAVIC Technologies case studies (Secondary, undated), SAP S/4HANA with FI & CO, MM, SD, PS, "Delivered full ERP transformation within 4 months". Disconfirming search run: "Vascon Engineers SAP S/4HANA SAVIC case study go-live" (found the two pages above); searched the text of AR22-AR26 and the FY26/Q1 FY27 filings for "S/4", "HANA" and the whole word "Tally": no hits, so Tally is not evidenced. The same SAP and Ellisys audit-trail paragraph also appears in the FY25 auditor's reports (AR25, standalone and consolidated), so SAP is in use across at least two financial years.

## D. Other errors and traps found while checking (not in the dossier or gtm)

1. Two figures called "6.11%": the MD&A prints "return on net worth 6.11%" (AR26 PDF p40) but Note 34 prints 6.11% as Return on Capital Employed and 4.36% as Return on Equity (PDF p79). Use the note.
2. "Debtors' turnover ... 4.22 days" (MD&A) is the same number as Note 34's trade-receivables turnover ratio 4.22 (a ratio, not days). Do not print it as days.
3. Ageing tables say "from due date" in the header and "from the date of transactions" in the footnote (AR26 PDF p61, p66). Do not call buckets "overdue".
4. FY24 consolidated revenue exists in two vintages (AR24 1,052.86 Cr incl. GMP and JV share; AR25 restated 763.53 Cr). The FY24 results filing says 1,037.23 Cr (21 May 2024) while AR24 says 1,052.86: the AR adds JV share of profit 15.63 Cr into revenue. Quote with the vintage.
5. Q1D27 p16 draws the Mar-25 external order book as 2,602 Cr; FY25 deck says 2,377 Cr (the difference is the Rs 225 Cr Royal Rides order of April 2025).
6. The company's net debt (Rs 151.74 Cr) deducts fixed deposits of which Rs 149.76 Cr carry a lien (Q1D27 p8 footnote). The balance-sheet net debt computed from published lines is Rs 95.28 Cr at Mar-26 against the company's Rs 91.41 Cr; at Jun-26 no balance sheet exists.
7. FY27 call (Q1T27 p6, analyst, not management): "pending collection is around INR116 crores ... unsold inventory around INR414 crores": both do reconcile to the deck (396 sold less 280 collected; unsold 414), so they may be quoted as deck figures, not as the analyst's.
8. Management has already missed or withdrawn several stated targets (FY26 EPC Rs 1,200 Cr; FY26 order intake Rs 1,500 Cr vs Rs 760 Cr achieved; Rs 4,000 Cr order book). PLAYBOOK rule 8: never print a scorecard of misses; but never print an old target as current either.
