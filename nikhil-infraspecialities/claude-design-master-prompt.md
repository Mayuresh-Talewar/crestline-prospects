## 1. What you are building

A 16-page booklet titled "Working-capital review", printed and posted to the Board of Directors of Nikhil Infraspecialities Private Limited. You produce one edition, for the reader in the reader table (see "Reader editions" below).
It answers one question for the reader: what if Crestline were already running at Nikhil Infraspecialities, how much money and how much time would it save?
Every word and every figure is supplied in the Content section at the end of this prompt. This prompt carries content only.

## 2. Reader editions

One edition, for the reader in the reader table in the Content section.
- The edition carries that reader's name and title on the cover's "PREPARED EXCLUSIVELY FOR" lines, and that reader's letter text, address block and salutation on page 2.
- Name the edition after its reader as given in the table. That label is for the file only and is never printed.
- Body copy is written in a neutral voice. Quotes are credited by name and title, as the Content gives them.

## 3. Rules you must follow

1. Use ONLY the text and figures in the Content section. Do not write new copy, captions, taglines, teasers, pull quotes or headings. Where the Content has no text for something, add none.
2. Copy every figure exactly as written: same digits, same decimals, same currency sign, same unit. Do not round, re-total, convert or recompute anything.
3. Headlines exactly as given in the headline table, in the two lines shown. Never reword them.
4. Footnotes exactly as given, on the page they belong to. Where a footnote carries a bracketed marker such as [SRC], print in its place the full line given for it under "Caveat lines" in the Content. The marker itself is never printed.
5. Text inside quotation marks in the Content is printed copy. Words outside quotation marks (for example "Data:", "Rows:", word counts in brackets, "No scenario mark") are instructions to you and are never printed.
6. No internal references in printed text. Never print identifiers, file names, bracketed markers, status words used as markers, or any note addressed to the team. A source is printed only in the words the Content gives for it.
7. No printed negatives about the booklet itself: never print what is missing, unknown, unchecked or left out ("not available", "we could not find"). Where there is no text, print nothing. Print a "not public" line only where the Content gives it as quoted copy.
8. No em dashes and no en dashes anywhere. Use a comma, a full stop, "to" or a middle dot (·) as the Content does.
9. Ownership words stay as written: the client certifies and pays; Nikhil Infraspecialities measures, prepares, submits and escalates. Do not swap them.
10. There is no Nikhil Infraspecialities logo in this booklet. Print the company name as text wherever it appears. Never draw, trace or imitate a company mark. The Crestline logo is the uploaded file, used as supplied and never redrawn.

### Booklet accuracy rules (apply to every page, every edition)
A1. **Claims about Crestline must be true today.** Make no claim about Crestline beyond the pasted text. No certification "in progress", no hosting region, no encryption claim, no client names, no "live in X days", and no present-tense claim that Crestline connects to the company's SAP or ERP. The pilot is one entity, one segment, read-only, 14 days, as the Content words it.
A2. **Every quote names the right speaker**, exactly as the Content credits it. Never move a quote to another person.
A3. **Ours versus the client's follows the product rule.** In cash-in steps, acknowledged, certified and collected are the client's acts; measuring, preparing, reviewing, submitting and invoicing are the company's own. Keep every OURS and CLIENT tag as given.
A4. **No "nothing went wrong" headlines.** Use only the headlines given; never soften or invert one.
A5. **Every value shown for a data set is given in the Content with its label.** Show no value that is not given there, and no value without its label.
A6. **Interest only at a rate the company discloses.** Print only the rate and interest figures the Content gives; never compute or label a rate as theirs.
A7. **Savings are scenarios.** Anything marked "with Crestline", "scenario" or "days less" is shown as a scenario, labelled "Scenario, not a forecast.", and kept distinct from the measured figure beside it. Scenario rows overlap and are never added together; print no total the Content does not give. Data the Content marks as history only carries no scenario mark.
A8. **Peers and comparatives** appear only where the Content prints them, with the vintage it gives. Never build a scorecard of missed promises.
A9. **No other company's data.** Do not bring in any company, name, figure or text from anywhere other than the Content, including from your memory. No demo numbers beside the company's figures.
A10. **Full address and correct names and titles** on the letter page and cover, exactly as the reader table gives them, per edition.
A11. **"You said" only for the reader's own words.** Never print "you said", "in your own words" or any second-person credit over a quote by someone other than that edition's reader. Use the speaker's name and title or the company's name as the Content gives it. Never print a note or flag on the page.
A12. **Never invent figures to fill a data set.** If the Content gives a stage, step or item without a value (for example the page 8 bill stages), give it without a number. Never create an "illustrative split" of a total, and never add a sentence about one.
A13. **No signed numbers in print.** Write inflow / outflow, increase / decrease. No "+" or "-" in front of a figure.
A14. **Every problem page shows the outcome with Crestline where the Content gives one.** Next to the "today" figure, give the "with Crestline" result from the Content, labelled with the days and cash it releases, plus "scenario, not a forecast". Use only the values the Content gives; if the Content gives no scenario value, show none (rule A12).
A15. **Figures from the rating documents are printed with their source and date**, as the Content gives them. An agency's expectation (cash accrual, term debt obligations) is printed as an expectation, never as a result. Nothing is computed from them.

1. Count the pages: exactly 16, in the order of the Content.

3. Pick every figure on every page and find it in the Content, character for character. Any figure you cannot find there, delete it.

5. Every data set: each value has its label, and nothing comes from a figure that is not in the Content.
6. Every scenario carries "Scenario, not a forecast." Data marked "No scenario mark" or "history only" carries none. No scenario totals added.
7. Every footnote is on its page, with each bracketed marker replaced by its full caveat line.
8. No word was added: no taglines, no footer teasers, no extra captions, no "Live in" line, no certification or hosting claim, no client names, no internal references, no printed negatives.
9. The Crestline logo is the uploaded file. No other logo appears.
10. The QR code on page 15 (and on page 16) opens crestlineintelligence.com/book-a-demo.
