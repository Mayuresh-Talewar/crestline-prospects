# Nikhil Infraspecialities: story angle (depth)

## Respect first
- A family-run Pune manufacturer, incorporated 16 Dec 2016, run by Balasaheb Pasalkar and his three sons, each holding 25% (CARE, 14 Dec 2023).
- Three plants (Jambhulwadi, Ambegaon Budruk, Mundhwa) making paver blocks, precast products, AAC blocks and ready-mix concrete, plus private building contracts (CRISIL, 6 Jan 2025).
- Steady growth: operating income Rs 36.00 Cr (FY22, CARE) to Rs 50.26 Cr (FY24, CRISIL); PAT Rs 3.39 Cr in FY24 at a 6.74% margin (CRISIL). Rating upgraded to BB+/Stable and limits raised from Rs 28.25 Cr to Rs 40 Cr in January 2025 (CRISIL).
- Promoters' experience "more than two decades" is the rating's first strength (CRISIL).

## Where the money waits
This is a supplier, so the story is its receivables and stock, not a contractor's certification queue.
- Gross current assets were 476 days at 31 Mar 2024, "driven by large receivables and inventory" (CRISIL). The company "needs to extend a long credit period to its customers" and holds work-in-process and finished stock "to ensure timely execution of its civil projects".
- CRISIL's own upgrade trigger is GCA "below 300 days". That is the gap the rating agency names: 476 against 300.
- Limits are used at "80% on average for the 12 months through November 2024" on cash credit of Rs 30.00 Cr (CRISIL).
- Customer concentration: NCPL, a group company, is a large buyer (CRISIL 30-40% / 40-50%; CARE 35-45%). How long a related buyer takes to pay is not in the filings. Garvis only; keep any customer-level framing out of the booklet.
- What the accounts cannot tell them: which customer, which delivery or site, which bill or desk, and since when. The rating gives one day figure (GCA) and no split between receivables, work-in-process and finished stock.

## Cautions for X-2
- Only one day measure exists (GCA 476). No debtor, creditor or inventory days: never derive them. Calc inputs are limited to GCA and the Rs 30.00 Cr cash credit; interest rate NOT FOUND, so no interest saving can print.
- Data is FY24, about 18 months old at today's date (2026-10-09). Say so on every figure.
- No order book, no ERP, no named finance head, no official site, no logo. LIGHT booklet only; letter addressed to a promoter-director with title "promoter" unless a primary title is found.
- Do not describe this company as an EPC, roads or NHAI contractor.
