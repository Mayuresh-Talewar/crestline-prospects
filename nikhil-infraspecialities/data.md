# Nikhil Infraspecialities Private Limited: data (step 1, 2026-10-09)

## VERDICT: LIGHT
Reasons:
- Real, rated, unlisted company, but a SMALL MANUFACTURER (paver blocks, precast, AAC, RMC) with some building contracts. It is NOT a roads / NHAI EPC contractor. The dossier's business description, clients and pain points are wrong.
- Latest primary financials are FY2024 (year to 31 Mar 2024, CRISIL, 6 Jan 2025). Revenue Rs 50.26 Cr. No FY25 figure in any primary source.
- The dossier headline (FY25 revenue Rs 127.81 Cr, +150%) has NO primary source and cannot be used.
- Strong working-capital signal (gross current assets 476 days), but only one measure (GCA). No debtor, creditor or inventory days, no order book, no ERP, no official website, no logo.
- Much of the story is receivables from a related party (NCPL): Garvis only. See sensitive-garvis.md.
- Rating is sub-investment grade (CRISIL BB+/Stable, A4+). Respect-first framing needed.

## What it does
- "manufactures AAC blocks, paver blocks, precast products and RMC and undertakes buliding [sic] construction work." CRISIL 6 Jan 2025, "About the Company".
- Plants: Jambhulwadi, Ambegaon Budruk, Mundhwa (Maharashtra). CRISIL 6 Jan 2025; CARE 14 Dec 2023 p2 (CARE lists paver blocks, precast, RMC; no AAC).
- Revenue mix: "75-80% of revenue from sale of construction material, of which 30-40% of the sales is to NCPL and the balance 15-20% comes from civil works." CRISIL 6 Jan 2025, "Extensive experience of the promoters". Civil works are private building contracts (CARE: "construction of private buildings"; CRISIL: "entering into private building contracts"). CRISIL also says its track record "with customers, including government agencies" supports the business; no government order or client is named anywhere.
- Classification (CARE p2): Construction Materials / Other Construction Materials.
