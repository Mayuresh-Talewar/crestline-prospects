# Montecarlo Limited: sensitive-garvis.md (GARVIS ONLY, never in a booklet or prompt)

1. Promoter guarantees: all bank working-capital borrowings carry personal guarantees of the promoters and of promoter-group entities (Montecarlo Asset Holdings LLP, Kanubhai M. Patel Trust), plus collateral on promoters' immovable property (AR25 note 23.1(iii)-(iv)). 99.95% held by one family trust.
2. Sponsor undertakings: Montecarlo guarantees and funds its HAM and smart-meter SPVs (cost overruns, annuity shortfall, debt service reserve, termination debt) (IR25). Bank guarantees outstanding Rs 342.17 Cr (note 42). Equity still to infuse about Rs 470 Cr over FY26-27 (IR25).
3. Related parties: large investments in subsidiaries (Rs 929.65 Cr non-current investments; Rs 196.68 Cr net new investment in subsidiaries' equity in FY25); perpetual loans converted to optionally convertible debentures (AR25 notes 5(c), 36). Receivable from subsidiary MVMPL: Rs 108.50 Cr EFT requested on 31 Mar 2025 at close of business, unsettled, so the asset was NOT derecognised (note 11(e)); India Ratings separately says about Rs 108 Cr was collected from one SPV on 2 Apr 2025 (IR25; same SPV not stated, so the link is an inference). This may flatter year-end receivables and borrowings; handle with care.
4. MSME: Rs 94.38 Cr of Rs 124.69 Cr MSME payables are past due; the company recognises NIL MSMED interest (note 25(d), "subject to exclusion of any kind of dispute"). A legal exposure under Section 16 of the MSMED Act and a tax disallowance issue (s.43B(h)). Do not publish in a booklet that pitches vendor timing.
5. Tax and legal: contingent liabilities FY25: VAT/CST 19.74 Cr, entry tax 3.72 Cr, DGFT duty drawback 2.60 Cr, GST 52.26 Cr (FY24 27.79 Cr, up). Income-tax Rs 59.07 Cr contingency closed after favourable ITAT order. Arbitration awards realised (Rs 14.70 Cr FY25; Rs 26.99 Cr and NHAI conciliation Rs 40.51 Cr in FY24) are inside "other operating revenue" (Rs 38.97 Cr FY25 vs 90.50 Cr FY24): FY24 revenue and profit carried one-off claim income.
6. Provisions and write-offs: onerous-contract provision Rs 26.32 Cr in FY24 (note 28; Rs 53.67 Cr balance FY25); doubtful debts/advances written off Rs 34.20 Cr FY24 and Rs 3.11 Cr FY25; ECL provision up from Rs 18.95 Cr to Rs 27.09 Cr, plus Rs 8.26 Cr new ECL on retention (note 13). Receivables over 3 years Rs 40.26 Cr, of which Rs 14.12 Cr flagged "significant increase in credit risk".
7. Customer concentration: PUVVNL (state discom) 10.0% of revenue. State entities 74% of order book (IR25 flags "state-level execution and payment risks"). Top 10 projects 74% of order book. Maharashtra 55%.
8. Execution delays: all six HAM projects behind schedule; time extensions obtained (IR25). The Nagpur smart-meter project faced consumer resistance (IR25).
9. Auditors: unmodified opinion FY25 (Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP and H. K. Shah & Co.). No emphasis of matter or qualification found in the standalone report. Joint-operation financials audited by other auditors (Other Matters).
10. Rating: IND AA-/Positive, solicited, cooperating. Voluntary issuer rating discontinued 26 Sep 2023 for regulatory reasons (not a default). Net leverage rose to 0.46x, interest cover fell to 6.5x (IR25). No default, no low or non-cooperating rating found.
11. Dossier errors to keep out of any outreach: wrong revenue basis, wrong CFO date, stale limits, "30-60 days" claim, inferred emails, "DRHP 2018" claim not found in own filings.
12. Namesake risk: Monte Carlo Fashions Ltd (textile) and the Montecarlo SPVs; never cite their ratings as the parent's.
