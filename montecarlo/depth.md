# Montecarlo Limited: depth.md (story angle)

Respect first. Thirty years from a 1995 Ahmedabad contractor to one of Gujarat's largest private EPC firms: Rs 4,518.80 Cr standalone revenue in FY25, an Rs 13,040 Cr order book (2.9x revenue), IND AA-/Positive from India Ratings (14 Jul 2025), about 62 sites across 14 states, and highways, energy, mining, metro and water under one roof. EBITDA margin has held near 10% for three years (company: 10.07% FY25). An unlisted company that publishes full audited standalone and consolidated accounts on its own website is rarer than it should be.

The question (from its own accounts, standalone, 365 days, closing balances):
- Bills wait longer at the client's end. DSO went from 62.4 days (FY24) to 93.4 days (FY25) while revenue fell 7.7%. Receivables rose Rs 319 Cr to Rs 1,156.49 Cr; 15.3% of the gross is older than six months (AR25 p.60).
- Work done but not yet billed: unbilled revenue Rs 639.99 Cr = 51.7 days of revenue (FY24 38.0). Retention Rs 442.67 Cr = 35.8 days (FY24 25.7, FY23 13.6). Together with receivables that is Rs 2,239 Cr of the company's own money sitting in the client-side chain.
- The cash flow shows it: cash generated from operations Rs 528.44 Cr (FY24) to Rs 150.51 Cr (FY25); net operating cash flow Rs 431.36 Cr to Rs 58.48 Cr. India Ratings names the cause: higher working-capital requirement, and notes the net working capital cycle extended to 35 days from 13.
- Vendor side carries the other half: payables Rs 958.44 Cr (95.8 days on construction expenses); Rs 469.52 Cr is past its due date, Rs 94.38 Cr of it owed to MSME suppliers. 46.7% of construction cost is subcontract work charges. The company borrowed to bridge: current borrowings Rs 344.87 Cr at year end (FY24 Rs 245.87 Cr), then Rs 50 Cr by 31 May 2025 once one SPV paid Rs 108 Cr. One payment moved the position; that is how lumpy the chain is.
- What the accounts cannot say: which client, which site, which bill, which desk holds a bill, and since when. Ageing runs from the transaction date and there is no split by client. Rs 639.99 Cr unbilled is one number across 62 sites.

Angle for the booklet: "Rs 12.38 Cr of revenue arrives every day; each day a bill waits is that much money." Crestline does not hold the money; it names the bill, the desk and the day. Where the client is a state discom or a state road corporation (74% of the order book is state entities, up from 29%), the wait is mostly theirs to release: the booklet must separate "ours to fix" (measurement, billing, submission) from "client's act" (certification, payment).

Do not say: SAP "confirmed across all sites" (own site links only), 30-60 day debtor realisation (not in any filing), any rate of interest (range only), any retention release term (not published).
