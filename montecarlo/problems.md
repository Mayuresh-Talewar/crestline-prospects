# Montecarlo Limited: problems.md (MC-S23)

Respect first. Montecarlo's 30th Annual Report (2024-25, accounts signed 27 Jun 2025) shows standalone revenue from operations of Rs 4,518.80 Cr, EBITDA as stated by the company Rs 454.99 Cr (10.07% of revenue, Crestline's arithmetic) and profit after tax Rs 231.30 Cr. One client accounts for Rs 450.53 Cr, 9.97% of revenue (segment note, AR25 p.81; the note words it "in excess of 10%"). Order book Rs 13,040 Cr at March 2025 (credit agency press release, 14 Jul 2025, as recorded in data.md MC-1; not re-opened this session).

## Problem 1: the wait to be paid grew by 31 days while revenue fell
- Receivable days 84.3 (FY23), 62.4 (FY24), 93.4 (FY25); receivables Rs 833.97, 837.20, 1,156.49 Cr (AR25 p.40, AR24 p.40; days = balance / (revenue / 365)). Revenue fell 7.72% in FY25.
- The growth sits in bills under six months old: Rs 655.17 Cr (FY24) to Rs 1,002.97 Cr (FY25). Older than six months: Rs 180.61 Cr of Rs 1,183.58 Cr gross, 15.3% (FY24 Rs 200.98 Cr of Rs 856.15 Cr, 23.5%). Ageing is from the date of the transaction, not the due date (AR25 p.60).
- The note states a credit period of 30 to 90 days for goods and milestone based payment for services (note 11(b)). The accounts cannot say which client, site or bill holds the days.
- Ours to fix: measuring, preparing, submitting, invoicing. The client's act: acknowledging, certifying, paying.
- Scenario: ten days off the wait is Rs 123.80 Cr released once; thirty days Rs 371.41 Cr (63.4 days left, above the 30 days that note 11(b) gives as the lower end for goods).

## Problem 2: work done and not yet billed, and retention held
- Unbilled revenue Rs 472.24, 509.59, 639.99 Cr = 47.7, 38.0, 51.7 days of revenue (note 15). Retention Rs 134.12, 344.65, 442.67 Cr = 13.6, 25.7, 35.8 days (note 39(c)). Release basis for retention is not published, so it stays a balance.
- Receivables plus unbilled: Rs 1,796.48 Cr, 145.1 days (Crestline's arithmetic); retention is a further Rs 442.67 Cr.
- Scenario on unbilled work: ten days Rs 123.80 Cr, thirty days Rs 371.41 Cr (no certification window is published).

## Problem 3: cash from operations fell from Rs 528.44 Cr to Rs 150.51 Cr
- Cash generated from operations before tax: Rs 278.98 (FY23), 528.44 (FY24), 150.51 Cr (FY25). FY25 working-capital changes: receivables Rs 328.43 Cr absorbed, other assets Rs 301.48 Cr absorbed, liabilities Rs 278.21 Cr provided, inventories Rs 30.09 Cr released (AR25 p.42).
- Project materials Rs 191.91 Cr = 44.4 days of materials consumed. Scenario: ten days Rs 43.22 Cr, thirty days Rs 129.65 Cr.
- Combined run, all three moved together: ten days Rs 290.82 Cr, thirty days Rs 872.46 Cr. One scenario, not the sum of rows.

Question the booklet asks: which bills and which retained rupees hold the 31 extra days.
