# Montecarlo Limited: data.md (MC-1, researched 2026-10-12)

## VERDICT: FULL
Reasons: unlisted, but it publishes full audited accounts (standalone and consolidated, auditors Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP and H. K. Shah & Co.) on its own site. Ageing of receivables and payables, unbilled revenue, retention, advances, MSME dues and a 3-year cash flow are all disclosed. One basis is available: STANDALONE, which is also the basis India Ratings uses. Missing: a single borrowing rate (range only), a single client payment term, management targets, retention release basis.

Basis used everywhere below: STANDALONE. The filing is in Rs lakh; shown here in Rs Cr (divide by 100). FY = year to 31 March. Days = closing balance / (annual flow / 365).
