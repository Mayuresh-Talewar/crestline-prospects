EPC | Design & Build

We have been engaged in Engineering, Procurement and Construction services & Design – Build for various sectors. Our clients avail all services under one roof which include Civil, Infrastructure, Structural / PEB, Electrical, Mechanical, HVAC, Firefighting, Transmission & Distribution, Plumbing, Landscaping etc. related to medium and large-scale projects.

We offer the complete spectrum of engineering, procurement and construction services for all sectors from concept to detailed design, fully-integrated self-perform construction and start-up, right through to plant support services during operations.

Together with our integrated EPC self-perform model, strategic partnerships, and proven experience in project delivery means we can offer optimum cost, shorter delivery schedules, and greater certainty of outcomes for our customers.

We have helped our customers achieve substantial amount of cost savings — including significant reductions in bulk material quantities, as well as equipment costs and construction direct and indirect costs — through value engineering and optimized designs.

ARWADE’S delivery model has proven to consistently result in the shortest schedules in the industry. By controlling the critical path, integrating teams, reducing interfaces, and managing project risks, we have reduced the time it takes to successfully deliver projects to our operating partners.

With a harmonious blend of engineering prowess, strategic procurement, and impeccable construction practices, we don’t just build structures; we craft legacies. Your vision, our expertise – the perfect synergy for a future where every structure stands tall in its brilliance. Elevate your expectations; experience EPC excellence with us.

With a fusion of cutting-edge design expertise and unparalleled construction prowess, we transform concepts into colossal realities. Our team thrives on challenges, specializing in crafting industrial spaces that not only meet but exceed your expectations. From groundbreaking designs that optimize functionality to seamless construction execution, we are the catalysts of your industrial vision.

At ARWADE we don’t just build structures; we engineer success stories.
