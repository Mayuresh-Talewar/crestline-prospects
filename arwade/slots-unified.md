## Keys

Convention. Lines starting with ">" are PRINTED copy, word for word. Every other line is an instruction to the designer and is never printed. A tag in square brackets inside a printed line is replaced by the caveat line it stands for, in full.

Currency is ₹, crore is "Cr". Every gap prints as NOT PUBLISHED, in capitals, everywhere.

Caveat tags:
- [SCN] > Scenario, not a forecast. Crestline arithmetic on the published figures: days x FY25 revenue per day (₹261.12 Cr / 365 = ₹0.7154 Cr; Infomerics rating rationale, 28 Jul 2025, p.5). Cash is released once. Scenarios overlap and are never added. Revenue is before GST and receivables carry GST, so the cash figures are conservative. No interest is shown: no borrowing rate is published.
- [ILL] > Illustrative. Per ₹100 Cr of new orders executed over 12 months, the period in which the 25 Jun 2025 order book is "expected to be executed" (Infomerics rating rationale, 28 Jul 2025, p.3). Crestline arithmetic on the published figures: ₹100 Cr / 365 = ₹0.2740 Cr a day. Scenario, not a forecast.
- [HIST] > History only. Figures as published; no scenario drawn.
- [FOOT] > Based only on Arwade's public credit-rating reports (the latest dated 28 Jul 2025) and its website, read on 8 Oct 2026.
- [SOURCES]: the sources paragraph given under p15, printed in full.
- [BASIS] > Arwade Infrastructure Limited, standalone, audited financial years ending 31 March, ₹ crore, as classified by Infomerics Valuation and Rating Limited. Revenue is total operating income as the agency reports it.
