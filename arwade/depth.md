# Depth: agencies' and management's own words (Arwade Infrastructure Limited)

Quotes copied from the text layer of each PDF; the pages holding tables were image-checked, narrative quotes were not re-imaged (graded: text layer, single column prose, low scramble risk). Speaker = the rating agency's analyst team, not management.

## Working capital and collections
- Infomerics 17 May 2023 p.3 (weakness): "Elongated receivable cycle leading to high working capital intensity. The firm's working capital intensity remained at 0.27 times in FY23 though improved from 0.31 times in FY22 with the decrease in average collection period to 72 days in FY23 from 151 days in FY22 and decrease in creditor days to 66 days in FY23 from 83 days in FY22. Further gross current asset remained high at ~185 days in FY23 though improved from 271 days in FY22 due to reduction in collection period."
- Same PR, sensitivity p.1-2: upward "Improvement in liquidity position marked by improvement in the operating cycle"; downward "Stretch in working capital cycle leading to deterioration in liquidity position."
- Infomerics 3 Jul 2024 p.2: the weakness is gone from the list; downward trigger still reads "Elongation in the operating cycle adversely impacting the liquidity position". No days printed.
- Infomerics 28 Jul 2025 p.2: downward factors "Elongation in the operating cycle impacting liquidity." and "Any liability arising out of corporate guarantee extended to group company." No days printed. Liquidity "Adequate"; "The current ratio stood comfortable at 1.52x"; "average fund-based utilisation for the past twelve months ended May 2025 remained moderate at ~39.46%".
- Reading: the agency measured the cycle once (FY22-23) and has since reduced it to a watch-item. FY24-25 days are unknown, so a "WC problem" claim cannot be proven from rating documents for those years. Trend FY22 to FY23 (collection 151 to 72 days) is the only dated evidence.

## Clients and concentration
- 2023 PR p.3: "In FY21 and FY22 top 5 clients constituted nearly 60% of the turnover. Moreover, as on April 21, 2023 the unexecuted order book position of ~Rs.255 crore, comprises of 53% share from single order of Talegaon Industrial Parks Private Limited. Further Shree Dhootpapeshwar Limited comprises of ~11% of the unexecuted work orders."
- 2025 PR p.3: "moderate client concentration risk, with the majority of revenues and order book contributed by few clients." No names or shares.
- 2023 PR p.2: "The company has been awarded a single work order to the tune of Rs.135.29 crore to be executed with 9 months period." Whether that order was completed, cancelled or closed: NOT FOUND (no later document names it; the 2024 and 2025 PRs show a Rs 330 Cr / Rs 338 Cr book without client breakdown). Do not call it live.

## Triggers (2025 PR p.2)
Upward: scale growth with profitability and sustained cash accruals; interest coverage sustained. Downward: scale or profitability moderation; gearing and coverage deterioration; operating-cycle elongation; guarantee liability to group company.

## Growth rationale, agency's words (2025 PR p.2)
"growth of ~61.61% to Rs. 261.12cr in FY25 from 162.03cr in FY24 ... on account of increase in new contract and tenders. The increase in topline is also due to completion of unexecuted work in hand." Order book: "equivalent to 1.29 x of the turnover of FY25 ... timely execution of orders will remain a key rating monitorable."
