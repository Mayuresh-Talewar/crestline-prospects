# Vikran Engineering: depth.md (story angle, VK-1)

Respect first. Vikran Engineering Limited grew revenue from Rs 524.31 Cr (FY23) to Rs 1,249.31 Cr (FY26) and PAT from Rs 42.84 Cr to Rs 91.70 Cr, listed on NSE and BSE in September 2025, and carries an order book of Rs 5,206.0 Cr at 31 March 2026 (Rs 2,044.3 Cr a year earlier), over half of it solar (investor deck, May 2026). Its audit trail is clean in the sense that matters here: one auditor, one entity, four years of statements that agree with each other where they overlap.

Then the question. In the same four years the money tied up between work done and cash received grew faster than the work. On the published accounts (standalone, year-end balance over annual flow, Crestline's arithmetic):
- Trade receivables went from 369.91 to 1,042.33 Cr; DSO 257.5, 215.4, 252.8, 304.5 days.
- Work done and not yet billed (contract assets) went from 194.64 to 863.94 Cr; 135.5, 134.3, 185.9, 252.4 days.
- Operating working capital went from 307.0 to 1,197.8 Cr, and cash from operations from +5.55 Cr to -436.99 Cr.
- The company's own contracts say 45 to 60 days, with retention after completion; Rs 204.88 Cr of retention sits inside receivables.

Where the bills wait is exactly what the accounts cannot say: which of the 190+ project and store locations, which RA bill, which certification desk, since when, and whose queue (client or ours). Of the Rs 1,047 Cr gross receivable, Rs 756 Cr is not yet due and Rs 291 Cr is overdue; of the overdue amount Rs 176.81 Cr is under six months. The Q4 call says year-end procurement and execution lifted both creditors and debtors, so part of this is timing; a year-end balance cannot show how much. The five largest customers are 83.4% of FY26 revenue, so a few relationships carry most of the wait.

The angle for the booklet: "A profitable, fast-growing company whose cash is waiting in two queues, certification and collection. Crestline shows which bill is in which queue, for how long, and who can release it." Savings only as 10-day and 30-day scenarios from the calc (about Rs 34.2 Cr and Rs 102.7 Cr per 10 and 30 days on one leg, by the revenue-per-day arithmetic; calc script to confirm). Never "pay vendors later". No interest figure: no single working-capital rate is disclosed.

Vendor side (for the second half of the story): trade payables 788.42 Cr, of which 312.64 Cr is unbilled; MSE principal dues 51.51 Cr with 3.38 Cr interest accrued unpaid; vendor retention 89.95 Cr; supplier bills discounted 14.24 Cr. Vendor terms in days: not published.
