# Vikran Engineering: problems (VK-S23)

Standalone, INR Cr, 31 March 2026 unless stated. Sources: Annual Report 2025-26 (audited, approved 22 May 2026, revised copy filed 31 Aug 2026) and the Prospectus restated financial information (examination report 18 Jul 2025). Basis for days: Crestline's arithmetic on the published accounts.

Respect first: revenue 524.31 (FY23) to 1,249.31 (FY26), profit after tax 42.84 to 91.70, listed 3 Sep 2025, consolidated order book 5,206 against 2,044 a year earlier.

## 1. Billing and certification: work done waits to become a bill (Vikran's steps, then the client's certificate)
- Contract assets 194.64 (FY23), 289.16, 466.37, 863.94 (FY26); 135.5, 134.3, 185.9, 252.4 days of revenue.
- Mechanism the Annual Report states: higher revenue recognised than progress billed in certain projects (Note 50 (e), page 225). The accounts do not say which projects, which bills, or how long each has waited.
- Crestline would measure each RA bill's own dates from measurement to certification and name whose move ends each wait.

## 2. Collection: bills raised wait to be paid (the client's act)
- Trade receivables 369.91, 463.90, 634.33, 1,042.33; 257.5, 215.4, 252.8, 304.5 days, against contracts that state payment in 45 to 60 days, retention after completion (Note 50 (g), page 225).
- Retention held by clients 204.88, inside receivables (19.7%), due after completion (Note 14 (b), page 185). No collection-to-term scenario is priced.
- Crestline would age each unpaid invoice against its own due date and rank by money x days past term.

## 3. Cash: operations have consumed cash for three years
- Cash from operations: inflow 5.55 (FY23); outflow 66.48, 129.09, 436.99 (FY26). Cash and bank balances 14.93, 49.95, 67.06, 219.72.
- A year-end balance cannot show how much of the build is timing and how much is queue. That needs the dates behind each bill.
