# Claude Design master prompt: Bucon Engineers working-capital review (one booklet, one edition)

Paste everything below this line into Claude Design. Upload the Crestline logo file before you send it. There is no Bucon Engineers logo: its name is set as text.

---

## 1. What you are building

A 16-page A4 portrait booklet titled "Working-capital review", printed and posted to the named reader at Bucon Engineers and Infrastructure Private Limited. You build ONE design and produce the one edition listed in the reader table.
It answers one question for the reader: what if Crestline were already running at Bucon Engineers, how much money and how much time would it save?
Every word and every figure is supplied in the Content section at the end of this prompt.

## 2. Reader edition

Build the one edition in the reader table. An edition changes ONLY three things: the cover's "PREPARED EXCLUSIVELY FOR" lines (the reader's name and title), the page 2 letter (the letter text and address block), and the page 2 salutation.
- Name the edition after its reader as given in the table. That label is for the file only and is never printed.
- Body copy is written in a neutral voice. Quotes are credited by name and title, as the Content gives them.

## 3. Rules you must follow

1. Use ONLY the text and figures in the Content section. Do not write new copy, captions, taglines, teasers, pull quotes or headings. If a field has no text, leave it empty.
2. Copy every figure exactly as written: same digits, same decimals, same currency sign, same unit. Do not round, re-total, convert or recompute anything.
3. Headlines exactly as given for each page, split into two lines as shown. Never reword them.
4. Footnotes exactly as given, on the page they belong to.
5. Text inside quotation marks in the Content is printed copy. Words outside quotation marks (for example "Data:", "Items:", "Running head", "Footer", word counts in brackets, "No scenario mark") are instructions to you and are never printed.
6. No internal references in printed text. Reference codes, ticket numbers, file names, tags in brackets, checker notes, status words used as markers, and any note addressed to the team are never printed. A source is printed only in the words the Content gives for it.
7. No printed negatives about the booklet itself: never print what is missing, unknown, unchecked or left out ("not available", "we could not find", "TBD", "placeholder"). Where a field is empty, leave the space empty. Print a "not published" line only where the Content gives it as quoted copy.
8. No em dashes and no en dashes anywhere. Use a comma, a full stop, "to" or a middle dot (·) as the Content does.
9. Ownership words stay as written: the client certifies and pays; Bucon Engineers measures, prepares, submits and escalates. Do not swap them.
10. There is no Bucon Engineers logo. The cover shows the reader's name and the company name as text only. Never draw, trace or imitate a company mark.

### Booklet accuracy rules (apply to every page)
A1. **Claims about Crestline must be true today.** Make no claim about Crestline beyond the pasted text. No certification "in progress", no hosting region, no encryption claim, no client names, no "live in X days", and no present-tense claim that Crestline connects to the company's ERP. The pilot is one entity, one segment, read-only, 14 days, as the Content words it.
A2. **Every quote names the right speaker**, exactly as the Content credits it. Never move a quote to another person.
A3. **Ours versus the client's follows the product rule.** In cash-in steps, acknowledged, certified and collected are the client's acts; measuring, preparing, reviewing, submitting and invoicing are the company's own. Keep every company and CLIENT tag as given.
A4. **No "nothing went wrong" headlines.** Use only the headlines given; never soften or invert one.
A6. **Interest only at a rate the company discloses.** None is disclosed. Print no interest figure and no rate. Never compute or label a rate as theirs.
A7. **Savings are scenarios.** Anything marked "with Crestline", "scenario" or "days less" is shown as a scenario, labelled "Scenario, not a forecast." Scenario rows overlap and are never added together; print no total the Content does not give. Data the Content marks as history only gets no scenario mark.
A8. **Comparatives** appear only where the Content prints them, with the vintage it gives. Never build a scorecard.
A9. **No other company's data.** Do not bring in any company, name, figure or design text from anywhere other than the Content, including from earlier booklets or your memory. No demo numbers beside the company's figures.
A10. **Full address and correct names and titles** on the letter page and cover, exactly as the reader table gives them.
A11. **Second person only for the reader's own words.** Never print "you said" or "in your own words" over a quote by someone other than the reader. Use the speaker's name and title or the company's name as the Content gives it. Never print a note or flag on the page.
A12. **Never invent figures to fill a chart.** If the Content gives a stage, step or item without a value (for example the page 8 bill timeline), give it without a number. Never create a split of a total, and never add a sentence about one.
A13. **No signed numbers in print.** Write inflow / outflow, increase / decrease. No "+" or "-" in front of a figure.
A14. **Every problem chart shows the outcome with Crestline.** Beside the "today" figure, give the "with Crestline" result from the Content (labelled with the days and cash it releases, plus "Scenario, not a forecast."). Use only the values the Content gives; if the Content gives no scenario value for a chart, show none (rule A12).
B1. **Day counts are never added.** The day counts on pages 4, 5 and 7 are shown side by side, at one date, and are never summed, subtracted or turned into a rupee figure.
B2. **Page 13 is text only.** No table, no figure.

## 4. Before you finish, check every page

1. Count the pages: exactly 16, A4 portrait, in the order of the Content.
2. Read every headline against the Content: same words, same punctuation.
3. Pick every figure on every page and find it in the Content, character for character. Any figure you cannot find there, delete it.
4. Search the whole booklet for en dash and em dash characters (Unicode 2013 and 2014). There must be none.
5. Every chart: every item labelled, nothing taken from a figure that is not in the Content.
6. Every scenario carries "Scenario, not a forecast." Data marked "No scenario mark" or "History only" carries none. No scenario totals added.
7. Every footnote is on its page.
8. No word was added: no taglines, no right-hand footer teasers, no extra captions, no "Live in" line, no certification or hosting claim, no client names, no internal references, no printed negatives.
9. No company logo is drawn. The name is text.
10. The QR code on page 15 (and on page 16) opens crestlineintelligence.com/book-a-demo.
11. Instruction words from the Content (word counts in brackets, "Data:", notes about decisions and checks) are not printed anywhere.
12. The edition differs from nothing else: the cover reader lines, the page 2 letter and the salutation are the reader table's.
