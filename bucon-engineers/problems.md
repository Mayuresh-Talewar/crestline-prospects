# Bucon Engineers: problems.md (deepened 2026-10-10)

Respect first. Operating income has held between ₹170 and ₹180 Cr for four years: FY22 ₹177.27 Cr, FY23 ₹177.40 Cr (CRISIL, 5 Jun 2024), FY24 ₹170.46 Cr, FY25 ₹171.24 Cr provisional (CRISIL, 2 Sep 2025). FY25 reported profit after tax ₹7.88 Cr provisional. Order book above ₹400 Cr as of July 2025, to be executed over the next 2 fiscals (CRISIL, 2 Sep 2025). Clients CRISIL names: BMC, Adani Enterprises Ltd, MMRDA, CIDCO.

All figures: CRISIL Ratings rationales, standalone, FY to 31 March, FY25 provisional. No annual report, call or company site exists. Only CRISIL's own words are used as cause; where CRISIL states none, the problem says what would be measured.

## Problem 1. Retention and deposits: the largest unsplit block (stage: retention; release is the client's act, recording the date is ours)
Figures: gross current assets 290 days at 31 Mar 2025; receivables 43 days; inventory 56 days (CRISIL, 2 Sep 2025; never added). CRISIL attributes the rest to "other current asset which includes retention money and earnest money deposits (realized after defect liability period of 2-3 years on average)". No day or rupee figure is given for that block.
Mechanism (stated by CRISIL): the money is released only after the defect liability period ends, 2 to 3 years on average.
Crestline would measure: per contract, the retention and deposit held, the date the defect liability period ends, and whose act releases it. Shown as a dated list, nearest release first.
Framing: the question is only whether each release has a date and a name against it.
Scenario (calc/bu-savings-output.txt): one day = ₹171.24 Cr ÷ 365. 10 days = ₹4.69 Cr released once; 30 days = ₹14.07 Cr. 290 priced as one block. No interest (no rate published). Scenario, not a forecast.

## Problem 2. One rupee, two places in the accounts (stage: billing and retention; ours to record)
Figures: the 5 Jun 2024 rationale reports "receivables of 124-140 days as there is high retention money" and gross current assets of 169 days "for past two fiscals". The 2 Sep 2025 rationale reports receivables of 43 days and puts retention in other current assets (290 days). The two ratings place retention differently. NEVER paired; 169 and 124 to 140 are not printed.
Mechanism: not stated by CRISIL. It may be a change in how the line is classified or a change in the business; the rationales do not say.
Crestline would measure: retention held contract by contract from the ERP's own documents, so one classification is used throughout.
Framing: a question of one definition, not of performance.

## Problem 3. Milestone billing builds work in progress (stage: billing and certification; measure, prepare, submit are ours; acknowledge and certify are the client's)
Figures: inventory 56 days at 31 Mar 2025; CRISIL: "the company bills on milestone basis, which leads to build-up of work-in-progress" (2 Sep 2025; 55 days in the 5 Jun 2024 rationale, a different date, not paired).
Mechanism (stated): work done between milestones is not billed until the milestone is reached.
Crestline would measure: work executed and measured but not yet billed, and bills submitted but not yet certified, per contract, with the days each has waited and the desk it sits at.
Framing: where the work is done and the bill is not yet out.

## Problem 4. No payment clock for any one client (stage: collection; the client's act)
Figures: clients named by CRISIL are BMC, Adani Enterprises Ltd, MMRDA and CIDCO; the majority of revenue is from the Mumbai and MMRDA regions (CRISIL, 2 Sep 2025). Receivables 43 days overall (31 Mar 2025). Share of any client, credit period, ageing and certification delays: not published (data.md).
Mechanism: not stated. CRISIL gives one company-wide figure.
Crestline would measure: days from bill submitted to acknowledged, certified and paid, client by client and contract by contract, so that each client's clock is seen on its own.
Framing: one company-wide figure is published; the question is what each client's own clock looks like.

## Problem 5. A book of more than ₹400 Cr adds retention as it is executed (stage: retention)
Figures: more than ₹400 Cr as of July 2025 over the next 2 fiscals (CRISIL, 2 Sep 2025); also more than ₹400 Cr as of March 2024 over the next 2 to 3 fiscals (CRISIL, 5 Jun 2024). Both are floors; no growth is stated. Segments named (roads, bridges, steel pipelines, warehouses, buildings), no split published.
Mechanism: each bill on the book may carry retention, which CRISIL says is released after a defect liability period of 2 to 3 years on average. CRISIL does not size the retention to come.
Crestline would measure: a calendar of expected retention releases by month and contract, from the ERP's bills and defect liability dates.
Framing: visibility of the future queue, not performance.

## Problem 6. The vendor side is not visible in public accounts (stage: vendor)
Figures: payables, creditor days, MSME dues, vendor advances, vendor terms, supplier concentration and subcontract share are all not published (data.md). CRISIL says only that the promoters have healthy relationships with suppliers.
Mechanism: none stated. No cause is claimed.
Crestline would measure: vendor bills due and paid against their own due dates, by site, so that the timing of what is paid out can be read beside what is waiting to come in. Paying vendors later is never counted as a saving.
Framing: the other half of the cash cycle, to be read from the ERP.

## Problem 7. Cash held while the days wait (stage: cash)
Figures: cash and bank around ₹4.65 Cr at 31 Mar 2025 and around ₹17 Cr at 31 Mar 2024; current ratio 2.18 times at 31 Mar 2025 (CRISIL, 2 Sep 2025 and 5 Jun 2024). Operating cash flow and the company's own working-capital cycle: not published.
Mechanism: not stated by CRISIL for the change in the balance. No cause is claimed.
Crestline would measure: the date each release is expected beside the date each large vendor and site payment is due, so the days the cash balance depends on are visible.
Framing: two year-end balances side by side, with no judgement attached.

## Not used (binding)
Ratings, limits, utilisation, the word CRISIL uses for the cycle's outlook, margin movement, the managers' names, dossier claims, the Delhi namesake.
