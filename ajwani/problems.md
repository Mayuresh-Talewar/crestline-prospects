# Ajwani Infrastructure: problems (step 2, 2026-10-09)

Respect first. A roads builder since 1975 and a company since 1986, municipal and state clients, quarries of its own, and CRISIL's own words "consistent performance and timely execution of projects" (CRISIL rating rationale, 19 Jun 2024). The problems below are not faults. They are what growth does to a bill's journey.

Basis note: CRISIL's "operating income" (FY23) and the annual return's "turnover" as the Companies Act defines it (FY25) are different measures. They are shown side by side with their own labels and never joined in a growth rate.

## Problem 1: the scale moved fast, and the bills in flight moved with it
- FY23 operating income Rs 383.85 Cr (CRISIL, 19 Jun 2024, Key Financial Indicators, standalone not stated).
- FY24 scale "over Rs 500 crore" (CRISIL, 19 Jun 2024; exact figure not published).
- FY25 turnover Rs 755.55 Cr, net worth Rs 240.70 Cr (company's annual return, form MGT-7, year to 31 Mar 2025, p.9, filed 12 Nov 2025). Net worth was Rs 205 Cr at 31 Mar 2024 (CRISIL).
- The accounts do not say which site, bill or desk holds the extra days, or since when.

## Problem 2: working capital is mostly deposits, retention and bills, not slow payers
- CRISIL, 19 Jun 2024: gross current assets of 195 to 317 days across the five fiscals to FY24, driven by earnest money deposits, security deposits and retention money. History only.
- Same document: bills are raised monthly or on milestones and payment is received within 30 days (CRISIL's statement of the company's practice).
- So the open question is where the rest sits between measurement, certification, invoice and payment, and which of those waits is the company's to move and which is the client's. No debtor, creditor or inventory days are published, so no current days figure is drawn.

## Problem 3: a day has a price, and nobody can see which day
- Value of a day = FY25 turnover Rs 755.55 Cr / 365 = Rs 2.07 Cr (Computed from MGT-7).
- Scenario (calc/ajwani-savings.out.txt): ten days off any one wait is Rs 20.7 Cr of cash released once; thirty days is Rs 62.1 Cr. No interest priced: no borrowing rate is published. Rows overlap and are never added. Scenario, not a forecast.
- Paying vendors later is never a saving and is not modelled.
