## Conflicts between sources
1. Revenue: dossier ~Rs 300 Cr (FY24) against CRISIL "over Rs 500 crore" (FY24). CRISIL wins; dossier discarded.
2. Titles: site says Sunil is Joint MD and Sagar Executive Director; MGT-7 says Managing Director and Whole-time director. Use site titles for address blocks, MGT-7 for statutory designation; flag in the letter page.
3. Pritish Navin Ajwani: MGT-7 p.13 records "Whole-time director" changing designation on 01 May 2024; at year end he is listed "Director" and paid under "other directors". Print "Director".
4. FY25 turnover Rs 755.55 Cr (MGT-7, Companies Act turnover) against CRISIL's "operating income" basis for earlier years: not the same measure, never put in one growth line.
5. Age: site "39 Years" against 1986 incorporation (40 years) against "over two decades" (CRISIL). Avoid a number, say "since 1975, a company since 1986".
6. Coincidence to avoid: FY25 turnover (755.55) is close to the Mar 2024 order book (754). Different years and measures; never pair them.
