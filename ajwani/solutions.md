Ownership of each wait follows the act that ends it. Ours: measure with the client, prepare, review, submit, invoice. The client's: acknowledge, certify, pay.

Savings are scenarios. Ten days on a wait the company owns and ten on a wait the client owns are each Rs 20.7 Cr; they overlap and are never added.
