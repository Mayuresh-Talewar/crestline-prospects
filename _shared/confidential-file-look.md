## 2. Conventions (with strength)
- Wording ladder: "Strictly Private & Confidential" (cover, advisers) > "Confidential" (running footer) > "Confidential and proprietary" (consultancies). Strong.
- Cover is mostly white and quiet: title, party, date, one confidentiality line. No stamps, no icons. Strong.
- Code name "Project X" instead of the company name (Goldman SEC exhibits; teaser guide https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/valuation/what-is-an-investment-teaser/). Moderate.
- Recipient control: "Book Number / Issued To" on cover (Bear Stearns). "Copy No. x of y" is the same convention per search summaries; I did not see a rendered one. Weak-Moderate. Disclaimer says return or destroy on request.
- Disclaimer is its own page 2, dense small type. Strong (3 CIMs).
- Running footer on every page: classification + page number. Strong.
- Fonts: serif (Times-like) for banks; Arial/Helvetica-type sans for consultancies. White page, black ink, one corporate blue accent (bar or title colour). Strong.
- Envelope: "Private & Confidential" / "To be opened by addressee only" is ordinary mail handling; such letters go unopened to the named person (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/inheritance-tax-manual/ihtm02092; phrase defined at https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/node/1357949). Tamper-evident envelopes exist (https://patents.google.com/patent/US7389906), no corporate example seen. Moderate.
- Board pack cover: company, meeting/date, version, document owner (https://idealsboard.com/white-papers/board-pack-template/). Moderate.

## 3. Recommended cover: "Numbered private file" (white paper, ink, Crestline blue)
A4 portrait, white uncoated stock, black ink, Crestline blue only:
1. Top edge: solid Crestline-blue bar, 8 mm high, bleeding off the right (Bear Stearns device).
2. Top-right, small serif: `Copy No. 1 of 1` and `Issued to: [Name], Executive Chairman` (printed, not blank: proves a person was chosen).
3. Middle: `Project [Codename]` large serif in ink; hairline rule; `Working-capital review of [Company] Limited`.
4. Under it, small: `Prepared for the Executive Chairman · [Month Year]`.
5. Bottom-left, three small lines (McKinsey order).
Exact wording:
- `Copy No. 1 of 1`
- `Issued to: [Name], Executive Chairman, [Company] Limited`
- `Project [Codename]`
- `Working-capital review of [Company] Limited`
- `Prepared for the Executive Chairman · [Month Year]`
- `Strictly Private & Confidential`
- `Prepared for the addressee only. Please do not forward or copy.`
- `Crestline Intelligence`
Codename: neutral noun, not military or animal flavour, e.g. "Project Ledger" or "Project Keel".
Mailing: plain white C4, printed `Private & Confidential` / `To be opened by addressee only`, name and designation. No window, no stamp.

## 4. Five inside-page conventions
1. Running footer on every page: `Strictly Private & Confidential · Project [Codename] · Copy 1 of 1 · page n`, 8 pt grey-ink sans.
2. Page 2 "Notice to the addressee": one dense paragraph (Bear Stearns style): prepared for you, figures drawn from the sources stated, not for onward distribution, please return or destroy on request. Plain language, no legal citations.
3. Distribution line under the notice: `Distribution: Executive Chairman only. 1 copy printed.` plus `Document no. CR-[yy]-001` and issue date.
4. Running header: section name left in small caps, `Project [Codename]` right, hairline under. Blue only on hairlines, the cover bar and key figures.
5. Back cover: `If received in error, please return unopened to [Crestline address].` plus contact, same type as the cover footer.

## 5. Do not cross into impersonating authority
Avoid: emblems, crests, seals, shield or Ashoka-style devices; red or "TOP SECRET / CLASSIFIED / RESTRICTED / SECRET" stamps; "Government of India", "Ministry", "SEBI", "Income Tax", "CBI", "Court", "Notice", "Summons", "Order", "Under Section" wording; file or case numbers that look like legal references; fingerprint, eagle or dossier imagery (see AVOID png); seizure-style tamper tape; a signature block implying an official. Crestline's name must appear: a private company is the sender. Do not call it an NDA or legally binding; "please do not forward" is a request, not a threat. Do not copy a real bank's or consultancy's wording (the McKinsey line is evidence only).

## 6. Ranking (impact / effort)
1. `Issued to` + `Copy No.` + codename: high impact, type only.
2. Blue top bar + quiet white serif cover: high, type/layout only.
3. Running footer and page-2 notice: medium-high, type/layout only.
4. Plain envelope with addressee line: high, no artwork.
5. Optional touches (kraft wallet, blue-ink initials on the copy number, tab): low-moderate, needs print production; skip unless budget allows.
No real artwork needed: everything is type, rule and one blue bar.
