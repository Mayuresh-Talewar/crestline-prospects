# Fixed Crestline pages: company-neutral copy

The Crestline product pages of the 16-page master design: pages 7, 8, 11, 14, 15 and 16. Any company pack copies these and fills the slots. Nothing here belongs to one company.
Convention: lines starting with ">" are PRINTED copy. Plain lines are production notes and are never printed. Words in {BRACES} are slots the company pack fills; a slot left empty drops its whole line.
Taken from the two packs that passed QA, with every company name, person, figure, date, client and site removed.

## Slots used on these pages

{COMPANY}: the company's short name as the pack prints it elsewhere.
{ACCOUNTING_SYSTEM}: the ERP or accounting system the company's own filings name. If no filing names it, print "ERP".
{PILOT_SCOPE}: the pilot as one phrase, for example "one to three EPC projects", "one to three domestic INR projects". One entity, one segment.
{LENS_01_FIGURE} to {LENS_05_FIGURE} and {LENS_01_NOTE} to {LENS_05_NOTE}: the company's own public figure for each lens, with its short note. A lens with no public figure prints "not published" and a one-line reason.
{FINDING}, {FINDING_TONE}, {ROOT_CAUSE_SUBJECT}, {DO_THIS_TARGET}: the sample finding on page 11, drawn from one public figure.
{ASK_QUESTION}, {ASK_ANSWER}: one what-if on the company's published figures, worked by the pack's calculator.
{SCOPE_CARD}: optional fourth trust card for a scope limit the pack must state (a segment not modelled, no foreign-currency book, no joint-venture roll-up).
{SOURCES_PARAGRAPH}: the pack's full source list with filing dates.
{STRIP_TOTALS}: the company's own published totals printed beside the page 8 timeline.

Rules that hold on every fixed page:
1. Once connected, never "connected". The adapter does not exist until the pilot builds it, so no line says Crestline reads, connects to or holds the company's system today.
2. No ISO, certification, hosting, region or encryption claim. None is in place.
3. Page 8 stages carry NO day values, no bar lengths that imply days and no "illustrative split". Step waits are not public.
4. Ownership follows the act that ends the wait: measure, prepare, review, submit and invoice are the company's; acknowledge, certify and pay or collect are the client's; joint measurement is both.
5. A figure on these pages comes only from the slot it sits in. The fixed copy carries none.
