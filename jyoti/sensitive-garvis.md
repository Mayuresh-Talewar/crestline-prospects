# Jyoti Structures Limited: SENSITIVE list (Garvis only, never printed)

Booklet-facing files refer to these items only by number. "p" = the page of the PDF in src/d. One line each.

- **S1 Insolvency and resolution.** The NCLT approved the resolution plan by order dated 27 Mar 2019; the resolution professional ran the company until 9 Nov 2021, the plan's effective date (AR FY24 p26; AR FY26 p78, key audit matter "implemented with effect from November 09, 2021").
- **S2 Restructured debt.** Plan NCDs are carried at NPV including redemption premium, Rs 1,979.39 Cr (AR FY26 p126 note 27). A debenture redemption reserve of Rs 1,813.38 Cr was created in FY22, and Rs 159.39 Cr of it moved to borrowings in FY26 (note 28). Borrowings total Rs 2,083.17 Cr, 96.42% of it plan instruments. The company calls its debt service coverage of 0.48 "low due to high debt obligations" (p127).
- **S3 Lender settlement.** On 17 Feb 2025 the board approved talks on a one-time settlement with lenders (filed 18 Feb 2025). 1,149 NCDs with Rs 11.49 Cr face value were later settled for Rs 4.12 Cr (filed 18 Jun 2025; AR FY26 p126).
- **S4 Auditor emphasis of matter on trade receivables, every year FY23 to FY26.** The reconciliation is "under process" and the auditor relies on management representations. Receivables were 76.30% of total assets at Mar 2026 (AR FY26 p77; AR FY25 p68; AR FY24 p84; AR FY23 p39). The opinion itself is unmodified, and the directors say "does not contain any qualification" (AR FY26 p21). The Q1 FY27 review (4 Aug 2026, res_q1fy27 p5) repeats the point, with a provision of Rs 15.25 Cr.
- **S4a FY25 extra emphasis.** NCLT extended the plan's payment timelines to 11 Jun 2024 for dissenting financial creditors, operational creditors and employees (AR FY25 p68).
- **S5 Unaudited branches and subsidiaries.** Five of seven foreign branches are management-certified, and the auditor is "unable to comment on the impact" (AR FY26 p77). Overseas subsidiaries are unaudited with their net worth "fully eroded"; related-party loans of Rs 349.82 Cr carry a provision of Rs 311.62 Cr (AR FY26 p78, p97).
- **S6 Non-fund-based limits dispute with lenders.**
  * Aug 2024: NCLT partly allowed the release of non-fund-based limits under the plan (filed 21 Aug 2024); NCLAT affirmed this on 9 Dec 2024.
  * 13 Oct 2025: the company filed a contempt application.
  * 16 Feb 2026: NCLT allowed it and directed release within one month (filed 18 Feb 2026).
  * Feb 2026: lenders appealed (filed 27 Feb 2026).
  * 26 May 2026: NCLAT allowed the appeal, with banks to respond within one month of a request, and gave liberty to revive the contempt application (filed 27 May 2026).
  * Link to data: margin money of Rs 148.74 Cr is 120.42% of guarantees outstanding.
- **S7 Client recovery dispute.** The company applied to NCLT to restrain West Bengal State Electricity Distribution Company Limited from recovering dues from before the insolvency process, including through a bank guarantee (filed 7 Jul 2025).
- **S8 Contingent liabilities.** Disputed taxes of Rs 135.67 Cr, to be settled at 42% under the plan if they crystallise; civil 7.90; arbitration 2.26; writ 0.92 (AR FY26 p114). Statutory credits of Rs 17.14 Cr await reconciliation (AR FY26 p127 note 23).
- **S9 Regulator query.** On 28 Aug 2026 NSE sought a clarification on the Q1 FY27 results under Regulation 33; "The response of the Company is awaited" (NSE index, pulled 8 Oct 2026).
- **S10 Internal auditor removed.** M/s. Bhushan Khot & Co. was removed "with immediate effect" on 4 Aug 2026 on the Audit Committee's recommendation. It had been appointed on 30 Apr 2026. KNBJ continues (filed 4 Aug 2026).
- **S11 Leadership vacancies.** The CEO resigned on 29 May 2026 "for personal reason". On 26 Aug 2025 his tenure had been extended to 31 Jul 2028 (filed 26 Aug 2025). The Chairman died on 17 Jul 2026. Neither post had been filled by 8 Oct 2026, and the CFO is the only executive director.
- **S12 Rights issue II.** It was 92.11% subscribed (Rs 459.69 Cr of 499.09) and its objects were revised on 6 May 2025. Rs 23.49 Cr was still unused after the 31 Mar 2026 deadline, which was extended to 31 Mar 2027. Most objects pay plan dues and the margin needed for new bank facilities (MA report p3, p7, p8, 4 Aug 2026).
- **S13 Investor and capital matters.** On 4 Aug 2026 the board considered raising up to Rs 250 Cr. Authorised capital was raised to Rs 320 Cr (board 20 Jul 2026; AGM 13 Aug 2026). There have been repeated rights issues and warrant conversions since 2024.
- **S14 Auditor change.** The statutory auditor changed from G.P. Sharma & Co. LLP (FY24) to SARC & Associates (FY25 and FY26). The reason was not read; check before any mention.
- **S15 Rating actions.** No rating of Jyoti Structures Limited was found (data.md section 13), so nothing is pending. The one possibly sensitive point: the company operates without a published rating.
