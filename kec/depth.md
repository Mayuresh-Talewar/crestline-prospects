**Scoreboard (C, command in section 8):** six year-end NWC promises were made (100, 110, 100, 110, about 119, about 112.5); on the scale printed at the time, the actual was above the promise in 6 of 6, by 12 to 37 days. Debt: the Feb 2025 promise for Mar-25 was **met** (4,558 against 5,000, best case 4,500); three promises for Mar-26 (4,500, 5,000, 5,500) read 6,722, 2,222 / 1,722 / 1,222 above. NWC at the quarter ends: 122, 130, 134, 122, 128, 138, 135, 137, 134.
**The pattern in one line:** each call places the cash in the next quarter (water funds, ADB for Afghanistan, Saudi retention, large collections that land days after the quarter closes), each quarter end then prints 6 to 37 days above the number promised, and the causes named are real and mostly sit on the client's side; no filing says which bill, which desk, since when. That is the gap a bill-level queue closes, and it is the KEC team's own first initiative ("daily/weekly war rooms", "Release of Retention through focused commercial closure", IP Q3 FY26 s18). Strength: Strong (nine transcripts + eight decks, one company). Disconfirming check done: the Mar-25 debt target was met, and the NWC target on the AR26 scale misses by 1 day; both are stated above.
Respectful framing for the CFO: the direction reached him on Mar-25 (122 from 134 in one quarter, debt down 1,016 in a quarter: 5,574 to 4,558, C) shows the lever works when collections land; the question is only visibility ahead of the date.
