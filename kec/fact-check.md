1. AR26 PDF p.68 (printed p.132), Internal Control Adequacy: "At KEC, Internal Control is a fundamental pillar of the Company's governance framework. ... This framework is integrated with the SAP ERP platform and is designed to safeguard assets, enhance operational efficiency, monitor key processes and ensure compliance with applicable laws and regulations." Filed with the FY26 statements, 16 May 2026.
2. AR26 PDF p.33 (printed p.62-63), Financial Capital, among "Key initiatives undertaken during the year to optimise financial efficiency": "Strengthened financial governance through an SAP ERP-integrated internal control framework, enhancing operational efficiency and safeguarding assets."
3. AR25 PDF p.64: internal control "integrated with the ERP platform, SAP"; AR25 PDF p.34 (manufacturing): "Implemented ML-based Auto PRS Allocation and SAP enhancements for improved planning". AR24 PDF p.29: "Automation of various processes through SAP and use of AI"; AR24 p.59 and AR23 p.60: monitoring "through the ERP system, SAP".
