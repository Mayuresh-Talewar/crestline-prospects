Management Commentary

We are pleased with the growth in Revenues and Operating Profitability for the
quarter. The growth is backed by strong performances in our T&D and Cables &
Conductors businesses. The performance on EBITDA margin front reflects slower
progress in water projects and closure costs associated with the completion of metro
projects. Despite these challenges, our bottom line has seen healthy growth, with
operating PBT and PAT rising by 37% and 32% respectively. During the quarter, we
secured multiple strategic orders, strengthening our order book and L1 position to
over Rs. 41,000 crore. With a strong focus on execution, expanding capacity, a robust
order book and a substantial tender pipeline, particularly in T&D and Civil, we remain
well positioned to drive sustained and profitable growth in the coming quarters.
Vimal Kejriwal
Managing Director & CEO
