4 Working-Capital / Pain Analysis (Crestline wedge)
· NWC days rose to 122 (FY25) from 112 (FY24); debtor days 95 -- ~3+ months of sales locked in receivables across 100+

   country operations. This is the exact O2C metric Crestline compresses.

· Net debt Rs 4,558 Cr (incl. acceptances) -- WC-driven borrowing; management explicitly running a debt-reduction +

   WC-discipline program (board-watched lever).

· Multi-geography, multi-currency T&D; + civil + railways execution -- retention, BG/LC load, mobilisation advances, variation

   claims across dozens of countries and segments. Dispersed cash-cycle visibility is the core gap.

· T&D; export business (SAE Towers, Americas/MEA) adds FX + cross-border collection complexity on top of domestic govt

   receivables (Power Grid, TRANSCOs, Railways).

· P2P: steel/conductor/cable/tower procurement + global subcontractors -- creditor-days & advance management at Rs

   23,500 Cr scale.

· Thin EBITDA (~7%) means every WC/interest day matters to PAT -- strong ROI narrative for a working-capital-intelligence

   layer.
