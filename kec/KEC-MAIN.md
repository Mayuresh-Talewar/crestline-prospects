# KEC International: the one file (KEC-6, 2026-10-07)

Everything here is KEC's own published figure or Crestline's calculator run on it. Consolidated, INR crore (Cr), unless marked. Source tags: AR26 = Annual Report 2025-26 (statements signed 16 May 2026); AR25, AR24, AR23 = earlier reports; IP-Q4 / IP-Q1 = investor presentations (16 May 2026, 10 Aug 2026); PR = press release; T-Q4 / T-Q1 = earnings calls (18 May 2026, 11 Aug 2026). "Crestline calc" = our arithmetic on KEC's figures. Pages are PDF pages. Detail and grades are in `data.md` and `data-addendum.md`.

**Four rules that bind every page of this file**
1. Interest is priced only at KEC's own disclosed range for consortium working-capital loans, 6.90 to 8.90% (AR26 Note 30, p.137). We never print a blended rate of our own as KEC's.
2. Customer concentration is printed as a rupee amount, never as a percentage (the note does not state its base).
3. Returns: only KEC's own return on net worth. No computed ROCE, and standalone and consolidated are never mixed.
4. The step-by-step waits of the cash cycle are left blank. We do not have KEC's SAP data, so no split of KEC's days is shown.

Scenarios are what a stated number of days would be worth if it moved. They are not promises. Rows overlap and are never added. Paying vendors later is never shown as a saving.

## 1. KEC in one paragraph

KEC International Limited (NSE: KEC, BSE: 532714; registered office RPG House, Worli, Mumbai, AR26 p.109) is an engineering and construction company that builds power transmission and distribution (T&D), civil, railway, water, oil and gas and renewables projects, and makes towers and cables. It reported revenue of 23,505.54 Cr for FY26 (AR26 p.117) and an order book of 36,267 Cr at 31 March 2026 (AR26 p.10). T&D is the largest segment at 15,883 Cr, then Civil 3,823, Cables and Conductors 2,217, Transportation 1,555, Renewables 516 and Oil and Gas 258 (IP-Q4 slide 29). It works in more than 110 countries (AR26 p.4); international revenue is 8,989.47 Cr (Crestline calc on AR26 p.157), with Saudi Arabia and the UAE the biggest overseas markets (T-Q4 p.2). Vimal Kejriwal is Managing Director and CEO, Rajeev Aggarwal is Chief Financial Officer and H.V. Goenka is Chairman (AR26 p.117, p.49). KEC states that its internal control framework is integrated with its SAP ERP platform (AR26 p.68). The version, the modules and the data access are not stated, so we say nothing about them.

Ranked by impact on the reader. Source detail is in `problems.md`.

One series only: AR25 printed 112, 112, 122 for FY23 to FY25 and AR26 restated them, so the two are never mixed on one chart. KEC does not publish its NWC definition, so we never restate it.
- **Their accounts cannot tell them:** which of the days is certification, which is client payment, which is stores, which is vendor timing; which projects carry them; which part is theirs to move and which needs the client.

### P1. Work done and certified, still not cash: contract assets 12,128, retention 4,923
Contract assets are up 62.4% since FY23 while revenue is up 36.0% (index FY23 = 100: 162.4 against 136.0, Crestline calc). Retention receivables went from 3,669.63 to 4,922.51 in FY26, up 34.1% against revenue up 7.6% (AR26 p.132 Note 20; Crestline calc). Contract assets absorbed 4,745.90 Cr of cash over four years (AR26 p.118 and earlier; Crestline calc). The auditor lists "Recoverability of Trade Receivables and Contract Assets" as a key audit matter (AR26 p.111). That is a statement about judgement, not a criticism. Management says Saudi contracts carry "20% retention" and are "now heading into completion" (T-Q4 p.9).
- **Their accounts cannot tell them:** which project and bill the 12,128 sits on; how much awaits certification, how much is retention not yet due, how much is disputed; since when; whose desk. Contract-asset ageing is not published (`data.md` s.14).

### P4. Growth was paid for by debt
Net debt including acceptances rose 47.5% while revenue grew 7.6% (IP-Q4 slide 30; AR26 p.117). Operating cash over FY23 to FY26 was 929.84 Cr against capex of 926.87 Cr (AR26 p.118; Crestline calc). Finance cost was 663.60 Cr in FY26, 40.0% of EBITDA of 1,659 (Crestline calc). One day of revenue is 64.4 Cr and one day of EBITDA is 4.55 Cr, so ten days of working capital is 38.8% of a year's EBITDA (Crestline calc).
- **Their accounts cannot tell them:** which projects absorbed the cash; the deepest funding need inside a quarter (a closing figure hides it); what each project's bills cost to carry.

**What the app ranks first (`priceLevers`).** Retention held above revenue growth (974.2 Cr, the client's act); bill and certify executed work 10 days sooner (644.0 Cr, partly KEC's (bill preparation and submission), partly the client's (certification)); collect invoiced receivables 10 days sooner (644.0 Cr, the client's clock); collect receivables overdue over six months (621.4 Cr).

**Caveats printed with these figures (QA, KEC-8).** Receivables: Receivable balances include GST; priced at revenue per day, which understates the cash by up to about 15%. Interest: Interest at the 6.90% to 8.90% KEC paid on consortium working-capital loans in FY26, assuming released cash repays those loans. Retention: Illustrative: holds retention to FY25 x FY26 revenue growth. Included in contract assets, so not additional to the contract-asset rows. Not a milestone-based figure. Overdue row: Overdue over six months back to the FY25 share (9.0%). Overlaps receivable collection. Days 188.3 / 101.0 / 289.3: Closing balances over revenue per day; receivables include GST, so days overstate; Crestline arithmetic, not KEC's definition. (GST-inclusive balances over ex-GST revenue: days are overstated, cash is not.) DPO 212.0 and the 510.7 for paying 10 days sooner: Includes interest-bearing acceptances (also in net debt); cost base is materials plus erection only. Certification is the only lever fully inside KEC, which is why it leads the CFO conversation even though retention ranks first on money.

## 5. The headline

| | |
|---|---|
| KEC's own target | NWC 137 to 110 days, 27 days (T-Q1 p.17) |
| Cash released at revenue per day | **Rs 1,738.8 Cr** (27 x 64.40, Crestline calc) |
| KEC's own debt target | 6,722 to about 5,500 = Rs 1,222 Cr (T-Q1 p.17) |
| NWC target against debt target | **142%** (1,738.8 / 1,222, Crestline calc) |
| Interest a year | **Rs 120 to 155 Cr** at 6.90 to 8.90% (Crestline calc) |

Read as: if the working-capital promise is met on FY26 revenue, the cash it releases is more than the debt promise needs. It comes before capex (328.68 Cr in FY26), dividend, tax and growth, which net debt also has to carry. From 30 June 2026 the gap is 24 days: 1,545.6 Cr released, 106.6 to 137.6 Cr a year. KEC gives no total revenue guidance, so no growth number is assumed. Every 1% of revenue growth held at 110 days needs 70.8 Cr of working capital (Crestline calc). The 142% compares a gross working-capital release with a net debt cut that also carries capex, dividend, tax and growth; 1,738.8 exceeds 1,222, so about 517 would not reduce the guided debt figure. The 6.90 to 8.90% range applies to the debt only if released cash retires the loans. This is a scenario built from KEC's public figures, never a promise.

## 6. CFO angle and CEO angle

**CFO, Mr. Rajeev Aggarwal** (full copy: `slots-cfo.md`, storyline `storyline-cfo.md`)
1. Lead with his own 110-day promise and the 27 days between it and 137.
2. Show the legs behind one number: certification, collection, stock, vendor timing, per project.
3. Price every day in cash and in interest at KEC's own 6.90 to 8.90% range.
4. The steps before the client certifies (measure, prepare, review, submit) are KEC's own desks, in KEC's control; certification itself is the client's act, which Crestline makes visible and chases with dates.
5. Treat payables as visibility only, never as a stretch, and say what is not claimed (no LC, acceptance or TReDS modelling).

**CEO, Mr. Vimal Kejriwal** (full copy: `slots-ceo.md`, storyline `storyline-ceo.md`)
1. Lead with the consequence: growth paid for by debt, net debt up 47.5%, operating cash negative.
2. Frame the same numbers as profit: interest saved moves profit before tax and profit after tax, not EBITDA margin.
3. At the 110-day target, interest saved is 15.2 to 19.6% of FY26 profit after tax of 605.59 Cr (Crestline calc, after tax at 23.3%).
4. Ask which job holds the most capital and whose desk it sits on, ours or the client's.
5. Keep to what the control tower can show today (portfolio overview, Ask Crestline brief, performance), at one segment.
