# Patel Engineering: problems

Three problems, on the company's own figures. Each figure carries its basis (STANDALONE or CONSOLIDATED), and a rupee figure is always priced on the same basis as the days it moves. Respect first: each opens with what the company has already done well.

## P1. Seventeen working capital days came back in one quarter
**What went right.** Net working capital days fell from 160 (FY23) to 145 (FY24) to 105 (FY25), 55 days out in two years.
**What the money did.** They rose to 120 at FY26 and 137 at 30 Jun 2026: 17 days in one quarter.
* Source: CONSOLIDATED, company stated. FY23 to FY26: Q4 FY26 investor presentation, slide 36, filed 14 May 2026. Q1 FY27: Q1 FY27 investor presentation, slide 14, filed 10 Aug 2026.
* Words: "the net working capital days is around 120 days" (Rahul Agarwal, Chief Financial Officer, Q4 FY26 call transcript PDF p8, call 14 May 2026, filed 20 May 2026); "net working capital days around 137 days" (same speaker, Q1 FY27 transcript PDF p7, call 11 Aug 2026, filed 17 Aug 2026).
* Basis: these days are consolidated, so every rupee figure priced on them uses FY26 consolidated revenue, Rs 5,102.74 Cr (Q4 FY26 audited results PDF p20, filed 14 May 2026). See calc/notes.md.
* **What the accounts cannot tell:** which site added the 17 days, which leg (receivable, retention, stock, payable), which bills, whose desk they sat on, and since when.

## P2. Collections are a strength, and two receivable figures do not share a basis
**What went right.** Net trade receivables fell from Rs 992.60 Cr (72.3 days) at 31 Mar 2025 to Rs 813.54 Cr (58.6 days) at 31 Mar 2026. The Managing Director on one large job: "we are getting paid monthly as and when we do the work" (Kavita Shirvaikar, Managing Director, Q4 FY26 transcript PDF p14, call 14 May 2026, filed 20 May 2026).
* Source: STANDALONE. Balances: Annual Report FY26 PDF p120, printed p213, filed 24 Aug 2026; FY24 Rs 752.49 Cr (62.3 days) from Annual Report FY25 PDF p169, filed 18 Aug 2025 (that report's vintage). Days computed on standalone revenue (src/pe-calc-output.txt).
* The CFO gives a different figure: "Receivable days is between 40 to 45." (Rahul Agarwal, Chief Financial Officer, answering a question about Q1 of this year, Q1 FY27 transcript PDF p13, call 11 Aug 2026, filed 17 Aug 2026). He did not say how it is measured. Ours is the standalone balance at 31 Mar 2026 over a full year's revenue. The two measure different things, so neither is wrong.
* Ownership: measuring, preparing, reviewing, submitting and invoicing a bill are the company's; acknowledging, certifying and paying are the client's.
* **What the accounts cannot tell:** which bills make the difference between the two figures, how many of those days are ours (bill not yet submitted or invoiced) and how many are the client's (not yet certified or paid), and each bill against its own due date.

## P3. Retention the CFO can size but the accounts do not show
**What went right.** The CFO knows the figure and the route to release it: "So, see, we have retention almost 5% for every contract and almost 100, between 200 to 250 crores is there as retention on the balance sheet."; "So, we generally get it released at the end of the project, but some projects allow us to withdraw against bank guarantees." (Rahul Agarwal, Chief Financial Officer, Q1 FY27 transcript PDF p15, call 11 Aug 2026, filed 17 Aug 2026).
* Size: Rs 200 to 250 Cr is 14.3 to 17.9 days of FY26 consolidated revenue (calc/pe-savings-output.txt). The CFO did not name a basis; it is read on the consolidated basis of the same call's working capital days. The figure is management's words; retention is not a separate line in the Annual Report FY26.
* Ownership: preparing and submitting the release request, and the guarantee where a contract allows one, are the company's; certifying the milestone and paying are the client's.
* **What the accounts cannot tell:** which contracts hold how much, which allow withdrawal against a guarantee, which have reached their release date, who prepares each release request, and how long each has waited.
