## S1. Arbitration claims and awards (the legacy claims trap)
* **Size, management's words** (all Rahul Agarwal, CFO):
  * Q1 T1Q26 PDF p14 (printed 13 of 18), call 11 Aug 2025: "We have arbitral claims and awards of combined around INR3,000 crores, so this is all [...] even if you consider 50%, 60% of this value coming in, with interest, the number will be much higher."
  * Q2 T1Q26 PDF p18 (printed 17 of 18): "So there are INR3,000 crores of arbitration claims and other claims awards and all. So what I was saying is that expected is 50%, 60% for the claims plus interest, so that overall number is pretty high. And we expect to recover these over the next 5 to 7 years because slowly, slowly it will start coming in every year because it goes through various stages of courts and everything."
  * Q3 T1Q26 PDF p19 (printed 18 of 18): "So new claim build-up is not happening. So eventually, all these claims will keep coming down."
  * Q4 T3Q26 PDF p8 (printed 7 of 21), call 16 Feb 2026: "So we have total awards and claims around INR 3,000 crores, around INR700 crores is awarded."
  * Q5 T4Q26 PDF p15 (printed 14 of 15), call 14 May 2026: "Out of the INR 700 crores, more than INR 400 crores is in high court right now. And balance is in district court." Then: "The balance INR 2,300 crores, there are multiple arbitrations going on. [...] If it has to be realized from courts, then overall it may take five to six to seven years."
* **Book value: NOT FOUND.** No filing gives the carrying amount of claims. The Rs 3,000 Cr is a gross claim value (management expects 50% to 60% plus interest). It must never be compared with or netted against the Rs 3,675.78 Cr contract asset balance: the bases differ.
* **Where claims sit on the balance sheet (filings):** receivables are net of Rs 404.24 Cr of advances received against awards and claims (AR26 PDF p120). Award receivables are interest bearing (same note). The FY25 rating rationale says WIP "includes unbilled revenue and arbitration claims pending awards relating to legacy projects and stock of land" and receivables "also include awards in favour of the company however, stuck in arbitration" (Infomerics, 6 Jun 2025, p4, standalone approach, image checked). In Nov 2023 ICRA wrote: "there have been instances of reversal of certain awards in the past by Supreme Court and the risks associated with any future reversal continue to remain" (ICRA rationale 30 Nov 2023, p2, image checked).
* **Cash realised:** FY25 about Rs 350 Cr under Vivad se Vishwas (T4Q25 PDF p7, Rahul Agarwal; Infomerics gives Rs 353.60 Cr). FY26 Rs 50 Cr: "INR 50 crores was for arbitration, which we got against BG" (T4Q26 PDF p12, printed 11 of 15). Promise P10: "maybe Rs. 50-Rs. 60 crores" for FY26 (T2Q26 PDF p17, 13 Nov 2025). Delivered at the low end.
* **Bank lines carved out for arbitration:** arbitration BG sublimits and a "BG for monetization of arbitration awards" appear in the 2 Jun 2026 rating letter annexure (NSE 3 Jun 2026, PDF p7 to p8, text layer).
* **Recommendation to Garvis:** a booklet may say that legacy claims sit inside unbilled work and cannot be separated from the accounts. It should not print the Rs 3,000 Cr or the court split.

## S2. Debt restructuring and security
* Lenders implemented an RBI S4A (Scheme for Sustainable Structuring of Stressed Assets) plan from the reference date 8 Aug 2017. Part B debt became 0.01% OCDs with a 7% IRR, repayable 5%, 20%, 25%, 25%, 25% from year 6 to year 10 (last 8 Aug 2027). AR26 PDF p124, printed p220.
* Security includes a first pari passu charge on "receivables more than 180 days, retention deposit", pledge of 93,50,927 promoter shares, pledge of 49% of Hitodi Infrastructure, and personal guarantees of Ms. Janky Patel (and Mr. Muthu Raj for WCTL). Same page.
* Covenants (listed NCD): total debt / net worth at most 2, total debt / EBITDA at most 4.75, DSCR at least 1, net worth at least Rs 32,000 Mn, PAT positive. Complied at 31 Mar 2026 (AR26 PDF p124, printed p221).
* OCD outstanding "around INR 100 crores, INR 120 crores", to be repaid by FY28 (T4Q26 PDF p12, Rahul Agarwal). Rating letter: OCD rated amount Rs 106.23 Cr.

* **Recommendation:** no booklet mention of Teesta VI, Sikkim tunnels, or safety, and no "on-site visibility" pitch that could read as a reference to it.
