## 3. Working-capital target

**None stated.** No receivable-days, NWC-days, cash-cycle or working-capital target appears in AR24, AR25, AR26 (front sections), the three decks, or the five transcripts (searched "working capital days / cycle / target", "receivable / debtor / collection days", "cash conversion cycle", "NWC", "target"; planted controls: "working capital" occurs on 10, 16 and 10 pages of AR24, AR25, AR26; "target" found T1 pdf p3). The closest things are third-party: CARE lists "Elongation of operating cycle with collection days remaining above 320 days" as a negative rating factor (CARE pdf p2), and Infomerics lists "Stretch in working capital cycle" (INF pdf p17, text layer only, not image-checked). Neither is the company's target. Our calculator scenarios must therefore use contract terms ("generally on terms of 30 to 90 days", note 6.7, AR26 p132) and step scenarios, not a company target.
