## 5. What Crestline does, and the money and time back (solutions.md, calc output)
Crestline sits above the ERP, rebuilds every RA bill and order from its dates, splits each wait into Afcons' steps (measure, prepare, submit, escalate) and the client's (certify, collect), prices it in rupees, names the desk, and closes tickets only when the source figure moves. Per day = 11,948.38 / 365 = ₹32.735 Cr. Rate = 7.55% to 9.95% (cash credit and working capital demand loans; AR26 PDF p.185, borrowings note).

- **Overlap note: rows overlap and are never added.** The ~1,000 is described by management as receivable plus unbilled, so it overlaps both 10/30-day rows; the 143 to 113 step is the same row as the 30-day step; the over-1-year row sits inside the receivable leg. The honest headline is one row, not a sum: 30 days is about ₹982 Cr once and ₹74 to ₹98 Cr a year before tax.
- **Time back** = days out of each wait (10 or 30), never management hours.
- **AF-6 caveats, binding:** 722.0 is a ceiling (27.0% disputed, allowance 281.70); PBT 386.79 is after the 76.51 one-time item (463.30 before; shares lower); percent-of-PAT is pre-tax interest over PAT and overstates, so the booklets do not print it; 123.4 days is balance over revenue per day, not a DSO (retention and 266.08 of arbitration interest sit inside receivables); NWC days follow the company's own formula (Q4 FY25 investor presentation p.39, reproduces 43, 38, 63, 113, 143); interest is saved only if the cash repays the cash credit or working capital loans; interest does not move EBITDA.

## 8. What we can and cannot promise
- **Can say (webapp-fit):** shows where cash is trapped, for how long, whose step; ageing on each document's own due date; ranked levers priced against the cost of financing; tickets that close on the re-measured figure; read-only.
- **Cannot say:** that it fixes margin, cost to complete, budget against actual or EAC; handles joint ventures, multi-currency, claims or arbitration workflow, BG/LC; connects to SAP today; goes live in days; any ISO, hosting or encryption claim; any saving from paying vendors later.
- **Pilot:** an SAP adapter is built (none exists today), read-only, for 1 to 3 domestic INR projects, 14 days from approval of access. Ownership: measure, prepare, submit = Afcons; certify, collect = client.
