## C. ERP, primary evidence
1. **RHP (dated 18 Oct 2024), restated financial statements, Note 52 "As at 30th June, 2024 and 31st March, 2024" (printed p.546, PDF 551):** "The Company was using accounting software i.e. 'SAP ECC' as its books of accounts till 20 November 2023. The Company has upgraded its accounting software to 'SAP S/4 Hana with rise' with effect from 21st November,2023. While SAP audit logging has been enabled throughout the year and captures all the changes made in the application system through an audit log, the same was not enabled for SAP ECC database for the period 01st April, 2023 to 20th November,2023. ... In SAP S/4HANA with rise, database is maintained on cloud with the service provider and hence the management cannot make any changes at database level except by way of raising a ticket with SAP S/4Hana with rise accounting software." (This is the Companies (Accounts) Rules audit-trail disclosure, so it is the auditors'/management's primary statement of which system holds the books.)
2. **RHP printed p.295 (PDF 300):** "We have implemented an SAP-based dashboard which provides real-time insights to management teams for informed decision-making ... We are in the process of migrating to a software for the preparation of annual project budgets ... We are digitalising our entire supply chain management process, including logistics, sub-contracting, procurement and storage. Similarly, the entire approval process for sub-contracting has been digitized." Also a Remote Monitoring System (IoT) for equipment fleet management.
3. **AR25 and AR26 auditor's reports** (AR26 PDF p.112, para vi): "has used accounting software systems for maintaining its books of account ... which have the feature of recording audit trail (edit log) facility and the same has operated throughout the year for all relevant transactions recorded in the software systems." The name SAP is not repeated in AR25/AR26 (full-text search of both PDFs for SAP/ERP/S/4/Oracle: no hits).
4. **Company case studies / sap.com customer story:** searched (web search "Afcons RISE with SAP S/4HANA customer story"); none found. Not claimed.
5. Not found: Primavera or other project-planning tools named in filings; job posts (not searched, not primary).
What this means for Crestline (fact only): books of account on SAP S/4HANA (RISE, vendor-hosted database) since 21 Nov 2023, SAP ECC before; audit logging on; project-site, procurement and subcontract workflows described as digitised on an SAP-based dashboard. Document-level data (receivable, contract-asset and payable ageing) is already produced per project by the company's own system. No filing says Afcons runs process mining or an O2C/P2P event log: do not imply it.

## D. Other errors and traps found while checking
- **Revenue vs total income** is the dossier's main hazard; the company headlines TOTAL INCOME everywhere (press release, presentation) and EBITDA margin is on total income (C). Print the label.
- **EBITDA is company-defined** and differs from the Board's Report figure by the ₹76.51 Cr labour-code charge in FY26 (C).
- **Net debt FY25**: management said "INR1,486 crores"; AR26's own table shows 1,572.74 (incl. leases) and C 1,465.16 ex-leases; FY25 gross 2,235.72 vs CFO's "2,230". Use AR figures.
- **"Book-to-bill 3.8x"** (Chairman, Q1PR) does not recompute (13,219 / 2,727 = 4.85): quote only as the company's.
- **Unbilled claims**: ₹659.87 Cr of variations on one JV, carried in non-current contract assets, is the subject of the first/second emphasis-of-matter paragraphs (data.md section 10).
- **Dossier "WC / execution: ... retention"**: retention is not a FY26 disclosed figure; the dated figure is FY24.
