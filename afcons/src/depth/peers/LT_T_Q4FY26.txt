Projects and Manufacturing structure being now formally redesignated as Projects, Products and
Manufacturing, PP&M. The only material change is the segregation of the Realty as a standalone

realignments to sharpen the strategic focus.

comprises Renewables, earlier it was under Infrastructure Projects, and Offshore Wind, which
was earlier forming part of Hydrocarbon in the Energy segment, along with the Onshore Wind

green ammonia production facilities under a BOO framework. For clarity, while these projects

of PP&M, reflecting the fundamentally different capital structures, investment horizons, and the
asset ownership characteristics of these businesses.
