## 2. NWC days: what the company counts, and the FY22/FY23 gap now closed
The company's definition is printed in the Q4 FY25 presentation p.39 (image checked): (current assets less cash and bank balances) less (current liabilities less current borrowings), divided by revenue from operations, x 365. `calc_af3.py` applies it to the RHP (PDF p.444, image checked) and AR26 (PDF p.165) balance sheets:

| | FY22 | FY23 | FY24 | FY25 | FY26 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Computed NWC days (C) | 42.9 | 37.9 | 62.8 | 113.2 | **142.8** |
| Company-printed (V) | 43 (Q3 FY25 pres p.34) | 38 (same) | 63 | 113 | 143 (AR26 p.18; CFO Q4T p.19) |
| CRISIL's own basis (V, RR 19 Aug 2026) | NF | NF | "less than 90" | 117 | 139 |

So data.md's "FY26 definition not stated, FY22/FY23 NF" is closed: FY26 143 reproduces on the stated formula (C), and FY22/FY23 were 43/38. Net working capital in rupees: 1,295 (FY22), 1,314 (FY23), 2,283 (FY24), 3,891 (FY25), 4,675 (FY26) Cr (C). The +80 days FY24 to FY26 is +127% (C). CRISIL's figures are on its own adjustment (its FY25 117 vs company 113), so never mix the two series. Strong (company print + recompute + third party).
