# Claude Design master prompt: Afcons working-capital review (one booklet, per-reader editions)

Paste everything below this line into Claude Design. Upload the Crestline logo file and, if you have it, the Afcons logo file before you send it.

## 1. What you are building

A 16-page A4 portrait booklet titled "Working-capital review", printed and posted to named readers at Afcons Infrastructure Limited. You build ONE design and produce one edition per reader listed in the reader table (see "Reader editions" below).
It answers one question for the reader: what if Crestline were already running at Afcons, how much money and how much time would it save?
Every word and every figure is supplied in the Content section at the end of this prompt. Your job is layout, type and charts, built on the master design described in section 2.

## 2. The design to reproduce (the master)

You have not seen the master file, so it is described here in full. Reproduce it; do not restyle it.

**Page and grid.** A4 portrait, 210 x 297 mm. Outer margin about 17 mm left and right, 16 mm top, 14 mm bottom. One text column, left-aligned, ragged right. Body copy measure about 60 to 70 characters. Generous empty space; nothing centred except the QR card contents.

**Type.** Poppins throughout, no serif anywhere.
- Headline: Poppins Medium, about 24 pt, line height 1.15, two lines. Line 1 in ink #161B28 (white on dark pages). Line 2, or the key phrase named in the headline table, in gold #B9975B (#D9C08F on dark pages). A full stop after the headline is in ink.
- Chapter label above the headline: Poppins Medium about 10 pt, gold, sentence case ("Chapter one").
- Running head at the top of every inner page: Poppins Medium about 6 pt, all caps, letter-spaced about 0.3 em, muted #6A7080. Left: the kicker given for that page in the Content. Right: Afcons Infrastructure Limited in caps on pages about the company, "CRESTLINE INTELLIGENCE" on pages about the product, "PUBLIC RECORD" on the public-record page; the Content says which. A gold hairline (0.5 pt, #D9C08F) runs under the running head across the full text width.
- Body: Poppins Regular 9 to 10 pt, ink #161B28 (on dark pages #C6CEE2). Lead paragraph under the headline 9.5 pt.
- Big figures: Poppins Bold, navy #22396F on cream pages, white on dark pages; key figures in gold #D9C08F on dark pages. Tabular figures.
- Small labels and eyebrows: Poppins Medium 6 to 7 pt, all caps, letter-spaced, muted.
- Footnotes: Poppins Regular 6 pt, muted #6A7080, at the foot of the page above the footer.
- Emphasis inside a closing line: Poppins Medium Italic in gold.

**Colours.**
- Navy field (cover, back cover, dark pages): #0D172F. Navy figure ink: #22396F.
- Cream inner page: #F6F2E9. Hairlines on cream: #DDCDB0. White cards on cream: #FFFFFF with a 1 px #E6E8EC border, radius about 14 px, a whisper shadow.
- Gold: #B9975B on cream, #D9C08F on navy. Muted text #6A7080. Ink #161B28.
- Verdict colours (only where a figure is judged): good #2F7D60, warning #A8620F, risk #B0342B.

## 3. Reader editions

Build ONE 16-page design. Then, for each row of the reader table in the Content section, produce one edition of it.
- An edition changes ONLY three things: the cover's "PREPARED EXCLUSIVELY FOR" lines (that reader's name and title), the page 2 letter (that reader's letter text and address block), and the page 2 salutation.
- Every other page, word, figure, chart and position is IDENTICAL across editions. Do not tailor any body page to a reader.
- Name each edition after its reader as given in the table (for example "Edition: <reader name>"). That label is for the file only and is never printed.
- Body copy is written in a neutral voice. Quotes are credited by name and title, as the Content gives them; never rewrite a credit into "you" for any edition.

## 4. Rules you must follow

1. Use ONLY the text and figures in the Content section. Do not write new copy, captions, taglines, teasers, pull quotes or headings. If a slot has no text, leave it empty.
2. Copy every figure exactly as written: same digits, same decimals, same currency sign, same unit. Do not round, re-total, convert or recompute anything.
3. Headlines exactly as given in the headline table, split into two lines as shown. Never reword them.
4. Footnotes exactly as given, on the page they belong to. Where a footnote carries a tag in brackets, print the caveat line that tag stands for (listed under "Keys" in the Content) in its place, in full.
5. Text inside quotation marks in the Content is printed copy. Words outside quotation marks (for example "Chart:", "Tiles:", word counts in brackets, "Template fallback", "No scenario mark") are instructions to you and are never printed.
6. No internal references in printed text. Slot ids, ticket ids, file names, tags in brackets, checker notes, "Verified", "Secondary" status words used as markers, and any note addressed to the team are never printed. A source is printed only in the words the Content gives for it.
7. No printed negatives about the booklet itself: never print what is missing, unknown, unchecked or left out ("not available", "we could not find", "TBD", "placeholder"). Where a slot is empty, leave the space empty. Print a "not public" line only where the Content gives it as quoted copy.
8. No em dashes and no en dashes anywhere. Use a comma, a full stop, "to" or a middle dot (·) as the Content does.
9. Ownership words stay as written: the client certifies and pays; Afcons measures, prepares, submits and escalates. Do not swap them.
10. The Afcons logo appears only if the client uploaded it, used as supplied. Never redraw, trace, recolour or imitate it. Without it, the cover shows the reader's name only and no company mark.

### Booklet accuracy rules (apply to every page, every edition)
A1. **Claims about Crestline must be true today.** Make no claim about Crestline beyond the pasted text. No certification "in progress", no hosting region, no encryption claim, no client names, no "live in X days", and no present-tense claim that Crestline connects to the company's SAP or ERP. The pilot is one entity, one segment, read-only, 14 days, as the Content words it.
A2. **Every quote names the right speaker**, exactly as the Content credits it. Never move a quote to another person.
A3. **Ours versus the client's follows the product rule.** In cash-in steps, acknowledged, certified and collected are the client's acts; measuring, preparing, reviewing, submitting and invoicing are the company's own. Keep every OURS and CLIENT tag as given.
A4. **No "nothing went wrong" headlines.** Use only the headlines given; never soften or invert one.
A5. **Charts drawn to scale.** Every bar, segment, column and point sits within 2% of its own value on ONE shared scale per chart, starting where the Content says. Every bar shows its value label.
A6. **Interest only at a rate the company discloses.** Print only the rate and interest figures the Content gives; never compute or label a rate as theirs.
A7. **Savings are scenarios.** Anything marked "with Crestline", "scenario", "dashed" or "days less" is drawn dashed and hollow with "Scenario, not a forecast." beside it. Scenario rows overlap and are never added together; print no total the Content does not give. Charts the Content marks as history only get no scenario mark.
A8. **Peers and comparatives** appear only where the Content prints them, with the vintage it gives. Never build a scorecard of missed promises.
A9. **No other company's data.** Do not bring in any company, name, figure or design text from anywhere other than the Content, including from earlier booklets or your memory. No demo numbers beside the company's figures.
A10. **Full address and correct names and titles** on the letter page and cover, exactly as the reader table gives them, per edition.
A11. **"You said" only for the reader's own words.** Never print "you said", "in your own words" or any second-person credit over a quote by someone other than that edition's reader. Use the speaker's name and title or the company's name as the Content gives it. If a headline says "your" or "you" about a quote, check it in every edition; if the quote is not that reader's, rewrite the credit to the speaker's name and title in that edition. Never print a note or flag on the page.

A12. **Never invent figures to fill a chart.** If the Content gives a stage, step or bar without a value (for example the page 8 bill timeline), draw it without a number. Never create an "illustrative split" of a total, and never add a sentence about one.
A13. **No signed numbers in print.** Write inflow / outflow, increase / decrease. No "+" or "-" in front of a figure.

## 5. Before you finish, check every page of every edition

1. Count the pages: exactly 16, A4 portrait, in the order of the Content.
2. Read every headline against the headline table: same words, same punctuation, line 2 in gold.
3. Pick every figure on every page and find it in the Content, character for character. Any figure you cannot find there, delete it.
4. Search the whole booklet for en dash and em dash characters (Unicode 2013 and 2014). There must be none.
5. Every chart: one scale per chart, each bar within 2% of its value, every bar labelled, nothing drawn from a figure that is not in the Content.
6. Every scenario mark is dashed and hollow and carries "Scenario, not a forecast." Charts marked "No scenario mark" or "history only" carry none. No scenario totals added.
7. Every footnote is on its page, with each bracketed tag replaced by its full caveat line.
8. No word was added: no taglines, no right-hand footer teasers, no extra captions, no "Live in" line, no certification or hosting claim, no client names, no internal references, no printed negatives.
9. The Afcons logo is the uploaded file or absent. Nothing redrawn.
10. The cover and back cover have the navy field with the gold curve lines; pages 4, 7 and 12 are navy; page 15 ends in the navy sources band; every other page is cream with the running head, gold hairline and "Strictly private · N / 16" footer.
11. The QR code on page 15 (and on page 16) opens crestlineintelligence.com/book-a-demo.
12. Instruction words from the Content (word counts in brackets, "Chart:", "Template fallback", notes about decisions and checks) are not printed anywhere.
13. Editions: compare each edition with the first. Only the cover reader lines, the page 2 letter and the salutation differ. No "you said" sits over a quote that is not that edition's reader's own.
