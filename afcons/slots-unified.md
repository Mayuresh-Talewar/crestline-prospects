## Keys and caveat lines used on every page
- **Sources (short form, with filing dates).** AR26 = Annual Report 2025-26, made available to NSE/BSE 8 Jul 2026 (page = printed page). Q4R = audited FY26 results and auditors' reports, board meeting 18 May 2026. Q4PR = Q4/FY26 release, 18 May 2026. Q4T = Q4 FY26 call transcript, call 19 May 2026, filed 22 May 2026. Q1R and Q1PR = Q1 FY27 results and release, 7 Aug 2026. Q1T = Q1 FY27 call transcript, call 10 Aug 2026, filed 13 Aug 2026. RHP = prospectus dated 18 Oct 2024 (FY22 to FY24 restated). AR25 = Annual Report 2024-25, approved 23 May 2025 (exchange filing date NOT FOUND). "PDF p." = PDF page of the transcript.
- **Basis.** Consolidated, ₹ Cr, net unless marked gross. Revenue = revenue from operations 11,948.38 (AR26 p.327). Per day = 11,948.38 / 365 = ₹32.735 Cr.
- **Rate.** 7.55% to 9.95% a year, cash credit and working capital demand loans (AR26 PDF p.185, borrowings note; FY25 9.09% to 10.35%). Interest is saved only if the released cash repays those loans.
- **Margin.** Crestline does not fix margin. Interest sits below EBITDA: saving it does not move EBITDA or the EBITDA margin. It adds to profit before tax in full; profit after tax rises by less.
- **Ownership.** Afcons: measure, prepare, submit, escalate. Client: acknowledge, certify, pay.
- **The CFO's 120 days** is printed only in his words, credited to him, never as a target.
- Caveat lines (reused where a figure appears):
  - **[C-DAYS]** "Days are the balance divided by revenue per day (₹32.735 Cr). Afcons does not publish them. 123.4 is not a DSO: retention and ₹266.08 Cr of interest on arbitration awards sit inside receivables."
  - **[C-GST]** "Receivables typically include GST and revenue does not (not measured for Afcons), so receivable cash figures are conservative."
  - **[C-OVL]** "Rows overlap and are never added. Scenario, not a forecast."
  - **[C-RATE]** "Interest only if the cash repays cash credit or working capital loans, at 7.55% to 9.95% (AR26 PDF p.185)."
  - **[C-NWC]** "Prices net working capital days at revenue per day; on the company's own formula (Q4 FY25 investor presentation printed p.39, PDF p.40)."
  - **[C-CEIL]** "A ceiling: 27.0% of gross receivables is disputed and the allowance is ₹281.70 Cr."
  - **[C-PBT]** "Profit before tax ₹386.79 Cr is after a ₹76.51 Cr one-time labour-code charge; before it, ₹463.30 Cr, and the shares would be lower."
  - **[C-PAT]** "Pre-tax interest divided by profit after tax overstates the effect on profit after tax."
