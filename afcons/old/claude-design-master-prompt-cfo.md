## 1. What you are building

A 16-page A4 portrait booklet titled "Working-capital review", printed and posted to one reader: Mr. Ramesh Kumar Jha, Chief Financial Officer, Afcons Infrastructure Limited.
It answers one question for him: what if Crestline were already running at Afcons, how much money and how much time would it save?
Every word and every figure is supplied in section 4. Your job is layout, type and charts, built on the master design described in section 2.

## 3. Rules you must follow

1. Use ONLY the text and figures in section 4. Do not write new copy, captions, taglines, teasers, pull quotes or headings. If a slot has no text, leave it empty.
2. Copy every figure exactly as written: same digits, same decimals, same rupee sign, same "Cr". Do not round, re-total, convert or recompute anything.
3. Headlines exactly as given, split into two lines as in the headline table below. Never reword them.
4. Footnotes exactly as given, on the page they belong to. Where a footnote carries a tag in brackets, such as [C-DAYS], print the caveat line that tag stands for (listed under "Keys" in section 4) in its place, in full.
5. Text inside quotation marks in section 4 is printed copy. Words outside quotation marks (for example "Chart:", "Tiles:", "(9 words)", "(proposed, set at signing)", "Template fallback", "No scenario mark") are instructions to you and are never printed. The word counts in brackets after headlines are never printed.
6. Charts are drawn to scale: every bar, segment, column and point sits within 2% of its own value on one shared scale per chart, starting at the scale given in the content. Every bar shows its value label.
7. Anything marked "with Crestline", "scenario", "dashed" or "30 days less" is drawn dashed and hollow, with "Scenario, not a forecast." beside it. Charts the content marks as history only get no scenario mark.
8. No em dashes and no en dashes anywhere. Use a comma, a full stop, "to" or a middle dot (·) as the content does.
9. Make no claim about Crestline beyond the pasted text. Do not add certifications, hosting regions, encryption, client names, "live in X days", or any present-tense claim that Crestline connects to SAP.
10. The Afcons logo appears only if the client uploaded it, used as supplied. Never redraw, trace, recolour or imitate it. Without it, the cover shows the reader's name only and no Afcons mark.
11. Do not bring in any company, name, figure or design text from anywhere other than section 4. No other company's name or data appears in this booklet except where section 4 itself prints it (the one peer, on page 13, and names inside quoted sources).
12. Ownership words stay as written: the client certifies and pays; Afcons measures, prepares, submits and escalates. Do not swap them.
