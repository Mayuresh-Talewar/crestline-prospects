Cross-check (computed; internal cross-check, never printed together): standalone (receivables + contract assets) / revenue x 365 is 252.8 days (FY24) and 260.9 days (FY25) in data.md, against CRISIL's 255 and 264. Two independent routes agree within 3 days. Both exceed the company's own 94 and 90 NWC days because the company nets advances and payables, whose definition is not published.
Trigger distance (indicative only, definitions differ): the FY26 standalone ISCR in the company's own annexure is 4.33 (filing 11 Aug 2026 PDF p.15) and debt equity 0.35; CRISIL and India Ratings each use their own adjusted interest cover with a 4.0 upward level, so the comparison is a pointer, not a measurement. What neither agency has stated as met is a sustained reduction in the working-capital cycle.
