                                                                                                                               KPIL's animal welfare initiatives are directed towards
                                                                                                                               strengthening the animal protection ecosystem through
                                                                                                                               humane, responsive and timely interventions.

                                                                                                                               The initiatives undertaken focused on providing rescue,
                                                                                                                               care and assistance to animals in distress. This included
                                                                                                                               immediate rescue, medical support, and rehabilitation
                                                                                                                               for injured and abandoned animals, benefitting 7,400
                                                                                                                               animals. KPIL also donated an animal ambulance that
                                                                                                                               has strengthened the emergency response measures
                                                                                                                               and helped alleviate suffering during critical situations.

                                                                                                                               KPIL also supported Federation of Indian Animals
                                                                                                                               Protection Organisation in its efforts to strengthen
                                                                                                                               the capacities of grassroots organisations, groups,
                                                                                                                               and individuals by facilitating regular seminars and
                                                                                                                               workshops, while also contributing to its initiatives
                                                                                                                               aimed at policy advocacy and reforms in the area of
                                                                                                                               animal welfare.
