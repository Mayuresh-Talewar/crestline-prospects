As per the provisions of Income Tax Act, 2025 (hereinafter referred as `the Act'), Dividend is taxable in the hands of
shareholders and accordingly, the Company is required to withhold taxes (including surcharge and cess) on the
dividend paid to its shareholders at the prescribed rates as per the applicable provisions of the Act. The withholding tax
rate would vary depending on the residential status of the shareholder and documents submitted by shareholder with
the Company/ RTA / Depository Participant.
