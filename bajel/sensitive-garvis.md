# Bajel Projects: sensitive items (Garvis only, BJ-1)

Booklet-facing files cite these by number only. One line each, with source. Rs Cr figures come from src/bj-calc-output.txt.

- **S1 Arbitration receivables (auditor Emphasis of Matter).**
  - FY26: EoM on Note 6(d), "invoked / ongoing arbitration proceedings with respect to three of its customers"; Rs 85.95 Cr net of provision (AR26 p246, p281, image-checked).
  - FY25: the same EoM with two customers, Rs 50.20 Cr (AR25 p214).
  - Customers named in Reg 30 letters: Purvanchal Vidyut Vitran Nigam (notice invoking arbitration, filed 9 May 2025) and Madhyanchal Vidyut Vitran Nigam (filed 5 Feb 2025). The third customer was not found in filings.
- **S2 Key Audit Matter on receivables.**
  - AR26 p246 to 247: recoverability of undisputed receivables of Rs 1,816.30 Cr, net of an impairment allowance of Rs 53.21 Cr.
  - AR25 KAM: closed-project receivables of Rs 126.76 Cr net (AR25 p214 Note 4(e)).
- **S3 Books backup and audit trail.**
  - AR26 Note 48, p327, image-checked: "backup of the books of account... were not maintained for the period from April 1, 2025 to June 30, 2025", and the audit trail was "not enabled for certain changes made... using privileged/administrative access rights for the period from April 1, 2025 to January 21, 2026". Repeated in the auditor's report.
  - AR25 carries a similar privileged-access exception.
  - Do not infer a system change from this; no filing links it to any ERP.
- **S4 Demerger legacy, income tax.** TDS credit of Rs 4.05 Cr for AY 2023-24 was not granted because customers deducted TDS on the demerged parent's PAN; the company appealed on 19 Feb 2024 (Reg 30 filing, 20 Feb 2024).
- **S5 Demerger legacy, vendor dispute.** M/s Aar Ess & Co (Sushil Kumar Rout) claims Rs 2.00 Cr under a 2018 work order issued as "erstwhile Bajaj Electricals Limited". Bombay High Court appointed an arbitrator by an order received 23 Jan 2025 (Reg 30 filing, 24 Jan 2025).
- **S6 GST regulator actions.**
  - Karnataka: demand of Rs 2.39 Cr (24 Jan 2025), then a show cause notice of Rs 2.62 Cr including penalty (filing 18 Feb 2025).
  - Bihar: demand of Rs 3.91 Cr (13 Mar 2025), then a show cause notice of Rs 2.37 Cr (filing 22 Jul 2025).
  - All arise from a "mismatch of GSTR3B and GSTR7". The company says it has a strong case.
- **S7 Related parties.**
  - Material RPT with associate AnantGrid Projects One Pvt Ltd and its subsidiary Lakadia A Power Transmission Ltd: postal ballot notice 4 Sep 2026, passed per the voting results filed 5 Oct 2026.
  - Working capital facilities are "supported by comfort letter from Jamnalal Sons Pvt. Ltd." (AR26 p291).
  - Employee benefit reimbursement rights sit with related-party insurers (AR26 p283 to 284, Note 39(C)).
- **S8 Rating actions (no downgrade found).**
  - CRISIL long-term upgraded to A+/Stable on 5 Aug 2026 (filed 6 Aug 2026).
  - A/Stable reaffirmed on 19 Dec 2025 (filed 20 Dec 2025).
  - History shows "Watch Developing" on 20 Jun 2023, during the demerger (CR26 rating history).
- **S9 Payment holdback.** The CFO said "one marquee customer held back payments in the last fortnight of March of around INR225 crores" (TR4 p14). CRISIL says it was "recovered in April / May 2026" (CR26 p2). The customer is not named anywhere; do not name or imply one.
- **S10 Leadership turnover.**
  - CEO Sanjay Bhagat resigned with effect from 31 Mar 2024 (filed 6 Mar 2024).
  - CFO Binda Misra was succeeded by Nitesh Bhandari from 1 May 2025 (filed 4 Apr 2025).
  - Executive Director Ajay Suresh Nagle gave up the Company Secretary role in May 2026 and retired as ED on 31 Aug 2026 (filed 31 Aug 2026).
  - Never print Sanjay Bhagat, Binda Misra or Ajay Suresh Nagle as current officers. Nitesh Bhandari is current.
- **S11 Deceased director.** Independent Director Dr Rajendra Prasad Singh died on 16 Jul 2026 (NSE filing, 17 Jul 2026). He still appears in AR26 KMP and director lists, so never name him as a current director.
- **S12 Disclosure vintage.** FY25 receivable ageing differs between AR25 p213 ("Not Due" Rs 169.94 Cr) and AR26 p280 (Rs 882.77 Cr) on the same total. FY25 borrowings were restated from Rs 121.00 Cr to Rs 356.59 Cr (AR26 p327 Note 49). Disclosed, not a qualification, but a reader could see it as moving goalposts.
- **S13 Public claims that conflict.** "Maintaining a negative working capital cycle" (AR26 p71) against "Net working capital stood at 124 days" (TR4 p7, CFO). Raise only as a question, never as a gotcha.
