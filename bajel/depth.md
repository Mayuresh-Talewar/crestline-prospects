## 3. Disconfirm step

One search per headline fact, designed to prove it wrong.

1. **"Debtor days ballooned 136 to 214."**
   - Searched the later CRISIL rationale and the AR26 ageing table.
   - CRISIL calls it "a one-off event" (D2), and AR26 ageing shows gross overdue fell from Rs 448.34 Cr to Rs 399.35 Cr.
   - Result: the headline is contradicted as framed. The 213.6 days is real arithmetic on current receivables only.
2. **"Fresh SAP go-live."**
   - Scanned every filing in src/d: 0 hits (planted hit found).
   - The SAP vendor page answers 403; its search snippet dates the ERP need to the Sep 2023 demerger.
   - Result: unsupported, and likely stale.
3. **"Borrowings Rs 367 Cr."**
   - Read Note 18 and Note 49: Rs 318.96 Cr is vendor bill discounting. FY25 was restated from Rs 121.00 Cr.
   - Result: the total is right, but it is mostly supplier finance, not bank working capital debt.
4. **"Cash from operations fell to Rs 21 Cr."**
   - Checked the consolidated cash flow (same, Rs 21.09 Cr) and FY24 (Rs 195.37 Cr).
   - Result: holds, and the three-year trend is worse than the dossier says.
5. **"Order book Rs 4,055 Cr."**
   - Cross-checked against AR26 unsatisfied obligations (Rs 3,441.82 Cr at 31 Mar 2026) and CRISIL's Q1 inflow "over ~Rs 1098 crores".
   - Result: holds.
6. **"Negative working capital cycle" (company's own claim).**
   - Tested against the CFO's 124 days and the balance sheet.
   - Result: contradicted inside the company's own documents (S13).
7. **Retention as the driver.**
   - Searched all ARs for a retention balance: NOT FOUND. Only the CFO's spoken Rs 100 Cr increase and CRISIL's qualitative line exist.
   - Result: real, but not measurable from filings.
