# depth.md - Relcon (RC-1)

Respect first: a 50-year Mumbai contractor (1973) with an unusually clean balance sheet (gearing 0.16x, interest cover 21.6x, networth Rs 346 Cr, FY24), two CRISIL upgrades in 15 months (BBB- to BBB to BBB+), an order book of ~Rs 2,456 Cr (30 Sep 2024) and fund-based limits only 21% used (CRISIL 13 Dec 2024).

The question: with that strength, gross current assets still stood at 199 days at 31 Mar 2024 (200-266 over the past three fiscals), and CRISIL expects the working capital cycle to stay around 200 days, driven by debtors, retention and deposits given to customers. Which bills, at which MCGM / MMRDA / MRVC desk, wait, and since when? The accounts cannot say. CRISIL's own upgrade trigger is GCA below 190 days.

Tone: a company that does not need the money; the offer is visibility, not rescue.
Limit: no DSO split, no retention figure, so no rupee value for a day. Supports a light booklet only.
