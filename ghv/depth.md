## Limits and utilisation
Fund-based utilisation 66% -> 65% -> 78% -> 87% (peak 99%) 2023-26; non-fund-based (BG/LC: 1,354 of 1,688 rated) 85%. Sanctioned fund-based limit cut 173 -> 166 -> 97 (Oct-24 to Jan-26) at the group's request; CRISIL notes part of the high utilisation is that cut.

## Triggers (11-Feb-26 § Rating sensitivity)
Downgrade: net cash accrual below Rs 175 cr sustained; falling order-book ratio; "stretch in the working capital cycle, resulting in higher debt". Upgrade: NCA above Rs 200 cr; sustained WC cycle. Negative outlook tied to "susceptibility to variation in working capital cycle leading to higher-than-expected reliance on debt". Watched: debt ~Rs 400 cr by Mar-26, utilisation staying near 90%.
