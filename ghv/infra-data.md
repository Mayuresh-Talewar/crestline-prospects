## 1. How GHV Infra came into the group (primary: AR FY25 PDF p27, p32; oo/poa.pdf; oo/pfa.pdf)
- Incorporated 19-Mar-1976 as Sindu Valley Technologies Ltd (AR FY25 PDF p35). Before: promoter seller Bhadra Paper Mills sold 5,14,860 shares on SPA of 7-May-24 to Chirag Dedhia, Arvind Sharma, Manisha Sharma (AR FY25 PDF p27).
- **18-Oct-24:** SPA by Mr Jahidmohmed H. Vijapura (Acquirer-1) with those three sellers for 5,14,860 shares (3.28% of the emerging capital) at Rs 30. Same day the Board approved a preferential issue of 1,50,00,000 shares. Open offer by Mr Jahidmohmed H. Vijapura + JHV Commercials LLP (Acquirer-2), PAC Mrs Husena Musamji: up to 40,82,000 shares (28.32%) at Rs 30 (Rs 12.246 Cr), offer 18-Feb to 5-Mar-25. **No shares were tendered.** Acquirers + PAC held 1,06,64,860 shares = **73.98%** after (oo/pfa.pdf item 7).
- JHV Commercials LLP "has control" of the company; GHV (India) Pvt Ltd and GHV-MHK (JV) are "entities where promoters have significant influence", w.e.f. 12-Dec-24 (AR FY25 PDF p76 / printed p82; FY26 AR p123). So GHV Infra was acquired by Mr Jahid Vijapura personally and through his LLP, not by GHV (India) Pvt Ltd. Mrs Husena Musamji reclassified promoter to public 25-Sep-26 (ann/...Change-in-Directorate-_250926).

## 6. Reconciliation with data.md (CRISIL)
- **"Combined" is not GHV India + GHV Infra only.** CRISIL 11-Feb-26 fully consolidates GHV India, GHV Infra and several JVs, and moderately consolidates other subsidiaries, associates and JVs (Annexure, list of 20-odd entities). So the CRISIL figures cannot be rebuilt from two entities.
- **Sum test, FY25 (computed):** combined op income 2,431 less GHV Infra 184.88 = 2,246; combined PAT 94 less 17.15 = 76.9. CRISIL's own text uses 2,428, 2,431 and "2,248" for FY25, so the residual 2,246 is within the typo range of "2,248" but this is circumstantial: GHV India's own FY25 standalone is NOT FOUND (private, no filing), and CRISIL does not state whether GHV India's Rs 110.5 Cr of billing to GHV Infra is eliminated. Verdict: does NOT tie as a proven sum; GHV Infra is 7.6% of combined revenue and 18.2% of combined PAT.
- **Ties:** UAE = 21% of the Rs 12,717 Cr combined order book at 30-Sep-25 (= Rs 2,671 Cr) vs Rs 2,645 Cr in the filing (Verified GHV Infra's; the share is gone since Sep-26). Maharashtra 45% / Gujarat 15% are GHV India work (Mumbai roads, viaducts, FGD in Gujarat) sub-contracted to GHV Infra, so the same rupee can sit in both order books (GHV Infra's own 31-Mar-25 book Rs 2,899 Cr "all sub-contracted from GHV India" per Acuite, data.md). Do not add GHV India's book and GHV Infra's book.
- **Does not tie:** CRISIL "debt 843 combined (FY25)" cannot be split; GHV Infra's FY25 debt was 30.9 Cr, all owed to GHV India (intra-group). Authum "140 Cr OCD" vs filings (Flags 2).
- **Now Verified as GHV Infra's (not GHV India's):** the UAE order and its conclusion; Jalna-Nanded Rs 1,250 Cr expressway; Ductor USD 90.25M; Cameroon EUR 630M LOI; order book 9,100 Cr (Mar-26); revenue 605.53 Cr / PAT 42.26 Cr FY26; the Rs 150 Cr Authum term loan; receivables are 100% related-party; ratings upgrade (Acuite BBB- to A/Stable, A3 to A1, 1-Jul-26, ann/...Credit-Rating).
