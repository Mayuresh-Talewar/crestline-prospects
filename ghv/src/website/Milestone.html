GHV

Milestone

    • Established in 1965

      Road Construction projects all over Gujarat

    •   1977-Ventured into irrigation (Pannam Irrigation Project, Gujarat). This major work completion of time infused confidence in the team.

        1981-Hot mix plant was introduced by Apollo a new technology at that period of time; it was purchased by GHV to be utilized to add quality to its work.

        1982-Surat Municipal Corporation / Lucknow Municipal Corporation awarded road work.

        1984-Mr. Hussain Vijapura ventures into the Hotel Business, started with budget Hotels.

    •   1982-1994 Surat Municipal Corporation / Lucknow Municipal Corporation road work completed successfully.

        1990- To strengthen Mr. Hussain hands, his children entered into their family business. This gave a great boast to the business. Their new energy with innovative ideas combined with Mr. Hussain Vijapura expertise and rich experience gave the organization the required energy to leap to the next phase of business. The children got their experience from their father, who was a hard taskmaster. Their knowledge acquired was from on job training on field.

        1994-GHV strategy for business expansion was by entering into Joint ventures. Their efforts were paid off by getting projects in Nepal.

    •   1996-1997 Roads Construction projects completed successfully in Nepal Hilitorian area.

        2001-During this year a Challenging job came to GHV which it grabbed. In 75 days period 2 lac cubic meter earthwork and 20000 cubic meters concrete lining had to be build. GHV strength in itself was visibly seen by the manner the work was completed. GHV took up this work and completed in 70 days.

        2001-Gujarat Earthquake was a big challenge to the organization to the very belief in its strength of quality time bound work and its Social commitment. GHV as a company was able to cope up as team in Social engineering and rehabilitation (World Bank Aided Project).

        2004- GHV went for further expansions. It entered into Airport Construction sector with Runway, Parking and Taxiway for Mumbai International Airport, Vadodara Airport, Pune Airport, Udaipur Airport, Patna Airport and Guwahati Airport.
