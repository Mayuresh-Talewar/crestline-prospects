ABOUT GHV

"GHV Group is known for its technological and engineering excellence in the field of infrastructure. Found in 1965, GHV is amongst the fastest growing group having a diversified portfolio in Infrastructure & Hospitality. The Group provides integrated Engineering Procurement and Construction (EPC) services for infrastructure sector with operations diversified across Highways, Railways, Buildings and Water & Irrigation verticals".

  Mr.JAHID VIJAPURA

Group Chairman

GROUP CHAIRMAN SPEAKS

Over the years GHV Group has built numerous projects across the Country. Quality, timely completion and commitment have helped GHV strengthen its place in the league of large Infrastructure companies.

When we look at our past performance, we continued to go further to meet the needs of our Clients, the challenges of the industry and the issues confronting the world. The efforts executed collectively by every single professional in our organization have resulted into enormous growth and tangible outcomes.

All of these instill in us the confidence to ride the next wave of growth.
