Social Responsibility The Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) policy of GHV Group is aligned with its overall commitment towards maintaining the highest standards of business ethics. CSR Strategy Group contributes to various social welfare activities to provide a better and sustainable way of life to the poor.It provides formal, free education and financial help to the needy. As part of infrastructure development, it has built bore wells in several villages in India and helped locals to overcome water scarcity. As part of the "Chief Minister Scheme of Plantation", it has conducted tree plantation drives in various parts of Madhya Pradesh. GHV (India) is also part of the Health Awareness Campaign 2017, as part of which it organises awareness camps and provides free medical attention to people from economically weaker sections of the society. Environmental GHV (India) complies with all applicable regulatory requirements. Its aim is to be at the forefront of the industry and reduce the adverse impact on the environment. In order to achieve this, it follows strict policies on a daily basis while operating its business.
