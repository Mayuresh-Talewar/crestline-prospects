        GHV Infra Projects Bags 109 Crore Civil & MEP Work Order in Jamshedpur

Strengthens Presence in Key Industrial Hub with Comprehensive Building, MEP, and
Finishing Scope

Mumbai, December 15, 2025: GHV Infra Projects Ltd. ("GHV Infra"), a fast-growing EPC
player in India's infrastructure and construction sector, announced that it has recently secured
a significant 109 crore work order in Jamshedpur, Jharkhand. The project has been awarded
by a reputed group entity and covers end-to-end civil construction, MEP (Mechanical,
Electrical & Plumbing) systems, and high-quality finishing works for a large building
development.

The new contract reinforces the company's expanding footprint in industrially strategic
regions such as Jamshedpur an established hub for manufacturing, engineering, and ancillary
industries. Infrastructure spending in these zones has accelerated in recent years, driven by
industrial capacity expansion and urban-industrial growth. The project will be executed over
a period of 30 months, with stringent quality, safety, and delivery benchmarks integrated into
the execution plan.

This win further strengthens GHV Infra's order book and reflects the increasing confidence of
marquee clients in the company's technical expertise, integrated capabilities, and project
management precision. The scope covering both heavy civil works and specialized MEP
systems showcases GHV Infra's ability to undertake multidisciplinary turnkey assignments.

In recent financial results, GHV Infra Projects reported strong momentum with over 125%
growth in revenue and more than 135% rise in net profit year-on-year for Q2 FY 2025­26. The
significant improvement in operating performance and steady project inflows have reinforced
market confidence in the company's growth trajectory.

Speaking on the development, Mr. Ajay Hans, Managing Director, GHV Infra Projects, said:
"Securing this 109 crore order in Jamshedpur is a strong endorsement of our execution
pedigree and our clients' trust in our multidisciplinary capabilities. Civil construction and
MEP integration are critical components of modern infrastructure development, and this
project allows us to demonstrate our engineering depth, safety-first approach, and timely
delivery culture. As industrial hubs across India continue to expand, we are committed to
partnering in this growth with reliable, high-quality infrastructure solutions."

The announcement comes at a time when GHV Infra is witnessing robust business
momentum. This new mandate aligns with the company's strategy of scaling its presence in
high-potential industrial corridors. It particularly strengthens its position in regions with
rising demand for integrated civil and MEP execution expertise.
