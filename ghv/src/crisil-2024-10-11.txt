			Update on GHV (India) Private Limited | 
			  | 
Note: None of the Directors on CRISIL Ratings Limited’s Board are members of rating committee and thus do not participate in discussion or assignment of any ratings. The Board of Directors also does not discuss any ratings at its meetings.
CRISIL Rating's 'CRISIL A/Stable/CRISIL A1' rating on the bank facilities of GHV (India) Pvt. Ltd (GHV) remains unaffected by reporting of its accounts by one of its bankers, Central Bank of India (CBI, the bank) under special mention account (SMA).
The reporting, done on August 1, 2024, was related to a trade finance facility of Rs 30 crore availed by the company from CBI. The payment for these concerned accounts were due on April 10, 2024, May 9, 2024 and May 12, 2024 and was paid by GHV before due date and in full.
Once the company became aware of the SMA reporting, a virtual joint lenders meet was called on August 30, 2024, seeking appropriate clarification. In the meeting, CBI confirmed that the payment was made by GHV within the due date and in full, and SMA reporting was attributable to technical issues. 
CRISIL Ratings has received a copy of the minutes of this meeting signed by the lead banker, Bank of Baroda, as well as all the other lenders of the company. Subsequently, the account was reclassified as standard with effect from August 28, 2024.
GHV continues to have adequate liquidity to ensure timely debt servicing. Unencumbered cash and bank balance and un-utilised bank lines stood at Rs 20 crore and Rs 30 crore, respectively, as on October 4, 2024.
GHV’s ratings continue to reflect the extensive experience of its promoters in the construction segment, improving scale of operations and adequate financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by limited geographical as well as revenue diversification and exposure to intense competition and cyclicality inherent in the construction industry.
