## p3 Chapter 1, what CRISIL is watching

> H: What CRISIL is watching: debt, cash accrual and the working-capital cycle
> GHV Group describes itself as building since 1965 (the company's own statement). GHV (India) Private Limited was incorporated in 2009 as the successor to the family firm GH Vijapura & Co (CRISIL, 11 Feb 2026).
> Debt around ₹400 Cr by 31 March 2026. CRISIL wrote that debt "may be brought down further to around Rs 400 crore by March 31, 2026", and called it a key rating sensitivity. This is CRISIL's expectation, not a commitment by the company. GHV (India) and GHV Infra combined, CRISIL 11 Feb 2026.
> A downgrade trigger CRISIL names: "stretch in the working capital cycle, resulting in higher debt". Same label.
> Net cash accrual: a downgrade if below ₹175 Cr on a sustained basis, an upgrade if above ₹200 Cr. Same label.
> Fund-based utilisation: averaged 87% over twelve months to January 2026, peaked at 99% in Q2 FY26, and was 90% in Q3 FY26. Same label.
> The working-capital cycle sits inside both the downgrade and the upgrade triggers.
> Footnote: the combined-reporting line.
