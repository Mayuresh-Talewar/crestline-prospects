# Problems (respect first): New Consolidated Construction Company Limited

Respect: building since 1946 (company site); operating income Rs 610.57 Cr FY25 against Rs 572.76 Cr FY24; order book Rs 2,183.86 Cr at 1 Jan 2026 (Acuite, 3 Feb 2026).

1. **Bills wait 100 days to be paid** (98 in FY24; Acuite, 3 Feb 2026, standalone, day base not stated). At Rs 610.57 Cr / 365 = Rs 1.67 Cr a day, ten days are Rs 16.73 Cr and thirty are Rs 50.18 Cr, released once (Crestline's arithmetic, scenario). The accounts cannot say which bill, site or desk, or since when. Ours: measure, prepare, review, submit, invoice. Client's: acknowledge, certify, pay.
2. **Retention of Rs 92.39 Cr** (Rs 102.84 Cr FY24; Acuite, 3 Feb 2026). It fell by Rs 10.45 Cr; it still equals 55.2 days of operating income (Crestline's arithmetic). The release basis is not published, so no release is priced. The accounts cannot say which bill's retention is due back, or when.
3. **Stock of 171 days** (164 FY24; Acuite, 3 Feb 2026), shuttering about 38% of inventory. No cost of materials is published, so it is shown and not priced.
