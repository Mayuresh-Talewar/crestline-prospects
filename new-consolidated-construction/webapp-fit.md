# Web app fit: New Consolidated Construction Company Limited (NC2-S23)

Source for every figure: Acuite Ratings and Research rationale, 3 Feb 2026, standalone FY25 (re-fetched raw 2026-10-12). No ERP is named in any primary source, so nothing says what the company runs; the pack says "ERP".

| Crestline screen | Fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Working capital, cash-cycle diagram | Partial | Stock days 171 and debtor days 100 are published; no creditor days, so no total |
| O2C, bill timeline and process map | Strong | Debtor days 100 anchor the payment wait; other stages are estimates, tagged |
| Retention tracking | Strong | Retention Rs 92.39 Cr FY25 (Rs 102.84 Cr FY24) is a published balance; release basis is not |
| Client screen | Partial | Named clients and mix are published; terms and ageing are not |
| Vendor and P2P screens | Not evidenced | No creditor days, payables, MSME or vendor terms published |
| Cycle simulator | One lever | Only receivables can be priced (revenue per day); no client term, so no floor applies |
| Cash movement, trend panels | No data | No cash flow statement or multi-year series on one basis |

Pilot scope: one to three EPC projects, read-only, once the adapter for the company's ERP is built.
