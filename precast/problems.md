# Precast India Infrastructures: problems (step 2, LIGHT, 3 problems)

Rules: CRISIL facts only (CRISIL Ratings, 4 Nov 2020 and 3 Aug 2023, standalone). Respectful. No number invented, no day, no saving, no interest. A with-Crestline mark names WHAT would be measured and never a figure. Counter-evidence is stated first: on the only rating view, money is not obviously trapped.

## Problem 1. A cycle funded by others is owed to others, on a date
Fact: "efficiently managed working capital cycle, which is funded by advances from customers and creditors, leading to minimal working capital debt" (CRISIL, 3 Aug 2023). Bank limit use about 3% on average, 12 months to March 2023 (CRISIL, 3 Aug 2023). 5.7% in the 12 months to July 2020 (CRISIL, 4 Nov 2020).
Reading: this is a strength. The question it leaves is project level: how much of each advance is earned out by work, and which creditor is due when. CRISIL lists "sizeable stretch in the working capital cycle" among its downward rating factors (3 Aug 2023).
Not knowable from the record: any amount of advances or creditors.
With Crestline (measured, no figure): advance received against work earned out, by project; creditor due date against payment date, by vendor; owner named.

## Problem 2. When execution waits, billing waits
Fact: revenue 130.92 (FY21), 102.81 (FY22) operating income, 104.8 (FY23) revenue, Rs crore. For FY23 CRISIL says revenue "remained modest due to lower booking in the fourth quarter of fiscal 2023 amid delay in execution of projects in hand on account of final changes in designs" (3 Aug 2023).
Reading: a delay on site reaches the bill. The record cannot say which project or which stage.
Not knowable: size of any delay, whose move it was.
With Crestline (measured, no figure): work done to bill raised, by project; the step of the bill that holds the wait; whose move ends it.

## Problem 3. Work in hand is a dated view
Fact: confirmed order book about Rs 120 Cr to be executed in 6 to 9 months (CRISIL, 4 Nov 2020); "Healthy order book" with no figure (CRISIL, 3 Aug 2023).
Reading: the last figure is almost six years old at the date of the booklet. Work in hand becomes cash only after it is done, billed and paid.
With Crestline (measured, no figure): for each order in hand, the days from work done to bill and from bill to cash, and who holds the wait.

KEPT OUT (not used anywhere): rating withdrawal and non-cooperation, ownership history, promoters, named clients, aggregator figures including Rs 158 Cr, PAT margins, interest coverage, any leader name.
