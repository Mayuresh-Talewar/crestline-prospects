Detailed rationale

CRISIL Ratings has reaffirmed its ‘CRISIL BBB/Stable/CRISIL A3+’ ratings on the bank loan facilities of Precast India Infrastructures Pvt Ltd (PIIPL).

 

The ratings continue to reflect the extensive experience of the promoters in the precast construction industry and the comfortable financial risk profile of PIIPL. These strengths are partially offset by improving, yet modest, scale of operations.

Key rating drivers and detailed description

Strengths:

  • Extensive experience of the promoters: The promoters have about three decades of experience in the construction industry. They started with the conventional construction contracting business in 1992 and ventured into precast construction in 2009-2010. Their technological capabilities, successful track record and healthy relationships with customers have helped the company grow its business over the years. PIIP was one of the early entrants into the precast construction business in India and has completed several marquee projects across Pune for players such as the Panchsheel group, DLF, Infosys and Cognizant. Its successful track record and credibility in the niche sector, driven by successful execution of over 200 projects of various sizes, support its market position. A reputed clientele and steady flow of repeat orders should continue to support the business.

 

  • Comfortable financial risk profile: The financial risk profile will remain supported by healthy profitability and efficiently managed working capital cycle, which is funded by advances from customers and creditors, leading to minimal working capital debt. Total outside liabilities to tangible networth ratio is estimated at 0.37 time as on March 31, 2023. The company will invest in land over the medium term, which will be funded through internal accrual and no fresh debt will be undertaken.

 

Debt protection metrics were strong, as indicated by interest coverage and net cash accrual to adjusted debt ratios of 14.45 times and 0.91 time, respectively, in fiscal 2023 majorly because of limited reliance on external debt. The financial risk profile is expected to remain comfortable over the medium term.

 

Weakness:

  • Improving, yet modest, scale of operations: Slabs, beams, columns and other construction items are casted in the company’s factory and then transported to the site. The high logistics cost results in the company mostly implementing its projects within vicinity of its facility, thereby restricting scalability. Thus, revenue was average at Rs 104.8 crore in fiscal 2023. Revenue remained modest due to lower booking in the fourth quarter of fiscal 2023 amid delay in execution of projects in hand on account of final changes in designs. The execution of these pending orders will lead to higher revenue in the current year. Healthy order book provides adequate revenue visibility over the medium term. Also, expected investment in mobile precast facilities should enable the company to scale up considerably. Sustenance of healthy operating efficiency, while scaling up operations, will remain critical and will be monitored.

Liquidity: Adequate

Bank limit utilisation was low at ~3% on average for the 12 months through March 2023. Cash accrual is expected to be Rs 16-23 crore against nil term debt obligation over the medium term, and the surplus will cushion the liquidity of the company.

 

Current ratio was healthy at 2.64 times as on March 31, 2022. Low gearing and moderate networth support its financial flexibility and provides the financial cushion required in case of any adverse conditions or downturn in the business.

Outlook: Stable

PIIPL should continue to benefit from the extensive experience of its promoters, reputed clientele and established market position.

Rating sensitivity factors

Upward factors:

  • Healthy revenue growth with steadily improving operating margin leading to cash accrual of Rs 30 crore or above
  • Sustained and comfortable financial and liquidity risk profiles

 

Downward factors:

  • Lower-than-expected revenue growth resulting in cash accrual below Rs 14 crore
  • Large, debt-funded capital expenditure (capex) or sizeable stretch in the working capital cycle weakening the capital structure

About the company

PIIPL was incorporated in December 2009 as a 50-50 joint venture between the Panchshil group of Pune and Bhate & Raje Construction Company Pvt Ltd (BRCCPL). Later, the promoters of BRCCPL acquired a majority stake in the company. PIIPL, based in Pune, manufactures pre-engineered concrete structures used in the construction of industrial, commercial and residential buildings.
