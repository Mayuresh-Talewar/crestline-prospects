Detailed Rationale

CRISIL has reaffirmed its 'CRISIL BBB/Positive/CRISIL A3+' ratings on the bank facilities of Precast India Infrastructures Pvt Ltd (PIIPL).
 
The ratings continue to reflect the extensive experience of the promoters in the precast construction industry and PIIPL's comfortable financial risk profile backed by healthy operating efficiencies. These strengths are partially offset by improving-yet-average scale of operations.
 
PIIPL recorded revenue of Rs. 128.11 crore in fiscal 2020, a 18% growth over previous year. Its operating margin continued to remain healthy at over 15% during fiscal 2020. The company's operations were not significantly impacted by COVID19, as it commenced its factory operations in May 2020. The company has recorded revenue of Rs.59 crore in H1-2021 and has confirmed order book of about Rs.120 crore to be executed in next 6-9 months. Further the company has two orders worth Rs.285 crore in pipeline, which are likely to commence in last quarter of this fiscal. The confirmed orders in hand and pipeline of orders provide strong revenue visibility.
 
Financial risk profile remains comfortable, with no major reliance on external debt, healthy cash accruals and surplus liquidity in the form of cash and FDs of Rs. 29.27crore as on March 31, 2020.

Analytical Approach

Preference shares from promoters have been treated as neither debt nor equity.

Key Rating Drivers & Detailed Description
Strengths 
* Extensive experience of promoters
The promoters have about three decades of experience in the construction industry. They are technocrats, who started conventional construction contracting business in 1992 and ventured into precast construction in 2009-10. Their technological capabilities and successful track record developed in the region and healthy relations with customers has helped the company to grow its business over the years. The company was one of the early entrants into precast construction business in India and has completed several marquee projects across Pune for players such as the Panchsheel group, DLF, Infosys, and Cognizant. Its successful track record and credibility in the niche sector, driven by successful execution of over 200 projects of various sizes support its market position. A reputed clientele and steady flow of repeat orders should continue to support the business.
 
* Comfortable financial risk profile
Financial risk profile should remain supported by healthy profitability and absence of any major, debt-funded capital expenditure (capex); also, most of the working capital requirement is funded by advances from customers and creditors, leading to limited debt. Gearing and total outside liabilities to tangible networth ratio were 0.06 time and 0.83 time, respectively, as on March 31, 2020; the ratios are projected at below 1 time over the medium term as well. Debt protection metrics were strong, with interest coverage and net cash accrual to adjusted debt ratios of 7.17 times and 3.73 time, respectively, in fiscal 2020.
 
* Healthy operating efficiencies
PIIPL uses the precast technology, which has more benefits such as lower construction time, uniform quality and limited labor requirement, as compared to conventional construction. Thus, operating profitability margin has been strong at over 15% during the three fiscals through 2020 and is likely to remain stable going forward as well. Return on capital employed ratio was 15.3% in fiscal 2020 and should exceed 19% over the medium term.
 
Weakness
* Improving-yet-average scale of operations
Currently, slabs, beams, columns and other construction items are casted in PIIPL's factory and then transported to the site; the high logistics cost leads to the company majorly implementing projects within its vicinity, thereby restricting scalability. Thus, revenue was average at Rs 128 crore in fiscal 2020 and is projected at just over Rs 145 crore in fiscal 2021. However, expected investment in mobile precast over the medium term should enable the company to scale-up considerably. Sustained track record of scale up in operations while maintaining healthy operating efficiencies remain critical and will be monitored.
Liquidity Adequate

Liquidity is likely to remain healthy. Absence of any yearly debt obligation permits the entire cash accrual, projected at Rs 18-21 crore in fiscals 2021 and 2022, to aid financial flexibility. Bank limit of Rs 5 crore was merely utilised at an average of 5.7% over the 12 months through July 2020. Further, unencumbered cash and bank balance were strong at Rs 29.27 crore as on March 31, 2020. The company also receives advances from customers and monthly bills that help it to comfortably complete projects without much dependence on fund-based bank lines.

Outlook: Positive

PIIPL should continue to benefit from the extensive experience of its promoters, reputed clientele and established market position.

Rating Sensitivity factors
Upward factors
* Revenue growth of 25% per annum and steady operating margin
* Sustained and comfortable financial and liquidity risk profiles
 
Downward factors
* Lower-than-expected revenue growth, resulting in cash accrual below Rs 15 crore
* Large, debt-funded capex or sizeable stretch in working capital cycle weakening capital structure
About the Company

PIIPL was incorporated in December 2009 as a 50-50 joint venture between the Panchshil group of Pune and Bhate & Raje Construction Company Pvt Ltd (BRCCPL). Later on, the promoters of BRCCPL acquired majority stake in the company. PIIPL, based in Pune, manufactures pre-engineered concrete structures used in construction of industrial, commercial and residential buildings.
