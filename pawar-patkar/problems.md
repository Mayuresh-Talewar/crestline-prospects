# Problems (PP-2, 2026-10-09). Respect first, then the question.

Respect: a Nashik-region contractor, a partnership in 1991 and a private limited company since 2006, Class 1A (Acuite Ratings rationale, 1 September 2025). Operating income 235.81 Cr (FY23), 500.35 Cr (FY24), 552.35 Cr (FY25, provisional); PAT 27.67 Cr (FY24), 32.95 Cr (FY25, provisional). First rating assigned 1 September 2025: ACUITE A-/Stable and A2+. All standalone. Source for every figure below: Acuite Ratings rationale, 1 September 2025, sections Key Financials, Key Rating Drivers, Moderate working capital operations. FY25 is provisional wherever printed.

## 1. The days nearly doubled in a year
- Gross current asset days: 55 (FY24) to 106 (FY25, provisional). Computed: 51 days more, 106 / 55 = 1.93x (92.7% more).
- Revenue over the same year: 500.35 to 552.35 Cr, 1.104x (10.4% more). Both standalone, same document.
- Acuite Ratings' reading: debtor and inventory days rose on high year-end billing and purchases (no figures given for either).
- Accounts cannot say: which site, which bill, which desk, since when.
- Crestline answer: Working Capital screen, one leg at revenue per day.

## 2. Nobody can yet see whose move ends each wait
- The rationale gives one number, 106 days, with no split between billed, unbilled and stock. Suppliers are on back-to-back arrangements tied to billing milestones, so when a bill moves decides when cash goes out as well as when it comes in.
- Crestline answer: O2C stages with the owner of each (company or client) and no days drawn, since step waits are not public.

## 3. A larger book to execute, on a longer clock
- Order book over 1,400 Cr at 30 June 2025, all Maharashtra, work for public authorities (MIDC, Maharashtra Housing Development Corporation Ltd, Maharashtra State Police Housing & Welfare Corporation Ltd, named by Acuite Ratings). Printed only as "over ₹1,400 Cr at 30 Jun 2025".
- Question, never a criticism: as that book is executed, which desk is each bill waiting on?
- Value of the question: one day = 552.35 / 365 = 1.513 Cr; ten days 15.13 Cr; thirty days 45.40 Cr (calc/pp-savings-output.txt). Scenario, not a forecast. Rows overlap.
