# Webapp fit: Pawar Patkar Constructions Private Limited (PP-2, 2026-10-09, verdict LIGHT)

Not usable: interest (no rate disclosed), unbilled vs billed split, ageing, EBITDA, creditor or inventory days, any named finance leader, address, logo, ERP.
Ownership in every wait: measure, prepare, review, submit and invoice are Pawar Patkar's; acknowledge, certify and pay are the client's. Client delay is never counted as theirs.
Payables never release cash: the calc asserts a stretch of vendor credit lands at today's figure with zero cash.
