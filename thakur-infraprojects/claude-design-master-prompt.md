## 1. What you are building

A 16-page A4 portrait booklet titled "Working-capital review", printed and posted to the named reader at Thakur Infraprojects Private Limited. You build ONE design and produce one edition per reader listed in the reader table (see "Reader editions" below).
It answers one question for the reader: what if Crestline were already running at Thakur Infraprojects, how much money and how much time would it save?
Every word and every figure is supplied in the Content section at the end of this prompt. This prompt carries content only. The design is yours to make.
The Crestline logo files supplied are used as supplied and are never redrawn. No Thakur Infraprojects logo is supplied: the company name is shown as text, and no company mark is made.

## 2. Reader editions

Build ONE 16-page design. Then, for each row of the reader table in the Content section, produce one edition of it.
- An edition changes ONLY three things: the cover's "PREPARED EXCLUSIVELY FOR" lines (that reader's name and title), the page 2 letter (that reader's letter text and address block), and the page 2 salutation.
- Every other page, word, figure and chart is IDENTICAL across editions. Do not tailor any body page to a reader.
- Name each edition after its reader as given in the table (for example "Edition: <reader name>"). That label is for the file only and is never printed.
- Body copy is written in a neutral voice. Quotes are credited as the Content gives them; never rewrite a credit into "you" for any edition.

## 3. Rules you must follow

1. Use ONLY the text and figures in the Content section. Do not write new copy, captions, taglines, teasers, pull quotes or headings. If an item has no text, leave it empty.
2. Copy every figure exactly as written: same digits, same decimals, same currency sign, same unit. Do not round, re-total, convert or recompute anything.
3. Headlines exactly as given for each page, split into two lines where the page needs it. Never reword them.
4. Footnotes exactly as given, on the page they belong to.
5. Lines starting with ">" are printed copy, exactly as written. Lines starting with "Data", "With Crestline", "Revenue by year", "Panel" or "Chart name" give the labels and values of the chart, panel or page they sit under; print each label and value as written. Lines that tell you what to do or not to do (for example "Never compute a figure that is not given here", "No numbers on this page", "Do not change, add or price any stage day") are instructions and are never printed.
6. No internal references in printed text. File names, ticket ids, checker notes and any note addressed to the team are never printed. A source is printed only in the words the Content gives for it.
7. No printed negatives about the booklet itself: never print what is missing, unknown, unchecked or left out ("not available", "we could not find", "TBD", "placeholder"). Where an item is empty, leave it empty. Print "Not public" or "Not published" only where the Content gives it as copy, and print nothing more for that item.
8. No em dashes and no en dashes anywhere. Use a comma, a full stop, "to" or a middle dot (·) as the Content does.
9. Ownership words stay as written: the client certifies and pays; Thakur Infraprojects measures, prepares, reviews, submits and invoices. Do not swap them.
10. The Crestline logo is used as supplied. Never redraw, trace or imitate it.

### Booklet accuracy rules (apply to every page)
A1. **Claims about Crestline must be true today.** Make no claim about Crestline beyond the pasted text. No certification "in progress", no hosting region, no encryption claim, no client names, no "live in X days", and no present-tense claim that Crestline connects to the company's ERP. The pilot is one to three projects, read-only, 14 days, as the Content words it.
A2. **Every quote names the right speaker**, exactly as the Content credits it. Never move a quote to another source.
A3. **Ours versus the client's follows the product rule.** In cash-in steps, acknowledged, certified and collected are the client's acts; measuring, preparing, reviewing, submitting and invoicing are the company's own. Keep every ownership tag as given.
A4. **No "nothing went wrong" headlines.** Use only the headlines given; never soften or invert one.
A5. **One basis per chart.** Every value is shown as given, each with its label. Nothing is made from a figure that is not in the Content.
A6. **No interest is priced.** The Content gives no borrowing rate and no interest figure. Never compute, label or add one.
A7. **Savings are scenarios.** Anything marked "With Crestline" or "Scenario" is shown as a scenario, carries the words "Scenario, not a forecast." and is never added to another scenario. Print no total the Content does not give.
A8. **Peers and comparatives** appear only where the Content prints them. None is printed here.
A9. **No other company's data.** Do not bring in any company, name, figure or text from anywhere other than the Content, including from earlier booklets or your memory. No demo numbers beside the company's figures.
A10. **Full address and correct names and titles** on the letter page and cover, exactly as the reader table gives them.
A11. **"You said" only for the reader's own words.** Never print "you said" or "in your own words" over words that are not the reader's own.
A12. **Never invent figures to fill a chart.** Show only the stage days the Content gives, each with its label, Published or Estimate. Never create another split of a total, and never add a sentence about one.
A13. **No signed numbers in print.** Write inflow / outflow, increase / decrease. No "+" or "-" in front of a figure.
A14. **Every problem chart shows the outcome with Crestline.** Beside today's figure, show the "With Crestline" result from the Content, labelled with the days and cash released and "Scenario, not a forecast." Use only the values the Content gives.
B1. **How Crestline saves it.** Beside every "With Crestline" result, print the two short lines the Content gives there, as written. Add none and shorten none.
B2. **Chart names.** "The cash cycle · Where the money sits, and who is holding it" and "Process map · One bill, from work done to cash in the bank" are printed exactly as given.
B3. **Labels stay as given.** Where the Content says "Not public" or "Not published", print that and never estimate, split or illustrate the figure. Where a stage day is labelled Estimate, print it with that label and the basis line, and never price it, add it or present it as measured.

## 4. Before you finish, check every page

1. Count the pages: exactly 16, A4 portrait, in the order of the Content.
2. Read every headline against the Content: same words, same punctuation.
3. Pick every figure on every page and find it in the Content, character for character. Any figure you cannot find there, delete it.
4. Search the whole booklet for en dash and em dash characters (Unicode 2013 and 2014). There must be none.
5. Every chart shows only the values in the Content, each with its label.
6. Every scenario carries "Scenario, not a forecast." No scenario totals are added.
7. Every footnote is on its page, as given.
8. No word was added: no taglines, no right-hand footer teasers, no extra captions, no "Live in" line, no certification or hosting claim, no Crestline client names, no internal references, no printed negatives.
9. No company logo of Thakur Infraprojects appears. The Crestline logo is the supplied file, not redrawn.
10. The QR code on page 15 (and on page 16) opens crestlineintelligence.com/book-a-demo.
11. Instruction lines from the Content are not printed anywhere.
12. Edition check: only the cover reader lines, the page 2 letter and the salutation differ between editions.
