# Riviera Infraprojects: problems (respect first)

Source for every figure: CARE press release, 20 Aug 2025, standalone; FY24 audited, FY25 unaudited.

Respect: total operating income FY24 553.64 Cr to FY25 813.82 Cr (up 47%, 813.82 / 553.64), profit after tax 30.19 to 59.62 Cr, cash from operations 39.81 to 54.67 Cr (p3), gearing 1.43 to 0.73 times, GCA days down from 159 to 135 (p2).

1. The days are one number. GCA days stand at 135 (FY25, unaudited). The release does not split them into bills, unbilled work, retention or stock, so nobody outside can say which bill, site or desk holds them. One day of total operating income is 2.23 Cr (813.82 / 365); ten days is 22.30 Cr and thirty is 66.89 Cr, a scenario on the assumption that the days are taken on total operating income.
2. The book is 3.6 times the year (2,941 Cr at 30 Jun 2025, p2) and about half is newly awarded and not started. The bills it will produce, and the certification waits inside them, have not formed yet. A bill-by-bill clock set up before they do is cheaper than one set up after.
3. Client and vendor clocks are not public: no payment term, debtor days, payables or vendor terms. The only client fact is the mix (about 45% from four named groups, p2).

Ownership: measure, prepare, review, submit and invoice are Riviera Infraprojects'; acknowledge, certify and pay are the client's.
