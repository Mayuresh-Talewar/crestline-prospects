# Riviera Infraprojects: story angle (source: CARE press release, 20 Aug 2025, standalone)

Respect first: incorporated 2020, revenue Rs 553.64 Cr to Rs 813.82 Cr in one year, PBILDT margin ~13%, gearing 1.43x to 0.73x, interest cover 22.60x, order book Rs 2,941 Cr (3.6x revenue), about 45% from Adani, Zydus, Torrent and Godrej groups. Backed by a group with 50 years and 250+ projects. A first-time A- from CARE.

The question: CARE says working capital intensity will rise as FY26 moves to Rs 1,100-1,200 Cr and it names GCA days beyond 175 as a downgrade trigger. GCA days stood at 159 (FY24) and 135 (FY25). Funding is largely interest-free mobilisation and material advances. Which bills, retention and unbilled work sit inside those 135 days, at which site and with which client desk, is not visible in any filing. Half the order book is newly awarded and yet to start, so the mix of work that will wait on certification has not formed yet.

Limits of the data: no debtor, creditor or inventory split, no retention figure, no ERP. The story rests on one measure (GCA days) and one forward statement by the rating agency.
