## FEASIBILITY VERDICT: NOT FEASIBLE (for a booklet about the EPC segment's project cash)

Blunt reading, every point sourced below:

1. **The EPC business is real but small and in run-off.** E&C segment revenue was Rs 237.22 Cr in FY26, 0.93% of group segment revenue (Computed). The order book is Rs 363.25 Cr at 31 Mar 2026, down 81.6% from Rs 1,974.64 Cr at 31 Mar 2024 (Computed), about 1.53 years of FY26 revenue. The company's own risk note says "Most of the existing projects are either nearing completion or have already been completed" (AR FY26 PDF p56). No new EPC order was filed on NSE between 1 Jan 2025 and 8 Oct 2026 (named search below). Q1 FY27 E&C revenue fell 11.56% year on year to Rs 47.35 Cr.
2. **It is published separately, which is the one thing in its favour.** Standalone R-Infra is a single E&C segment (AR FY26 PDF p185) and the consolidated segment note gives E&C revenue, result, assets and liabilities (PDF p285 to 286). So the numbers exist.
3. **But the balances measure legacy disputes, not a live project cash cycle.** 96.7% of standalone gross trade receivables are provided for as expected credit loss, 85.88% are older than 3 years, contract liabilities equal 1,177 days of FY26 revenue and 56.93% of trade payables are older than 3 years (all Computed). Net receivable days swung from 395.9 (FY24) to 774.8 (FY25) to 110.6 (FY26) mainly because the provision moved, not because collections moved. Any "days" figure priced off these balances would be a reading of old arbitration and write-offs, not of milestone billing, retention or advances on running jobs.
4. **A reliability point on the standalone accounts is listed for Garvis as S1 and S2** in sensitive-garvis.md. It does not stop figures being cited as filed, but it weighs against pricing days off them.
5. **No call transcripts, no investor deck on EPC, no working-capital target, no retention figure.** Management says almost nothing about EPC collections, receivables or advances (depth.md).
6. **Interest:** standalone bank borrowings are nil at 31 Mar 2025 and 31 Mar 2026, so under the Garvis BASIS ruling no interest is printed. The only disclosed rate on a named facility is 11.50% on a Rs 85.00 Cr secured inter-corporate deposit.

**If the client builds regardless (Garvis ruling: build, honestly scoped):** the most that can be done honestly is a LIGHT booklet that (a) names the four E&C projects and their filed progress, (b) shows the filed order book, contract liability and ageing tables as filed with their basis, (c) draws process stages without per-stage days, and (d) prices no days, no cash release and no interest. A FULL booklet with "today vs with Crestline" day savings is not supportable on this data.
