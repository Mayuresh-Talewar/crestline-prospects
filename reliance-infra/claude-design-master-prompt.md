# Claude Design master prompt: Reliance Infrastructure working-capital review (one booklet, per-reader editions)

Paste everything below this line into Claude Design. Upload the Crestline logo file and the Reliance Infrastructure logo file, reliance-infra/assets/rinfra-logo.png, before you send it.

## 1. What you are building

A 16-page A4 portrait booklet titled "Working-capital review", printed and posted to named readers at Reliance Infrastructure Limited. You build ONE design and produce one edition per reader listed in the reader table (see "Reader editions" below).
It answers one question for the reader: what if Crestline were already running at Reliance Infrastructure, what would it measure on the running jobs that the filings cannot show?
Every word and every figure is supplied in the Content section at the end of this prompt. Your job is layout, type and charts, built on the master design described in section 2.

## 2. The design to reproduce (the master)

You have not seen the master file, so it is described here in full. Reproduce it; do not restyle it.

**Page and grid.** A4 portrait, 210 x 297 mm. Outer margin about 17 mm left and right, 16 mm top, 14 mm bottom. One text column, left-aligned, ragged right. Body copy measure about 60 to 70 characters. Generous empty space; nothing centred except the QR card contents.

**Type.** Poppins throughout, no serif anywhere.
- Headline: Poppins Medium, about 24 pt, line height 1.15, two lines. Line 1 in ink #161B28 (white on dark pages). Line 2, or the key phrase named in the headline table, in gold #B9975B (#D9C08F on dark pages). A full stop after the headline is in ink.
- Chapter label above the headline: Poppins Medium about 10 pt, gold, sentence case ("Chapter one"). A page the Content gives no chapter label has none.
- Running head at the top of every inner page: Poppins Medium about 6 pt, all caps, letter-spaced about 0.3 em, muted #6A7080. Left: the kicker given for that page in the Content. Right: RELIANCE INFRASTRUCTURE LIMITED in caps on pages about the company, "CRESTLINE INTELLIGENCE" on pages about the product, "PUBLIC RECORD" on the public-record page; the Content says which. A gold hairline (0.5 pt, #D9C08F) runs under the running head across the full text width.
- Body: Poppins Regular 9 to 10 pt, ink #161B28 (on dark pages #C6CEE2). Lead paragraph under the headline 9.5 pt.
- Big figures: Poppins Bold, navy #22396F on cream pages, white on dark pages; key figures in gold #D9C08F on dark pages. Tabular figures.
- Small labels and eyebrows: Poppins Medium 6 to 7 pt, all caps, letter-spaced, muted.
- Footnotes: Poppins Regular 6 pt, muted #6A7080, at the foot of the page above the footer.
- Emphasis inside a closing line: Poppins Medium Italic in gold.

**Colours.**
- Navy field (cover, back cover, dark pages): #0D172F. Navy figure ink: #22396F.
- Cream inner page: #F6F2E9. Hairlines on cream: #DDCDB0. White cards on cream: #FFFFFF with a 1 px #E6E8EC border, radius about 14 px, a whisper shadow.
- Gold: #B9975B on cream, #D9C08F on navy. Muted text #6A7080. Ink #161B28.
- Verdict colours (only where a figure is judged): good #2F7D60, warning #A8620F, risk #B0342B.
- Chart fills, from the Crestline chart spec (pastel fill, darker ink for lines and labels): sky #9CC5EE / #567FAC, mint #9ED9BF / #549678, amber #F3CF86 / #B38540, rose #F1B3AB / #C46C61, lavender #C3B6EE / #7D71BD, peach #F5C29D / #C38459, slate #C3C9D3 / #6F7783. Gridlines #EBEDF0 dashed, axis text #98A2B3.
- No purple-to-blue page gradients, no glow, no glass, no stock photos, no emoji, no icon rows.

**Footer on every inner page.** Left: "Strictly private · N / 16" in Poppins Regular 6 pt muted. Nothing on the right unless the Content gives a footer line for that page.

**Watermark.** On cream pages, a very faint (about 6% opacity) enlarged Crestline mark bleeds off one bottom corner. Use the uploaded Crestline logo mark for it. If no logo is uploaded, leave the watermark out.

**Page 1, cover.** Full navy field. Top: "STRICTLY PRIVATE & CONFIDENTIAL" left and "COPY NO. 1 OF 1" right, 6 pt caps, letter-spaced, white; a thin white hairline (30% opacity) under them; the line "Prepared for the addressee only. **Chapter pages (cream).** Chapter label, two-line headline, lead paragraph, then the content. **Chart style (all charts).**
- Bars are "jelly" fills: a soft 100-degree gradient from one pastel into its neighbour (sky into lavender for work waiting to be billed; mint for progress; slate for neutral or opening values). A 1 px edge in the hue's ink. Rounded ends (pill bars on horizontal charts, 6 to 8 px top radius on columns).
- Every bar and point carries its value label (9 pt Medium, ink, or gold on dark pages). No reported bar without its value. With-Crestline marks are the one exception: they carry words, never values.
- No axis lines, no tick marks; horizontal dashed gridlines only where a scale is shown; axis labels 7 pt #98A2B3.
- Legends are 8 px rounded squares with 7 pt labels.
- With-Crestline marks are drawn DASHED and HOLLOW: a dashed 1 pt outline with no fill for boxes, pills, cards and table columns, divided into equal-width label cells where the Content says so, and the caption "What Crestline would measure, not a figure." printed beside or under them. A with-Crestline mark must never look like a reported figure, is never sized to a value and never carries a number.
- Hatched fills (diagonal 135-degree stripes) only where the Content says "hatched".

**Page 15, invitation.** Cream top. Headline, then the body in 14 pt with its last clause in gold italic. ## 3. Reader editions

Build ONE 16-page design. Then, for each row of the reader table in the Content section, produce one edition of it.
- An edition changes ONLY three things: the cover's "PREPARED EXCLUSIVELY FOR" lines (that reader's name and title), the page 2 letter (that reader's letter text and address block), and the page 2 salutation.
- Every other page, word, figure, chart and position is IDENTICAL across editions. Do not tailor any body page to a reader.
- Name each edition after its reader as given in the table (for example "Edition: <reader name>"). That label is for the file only and is never printed.
- Body copy is written in a neutral voice. Quotes are credited by name and title, or to the Annual Report, as the Content gives them; never rewrite a credit into "you" for any edition.

## 4. Rules you must follow

1. Use ONLY the text and figures in the Content section. Do not write new copy, captions, taglines, teasers, pull quotes or headings. If a slot has no text, leave it empty.
2. Copy every figure exactly as written: same digits, same decimals, same currency sign, same unit. Do not round, re-total, convert or recompute anything.
3. Headlines exactly as given in the headline table, split into two lines as shown. Never reword them.
4. Footnotes exactly as given, on the page they belong to. Where a footnote carries a tag in brackets, print the caveat line that tag stands for (listed under "Keys" in the Content) in its place, in full.
5. Text inside quotation marks in the Content is printed copy. Words outside quotation marks (for example "Chart:", "Card", "With Crestline mark", "No chart", "History only", "LIGHT edition instruction") are instructions to you and are never printed.
6. No internal references in printed text. Tags in brackets, checker notes and any note addressed to the team are never printed. A source is printed only in the words the Content gives for it.
7. No printed negatives about the booklet itself: never print what is missing, unknown, unchecked or left out ("not available", "we could not find", "TBD", "placeholder"). Where a slot is empty, leave the space empty. Print a "not published", "not in any filing" or "not public" line only where the Content gives it as quoted copy.
8. No em dashes and no en dashes anywhere. Use a comma, a full stop, "to" or a middle dot (·) as the Content does.
9. Ownership words stay as written: the client acknowledges, certifies and pays; Reliance Infrastructure measures, prepares, reviews, submits and invoices. Do not swap them.
10. The Reliance Infrastructure logo appears only if the client uploaded it, used as supplied and printed small (no wider than about 14 mm). Never redraw, trace, recolour, stretch or imitate it. Without it, the cover shows the reader's name only and no company mark.
11. This booklet prints no rupee-per-day value, no savings figure and no interest.

### Booklet accuracy rules (apply to every page, every edition)
A1. **Claims about Crestline must be true today.** Make no claim about Crestline beyond the pasted text. No certification "in progress", no hosting region, no encryption claim, no client names, no "live in X days", and no present-tense claim that Crestline connects to the company's ERP. The pilot is one entity, read-only, 14 days, as the Content words it.
A2. **Every quote names the right speaker**, exactly as the Content credits it. Never move a quote to another person.
A3. **Ours versus the client's follows the product rule.** In cash-in steps, acknowledged, certified and collected are the client's acts; measuring, preparing, reviewing, submitting and invoicing are the company's own. Keep every "Ours", "The client's", "The vendor's" and CLIENT tag as given.
A4. **No "nothing went wrong" headlines.** Use only the headlines given; never soften or invert one.
A5. **Charts drawn to scale.** Every reported bar sits within 2% of its own value on ONE shared scale per chart, starting where the Content says. Every reported bar shows its value label. With-Crestline marks are not drawn to any value.
A6. **No interest.** No interest is priced in this booklet. Print only the line the Content gives about it; never compute, print or label a rate.
A7. **No savings and no scenarios.** Anything marked "with Crestline" is drawn dashed and hollow, carries no value, and is captioned "What Crestline would measure, not a figure." Print no total the Content does not give. Charts the Content marks as history only get no mark.
A8. **Peers and comparatives** appear only where the Content prints them. This booklet prints none. Never build a scorecard of missed promises.
A9. **No other company's data.** Do not bring in any company, name, figure or design text from anywhere other than the Content, including from earlier booklets or your memory. No demo numbers beside the company's figures.
A10. **Full address and correct names and titles** on the letter page and cover, exactly as the reader table gives them, per edition.
A11. **"You said" only for the reader's own words.** Never print "you said", "in your own words" or any second-person credit over a quote by someone other than that edition's reader. Use the speaker's name and title or the company's name as the Content gives it. Never print a note or flag on the page.
A12. **Never invent figures to fill a chart.** If the Content gives a stage, step or bar without a value (for example the page 8 bill timeline and every with-Crestline mark), draw it without a number. Never create an "illustrative split" of a total, and never add a sentence about one.
A13. **No signed numbers in print.** Write inflow / outflow, increase / decrease. No "+" or "-" in front of a figure.
A14. **Every problem chart shows what Crestline would measure.** Beside or on top of the "today" figure, draw the with-Crestline mark the Content describes: dashed outline or hollow marker, equal-width label cells, never sized to a value, labelled only with the words the Content gives, and captioned "What Crestline would measure, not a figure." The Content gives no days, cash or interest for any mark; never add one (rule A12).

1. Count the pages: exactly 16, A4 portrait, in the order of the Content.

7. Every footnote is on its page, with each bracketed tag replaced by its full caveat line.
8. No word was added: no taglines, no extra captions, no "Live in" line, no certification or hosting claim, no client names beyond those the Content gives, no internal references, no printed negatives.
9. The Reliance Infrastructure logo is the uploaded file, printed small, or absent. Nothing redrawn.
10. The cover and back cover have the navy field with the gold curve lines; pages 4, 7 and 12 are navy; page 15 ends in the navy sources band; every other page is cream with the running head, gold hairline and "Strictly private · N / 16" footer.
11. The QR code on page 15 (and on page 16) opens crestlineintelligence.com/book-a-demo.
