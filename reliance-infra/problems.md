# Reliance Infrastructure Limited: problems (ticket RI 3, LIGHT form)

Prepared 8 Oct 2026. Running projects only: Kudankulam, the Mumbai Metro Line 4 packages, Chordaha to Gorhar. Every figure is from `data.md`, as filed, with document, PDF page, filing date and basis. No priced days, no interest, no savings figures (Garvis rulings, ticket RI 3).

## The question

Borrowings from banks are nil on the standalone balance sheet at 31 Mar 2025 and 31 Mar 2026 (AR FY25 PDF p185, p187, filed NSE 17 Jul 2025; AR FY26 note 18, PDF p166, filed NSE 23 Jul 2026, where the borrowings table lists no bank line; STANDALONE). The book of running jobs is smaller and focused; the company's own words: "The Division remains focused on efficient execution of its current order book" (AR FY26 PDF p56). So the question is a forward one: how cleanly does each running job turn into cash?

## Problem 1. Progress is published per project; billing is not

| Project | 31 Mar 2024 | 31 Mar 2025 | 31 Mar 2026 |

Sources: Kudankulam, AR FY24 PDF p31 (filed NSE 12 Jul 2024), AR FY25 PDF p72 (filed NSE 17 Jul 2025), AR FY26 PDF p50 (filed NSE 23 Jul 2026), each "as on March 31" of that year. Chordaha to Gorhar, AR FY26 PDF p51 (filed NSE 23 Jul 2026), "overall 75% as on March 31, 2026". Metro Line 4, AR FY26 PDF p50. Basis: the company's MD&A, project level, as filed.

What the filings cannot tell: for each job, how much of that progress has been billed, how much certified, and how much collected. Progress says the work moved. It does not say the cash followed it.

## Problem 2. Work done and not yet billed is one entity total

| Contract assets, gross (STANDALONE) | Opening FY26 | Closing FY26 |
|---|---|---|
| Rs Cr | 116.52 | 104.10 |

Source: AR FY26 note 24.3, PDF p172 (opening and closing); note 13, PDF p161, where the closing figure is shown as its considered good and credit impaired parts (Computed: 69.54 + 34.56 = 104.10); filed NSE 23 Jul 2026. Basis: standalone, gross, whole entity. Contract assets include retention receivable (AR FY25 PDF p192, filed NSE 17 Jul 2025), so the line holds two different things: work done and not yet billed, and retention the client holds.

What the filings cannot tell: which running job holds it, how much is unbilled work against how much is retention, and since when.

## Problem 3. No filing shows how long a running bill waits, or at whose desk

* No working capital cycle, receivable days or working capital target appears in AR FY24, AR FY25, AR FY26, the presentation to shareholders (filed NSE 7 Dec 2025), the Q4 FY26 results (filed NSE 23 May 2026) or the Q1 FY27 results (filed NSE 14 Aug 2026). Searched by script; depth.md section 4.
* No management statement on E&C collections, retention release or customer advances was found in the same documents (depth.md section 2).
* Retention is not published as its own figure: it sits inside trade receivables (AR FY26 PDF p158), contract assets (AR FY25 PDF p192) and trade payables (AR FY26 PDF p167).

What the filings cannot tell: for each RA bill on each running job, the days from measured to submitted (ours), submitted to certified (the client's), certified to invoiced (ours), invoiced to collected (the client's), and who held it at each step. That is a question about time, and annual accounts record balances, not time.
