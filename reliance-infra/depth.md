# Reliance Infrastructure Limited: depth (RI-1)

Management's own words on E&C execution, collections, receivables and advances; promises against delivery; working-capital target; disconfirm step; live-deal check. Every quote is word for word from the rendered page image ("image-checked"). Computed figures from `src/ri-calc.out.txt`. Sensitive matters are not here (see sensitive-garvis.md).

## 1. Who speaks, and how little

There are no earnings calls. NSE announcements from 1 Jan 2025 to 8 Oct 2026 (203 items) contain no analyst meet or con-call item, and no transcript was found on the web. The only investor deck in the period (7 Dec 2025) has no E&C figures. So the company's voice on E&C is the unsigned Management Discussion and Analysis inside each annual report (part of the Board's report), plus what India Ratings attributes to management. Speakers are labelled accordingly. No named executive is quoted on E&C anywhere in the documents read.

## 2. Management's words

**On E&C execution and pipeline**

1. "E&C Division focuses on execution of orders at hand and envisages consolidating its order book in coming year through targeted bidding of E&C opportunities with scope for Value Engineering."
   Speaker: the Company, MD&A. AR FY24, PDF p31 (printed 30), filed NSE 12 Jul 2024. Image-checked.

2. "Most of the existing projects are either nearing completion or have already been completed. This poses a potential business continuity risk till Company secures new contracts."
   Speaker: the Company, MD&A, Risks and Concerns. AR FY25, PDF p77 (printed 75), filed NSE 17 Jul 2025. Image-checked.

3. "Most of the existing projects are either nearing completion or have already been completed. The Division remains focused on efficient execution of its current order book and aims to strengthen its pipeline through selective and strategic bidding opportunities, with emphasis on value engineering to enhance competitiveness."
   Speaker: the Company, MD&A, Risks and Concerns. AR FY26, PDF p56 (printed 53), filed NSE 23 Jul 2026. Image-checked. Note the FY25 sentence on "business continuity risk" is dropped in FY26; the state of the projects is described in the same words.

4. "During the year, efforts remained focused on the timely and efficient execution of existing contracts while maintaining high standards of quality and safety."
   Speaker: the Company, MD&A, E & C Sector. AR FY26, PDF p50 (printed 47). Image-checked. The same sentence appears in AR FY25 PDF p72.

5. "Turnkey BESS system integration for utility and C&I projects, anchored on Reliance Infra's established EPC track record"
   Speaker: the Company, presentation to shareholders, PDF p24 (slide 23), filed NSE 7 Dec 2025. Image-checked. This is the only forward use of the EPC capability in the deck, and it is for a battery business, not the E&C order book.

**Attributed to management by the rating agency (India Ratings' words, not management's)**

6. "The company's E&C business is likely to turnaround in FY26, driven by management's expected order flows in new age business."
   India Ratings rationale 11 Jul 2025, PDF p2, filed NSE 11 Jul 2025. Image-checked.

7. "In addition, the company is likely to undertake a few large E&C orders within the group, which would improve the revenue visibility in the near-to-medium term."
   Same rationale, PDF p4. Image-checked.

**On collections, receivables and advances**

8. "While the management has indicated that a significant portion of these payables are under dispute and not immediately payable, they continue to weigh on liquidity."
   India Ratings' words attributing to management, on trade payables of INR12.9 billion and customer advances of INR5.1 billion (FY25). Rationale 11 Jul 2025, PDF p3. Image-checked.
