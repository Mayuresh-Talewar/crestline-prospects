## 1. What you are building

A 16-page A4 portrait booklet titled "Working-capital review", printed and posted to the Board of Yashnand Engineers and Contractors Private Limited. There is one edition, for the reader in the reader table.
It answers one question for the reader: what if Crestline were already running at Yashnand, how much money and how much time would it save?
Every word and every figure is supplied in the Content section at the end of this prompt. The design is yours.

## 2. Reader edition

One edition. The cover's "PREPARED EXCLUSIVELY FOR" lines, the page 2 letter and the salutation are the reader table's. Every other page is as the Content gives it.

## 3. Rules you must follow

1. Use ONLY the text and figures in the Content section. Do not write new copy, captions, taglines, teasers, pull quotes or headings. If a line has no text, leave it empty.
2. Copy every figure exactly as written: same digits, same decimals, same currency sign, same unit. Do not round, re-total, convert or recompute anything.
3. Headlines exactly as given. A slash inside a headline marks where the headline breaks into two lines and is never printed.
4. Footnotes exactly as given, on the page they belong to.
5. Lines that start with ">" are printed copy. Text after a colon in the same line is printed with it. Position words at the start of a line ("KICKER", "HEADLINE", "Lead", "Body", "Closing line", "Footnote", "Footer", "Hero figure", "Side stat", "Data", "With Crestline", "Chart name", "Stages in this order") say what the line is and are never printed. Printed labels that are part of the copy itself (for example FINDING, ROOT CAUSE, DO THIS, Owner) are printed.
6. No internal references in printed text. File names, ticket ids, notes to the team and status markers are never printed. A source is printed only in the words the Content gives for it.
7. Print no negatives about the booklet itself. Where the Content gives "Not published" or "Not public" as quoted copy, print it as given; where a line is empty, leave it empty.
8. No em dashes and no en dashes anywhere. Use a comma, a full stop, "to" or a middle dot (·) as the Content does.
9. Ownership words stay as written: the client acknowledges, certifies and pays; Yashnand measures, prepares, reviews, submits and invoices. Do not swap them.
10. No company logo is supplied. Show the company name as text only. Crestline's own logo files, if uploaded, are used as supplied and never redrawn.
B1. Chart names are printed exactly as given in "Chart name". Each chart carries its "Data" lines, then its "With Crestline" lines, then its footnote.
B2. Where a chart has "With Crestline" lines, the two lines under "How Crestline saves it" are printed beside that result, exactly as given.
B3. Every chart page carries the sentence "Never compute a figure that is not given here." as an instruction to you, not as printed copy: it means you create no figure of your own.
B4. Every value marked Estimate is printed with the word Estimate, every value marked Published with the word Published. The basis line and the WHOLE RA BILL line under the process map are printed exactly as given.

### Booklet accuracy rules (apply to every page)
A1. Claims about Crestline must be true today. Make no claim about Crestline beyond the pasted text: no certification, no hosting region, no encryption claim, no client names, no "live in X days", and no present-tense claim that Crestline connects to the company's systems. The pilot is one entity, one segment, read-only, 14 days, as the Content words it.
A2. Every quote names the speaker exactly as the Content credits it.
A3. Ours versus the client's follows the product rule. Acknowledged, certified and paid are the client's acts; measuring, preparing, reviewing, submitting and invoicing are Yashnand's own. Keep every owner tag as given.
A4. Use only the headlines given. Never soften or invert one.
A5. Every value printed on a chart is exactly the value the Content gives, and every value on a chart is labelled.
A6. No interest is priced anywhere. Print no rate and no interest figure.
A7. Savings are scenarios. Anything marked "With Crestline", "scenario" or "days less" is labelled "Scenario, not a forecast." Scenario rows overlap and are never added together; print no total the Content does not give.
A8. FY24 figures are CRISIL's estimates and are printed with that label wherever the Content gives it.
A9. No other company's data. Bring in no company, name or figure from anywhere other than the Content.
A10. Names and titles on the cover and the letter page are exactly as the reader table gives them. No address is printed, because none is given.
A11. Never create a stage value, an "illustrative split" or any figure to fill a gap. A line without a value is printed without a number.
A12. No signed numbers in print. Write inflow / outflow, increase / decrease. No "+" or "-" in front of a figure.
