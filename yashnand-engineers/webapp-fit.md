# Web app fit: Yashnand Engineers and Contractors

Source: CRISIL Ratings rationale, 5 December 2024, standalone. FY24 is CRISIL's estimate. Government civil and building EPC (Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra), so the O2C bill timeline, client ageing and retention tracking apply directly.

| App screen | Fit | What the published data supports |
|---|---|---|
| Ask Crestline | Yes | Once connected, any question on the bills |
| Working capital (cash cycle) | Partial | Stock 29 days, debtors 120 days. No payables, no contract assets, so no total |
| O2C (bill timeline, "Process map") | Yes | Payment wait 120 days is published; other stages are estimates scaled from it |
| Client | Partial | Client type only (government agencies). No names, terms or ageing |
| P2P and Vendors | No data | No payables, creditor days or vendor terms published |
| Tickets | Yes | Once connected |
| Retention | No figure | CRISIL says deposits and retention are held by departments; no amount |

Charts used: The cash cycle (partial), Process map, scenario bars. Skipped: Pressure on the cycle (no rate, no balances), Cash movement (no cash flow statement), How these four move together (single year).
ERP: none named in the source, so "ERP" is printed.
