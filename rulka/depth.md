## 3. Disconfirm step (one test per headline, designed to prove it wrong)

1. **"The cash conversion cycle improved from 143 to 97 days" (TJ p3).** Test: rebuild from the audited ratio note. Result: 97 holds (97.78 on average balances, §4), but inventory days in AR26 note 35 went UP, 69.58 to 76.97, while the call says DOWN, 90 to 67; and 143 and 90 rebuild from nothing filed. On the closing basis the cycle fell 66.80 days, of which 38.45 days is DPO (paying suppliers later) and 27.30 days is DIO; DSO moved 1.05 days (§8). **Partly disconfirmed**: the improvement is real, its source is the supplier side and stock, not collection.
2. **"Turnaround in operating cash flow" (AR26 p39, TJ p3).** Test: strip the payables line from the FY26 cash flow (AR26 p91). Without the rise of 1,324.37 lakh in trade payables, OCF would have been minus 712.61 lakh (§8). MSME payables alone rose 983.54 lakh and are now 68.89% of payables (§3, §8). **Disconfirmed as a collection story; confirmed as a cash number.** Suppliers, most of them MSMEs, financed the turnaround.
3. **"Debtor days improved from 120 to 103" (TJ p3).** Test: ageing note and closing basis. Closing DSO is flat (119.74 to 118.69); receivables more than 6 months past due went from 3.61% to 26.77% of the book (AR26 note 14 p101, §3). The 103 (102.66) is an average of two year ends, and its FY25 partner (117.12) mixes vintages (§4). **Disconfirmed on the closing basis.**
4. **"Unexecuted order book about 144 crores" (TJ p4).** Test: earlier filings. The same 144 was given at H1 (TD p5) while the H1 media release said 92 orders in hand. **Conflict, not settled**: print only Rs 143.85 Cr with AR26's own words, "total order book for the year 2025-26".
5. **"Working capital will 100% improve in the next half" (TD p10).** Test: H2 against H1 is not available on one basis (H1 balance sheet is old basis). Year end evidence: overdue receivables up tenfold, closing DSO flat. **Not supported by the year end accounts**; kept internal, factual, never as a scorecard.
6. **Live deals.** No cancellation, termination or short closure in 162 NSE announcements to 08 Oct 2026 (0 hits). No single order is cited in the booklet data.

## 4. What the accounts cannot tell them (Crestline's opening)
- Which bills make up the Rs 9.59 Cr over six months past due: client, site, certified or disputed, and since when (note 14 gives only buckets).
- How long WIP of Rs 7.93 Cr waits between work done and invoice, per site. Until FY26 that money was not even visible: it sat inside receivables (the 905.30 lakh reclassification, data.md Vintage).
- When each MSME supplier falls due against the 45 day statutory limit: payables rose to Rs 25.56 Cr, two thirds of it MSME, and the cycle's improvement leans on them.
- Retention of Rs 5.00 Cr: which projects, which release dates.
- Interest at the disclosed 8.25% (term loan) or repo plus 3.00% (overdraft): one day of FY26 revenue is 30.19 lakh, carrying 2.49 lakh a year at 8.25% and 2.87 lakh at 9.50% (§5). Scenario arithmetic only; interest is saved only if the cash repays the overdraft.
