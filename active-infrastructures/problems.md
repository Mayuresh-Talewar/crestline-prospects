# Active Infrastructures Limited: problems (step 2)

Basis: Annual Report 2025-26 ("AR FY26"), consolidated, year ended 31 Mar 2026, accounts signed Nagpur 25 May 2026. Pages are printed page numbers, PDF page in brackets. FY26 only: no FY24 or FY25 comparative is used.

## Respect first (the story opens here)

A Nagpur builder listed on NSE Emerge (AR FY26 p4, PDF p7), with consolidated revenue from operations of Rs 96.57 Cr and profit after tax of Rs 10.28 Cr in FY26 (AR FY26 p133, PDF p136). An unexecuted order book of about Rs 125.33 Cr across 4 active projects at 31 Mar 2026, and a separate Rs 92.43 Cr in its 50.5% owned roads subsidiary (AR FY26 p11, PDF p14). Two entities, never added.

## Problem 1: the bills wait 188.7 days to be paid

- Trade receivables Rs 49.9 Cr (4,993.50 lakh) at 31 Mar 2026, consolidated (AR FY26 p132, PDF p135; Note 8, p150, PDF p153).
- Revenue from operations Rs 96.57 Cr (9,656.65 lakh) for FY26 (AR FY26 p133, PDF p136).
- 4,993.50 / 9,656.65 x 365 = 188.7 days. This is Crestline's arithmetic on the published accounts, not a figure management states.
- What the accounts cannot say: which site, which running bill, which client desk, and since when.
- Scenario (calc/ac-savings-output.txt): ten days less is Rs 2.65 Cr released once; thirty days less is Rs 7.94 Cr. No interest.

## Problem 2: contract work done and not yet billed

- Inventories, services (cost of services incurred, not yet billed): Rs 19.92 Cr (1,992.22 lakh) at 31 Mar 2026, consolidated (AR FY26 Note 7, p150, PDF p153).
- That money has left the company as cost. It waits for a measurement, a certificate or an invoice.
- Ours: measure with the client, check, prepare, review, submit, invoice. The client's: acknowledge, certify, pay.
- What the accounts cannot say: which pieces wait on the company's desk and which on the client's.
- No scenario value is given, because the calculator prices the receivable leg only.

## Problem 3: the accounts name the risk, and cannot name the bill

- Under working capital risk the AR FY26 lists "Delayed payments in public sector contracts", with the mitigation "Diversified clientele and factoring facilities" (AR FY26 p12, PDF p15).
- The Managing Director's message states the aim to "strengthen cash flow controls" (Nitesh Sanklecha, Managing Director, AR FY26 p4, PDF p7).
- What the accounts cannot say: which payer, which bill, how long it has sat.
- No scenario value is given.
