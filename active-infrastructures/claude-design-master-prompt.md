# Working-capital review for Active Infrastructures: one booklet, per-reader editions

## 1. What you are building

A 16-page A4 portrait booklet titled "Working-capital review", printed and posted to named readers at Active Infrastructures Limited. You build ONE design and produce one edition per reader listed in the reader table (see "Reader editions" below).
It answers one question for the reader: what if Crestline were already running at Active Infrastructures, how much money and how much time would it save?
Every word and every figure is supplied in the Content section at the end of this prompt. Your job is layout, type and charts.

## 2. Reader editions

Build ONE 16-page design. Then, for each row of the reader table in the Content section, produce one edition of it.
- An edition changes ONLY three things: the cover's "PREPARED EXCLUSIVELY FOR" lines (that reader's name and title), the page 2 letter (that reader's letter text and address block), and the page 2 salutation.
- Every other page, word, figure, chart and position is IDENTICAL across editions. Do not tailor any body page to a reader.
- Name each edition after its reader as given in the table (for example "Edition: <reader name>"). That label is for the file only and is never printed.
- Body copy is written in a neutral voice. Quotes are credited by name and title, as the Content gives them; never rewrite a credit into "you" for any edition.

## 3. Rules you must follow

1. Use ONLY the text and figures in the Content section. Do not write new copy, captions, taglines, teasers, pull quotes or headings. If a field has no text, leave it empty.
2. Copy every figure exactly as written: same digits, same decimals, same currency sign, same unit. Do not round, re-total, convert or recompute anything.
3. Headlines exactly as given in the headline table, split into two lines as shown. Never reword them.
4. Footnotes exactly as given, on the page they belong to.
5. Lines in the Content that start with a > mark are printed copy: print everything after the > mark, except that on lines starting "> KICKER:", "> HEADLINE:" and "> Footer:" the words KICKER, HEADLINE and Footer only say where the text goes and are not printed. Text inside quotation marks that is part of an instruction line is printed copy too. Every other line (for example those beginning "Chart:", "Tiles:", "Hero", "Running head", "Do not invent step waits", or "Text only") is an instruction to you and is never printed.
6. Nothing that is a working note is printed: no field names, no file names, no codes in brackets and no note addressed to anyone. A source is printed only in the words the Content gives for it.
7. No printed negatives about the booklet itself: never print what is missing, unknown, unchecked or left out ("not available", "we could not find", "TBD", "placeholder"). Where a field is empty, leave the space empty. Print a "not published" or "not public" line only where the Content gives it as printed copy.
8. No em dashes and no en dashes anywhere. Use a comma, a full stop, "to" or a middle dot (·) as the Content does.
9. Ownership words stay as written: the client certifies and pays; Active Infrastructures measures, prepares, reviews, submits and invoices. Do not swap them.
10. There is no Active Infrastructures logo and no Crestline logo in this job. The company name prints as plain text. Never draw, trace or imitate a logo.

### Booklet accuracy rules (apply to every page, every edition)
A1. **Claims about Crestline must be true today.** Make no claim about Crestline beyond the pasted text. No certification "in progress", no hosting region, no encryption claim, no client names, no "live in X days", and no present-tense claim that Crestline connects to the company's ERP. The pilot is one entity, one segment, read-only, 14 days, as the Content words it.
A2. **Every quote names the right speaker**, exactly as the Content credits it. Never move a quote to another person.
A3. **Ours versus the client's follows the product rule.** In cash-in steps, acknowledged, certified and collected are the client's acts; measuring, preparing, reviewing, submitting and invoicing are the company's own. Keep every company and CLIENT tag as given.
A4. **No "nothing went wrong" headlines.** Use only the headlines given; never soften or invert one.
A5. **Charts drawn to scale.** Every bar, segment, column and point sits within 2% of its own value on ONE shared scale per chart, starting where the Content says. Every bar shows its value label.
A6. **Interest only at a rate the company discloses.** Print only the rate and interest figures the Content gives; never compute or label a rate as theirs.
A7. **Savings are scenarios.** Anything marked "scenario", "dashed" or "days less" is drawn dashed and hollow with "Scenario, not a forecast." beside it. Scenario rows overlap and are never added together; print no total the Content does not give. Charts the Content marks as having no scenario mark get none.
A8. **Peers and comparatives** appear only where the Content prints them. Never build a scorecard of missed promises.
A9. **No other company's data.** Do not bring in any company, name, figure or design text from anywhere other than the Content, including from your memory. No demo numbers beside the company's figures.
A10. **Full address and correct names and titles** on the letter page and cover, exactly as the reader table gives them, per edition.
A11. **"You" only for the reader's own words.** Never print "you said", "in your own words" or any second-person credit over a quote by someone other than that edition's reader. Use the speaker's name and title or the company's name as the Content gives it. Never print a note or flag on the page.
A12. **Never invent figures to fill a chart.** If the Content gives a stage, step or bar without a value (for example the page 8 bill timeline), draw it without a number. Never create a split of a total, and never add a sentence about one.
A13. **No signed numbers in print.** Write inflow / outflow, increase / decrease. No "+" or "-" in front of a figure.
A14. **Every problem chart shows the outcome with Crestline.** Beside or on top of the "today" figure, draw the scenario result from the Content (dashed outline or hollow marker, same scale, labelled with the days and cash it releases, plus "Scenario, not a forecast."). Use only the values the Content gives; if the Content gives no scenario value for a chart, show none (rule A12).

### Additional rules
B1. **Every chart starts at zero.** Bars and tracks begin at zero on their own scale. A scenario bar is never drawn longer than its value on that scale.
B2. **Never compute a figure that is not given.** If a figure is not written on the page you are building, it does not appear on that page.
B3. **Debtor days always carry their label.** Wherever debtor days print, the words "Crestline's arithmetic on the published accounts" print with them, as the Content gives.
B4. **Pages with no figure stay without one.** Where the Content says a page has no figures or no numbers, print none, including in tiles, captions and chart labels.
B5. **Lens pages that read "Not published" keep that wording and its reason.** Never replace it with an estimate or a blank.

## 4. Before you finish, check every page of every edition

1. Count the pages: exactly 16, A4 portrait, in the order of the Content.
2. Read every headline against the headline table: same words, same punctuation, line 2 in gold.
3. Pick every figure on every page and find it in the Content, character for character. Any figure you cannot find there, delete it.
4. Search the whole booklet for en dash and em dash characters (Unicode 2013 and 2014). There must be none.
5. Every chart: one scale per chart starting at zero, each bar within 2% of its value, every bar labelled, nothing drawn from a figure that is not in the Content.
6. Every scenario mark is dashed and hollow and carries "Scenario, not a forecast." Charts marked "No scenario mark" carry none. No scenario totals added.
7. Every footnote is on its page, exactly as given.
8. No word was added: no taglines beyond the one given, no right-hand footer teasers, no extra captions, no "Live in" line, no certification or hosting claim, no client names, no working notes, no printed negatives beyond the copy given.
9. No logo anywhere. The company name is plain text.
10. The QR code on page 15 (and on page 16) opens crestlineintelligence.com/book-a-demo.
11. Instruction lines from the Content (every line that does not start with > and every label word such as KICKER) are not printed anywhere.
12. Editions: compare each edition with the first. Only the cover reader lines, the page 2 letter and the salutation differ. No "you" credit sits over a quote that is not that edition's reader's own.
