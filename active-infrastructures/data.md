# Active Infrastructures Limited: data (step 1 research)

Researched 2026-10-09. Rs lakh unless stated (100 lakh = 1 crore). All figures read from page images of the company's own annual reports. The dossier was used only as a checklist and is not cited.

## VERDICT: FULL

Reasons
- Listed on NSE Emerge (SME). Audited consolidated and standalone accounts, MD&A, ratio table, order book, borrowing rates and receivable ageing are all in the company's own FY26 annual report (year ended 31 Mar 2026).
- Strong working-capital subject on a single basis: consolidated closing receivables Rs 4,993.50 lakh on revenue from operations Rs 9,656.65 lakh = 188.7 days (Computed); services work in progress (cost incurred, not yet billed) rose from Rs 44.68 lakh to Rs 1,992.22 lakh.
- Named, reachable finance leader (CFO Gautam Ramesh Jain, since 2 Sep 2024).
- Caveats for steps 2 and 3: small company (about Rs 96.6 Cr revenue from operations); no credit rating found; no ERP named in any filing (NOT FOUND); management states turnover ratios, not days; inventory is mostly building (real estate) stock, so inventory days are not an EPC measure; the dossier's headline revenue, PAT and D/E are stale or unsupported (see Dossier check).

## Conflicts between sources

1. FY25 AR MD&A prints revenue from operations as 3,663.10 (and the About page prints 366.10); the audited P&L shows 3,607.02, and 3,663.10 is total income.
2. FY25 AR consolidated cash flow shows FY24 profit before tax 1,406.17; the consolidated P&L shows 1,242.89.
3. FY25 comparatives were changed in the FY26 AR: standalone finance cost 10.93 became 2.59 and EPS 5.27 became 3.79 in the board report; consolidated EPS for FY25 is 8.79 in the P&L and 6.32 in the board report, in the same document.
4. RoE: FY25 AR 36.22%; FY26 AR 9%.
5. Business description: FY25 AR MD&A shows order book "Water Infrastructure 100%" and a roads and highways narrative, while Note 1 says the entity is primarily in construction and sale of commercial units, and FY25 standalone revenue includes office blocks 1,850.00 of 3,607.02.
6. Title of the MD: "Managing Director" in the appointment resolution and signatures; "Chairman-Managing Director" in committee tables; the Board's Chairperson is Ms. Asha Sampath.
7. Other dossier conflicts are listed in the Dossier check table.
