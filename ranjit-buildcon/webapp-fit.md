# Web app fit: Ranjit Buildcon (RN-S23)
All claims are true of the app today (app CLAUDE.md, lib\navigation.ts). Nothing here says the app is connected to Ranjit Buildcon's ERP: no ERP is named in the source, and no adapter exists until a pilot builds one.

| What the published record leaves open | App screen that answers it once connected |
|---|---|
| Which bill waits, at which stage, for how long (CARE gives only 220 gross current asset days) | Project O2C (Cash In): bills rebuilt from event dates, with the discovered process map; ownership by the act that ends the wait |
| Where unbilled work and receivables sit | Project Working Capital: position, cash-cycle diagram, simulator, ranked levers |
| Debtors older than six months, retention money (named by CARE, no amount) | Project O2C and Client screens: ageing against each document's own due date; retention tracked bill by bill |
| Which client pays slow | Client screen (per project) and Clients (portfolio) |
| Vendor timing against agreed terms | Project Vendors and P2P: early payment shown as cash given away, late payment never a saving |
| What a day is worth | Simulator and Do-this-first ranking (priceLevers): cash released once; carry only at a disclosed rate |
| Turn a finding into a commitment | Alerts & Tickets: raise, owner told, re-measured, closed on the number |
| Ask a question about the whole book | Ask Crestline (company and project scope) |

Fixed limits: read-only; not an ERP; no model produces a number; NA where the source did not supply a figure.
Fit for this company: LIGHT. One priceable lever on published figures (GCA days as one block on the receivable leg). Charts 1 to 4 of the catalogue have no data and are skipped.
