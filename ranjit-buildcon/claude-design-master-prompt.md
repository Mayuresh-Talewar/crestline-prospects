## 1. What you are building

A 16-page A4 portrait booklet titled "Working-capital review", printed and posted to a named reader at Ranjit Buildcon Limited. You build ONE design and produce one edition per reader listed in the reader table (see "Reader editions" below).
It answers one question for the reader: what if Crestline were already running at Ranjit Buildcon, how much money and how much time would it save?
Every word and every figure is supplied in the Content section at the end of this prompt. This prompt gives content only. Colours, type, layout and the drawing of every chart are your own design decisions.
Lines in the Content that start with ">" are printed copy. Every other line is an instruction to you and is never printed.
Use the Crestline logo file as supplied, never redrawn. Ranjit Buildcon's logo is not used.

## 2. Reader editions

Build ONE 16-page design. Then, for each row of the reader table in the Content section, produce one edition of it.
- An edition changes ONLY three things: the cover's "PREPARED EXCLUSIVELY FOR" lines (that reader's name and title), the page 2 letter (that reader's letter text and address block), and the page 2 salutation.
- Every other page, word, figure and chart is IDENTICAL across editions. Do not tailor any body page to a reader.
- Name each edition after its reader as given in the table (for example "Edition:" followed by the reader's name). That label is for the file only and is never printed.
- Body copy is written in a neutral voice. Quotes are credited by name and title, as the Content gives them; never rewrite a credit into "you" for any edition.

## 3. Rules you must follow

1. Use ONLY the text and figures in the Content section. Do not write new copy, captions, taglines, teasers, pull quotes or headings. If a line has no text, leave it empty.
2. Copy every figure exactly as written: same digits, same decimals, same currency sign, same unit. Do not round, re-total, convert or recompute anything.
3. Headlines exactly as given in the Content, split into two lines as shown. Never reword them.
4. Footnotes exactly as given, on the page they belong to.
5. Printed copy is the lines that start with ">". Words in the other lines (for example "Data:", "Chart name:", "Text only", counts and notes) are instructions to you and are never printed.
6. No internal references in printed text: no ids, file names, checker notes or notes addressed to the team. A source is printed only in the words the Content gives for it.
7. No printed negatives about the booklet itself: never print what is missing, unknown, unchecked or left out ("not available", "we could not find"). Where a line is empty, leave it empty. Print a "Not published" or "Not public" line only where the Content gives it as printed copy.
8. No em dashes and no en dashes anywhere. Use a comma, a full stop, "to" or a middle dot (·) as the Content does.
9. Ownership words stay as written: the client certifies and pays; Ranjit Buildcon measures, prepares, submits and escalates. Do not swap them.
10. Ranjit Buildcon's logo is not used. The cover shows the reader's name only and no company mark.

### Booklet accuracy rules (apply to every page, every edition)
R1. **Claims about Crestline must be true today.** Make no claim about Crestline beyond the Content. No certification "in progress", no hosting region, no encryption claim, no client names, no "live in X days", and no present-tense claim that Crestline connects to the company's ERP. The pilot is one entity, one segment, read-only, 14 days, as the Content words it.
R2. **Every quote names the right speaker**, exactly as the Content credits it.
R3. **Ours versus the client's follows the product rule.** In cash-in steps, acknowledged, certified and collected are the client's acts; measuring, preparing, reviewing, submitting and invoicing are the company's own. Keep every owner label as given.
R4. **No "nothing went wrong" headlines.** Use only the headlines given.
R5. **Every value is printed exactly as the Content gives it, with its label.** Never show a value the Content does not give.
R6. **Interest only at a rate the company discloses.** No rate is disclosed, so no interest figure appears anywhere. Never compute or label a rate as the company's.
R7. **Savings are scenarios.** Anything marked "With Crestline", "scenario" or "days less" is shown as a scenario and carries the words "Scenario, not a forecast." beside it. Scenario rows overlap and are never added together; print no total the Content does not give.
R8. **Peers and comparatives** appear only where the Content prints them. None are given.
R9. **No other company's data.** Do not bring in any company, name, figure or text from anywhere other than the Content, including from earlier booklets or your memory.
R10. **Full address and correct names and titles** on the letter page and cover, exactly as the reader table gives them.
R11. **"You said" only for the reader's own words.** Never print "you said" or "in your own words" over words by someone else.
R12. **Never invent figures.** Print a stage value only where the Content gives it, with its Estimate tag. Never create a value for a stage the Content leaves without one, and never add a sentence about a split.
R13. **No signed numbers in print.** Write inflow / outflow, increase / decrease. No "+" or "-" in front of a figure.
R14. **Every "With Crestline" result is shown beside the "today" figure it moves**, using only the values the Content gives, labelled with the days and the cash it releases and "Scenario, not a forecast."

B1. **Dating.** Every figure taken from the CARE press release of 7 December 2023 is printed as published in December 2023, as the Content words it. No page states or implies a current position.
B2. **Estimates.** Every stage value on page 8 is an Estimate and is printed with the tag "Estimate". An estimate is never priced and is never described as a measured or published day.
B3. **Never compute a figure that is not given in the Content.** This applies to every page with data.
B4. **Page 13 carries no numbers**, apart from the page footer.

## 4. Before you finish, check every page of every edition

1. Count the pages: exactly 16, A4 portrait, in the order of the Content.
2. Read every headline against the Content: same words, same punctuation, same two lines.
3. Pick every figure on every page and find it in the Content, character for character. Any figure you cannot find there, delete it.
4. Search the whole booklet for en dash and em dash characters (Unicode 2013 and 2014). There must be none.
5. Every data page: each value is printed and labelled exactly as given, and nothing is shown that is not in the Content.
6. Every scenario carries "Scenario, not a forecast." No scenario totals added. Every page 8 stage value carries "Estimate".
7. Every footnote is on its page, as given.
8. No word was added: no taglines, no right-hand footer teasers, no extra captions, no "Live in" line, no certification or hosting claim, no client names, no internal references, no printed negatives.
9. Ranjit Buildcon's logo is absent. The Crestline logo is the uploaded file, not redrawn.
10. The QR code on page 15 (and on page 16) opens crestlineintelligence.com/book-a-demo.
11. Instruction lines from the Content (those not starting with ">") are not printed anywhere.
12. Editions: compare each edition with the first. Only the cover reader lines, the page 2 letter and the salutation differ.
