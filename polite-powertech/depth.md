# Polite Powertech: story angle (respect first, then where bills wait)

Respect: a Surat firm that took over a 2006 proprietary electrical business on 31 Dec 2023 and, in its first full year (FY25), billed Rs 155.63 Cr, earned Rs 21.64 Cr EBITDA (13.91% margin) and Rs 13.02 Cr PAT. 113 projects completed by 31 Dec 2025, 106 of them in Gujarat, and Rs 313.56 Cr of orders at that date, 82.41% from PSUs and state utilities. Borrowing is light: Rs 1.99 Cr drawn on a Rs 10 Cr cash credit at 8.35% (30 Sep 2025). Accounts run on Tally Prime.

The question (company-published numbers only): receivable days 118 in FY25 and 130 in H1 FY26 (company's figure); 27.24% of receivables over six months past due at March 2025 and 27.94% at September 2025; retention receivable Rs 24.78 Cr at March 2025 and Rs 27.39 Cr at September 2025, released after a 1-2 year defect period or against a bank guarantee. Operating cash flow was negative in FY24 (Rs -3.62 Cr), FY25 (Rs -0.46 Cr) and H1 FY26 (Rs -8.17 Cr) while profit was positive; vendors carried the gap (payable days 164 in FY25 against a 60-75 day target). Management aims at 100 receivable days. The accounts cannot show which bill, at which client desk, since when. Top customer is 36% of revenue and the top ten 93%; Rs 100 Cr of IPO money is for working capital. Crestline would show the bill, the stage and the desk behind each of those days.
