# Claude Design master prompt: Polite Powertech working-capital review (one booklet, per-reader editions)

Paste everything below this line into Claude Design. Upload the Crestline logo file before you send it. There is no Polite Powertech logo file.



## 1. What you are building

A 16-page A4 portrait booklet titled "Working-capital review", printed and posted to named readers at Polite Powertech Limited. You build ONE design and produce one edition per reader listed in the reader table (see "Reader editions" below).
It answers one question for the reader: what if Crestline were already running at Polite Powertech, how much money and how much time would it save?
Every word and every figure is supplied in the Content section at the end of this prompt. Your job is the layout of these pages and their charts.

## 2. Reader editions

Build ONE 16-page design. Then, for each row of the reader table in the Content section, produce one edition of it.
- An edition changes ONLY three things: the cover's "PREPARED EXCLUSIVELY FOR" lines (that reader's name and title), the page 2 letter (that reader's letter text and address block), and the page 2 salutation.
- Every other page, word, figure, chart and position is IDENTICAL across editions. Do not tailor any body page to a reader.
- Name each edition after its reader as given in the table (for example "Edition: <reader name>"). That label is for the file only and is never printed.
- Body copy is written in a neutral voice. Quotes are credited by name and title, as the Content gives them; never rewrite a credit into "you" for any edition.

## 3. Rules you must follow

1. Use ONLY the text and figures in the Content section. Do not write new copy, captions, taglines, teasers, pull quotes or headings. If an item has no text, leave it empty.
2. Copy every figure exactly as written: same digits, same decimals, same currency sign, same unit. Do not round, re-total, convert or recompute anything.
3. Headlines exactly as given. Never reword them.
4. Footnotes exactly as given, on the page they belong to. Where a footnote carries a tag in brackets, print the caveat line that tag stands for (listed under "Keys" in the Content) in its place, in full.
5. Lines starting with ">" and text inside quotation marks in the Content are printed copy, word for word. A leading label inside a ">" line, such as KICKER:, HEADLINE:, Headline:, Lead:, Footer: or Vertical tagline:, names the position and is not printed. Lines starting with "-" under a Data heading give the labels and values to show. Words outside quotation marks and outside lines starting with ">" (for example "Data:", "Chart name:", "With Crestline:", "Footnote:", "How Crestline saves it" headings, "Kicker:") are instructions to you and are never printed.
6. No internal references in printed text. File names, tags in brackets, checker notes, status words used as markers (other than the tags "Published" and "Estimate" that the Content gives for the process map stages), and any note addressed to the team are never printed. A source is printed only in the words the Content gives for it.
7. No printed statements about the booklet's own gaps: never print what is missing, unknown, unchecked or left out ("not available", "we could not find", "TBD", "placeholder"). Where an item is empty, leave the space empty. Print a "Not published" or "Not public" line only where the Content gives it.
8. No em dashes and no en dashes anywhere. Use a comma, a full stop, "to" or a middle dot (·) as the Content does.
9. Ownership words stay as written: the client certifies and pays; Polite Powertech measures, prepares, submits and escalates. Do not swap them.
10. Use the Crestline logo files as supplied and never redraw them. Polite Powertech has no logo file: print its name as text only, and never draw, trace or imitate a company mark.

### Booklet accuracy rules (apply to every page, every edition)
A1. **Claims about Crestline must be true today.** Make no claim about Crestline beyond the pasted text. No certification "in progress", no hosting region, no encryption claim, no client names, no "live in X days", and no present-tense claim that Crestline connects to the company's SAP or ERP. The pilot is one entity, one segment, read-only, 14 days, as the Content words it.
A2. **Every quote names the right speaker**, exactly as the Content credits it. Never move a quote to another person.
A3. **Ours versus the client's follows the product rule.** In cash-in steps, acknowledged, certified and collected are the client's acts; measuring, preparing, reviewing, submitting and invoicing are the company's own. Keep every OURS and CLIENT tag as given.
A4. **No "nothing went wrong" headlines.** Use only the headlines given; never soften or invert one.
A5. **Values exactly as given.** Every value shown against a label matches the Data line for it. Every label carries its value.
A6. **Interest only at a rate the company discloses.** Print only the rate and interest figures the Content gives; never compute or label a rate as theirs.
A7. **Savings are scenarios.** Anything marked "With Crestline", "scenario" or "days less" is labelled "Scenario, not a forecast." and is never presented as a reported figure. Scenario rows overlap and are never added together; print no total the Content does not give.
A8. **Peers and comparatives** appear only where the Content prints them, with the vintage it gives. Never build a scorecard of missed promises.
A9. **No other company's data.** Do not bring in any company, name, figure or design text from anywhere other than the Content, including from earlier booklets or your memory. No demo numbers beside the company's figures.
A10. **Full address and correct names and titles** on the letter page and cover, exactly as the reader table gives them, per edition.
A11. **"You said" only for the reader's own words.** Never print "you said", "in your own words" or any second-person credit over a quote by someone other than that edition's reader. Use the speaker's name and title or the company's name as the Content gives it. If a headline says "your" or "you" about a quote, check it in every edition; if the quote is not that reader's, rewrite the credit to the speaker's name and title in that edition. Never print a note or flag on the page.

A12. **Never invent figures to fill a chart.** If the Content gives a stage or item without a value, show it without a number. Never create a split of a total, and never add a sentence about one.
A13. **No signed numbers in print.** Write inflow / outflow, increase / decrease. No "+" or "-" in front of a figure.

A14. **Every problem chart shows the outcome with Crestline.** Beside the "today" figure, show the "With Crestline" result from the Content, labelled with the days and cash it releases, plus "Scenario, not a forecast". Use only the values the Content gives; if the Content gives no scenario value for a chart, show none (rule A12).

## 4. Before you finish, check every page of every edition

1. Count the pages: exactly 16, A4 portrait, in the order of the Content.
2. Read every headline against the Content: same words, same punctuation.
3. Pick every figure on every page and find it in the Content, character for character. Any figure you cannot find there, delete it.
4. Search the whole booklet for en dash and em dash characters (Unicode 2013 and 2014). There must be none.
5. Every chart: each value matches its Data line, every label carries its value, nothing shown from a figure that is not in the Content.
6. Every scenario value carries "Scenario, not a forecast." No scenario totals added.
7. Every footnote is on its page, with each bracketed tag replaced by its full caveat line.
8. No word was added: no taglines, no extra captions, no "Live in" line, no certification or hosting claim, no client names, no internal references, no statements about gaps.
9. No Polite Powertech logo or mark appears anywhere. The Crestline logo files are as supplied.
10. The QR code on page 15 (and on page 16) opens crestlineintelligence.com/book-a-demo.
11. Instruction words from the Content ("Data:", "Chart name:", "With Crestline:", "Footnote:", "Kicker:", notes about decisions and checks) are not printed anywhere.
12. Editions: compare each edition with the first. Only the cover reader lines, the page 2 letter and the salutation differ. No "you said" sits over a quote that is not that edition's reader's own.
