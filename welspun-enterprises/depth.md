# Welspun Enterprises: story angle (depth)

Figures are in data.md with source and page; none are repeated here without it. Consolidated basis. EPC cash only: HAM annuity receivables and BOT equity are out of scope.

## Respect first (what is true and worth saying)

- A builder that has already run the full life of an asset twice. It sold a portfolio of road assets to Actis (gain Rs 483 Cr in FY23, Q4 FY26 deck p24, image-checked) and in Aug 2026 signed to divest Aunta-Simaria (call 5 Aug 2026; amount from the call text layer only).
- It turned itself from a road developer into a water, tunnelling and transport EPC house: FY26 segment revenue is Transport 1,424.98, Water 1,239.73, Tunnelling and Rehabilitation 950.67 (AR PDF p217).
- EBITDA grew from 391 (FY23) to 845 (FY26) while revenue was roughly flat in FY26 (-2%). Margin came from mix, not volume.
- Customers are central government, state companies and large municipal corporations (AR PDF p256). That is the best credit in the country and also the slowest to move paper.
- It is on SAP S/4HANA and says so in its own report and on its own call.

## The question (where do the bills wait?)

The margin is made on site. The cash is made on a desk. In FY26 three balances grew while revenue shrank:

| Balance | FY25 | FY26 |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | 3,695.34 | 3,615.38 (-2.2%) |
| Contract assets (work done, not yet billed) | 1,181.07 | 1,318.41 (+11.6%) |
| Trade receivables | 336.43 | 592.20 (+76.0%) |
| Receivables overdue by under six months (of gross) | 260.25 of 338.29 | 307.79 of 595.72 |

Nothing in the accounts says a single bill is wrong. What they cannot say is where each one is waiting. Contract assets are work done but unbilled: the wait is between the site, the measurement, the certificate and the invoice. Receivables are billed but not paid: the wait is the client's desk. The annual report gives one number for each, at one date, for the whole group.

Three things the accounts cannot answer, and the booklet should ask rather than assert:
1. **Which site?** Four customers are over 10% of revenue (Rs 2,559.53 Cr) but the report does not name them or their projects. The consolidated balance mixes UP Jal Jeevan, Mumbai plants, tunnelling and road EPC.
2. **Which bill, and at which desk?** Ageing is by due date, in buckets. A bucket does not say whether the bill is awaiting measurement, certification, a budget release or a signature.
3. **Since when?** A year-end balance cannot tell a three-week wait from a three-month one. Contract-asset days (computed 91 to 126) is an average over a year.

## Handling notes for the writer

- Do not call receivables "bad". The report's own ECL on receivables is 0.59% (AR p203); the customers pay. The point is the clock, not the credit.
- Do not call management's turnover ratios "days". They are turnover ratios; days are our arithmetic (data.md section 2) and each stands alone.
- Customer advances (contract liabilities Rs 901.49 Cr) fund much of the work. Say so: it is a reason the sequence of bills matters, not a sign of weakness.
- WMEL's own words on delay are about orders awaiting release (Q1 FY27 call), not payments. Do not stretch it.
- No interest-saved headline: the company has no EPC borrowing and no disclosed average rate (data.md section 3). Any money line needs a Garvis ruling first.
- Respectful tone on the numbers that fell: FY26 revenue met the company's own guidance of Rs 3,600 Cr (call 15 May 2026, Lalit Jain, p8 text layer); say that before the dip.
- Never imply Crestline sees their ERP. They have SAP S/4HANA; Crestline would be a read-only layer in a pilot.
