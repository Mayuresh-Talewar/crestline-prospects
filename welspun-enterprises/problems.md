# Welspun Enterprises: problems (WE-2 story)

Entity: Welspun Enterprises Limited, consolidated. "AR FY26" = Annual Report 2025-26 (consolidated statements signed 14 May 2026). Page = printed page, PDF page in brackets, as graded in data.md.

## Respect first
WEL holds an order book of Rs 18,729 Cr at 30 Jun 2026 (Q1 FY27 investor presentation, 4 Aug 2026, p7), earned a reported profit after tax of Rs 392.77 Cr in FY26 (AR FY26 p352, PDF p217) and EBITDA of Rs 845 Cr (Q4 FY26 investor presentation, 14 May 2026, p24), and runs on SAP S/4HANA (AR FY26 pp124-125, PDF p103). Its customers are central government agencies, state owned companies and large municipal corporations (AR FY26 p431, PDF p256). The credit is not the question. The clock is.

## Problem 1. Work done is growing while revenue is not
- Revenue from operations: Rs 3,695.34 Cr in FY25 (restated) to Rs 3,615.38 Cr in FY26, 2.2% lower (AR FY26 p262, PDF p172, year to 31 Mar 2026). Management's reason: "extended monsoons and longer project clearance cycle" (AR FY26 MD&A p122, PDF p102).
- Contract assets (work done, not yet billed): Rs 1,181.07 Cr at 31 Mar 2025 to Rs 1,318.41 Cr at 31 Mar 2026, 11.6% higher (AR FY26 p354, PDF p218). In FY26 cost plus profit of Rs 2,979.39 Cr was recognised and Rs 2,842.05 Cr was billed (same page).
- Contract asset days at 31 Mar 2026: 133.1 (closing contract assets / Rs 9.905 Cr a day; Crestline analytical measure, not an accounting standard; the report states no such days).
- What the accounts cannot say: which site, which measurement or certificate the work is waiting on, and since when.

## Problem 2. Billed, not yet paid, grew faster still
- Trade receivables: Rs 336.43 Cr to Rs 592.20 Cr, 76.0% higher (AR FY26 pp262 and 354, PDF pp172 and 218, 31 Mar 2025 and 31 Mar 2026).
- Receivable days, computed from the published turnover of 7.79 times (FY25: 8.83): 46.9 days (FY25: 41.3). History only; never added to contract asset days (AR FY26 p122 and p327, PDF pp102 and 204).
- The stated payment terms are a credit period of 45 to 90 days, with retention released at the end of the project (AR FY26 p431, PDF p256, standalone note). Payment is the client's act.
- What the accounts cannot say: which client, which bill, and how far inside or outside its own term.

## Problem 3. One number per balance, at one date, for the whole group
- The report gives one figure for each balance at 31 Mar 2026. It consolidates road, water and tunnelling contracts (segment revenue Rs 1,424.98 Cr, Rs 1,239.73 Cr, Rs 950.67 Cr; AR FY26 pp352-353, PDF p217). Four customers each exceed a tenth of revenue and are not named.
- A year-end balance cannot tell a three-week wait from a three-month one. That is a question for the dates in SAP S/4HANA, in a read-only pilot. No customer is named as slow: the filings do not say who is.
