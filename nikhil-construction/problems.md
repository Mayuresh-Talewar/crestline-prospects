# Nikhil Constructiongroup: problems (respect first)

Basis for every figure: standalone, year to 31 March, Rs Cr. Sources: CRISIL rating rationale of 5 Aug 2025 (FY24 and FY25 figures, GCA days, order book, limits) and CRISIL rating rationale of 2 Feb 2024 (FY22 and FY23 figures, earlier GCA days). Both re-read from the raw page text (see qa-report.md).

## Respect
A Pune company founded in 1995, now rated Crisil BBB+/Stable and A2 (reaffirmed 5 Aug 2025). Operating income Rs 309.5 Cr (FY23) to Rs 433.46 Cr (FY24) to Rs 447.15 Cr (FY25); reported PAT Rs 21.46 Cr to Rs 34.33 Cr to Rs 37.44 Cr. Rated bank limits raised to Rs 323 Cr from Rs 226 Cr. Order book over Rs 1,300 Cr at 30 Jun 2025. CRISIL calls the financial risk profile "above average" and the order book "healthy".

## Problem 1: the money a growing book needs is sitting in current assets
CRISIL: gross current assets "sizeable at 188-275 days for the three fiscals through 2025 with GCA days at 275 as on Mar-25" (5 Aug 2025). Earlier: 175-230 days for the three fiscals through 2023 (2 Feb 2024). Operating income over the same stretch rose from Rs 197.94 Cr (FY22) to Rs 447.15 Cr (FY25). One day of FY25 revenue is Rs 447.15 Cr / 365 = Rs 1.225 Cr, so ten days is Rs 12.25 Cr and thirty days is Rs 36.75 Cr (scenario, calc/nk-savings.out.txt).
What the accounts cannot say: which bills, deposits or retention make up the 275 days, on which site, at which client desk, since when. CRISIL publishes gross current assets days as one figure; no split is published, and 275 is not a debtor figure.

## Problem 2: the days are measured at a year-end that is heavy
CRISIL: "around 45% of revenue booking during last quarter of fiscal" (5 Aug 2025). Bills raised close to 31 March meet the 31 March snapshot, so the year-end figure carries the queue at its fullest. CRISIL also notes that security deposits given to institutions "add to the GCA days".
Cannot say: how much of the 275 is year-end timing and how much stays through the year; which deposits are due for release and when.

## Problem 3: a larger book is more bills to move
Orders over Rs 1,300 Cr at 30 Jun 2025, to be executed over the next 18 to 30 months (CRISIL, 5 Aug 2025), against revenue of Rs 447.15 Cr in FY25. Execution turns into measurement, RA bills, acknowledgement, certification and payment. CRISIL names working capital as the point to keep monitoring "as the business scales up".
Cannot say: where each bill waits as the book executes. Ownership: measuring, preparing, reviewing, submitting and invoicing are the company's; acknowledging, certifying and paying are the client's. CRISIL does not say who delays what, and neither does this booklet.
