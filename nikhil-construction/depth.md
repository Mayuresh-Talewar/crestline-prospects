# Nikhil Constructiongroup: story angle (respect first, then where bills wait)

All figures from data.md (CRISIL, 05 Aug 2025 and 02 Feb 2024; standalone).

## Respect first
- A Pune family firm founded in 1995 that is now an A2/BBB+ rated civil contractor: revenue Rs 197.94 Cr (FY22) to Rs 447.15 Cr (FY25), PAT Rs 9.67 Cr to Rs 37.44 Cr.
- Order book Rs 1,357 Cr (30 Jun 2025), about three years of work, 35-40% from NHAI/MoRTH, rest PWD and municipal corporations.
- Rated limits raised from Rs 226 Cr to Rs 323 Cr: lenders (Bank of Maharashtra, HDFC, ICICI) are extending more, which says the book is trusted.
- Debt stayed modest: debt / net worth 0.99x, utilisation about 62%.

## Then the question
The rater's one named weakness is working capital: gross current assets of 275 days at March 2025 (188-275 across three years), up against receivables of 21-73 days and inventory of 57-61 days. The rater also notes about 45% of revenue is booked in the last quarter and that security deposits to institutions (PMC, PCMC) add to those days.
Question for the reader: with a book three times the year's revenue to execute, which bills, deposits and retention are waiting, at which client desk, and since when? The accounts give a range of days; they do not say site, bill or desk.

## Cautions for the writer
- GCA days are one number for current assets, not a debtor figure; never present 275 as DSO.
- "Margins 14.7%" is unlabelled; do not call it EBITDA.
- No creditor days, no ERP, no interest rate: do not print any.
- Never state who delays payment as fact; CRISIL does not.
- Related supplier Nikhil Infraspecialities: keep out (sensitive file).
