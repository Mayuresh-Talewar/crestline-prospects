# Patel Infrastructure Limited: depth.md (story angle, step 1)

Respect first: a 1972 family firm that grew revenue from operations from Rs 1,984 Cr (FY23) to Rs 3,577 Cr (FY25), holds an AA-class contractor status with Gujarat R&B, runs SAP S/4 HANA live at head office and project sites (AR), and carries an order book of Rs 14,153 Cr (Dec 2025), mostly NHAI, MSRDC and GUVNL. It sold two HAM SPVs (Rs 303 Cr, Note 13.2) and set up a renewable arm.

The question (where bills wait, from the filings only):
1. Built but not billed: unbilled revenue Rs 657 Cr at March 2025 is 67 days of revenue (89 in FY24, 94 in FY23). CARE names the cause: Ganga Expressway change-of-scope approval pending.
2. Billed but not paid: receivables Rs 316 Cr, 32 days of revenue, up from 22; 95% under six months from due date.
3. Material in the yard: inventory Rs 464 Cr, 133 days of material consumed (down from 192).
4. Together: about 193 days from material bought to cash in, against 39 days of vendor credit on the construction-cost base (FY25), by Crestline's arithmetic on the standalone accounts.
5. Cash: operating cash was minus Rs 205 Cr in FY23, plus Rs 163 Cr in FY24, plus Rs 37 Cr in FY25; borrowings Rs 1,121 Cr; fund-based limits 97% used (CARE); rating cut to BBB+ on working-capital intensity.
6. What the accounts cannot say: which RA bill, which site and which desk the unbilled Rs 657 Cr is sitting at, how long since, and who at the client must certify. Retention is not shown apart from deposits.
Angle: the company is already building scale; the unanswered thing is the bill-level clock behind one number. Do not claim a saving; scenarios only, on the 67-day and 32-day legs.
