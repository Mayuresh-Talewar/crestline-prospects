# Patel Infrastructure Limited: problems.md (PI-S23)

Basis: standalone, ₹ lakh as printed, shown in ₹ Cr, 365 days, year-end balances. Documents: Annual Report FY25 (year to 31 March 2025, audit report signed 21 July 2025), "AR25"; Annual Report FY24 (signed 4 September 2024), "AR24". Days are Crestline's arithmetic on the published accounts.

Respect first. Revenue from operations grew from ₹1,983.80 Cr (FY23) to ₹2,950.74 Cr to ₹3,576.65 Cr (FY25), profit after tax from ₹62.02 Cr to ₹72.60 Cr to ₹123.22 Cr (AR24 p.62, AR25 p.54), and the cash cycle shortened at every year end, 266.7 to 226.4 to 193.2 days. SAP S/4HANA is live at the head office and project sites (AR25 p.21). The company is already managing the cycle. The questions below ask where the next days sit.

## Problem 1. Built work waiting to be certified and billed: 67.1 days, ₹657.48 Cr
- Unbilled revenue (contract assets) ₹657.48 Cr at 31 March 2025, from ₹716.33 Cr and ₹509.10 Cr (AR25 p.81 Note 15, p.101 Note 33b; AR24 p.86). That is 67.1 days of revenue, from 88.6 and 93.7 days.
- Roll (Note 33b): opening ₹716.33 Cr + revenue recognised ₹3,415.44 Cr − invoiced ₹3,474.28 Cr = ₹657.48 Cr (the printed lines differ by ₹0.01 Cr through rounding).
- Cannot say from the accounts: which bills, which sites, which client desk must certify, how long each has waited, and which step is the company's own.
- Ours and the client's: measure, prepare, review, submit and invoice are ours; acknowledge and certify are the client's.

## Problem 2. Project materials in stock: 132.7 days, ₹464.31 Cr
- Inventory ₹464.31 Cr at 31 March 2025, from ₹499.72 Cr and ₹325.31 Cr (AR25 p.53; AR24 p.61). On material consumed ₹1,277.57 Cr (Note 29, AR25 p.98) that is 132.7 days, from 182.5 and 192.1 days.
- Cannot say: which sites hold it, which materials have sat unissued, and which stock to draw down first.
- Priced only on material consumed per day (₹3.500 Cr), never on revenue.

## Problem 3. Billed and waiting to be paid: 32.3 days, ₹316.19 Cr
- Receivables ₹316.19 Cr, 32.3 days, up from 22.0 days a year earlier and down from 97.2 days two years earlier. Of ₹318.27 Cr gross, ₹303.76 Cr is under six months from its due date and ₹14.51 Cr is older (Note 11.6, AR25 p.79); a year earlier ₹14.02 Cr was older.
- Cannot say: which client, which invoice, against which payment term (none is published).
- Because no client term is published, collection is shown at a ten day step only. Thirty days would take it to 2.3 days.

## What is not raised
Vendor credit (38.9 days, from 116.3) is shown, never priced, and never better when higher. Retention is not priced: the accounts print only a combined line with security deposits. Interest is priced only at the disclosed 12.75% on the NCDs, counted up to ₹173.62 Cr.
