# Webapp fit: Velji Ratna Sorathia Infra (VR-S23)

Crestline would sit above the company's ERP (not named in any source) and answer where each bill waits. Fit is real for an EPC contractor billing RA bills to government clients at FY25 turnover of Rs 1,062.54 Cr (MGT-7). Fit is unproven on data: the annual return carries no balance sheet.

| Screen | Needs from the source | Public today |
|---|---|---|
| Working Capital, cash-cycle diagram, simulator | stock, unbilled, receivables, payables and rates | Turnover only. Value of a day computed. |
| O2C, Process map | bill events and dates | None. Every stage "Not public". |
| P2P, Vendors | orders, payables, vendor terms | None. |
| Client | client terms, payment dates | None. |
| Tickets, Ask | any measured figure | Sample finding on turnover growth only. |

Pilot scope as ruled in the pack: one to three EPC projects, read-only, 14 days, once connected.
