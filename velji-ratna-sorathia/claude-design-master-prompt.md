## 1. What you are building

A 16-page A4 portrait booklet titled "Working-capital review", printed and posted to a named reader at Velji Ratna Sorathia Infra Private Limited. You build one edition for the reader in the reader table (see "Reader editions" below).
It answers one question for the reader: what if Crestline were already running at Velji Ratna Sorathia Infra, how much money and how much time would it save?
Every word and every figure is supplied in the Content section at the end of this prompt. This prompt carries content only; the design is yours.

## 2. Reader editions

Build the 16-page booklet, then one edition for each row of the reader table in the Content section.
- An edition changes only three things: the cover's "PREPARED EXCLUSIVELY FOR" lines (the reader's name and title), the page 2 letter (the letter text and address block), and the page 2 salutation.
- Every other page, word and figure is identical across editions.
- Name each edition after its reader as given in the table (for example "Edition: <reader name>"). That label is for the file only and is never printed.
- Body copy is written in a neutral voice. Quotes are credited by name and title, as the Content gives them.

## 3. Rules you must follow

1. Use only the text and figures in the Content section. Do not write new copy, captions, taglines, teasers, pull quotes or headings. If something has no text, leave it empty.
2. Copy every figure exactly as written: same digits, same decimals, same currency sign, same unit. Do not round, re-total, convert or recompute anything.
3. Page headings exactly as given in each page's HEADLINE line, never reworded.
4. Footnotes exactly as given, on the page they belong to. Where a footnote carries a tag in square brackets, print the caveat line that tag stands for (listed at the top of the Content, under "Caveat lines") in its place, in full.
5. Lines in the Content that start with ">" are printed copy. Lines that do not start with ">" (for example "Chart name:", "No numbers on this page.", notes in plain text) are instructions to you and are never printed. Words such as KICKER, HEADLINE, HERO, TO, FROM, Footer, Footnote and Data label a line for you and are not printed as labels unless the text after them says so.
6. No internal references in printed text: no file names, no tags in square brackets, and no note addressed to the team. A source is printed only in the words the Content gives for it.
7. No printed negatives about the booklet itself: never print what is missing, unknown, unchecked or left out ("not available", "we could not find", "TBD", "placeholder"). Where nothing is given, leave it empty. The words "Not public" and "Not published" are printed only where the Content gives them as copy.
8. No em dashes and no en dashes anywhere. Use a comma, a full stop, "to" or a middle dot (·) as the Content does.
9. Ownership words stay as written: the client certifies and pays; Velji Ratna Sorathia Infra measures, prepares, submits and invoices. Do not swap them.
10. No Velji Ratna Sorathia Infra logo is used. The cover shows the reader's name and title only, with no company mark.

B1. **"Not public" and "Not published" are the answer, not a gap to fill.** Every stage of the Process map reads "Not public". Never add a day count, an estimate, a range or an "illustrative split" to any stage, and never add a sentence about one.
B2. **Series are data.** The turnover and net worth for FY21 to FY25 are printed exactly as given, nothing else is derived from them.
B3. **Only the figures given.** The only per-day figure is ₹2.9111 Cr and the only scenario figures are ₹29.11 Cr (ten days) and ₹87.33 Cr (thirty days). No other scenario, no interest, no total across rows.

### Booklet accuracy rules (apply to every page, every edition)
A1. **Claims about Crestline must be true today.** Make no claim about Crestline beyond the pasted text. No certification, no hosting region, no encryption claim, no client names, no "live in X days", and no present-tense claim that Crestline connects to the company's ERP. The pilot is one to three EPC projects, read-only, 14 days, as the Content words it.
A2. **Every quote names the right speaker**, exactly as the Content credits it.
A3. **Ours versus the client's follows the product rule.** In cash-in steps, acknowledged, certified and collected are the client's acts; measuring, preparing, reviewing, submitting and invoicing are the company's own. Keep every owner tag as given.
A4. **No "nothing went wrong" headlines.** Use only the headlines given.
A6. **Interest only at a rate the company discloses.** None is disclosed, so no interest figure appears anywhere.
A7. **Savings are scenarios.** Anything marked "with Crestline", "scenario" or "days less" carries "Scenario, not a forecast." beside it and must never read as a reported figure. Scenario rows overlap and are never added together; print no total the Content does not give.
A8. **Peers and comparatives** appear only where the Content prints them. None are printed.
A9. **No other company's data.** Do not bring in any company, name, figure or text from anywhere other than the Content, including from earlier booklets or your memory. No demonstration figures beside the company's figures.
A10. **Full address and correct names and titles** on the letter page and cover, exactly as the reader table gives them.
A11. **"You said" only for the reader's own words.** Never print "you said" or "in your own words" over words that are not the reader's.
A12. **Never invent figures to fill a chart.** If the Content gives a stage, step or row without a value, print it without a number.
A13. **No signed numbers in print.** Write inflow / outflow, increase / decrease. No "+" or "-" in front of a figure.

## 4. Before you finish, check every page

1. Count the pages: exactly 16, A4 portrait, in the order of the Content.
2. Read every page heading against its HEADLINE line: same words, same punctuation.
3. Pick every figure on every page and find it in the Content, character for character. Any figure you cannot find there, delete it.
4. Search the whole booklet for en dash and em dash characters (Unicode 2013 and 2014). There must be none.
5. Every chart or table: every value is the Content's value, nothing is drawn from a figure that is not in the Content.
6. Every scenario carries "Scenario, not a forecast." No scenario totals added.
7. Every footnote is on its page, with each bracketed tag replaced by its full caveat line.
8. No word was added: no taglines, no right-hand footer teasers, no extra captions, no "Live in" line, no certification or hosting claim, no client names, no internal references, no printed negatives.
9. No company logo of Velji Ratna Sorathia Infra appears. Nothing is redrawn.
10. The QR code on page 15 (and on page 16) opens crestlineintelligence.com/book-a-demo.
11. Instruction words from the Content (labels such as KICKER, HEADLINE, Chart name, Data, and any plain-text note) are not printed.
12. Compare each edition with the first. Only the cover reader lines, the page 2 letter and the salutation differ.
