# Problems (VR-S23), respect first

1. **Size has doubled, and the days are invisible.** Turnover Rs 512.23 Cr (FY21) to Rs 1,062.54 Cr (FY25), net worth Rs 58.00 Cr to Rs 293.18 Cr (Form MGT-7 annual returns, vrsipl.com, read 12 Oct 2026). At that size one day of turnover is Rs 2.9111 Cr. The returns say nothing about how many days a bill waits.
2. **A large order book needs billing to keep pace.** Unexecuted order book Rs 3,329.77 Cr at 30 Jun 2024, six states, eight verticals (Infomerics, 3 Dec 2024, the agency's figure). More work in more places means more bills in more queues. Where they wait is not public.
3. **Who holds each wait is not public either.** No client terms, retention, vendor terms or payables are published, so ownership (ours: measure, prepare, review, submit, invoice; client's: acknowledge, certify, pay) cannot be assigned from filings. The pilot would do it from the ERP's own dates.

No figure here is paired with any other basis. Nothing sensitive is used.
