# B. G. Shirke: depth (SH-1)

Refs R1 to R10, C1, I1, W1 as in data.md. Quotes are copied from the agency's own release text (Ind-Ra JSON page source; CARE and ICRA image-checked). Every number in my own words is a row in src/sh-calc-output.txt.

## 1. Working capital and liquidity, in the agencies' own words

**Ind-Ra, 20 Feb 2026 (R1), the latest:**
- Gross working capital "increased to 44% in FY25 (FY24: 36%; FY23: 31%), primarily due to higher receivable days, which were largely related to the spilling over of receivables into the next financial year."
- Net working capital "remained stable at 17% in FY25 (FY24: 17%; FY23: 14%), aided by higher mobilisation advances."
- "BGSCTPL's net working capital cycle has elongated from a low single-digit historically, as the company takes advantage of price discounts by timely/advance payments to vendors."
- "Ind-Ra expects the company's working capital requirements to remain broadly in line with FY25 levels over the medium term."
- Liquidity "Adequate": fund based limits used 27% and non fund based 65% on average over the 12 months to December 2025; unencumbered cash including mutual funds INR 15.48 bn at 1HFY26.

**Ind-Ra, 6 Dec 2024 (R2):** most authorities "have started charging interest on mobilisation advances, making them less commercially attractive; only Maharashtra Housing and Area Development Authority's (MHADA) and City and Industrial Development Corporation's (CIDCO) mobilisation advances do not entail an interest pay-out."

**Ind-Ra, 29 Jun 2021 (R6):** "despite an increase in the inventory days (including unbilled revenue) to 227 days in FY21 (FY20: 192 days), the overall net working capital days improved to 18 days (25 days), led by a rise in the payable days (including mobilisation advances) to 209 days (167 days)."

**CARE, 27 Jul 2018 (C1 p2, consolidated):** headed "Stretched collection period in FY18". Collection period "remains stretched at 115 days for the year ended FY18 factoring in unbilled revenue and retention money receivables". Also names "recovery of stuck receivables in EPC business" as a key sensitivity.

**ICRA, Jan 2012 (I1 p5):** delays "increase working capital requirements due to build up of inventory/receivables"; working capital intensity went "from 11% in FY 2010 to 17% in FY 2011" and to "44% in the first half of the current fiscal", on "largely uncertified works" at one state housing client.

What this means for Crestline, plainly: the agency names the exact mechanism (receivables spilling over a year end, unbilled and uncertified work, advances doing the funding). It does not name which site, which bill or which desk. That gap is the pitch.

## 3. Rating sensitivities (R1, 20 Feb 2026)

- Positive: sustaining profitability while "maintaining or improving diversification across segments, geographies, and counterparties"; and "a continued improvement in the governance structure, including stronger availability of high quality financial and operational data".
- Negative: "a decline in the profitability and/or an elongation of the working capital cycle"; "any material fund outflows to any group companies/ promoter payouts"; net adjusted leverage above 0.75x and/or total outside liabilities to EBITDA above 3.5x, sustained.

Two of these speak directly to Crestline's job: "elongation of the working capital cycle" is a downgrade trigger, and "high quality financial and operational data" is an upgrade trigger. Both were also in R2.
