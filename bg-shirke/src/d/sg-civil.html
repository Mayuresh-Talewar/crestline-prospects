CIVIL DIVISION

What began as a dream in 1944 has become a concrete reality over 7 decades. It has been our company’s mission to invent new technologies that aid in civil engineering and help it grow as an industry, and keeping it toe to toe with the needs of this country.

With massive integrated housing projects, towering commercial buildings, sophisticated special purpose constructions, modern airports, luxurious hospitality complexes, stylish malls, smart IT infrastructure and distinguished flyovers, bridges and highways , we have left our mark on history while we sustainably build for the future.
