## 2 The trend (one basis at a time; bases are never mixed)

**Basis A: RHP restated consolidated, company formula (D1 p38), FY24 to FY26**

* Receivable days 77, 67, 88. Payable days 91, 107, 109. Inventory days 71, 109, 66. Net working capital days 57, 69, 45.
* Receivables doubled in FY26 (Rs 50.77 Cr to Rs 108.96 Cr, growth 114.6%) while revenue grew 64.2% (gw-calc).
* Past due more than 6 months was Rs 14.84 Cr, Rs 11.65 Cr and Rs 11.95 Cr in FY24, FY25, FY26, so its share of receivables fell from 23.0% to 11.0% (gw-calc).

**Basis B: same accounts, what the company formula omits (D1 p369 and p39)**

* Contract assets (unbilled plus retention pending milestone): 64.6, 53.8, 39.3 days of revenue for FY24, FY25, FY26. Receivables plus contract assets: 140.8, 120.3, 126.3 days (gw-calc).
* Retention at 31 Mar 2026: Rs 35.96 Cr, 28.7 days of FY26 revenue, 42.8% of it held more than 12 months (gw-calc).
* Contract liabilities (advances and billing in excess) Rs 88.00 Cr at 31 Mar 2026, which the company formula also omits; this is the customer funding CRISIL refers to.

**Basis C: cash (D4 slide 28, Rs Cr)**: working capital absorbed Rs 21.4 Cr in FY24, released Rs 2.6 Cr in FY25 and absorbed Rs 17.7 Cr in FY26. Operating cash flow was 87.5% of PAT in FY26 against 126.9% in FY25 (gw-calc).

**Basis D: rating agencies, own GCA measure** (CRISIL and Acuite, standalone until 2025, combined from Apr 2026): 827 days FY21 (Acuite) to 179 days at 31 Mar 2024 (CRISIL 2025) and "186-240 days" for the three years to FY25 (CRISIL 2026). The two CRISIL statements are in the same family; the Acuite figure is another agency's computation.

Reading: the headline 45 day NWC in FY26 looks like the best of three years, but on the same accounts receivables plus unbilled and retention sit at 126 days of revenue, receivables grew much faster than revenue, and working capital took cash in FY26. The accounts cannot say which project, which client, which bill or since when.

## 3 Disconfirm step (one search per headline fact)

1. **"Working capital improved in FY26 (45 days)."** Test: the company's own cash flow. Result: FY26 working capital change was an outflow of Rs 17.7 Cr (D4 slide 28) and receivable days rose from 67 to 88 (D1 p38). Finding holds only on the company's formula; it is not an improvement in cash terms.
2. **Receivable days.** Test: the rating rationale. Conflict: D1 p38 prints 77 (FY24) and 67 (FY25); CRISIL D6 says "debtor days of more than 100 days" over the three years to FY25. CRISIL D7 notes its debtors include retention money, which would explain the gap, but neither source reconciles it. Both are reported; neither is picked.
3. **Borrowings and lenders.** Test: rating annexure against the RHP. Conflict: D6 (2 Apr 2026) lists cash credit of Rs 15.25 Cr from Bank of India and Rs 30 Cr from TJSB Sahakari Bank; D1 p360 says the Bank of India and TJSB cash credit facilities "had been surrendered during the year ended 31 March 2025", and D1 p434 (3 Aug 2026) lists only the ICICI Bank cash credit and a Bank of India overdraft. Reported as a conflict inside the sources; the RHP is the later, certified document.
4. **FY25 revenue.** Test: rating table. D6 prints operating income Rs 278.57 Cr for FY25 (combined); D1 p84 prints Rs 2,783.27 Mn. Same order; small definitional difference not reconciled. The dossier's Rs 244.7 Cr (DRHP) could not be tested: DRHP not read.
5. **CFO.** Test: post listing filing. D5 (17 Sep 2026) names Sanjay Sawant as CFO; D1 p323 shows Vivek Baid resigned 31 Jul 2026. Holds.
6. **Order book is live.** Test: company stock exchange filings 17 Sep to 6 Oct 2026 (13 PDFs). No cancellation, termination or short closure. D3 and D4 repeat "Rs. 982 crores". Holds.
7. **SAP.** Test: full text search of D1 and D4. SAP confirmed (D1 p281); no version named. S/4HANA stays unsupported.
