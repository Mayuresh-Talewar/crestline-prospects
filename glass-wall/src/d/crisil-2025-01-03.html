Detailed Rationale

CRISIL Ratings has reaffirmed its ‘CRISIL BBB+/Stable/CRISIL A2’ ratings on the bank facilities of Glass Wall Systems India Private Limited (GWSIPL).

 

The rating reflects GWSIPL's extensive industry experience of the promoters and moderate financial profile. These strengths are partially offset by its exposure to inherent cyclicality in demand and volatility in raw material prices and moderate working capital cycle

Analytical Approach

CRISIL Ratings has evaluated the standalone business and financial risk profiles of GWSIPL.

Key Rating Drivers & Detailed Description

Strengths:

  • Extensive industry experience of the promoters: The promoters have experience of over three decades in façade engineering industry. This has given them an understanding of the dynamics of the market and enabled them to establish relationships with suppliers and customers.

 

  • Moderate financial profile: GWSIPL capital structure has been at moderate healthy level due to limited reliance on external funds yielding gearing of 0.16 and total outside liabilities to adj tangible net worth (TOL/ANW) of 1.18 for year ending on 31st March 2024. GWSIPL’s debt protection measures have also been at comfortable level despite leverage due to moderately healthy profitability. The interest coverage and net cash accrual to total debt (NCATD) ratio are at 5.22 times and 1.18 times for fiscal 2024. GWSIPL debt protection measures are expected to remain at a similar level over medium term.

 

Weaknesses:

  • Exposure to inherent cyclicality in demand and volatility in raw material prices: The facade engineering industry is cyclical and moves in-line with the level of activity in the construction sector. The company didn’t have an escalation clause in contract due to which there were operating loss in fiscal 2021 & 2022 and modest operating margin of around 4.3% in fiscal 2023. To mitigate that the company has an escalation clause in their contracts. Escalation clause and increasing contribution from export segment, operating margin improved to ~15% in fiscal 2024. Sustenance of improved operating margin while maintaining scale will remain key monitorable.

 

  • Moderate working capital cycle: Gross current assets were at 180-240 days over the three fiscals ended March 31, 2024. Its moderate working capital management is reflected in its gross current assets (GCA) of 179 days as on March 31, 2024. The company also has debtors more than 6 months which also consists of retention money. No major bad debts expected over the medium term. Working capital requirements is also supported by advances from customers along with internal accruals. Limited utilization of working capital limits from bank provides financial flexibility. Working capital cycle is expected to remain moderate and in the similar range over the medium term.

Liquidity: Adequate

Liquidity is adequate marked net cash accruals expected to be over Rs 48 crores against repayment obligations of Rs 1-3 crores over medium term. Bank Limit Utilization is 20.6% utilized for the last 11 months ending October 2024. The company has cash and cash equivalent of Rs 51.75 crores as on September 30,2024 out of which Rs 28.47 crores is unencumbered cash.

Outlook: Stable

CRISIL Ratings believe GWSIPL will continue to benefit from the extensive experience of its promoter, and established relationships with clients.

Rating sensitivity factors

Upward factors

  • Improvement in scale of operation and sustenance of operating margins leading to higher-than-expected accruals.
  • Improvement in working capital cycle with GCA below 150 days.

 

Downward factors

  • Decline in net cash accruals on account of decline in revenue or operating profits below 15%
  • Large debt-funded capital expenditure weakens capital structure
  • Witnesses a substantial increase in its working capital requirements thus weakening its liquidity & financial profile.

About the Company

GWSIPL was established as a partnership firm later in 2002 and was converted into private limited company in 2010. It undertakes turnkey projects for facade construction for which it undertakes design, manufacturing and installation of facade systems.
