Detailed Rationale

Crisil Ratings has upgraded its ratings on the bank facilities of Glass Wall Systems India Limited (GWSPL – part of GW group, formerly known as Glass Wall Systems India Private Limited) to ‘Crisil A-/Stable/Crisil A2+’ from ‘Crisil BBB+/Stable/Crisil A2.

 

The rating upgrade factors in strengthening the group’s business risk profile owing to healthy operating performance while maintaining comfortable financial risk profile.

 

Group’s market position has improved, driven by increasing demand from both domestic and export customers and ability to pass on US tariffs. Group has reported the operating performance of Rs 278.6 crores in fiscal 2025 against Rs 304 croroes in fiscal 2024. Decline in revenue is on account of US election leading to shifting of billing in fiscal 2026. Group is expected to report the double-digit growth in fiscal 2026 and fiscal 2027 primarily driven by comfortable unexecuted orderbook of more than Rs 600 crores as on date.

 

Operating margins are also on improving trend for the past 3 fiscal year ended fiscal 2025 at 26.39% against 17.94% in fiscal 2024 and 4.95% in fiscal 2023 and same is expected to sustain above 23% over the medium term. Improvement in operating margin is on account of better yield per out, increasing export market share and customized architectural façade solution, price escalation clause coverage leading higher margins.

 

There can be supply chain disruption due to ongoing Middles East escalation  for finished glass products, however company is expected to be covered for the next 3 to 4 months in the market and order backed contracts with its supplier. However, same will remain monitorable with the ongoing developments.

 

The rating reflects GWSIL’s extensive industry experience of the promoters and moderate financial profile. These strengths are partially offset by their exposure to inherent cyclicality in demand and volatility in raw material prices and moderate working capital cycle

Analytical Approach

Crisil Rating has revised its analytical approach, has combined the business and financial risk profile of GWSIL and Yes Systems Private Limited (YSL). Earlier, Crisil Ratings had considered the standalone business and financial risk profiles of GWSIL. The revision in analytical approach is based on 100% acquisition of the group company YSL with the share swap agreement of Rs 155 crores.

 

Please refer Annexure - List of Entities Consolidated, which captures the list of entities considered and their analytical treatment of consolidation.

Key Rating Drivers - Strengths 

Established market presence backed by extensive experience of promoters: The promoters have experience of over three decades in façade engineering industry. This has given them an understanding of the dynamics of the market and enabled them to establish relationships with suppliers and customers. Along with a professional management set-up, the promoters have developed an extensive network of suppliers and customers in the domestic as well as export markets. The promoters' track record and ability to manufacture tailor-made products providing an advantage, especially when understanding price and demand trends of glasses and metal is critical.

 

Comfortable financial risk profile: Financial risk profile of GW group continues to remain comfortable with marginal utilisation of debt (both short term and long term). Networth remain strong at Rs 175 croroes as on March31 2025 and Rs 260 croroes as on December 31, 2025. Capital structure remains comfortable with gearing at 0.05 and total outside liability to adjusted networth at remained around 0.8 times as on March 31, 2025. Capital structure is expected to improve over the medium term with no debt funded capex plans. Debt protection metrics remain strong with interest coverage expected to remain above 30 times over the medium term.

Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses 

Exposure to inherent cyclicality in demand and volatility in raw material prices: The facade engineering industry is cyclical and moves in-line with the level of activity in the construction & real estate sector. The company didn’t have an escalation clause in contract due to which there were operating losses in fiscal 2021 & 2022 and modest operating margin of around 4.3% in fiscal 2023. To mitigate that the company has an escalation clause in their contracts. Post the escalation clause and increasing contribution from export segment, operating margin improved to 17.94% in fiscal 2024 to 26.39% in fiscal 2025. Due to the ongoing war Middle East escalation, sustenance of operating margin above 23% while maintaining scale will remain key monitorable.

 

Moderate working capital cycle: Working capital operation continues to remain moderate with gross current asset days between 186-240 days for the past 3 fiscal year ended 2025. It majorly comprises of debtor days of more than 100 days and inventory period of 90 days. The company also has debtors more than 180 days of Rs 8 to 9 crores, however no major bad debts are expected over the medium term. Working capital requirements are also supported by advances from customers along with internal accruals. Limited utilization of working capital limits from bank provides financial flexibility. Working capital cycle is expected to remain moderate and in the similar range over the medium term.

Liquidity Strong

Liquidity is marked by cash accruals of more than Rs 70 croroes against the repayment obligation of Rs 1.7 crores to Rs 2 crores. Bank limit utilisation has remained low at 9% for fund-based limits and 48% of non-fund-based limits for the past 6 months ended February 2026. Company has a cash and bank balance of Rs 120 croroes as on December 2025 out of which Rs 25 croroes is unencumbered and current ratio stood at 1.7 times as on March 31, 2025. With no debt funded capex planned and minimal obligation liquidity profile is expected to remain comfortable over the medium term.

Outlook Stable

Crisil Ratings believe GWSIL will continue to benefit from the extensive experience of its promoter, and established relationships with clients.

Rating sensitivity factors

Upward factors

  • Improvement in scale of operation and sustenance of operating margins above 23% leading to cash accruals above Rs 120 crores.
  • Sustenance of financial risk profile.
  • Improvement in working capital cycle with GCA below 150 days.

 

Downward factors

  • Decline in net cash accruals on account of decline in revenue or operating profits below 15%
  • Large debt-funded capital expenditure weakens capital structure
  • Witnesses a substantial increase in its working capital requirements thus weakening its liquidity & financial profile.

About the Company

GWSPL, a major facade contractor and turnkey solutions provider, has undergone a transformation from a partnership firm to a public limited company, preparing for an Initial Public Offering (IPO) in late 2025. The company was originally established as a partnership firm in 2002 before becoming a private limited company in 2010. The company operates in India, the US, and Australia. In August 2025 GWSIL has acquired YSL. Company has also filed DRHP iin September 2025 for listing.
