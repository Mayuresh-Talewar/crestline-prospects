# Effwa: story angle (Step 1)

## Respect first
- Two promoters with over three decades each in wastewater, who took the company public in July 2024 and have since grown revenue from Rs 145.16 Cr (FY24) to Rs 253.29 Cr (FY26), with PAT Rs 28.62 Cr and a first dividend (AR FY26).
- Repeat clients are the proof: CRISIL counts 29+ projects completed for Tata Steel, the 4th project for Afcons and the 7th contract for SAIL (09 Jan 2026), plus IOCL, BPCL and now a Rs 313 Cr JSW Steel order.
- Engineering-led: about 75% of 145 staff are engineers; ZLD design in house, which CRISIL credits with limiting subcontracting risk.
- Operating cash flow turned positive in FY26 (Rs 29.43 Cr), and receivables grew 9.5% against revenue growth of 36.8%. Management said it was the result of "stringent credit control".

## Then the question: where do the bills wait?
Anchor facts (all FY26 standalone, AR FY26 unless stated):
1. Rs 128.71 Cr is owed by clients, 185.5 days of revenue on the closing balance. A year earlier it was 231.9 days, two years earlier 168.3.
2. Rs 34.82 Cr (27.1%) sits more than six months past its due date, up from Rs 21.87 Cr (18.6%) a year earlier. The company shows all of it as undisputed and good, so these are bills waiting, not bills in dispute.
3. Rs 28-30 Cr of the receivable is retention, released after the defect liability period; the company is trying to replace it with bank guarantees and "a couple of clients" have agreed.
4. Year-end billing peaks: 74% of H2 FY25 revenue was booked in the last quarter (call 09 May 2025). A bill raised in March is the one that waits.
5. Order book about Rs 750 Cr heading to Rs 1,000 Cr, with 12-36 month projects and a stated target of Rs 350 Cr+ revenue next year. Rated limits of Rs 230 Cr (CRISIL) mean the Rs 160 Cr guarantee line and Rs 67 Cr cash credit carry all of it.

Questions the accounts cannot answer (the Crestline gap): which site, which milestone, which client desk, which certificate is the one in the six-month-plus bucket; how long each of certification, submission and client payment takes; which part of the Rs 34.82 Cr is retention (not due) versus a certified bill (due); how much of the delay is Effwa's own paperwork versus the client's queue. With an ERP implemented in FY26 the records exist; what is missing is the time-and-desk view above it.

## Respectful framing rules
- Never "Effwa is slow to collect". Say: public-sector clients (61% of revenue) pay on their own calendar; retention is contractual; year-end billing is a feature of milestone EPC.
- No claims about their ERP's quality; say "the ERP records what happened; Crestline reads how long each step took and whose desk it sat on".
- Interest only at a disclosed rate: none found, so no interest figure.
