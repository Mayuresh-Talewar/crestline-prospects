# Effwa Infra & Research Limited: where Crestline fits (EF-S23, 2026-10-09)

Basis: standalone, audited, FY ended 31 March 2026. Source: Annual Report 2025-26 (13th), accounts signed 12 May 2026, filed September 2026. Screens below exist in the app today (checked against lib/navigation.ts and the product context).

## What Effwa is, in the terms the app uses
- Water and wastewater EPC with operation and maintenance. Work is billed by milestone, on projects of 12 to 36 months, at five to six sites (management, as of May 2026).
- One legal entity, no subsidiaries. So one portfolio, a handful of projects: the project-scoped screens are the natural unit.
- An ERP was introduced in FY26 (Chairperson's letter, AR FY26). Vendor not stated, so none is named anywhere. The pitch is Crestline above it, read-only, alongside it.

## Screens that answer Effwa's question (where does the next ten days sit, bill by bill)
| Effwa question | Screen | What it would show once connected |
|---|---|---|
| Which bills are waiting, for how long | O2C (Cash In), project scope | each bill rebuilt from its own dates: prepared, submitted, acknowledged, certified, invoiced, paid |
| Whose move ends each wait | O2C process view | the wait is the client's if it ends in their act (acknowledge, certify, pay), Effwa's if it ends in measure, prepare, review, submit or invoice |
| What is ten days worth | Working Capital, cycle simulator | receivable leg moved at its measured daily rate; stops at the contract's own payment term |
| Who should act | Actionables, Raise ticket, Alert owner | named bills with a deadline, an owner and a baseline |
| Did it move | Alerts & Tickets, auditor | re-measures the same figure from fresh data, closes only on the number |
| Ask in words | Ask Crestline | "What if bills were paid ten days sooner?" answered by the simulator inside the contract's bounds |

## Not a fit, and not claimed
- Vendor side (P2P): the accounts give trade payables only as a level (Rs 25.92 Cr), no vendor terms. The pack shows P2P as "Not published", never a payables-days figure, and never paying later as a saving.
- Client lens: no client-level receivable split is published. "Not published".
- Cost control, budget against actual, scheduling: Crestline is none of these (fixed page 14 keeps "Not a margin or cost-control tool.").

## What the pack can say about the ERP, and what it cannot
- Can: "your ERP, introduced in FY26"; Crestline reads it read-only, alongside it, once connected; the adapter is built first; 14 days of read-only analysis start after the connection is approved.
- Cannot: name a vendor; say Crestline connects today; say anything about the ERP's quality or data.

## Pilot scope
One entity, one segment, read-only, 14 days, on one to three project sites of Effwa's choice, in rupees.
