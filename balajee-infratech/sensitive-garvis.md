## Other items in the rationales
- Bank guarantees worth Rs 2.36 crore were invoked by one client over two years; management called it a one-off (ICRA-23, ICRA-24 p1; the 2024 text says "amicably settled"). The amount was paid to the banks before the bank paid under the guarantee.
- Declining profitability: PBILDT margin 7.13% in FY24 against 10-11% in FY21-23 (CARE p2). FY24 PAT margin rose only through "profit from liquidation of investments" (CARE p2).
- Operating cash flow Rs 0.93 crore in FY24 "owing to significant blockage of funds in the inventory" (CARE p3).
- Client concentration: top three clients 86% of the unexecuted order book at 31 Mar 2023 and 83% at 30 Nov 2023 (ICRA-23, ICRA-24). Top five orders about 74% (Mar 2023). Latest (CARE) gives no client split.
- CARE's own negative triggers: GCA days above 265, TOL/TNW above 1.50x (FY24 actual 1.37x), TOI below Rs 600 crore, PBILDT margin below 6% (CARE p1).
- "Stuck orders" are excluded from CARE's order book (CARE p1); the amount is not disclosed.
- The company "has, in the past, availed interest-bearing mobilisation" advances (ICRA-24 p1); CARE (Apr 2025) describes receipt of interest-free mobilisation advances. Both quoted; the difference is not explained.
