# Claude Design master prompt: Balajee working-capital review (one booklet, per-reader editions)

Paste everything below this line into Claude Design. Upload the Crestline logo file and the Balajee logo file before you send it. The Balajee logo is used exactly as supplied.

---

## 1. What you are building

A 16-page A4 portrait booklet titled "Working-capital review", printed and posted to named readers at Balajee Infratech and Constructions Private Limited. You build ONE design and produce one edition per reader listed in the reader table (see "Reader editions" below).
It answers one question for the reader: what if Crestline were already running at Balajee, how much money and how much time would it save?
Every word and every figure is supplied in the Content section at the end of this prompt. Your job is layout, type and charts, in the design canvas you are working in.

## 2. Reader editions

Build ONE 16-page design. Then, for each row of the reader table in the Content section, produce one edition of it.
- An edition changes ONLY three things: the cover's "PREPARED EXCLUSIVELY FOR" lines (that reader's name and title), the page 2 letter (that reader's letter text and address block), and the page 2 salutation.
- Every other page, word, figure, chart and position is IDENTICAL across editions. Do not tailor any body page to a reader.
- Name each edition after its reader as given in the table (for example "Edition: <reader name>"). That label is for the file only and is never printed.
- Body copy is written in a neutral voice. Quotes are credited by name and title, as the Content gives them; never rewrite a credit into "you" for any edition.

## 3. Rules you must follow

1. Use ONLY the text and figures in the Content section. Do not write new copy, captions, taglines, teasers, pull quotes or headings. If a line has no text, leave it empty.
2. Copy every figure exactly as written: same digits, same decimals, same currency sign, same unit. Do not round, re-total, convert or recompute anything.
3. Headlines exactly as given for each page, split into two lines as shown. Never reword them.
4. Footnotes exactly as given, on the page they belong to.
5. Lines that begin with ">" in the Content are printed copy; the ">" mark itself is not printed. Every other line is an instruction to you and is never printed.
6. No internal references in printed text. Reference codes, file names, status words used as markers and any note addressed to the team are never printed. A source is printed only in the words the Content gives for it.
7. No printed negatives about the booklet itself: never print what is missing, unknown or left out. Where a line is empty, leave the space empty. Print a "Not published" line only where the Content gives it as printed copy.
8. No em dashes and no en dashes anywhere. Use a comma, a full stop, "to" or a middle dot (·) as the Content does.
9. Ownership words stay as written: the client certifies and pays; Balajee measures, prepares, reviews, submits and invoices. Do not swap them.
10. The Balajee logo appears only if the client uploaded it, used as supplied. Never redraw, trace, recolour or imitate it. Without it, the cover shows the reader's name only and no company mark.

### Booklet accuracy rules (apply to every page, every edition)
B1. **Claims about Crestline must be true today.** Make no claim about Crestline beyond the pasted text. No certification "in progress", no hosting region, no encryption claim, no client names, no "live in X days", and no present-tense claim that Crestline connects to the company's ERP. The pilot is one entity, one segment, read-only, 14 days, as the Content words it.
B2. **Every quote names the right speaker**, exactly as the Content credits it. Never move a quote to another person.
B3. **Ours versus the client's follows the product rule.** In cash-in steps, acknowledged, certified and collected are the client's acts; measuring, preparing, reviewing, submitting and invoicing are the company's own. Keep every OURS and CLIENT tag as given.
B4. **No "nothing went wrong" headlines.** Use only the headlines given; never soften or invert one.
B5. **Charts drawn to scale.** Every bar, segment, column and point sits within 2% of its own value on ONE shared scale per chart, starting at zero. Every bar shows its value label.
B6. **Interest only at a rate the company discloses.** Print only the rate and interest figures the Content gives; never compute or label a rate as theirs.
B7. **Savings are scenarios.** Anything marked "with Crestline", "scenario", "dashed" or "days less" is drawn dashed and hollow with "Scenario, not a forecast." beside it. Scenario rows overlap and are never added together; print no total the Content does not give. Columns the Content gives as reported history get no scenario mark.
B8. **Peers and comparatives** appear only where the Content prints them, with the vintage it gives. Never build a scorecard of missed promises.
B9. **No other company's data.** Do not bring in any company, name, figure or design text from anywhere other than the Content, including from earlier booklets or your memory. No demo numbers beside the company's figures.
B10. **Full address and correct names and titles** on the letter page and cover, exactly as the reader table gives them, per edition.
B11. **"You said" only for the reader's own words.** Never print "you said", "in your own words" or any second-person credit over a quote by someone other than that edition's reader. Use the speaker's name and title or the company's name as the Content gives it. If a headline says "your" or "you" about a quote, check it in every edition; if the quote is not that reader's, rewrite the credit to the speaker's name and title in that edition. Never print a note or flag on the page.

B12. **Never invent figures to fill a chart.** If the Content gives a stage, step or bar without a value (for example the page 8 bill timeline), draw it without a number. Never create an "illustrative split" of a total, and never add a sentence about one.
B13. **No signed numbers in print.** Write inflow / outflow, increase / decrease. No "+" or "-" in front of a figure.

B14. **Every problem chart shows the outcome with Crestline.** Beside or on top of the "today" figure, draw the "with Crestline" result from the Content (dashed outline or hollow marker, same scale, labelled with the days and cash it releases, plus "scenario, not a forecast"). Use only the values the Content gives; if the Content gives no scenario value for a chart, show none (rule B12).

## 4. Before you finish, check every page of every edition

1. Count the pages: exactly 16, A4 portrait, in the order of the Content.
2. Read every headline against the Content: same words, same punctuation, line 2 in gold.
3. Pick every figure on every page and find it in the Content, character for character. Any figure you cannot find there, delete it.
4. Search the whole booklet for en dash and em dash characters (Unicode 2013 and 2014). There must be none.
5. Every chart: one scale per chart, each bar within 2% of its value, every bar labelled, nothing drawn from a figure that is not in the Content.
6. Every scenario mark is dashed and hollow and carries "Scenario, not a forecast." Reported history columns carry none. No scenario totals added.
7. Every footnote is on its page, exactly as given.
8. No word was added: no taglines, no right-hand footer teasers, no extra captions, no "Live in" line, no certification or hosting claim, no client names, no internal references, no printed negatives.
9. The Balajee logo is the uploaded file or absent. Nothing redrawn.
10. The QR code on page 15 (and on page 16) opens crestlineintelligence.com/book-a-demo.
11. Instruction lines from the Content (every line that does not begin with ">") are not printed anywhere.
12. Editions: compare each edition with the first. Only the cover reader lines, the page 2 letter and the salutation differ. No "you said" sits over a quote that is not that edition's reader's own.
