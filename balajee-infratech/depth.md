# Balajee Infratech: story angle (step 1)

Every figure here is in data.md with its source. This file is framing only.

## Respect first
- Founded 1995 as a group by the late Pashupatinath Dokania, starting with mining work at the Jamnagar refinery for L&T (company site); the company was incorporated 18 Oct 1999 (MGT-7). Now run by his four sons, who hold 98.99% between them (MGT-7).
- Repeat work from named industrial clients: ITD Cementation, L&T, ArcelorMittal Nippon Steel, Afcons (CARE, ICRA).
- Growth: Rs 672.13 crore (FY23) to Rs 922.70 crore (FY24) in TOI (CARE); Rs 963.004 crore turnover in FY25 on the annual-return definition (MGT-7).
- Work with real engineering range: airport runways and ILS works, iron-ore tailing dams, test tracks, quarrying and crushing, all across ten states (CARE; company site).
- CARE says the track record is of more than two decades, delays have been granted extension "without penalty" because they sit with counterparties (CARE p2), and liquidity is adequate.

## Then the question: where do the bills wait?
CARE's own words give the question, with no outside material needed.
1. The company works for private corporates on credit of 30 to 60 days, and retention is held until the project completes (CARE p2).
2. Inventory (work in progress) "increased continuously in the last two years ended FY24 owing to delay in work certification and approval of escalation claims by certain counterparties" (CARE p2).
3. The result is adjusted GCA days of 229 in FY24 against 246 in FY23 (CARE p2), against a 30 to 60 day credit period. These are different measures: set them side by side as "what the terms say" and "what the accounts show", never as a computed difference.
4. The gap was bridged by creditors, mobilisation advances and working-capital borrowings (CARE p2). Fund-based plus non-fund-based limits ran at 85% utilisation on average to Jan 2025 (CARE p3). Operating cash flow was Rs 0.93 crore in FY24 (CARE p3).
5. About 42% of the Rs 1,705 crore order book had not started or was under 20% billed (CARE p1-2). Each new project opens the same queue: measure, certify, bill, hold retention, release.

The question a CFO or finance director can recognise: for each running bill and each escalation claim, which desk holds it today (ours to measure and prepare, the client's to certify), and since when? The accounts give the total; they cannot give the bill, the site or the desk.

## Where Crestline's frame fits (no ERP implied)
- Time x money on certification and escalation claims by site; bills waiting on the client's certification are the client's act, not the company's.
- Retention release dates priced in rupee-days.
- Mix of work (EPC civil against aggregates/RMC supply) reads as two cash clocks inside one company; CARE gives the 23% aggregates/RMC share of FY24 TOI (CARE p2), which should not be restated as a day figure.
