# Balajee Infratech: problems (step 2)

Every figure is Balajee Infratech and Constructions Private Limited, standalone. CARE = CARE Ratings press release, 23 Apr 2025 (audited FY23 and FY24). MGT-7 = Form MGT-7 for FY2024-25, filed Dec 2025. Re-traced against the raw text on 2026-10-09 (see qa-report.md).

## Respect first
- Total operating income ₹672.13 Cr (FY23) to ₹922.70 Cr (FY24), 37% growth. CARE p2-3.
- Profit after tax ₹24.97 Cr to ₹40.20 Cr. CARE p3.
- Order book ₹1,705 Cr at 31 Dec 2024, 1.85 times FY24 total operating income (CARE counts it excluding stuck orders). CARE p1.
- A track record of more than two decades; repeat contracts from reputed clients (names not printed). CARE p1-2.
- FY25 turnover ₹963.00 Cr on the Companies Act definition (MGT-7 p9). A different measure from total operating income. Prints once, never in a series with it, never in the calculation.

## Problem 1: work done waits to be certified
- CARE p2: "The company's inventory has increased continuously in the last two years ended FY24 owing to delay in work certification and approval of escalation claims by certain counterparties".
- Result in the accounts: adjusted gross current asset days 229 in FY24 (246 in FY23), excluding liquid investments. CARE p2. One measure, one basis.
- Certification is the client's act. The accounts give a total; they do not give the bill, the site, the desk or since when.

## Problem 2: escalation claims wait for approval
- Same CARE sentence. CARE p2 also says claims for cost escalations "filed with the client" are assessed "on case-to-case basis".
- Approval is the client's act. A claim is cash waiting on a desk; the accounts do not say which claim, how old, or whose desk.

## Problem 3: retention, and every new project opens the queue again
- CARE p2: "The retention deduction provision until project completion contributes to BICPL's working capital intensity."
- CARE p2: around 42% of the order book is yet to commence or at a nascent stage (billing of less than 20%). CARE p2: "majority projects are at nascent stage of execution" and the ability to manage working capital "shall remain a key monitorable".
- Retention release is the client's act; measuring, preparing and submitting the bill are the company's. Each running bill and each retention balance has a date that matters; the accounts hold the total only.

## Not used here (basis or sensitivity)
Client credit period of 30 to 60 days (CARE p2): a contractual term, not the same basis as the 229 days; not printed beside it. Operating cycle, debtor and inventory days, any creditor days, any interest rate, the rating grade and everything in sensitive-garvis.md.
